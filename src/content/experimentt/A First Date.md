Chloe never lets go of his hand as they walk. Maybe this would have been uncomfortable in any other situation, but it doesn't feel that way now. It's partially affected by Beth who's actively clinging to Caid's arm a few feet ahead, but he tries his best not to stare.

"What's wrong?" Chloe asks as she notices his eyes locked to the ground.

Ryan looks over, "Oh, I..." he glances back at the two lover birds ahead of them.

Chloe giggles as she lets go of his hand and grapples onto his arm, "Is this better?"

Ryan can feel his face grow hot even though he swears its below freezing at the moment. This physiological reaction only makes Chloe's dimples rise as her eyelashes lower just a bit.

"Have you ever been on a date before?" she asks as they continue to walk, the ordinary action functioning surprisingly easily given that Chloe's practically hanging off of him, "Would you believe me if I said this was my first?"

"Well," Ryan starts, he remembers her saying that she never dated in high school so it stands to reason that this would be her first date. Though he doesn't want to say that obvious fact aloud, "I guess we're the same."

"Is that so?" Chloe replies knowingly.

Ryan rubs the back of his head as he tries his best not to think about the pressure on his arm. They've slept in the same bed before, but that was sleep—something necessary and functional—this is neither. Not only that, normally when they talk, they're alone, but Beth and Caid are within earshot which means their conversation could be unofficially recorded in someone else's memory. Honestly, he didn't think he'd get so bothered by her distance... well, not bothered, more like... embarrassed? He just hopes that she can't see right through him like she has in the past.

With a deep breath, he forces himself to switch gears and think about something else. The first thing that pops in his mind completely erases any lingering hesitancy in favor of more reflective contemplation. 

"It's just like she said," he mutters softly to himself.

"Just like who said?" Chloe asks, listening quite attentively if she managed to hear his murmur.

Ryan glances off at the side of the road, "It's... it's nothing important."

The side clinging-hug thing quickly ends as Chloe voluntarily transitions back to simply holding hands. Ryan's not upset by this, but he does notice that a certain, subtle sense of pride dissipate as Chloe's body is carefully pulled away. Perhaps his former embarrassment may not have been the only thing he was feeling.

"Even if it's not important, you can still tell me," Chloe offers innocently.

Swallowing and taking a deep breath, Ryan orders his thoughts, "Beth made a comment earlier," Chloe's face drops as she glares at her best friend, "she said that Blake acts different when I'm around. I guess... I was just thinking about whether that's true for everyone. Weird thought right?"

Ryan's ramblings don't strike Chloe as odd, however, as she squeezes his hand a bit, "I don't think so. Blake does act different when you're around—and so do I. I think everyone behaves a bit differently when around different people."

"Yeah," Ryan comments lightly, "That makes sense... and sorry," Chloe looks over, "this probably isn't what most people talk about on a first date."

Chloe raises and eyebrow with a mildly exasperated look, "You really think I care about that?"

"Well no," Ryan tries.

"Always saying what you mean and meaning what you say is one of your best qualities Ryan," Chloe explains causing Ryan to freeze up a little, "I never have to guess what you mean, and honestly, you're kind of an open book most of the time."

"Really," Ryan responds, genuinely shocked by the assumption.

Chloe nods, "You may not open up about your past very easily, but I can tell you're still trying," she squeezes his hand again, "I think that's what I love most about you."

Ryan purses his lips and stares at the road below them. No one has ever said that about him. Not even close. He's not really sure how he's supposed to feel right now. Grateful? Confused? Both? If only he understood this sort of thing better, then he'd know what to say.

"Now," Chloe begins, "What do you love about me? That's a more fitting date question, don't you think?"

He knows he'll need a moment to think about that one and, thankfully, Chloe predicts that. She watches him closely but not in an expectant manner—not at all—instead she just waits patiently and occasionally checks in on their friends ahead. This offers Ryan enough time to look off at the sky, particularly the wispy clouds, and just let his thoughts simmer.

"I don't know," he begins, "it's hard to explain."

Chloe shrugs her shoulders, "Is it supposed to be easy?"

Ryan takes a deep breath, he knows he has to say something, it wouldn't be fair otherwise, "I guess... I've never had someone... someone who... erm..." he glances over to find Chloe quietly smiling at him as if every word were a gift, "I guess I've never had someone think about what I want."

Chloe's smile only grows larger, "See, was that so hard?"

"Hey guys," Beth calls out from up ahead, drawing both of their attention, "We're almost there."

As previously stated, their destination resides at the very edge of town: one of the first buildings after the fields end. Fixed to the front wall above the doors is a large pink sign with some fancy letters. The name doesn't ring a bell in Ryan's mind as it's French or maybe Italian, but it does make Chloe's eyes sparkle at the sight of it.

"Are you serious?" Caid comments dryly, "We came out to a perfume store?"

Beth's mouth drops, "A perfume store? This is not just a perfume store. This is the single best place to buy fragrances in existence."

"I'm surprised a small town has one," Chloe adds.

Beth glances back, "I know right. When I saw it, I was like 'oh my God we have to go'. And here we are."

Ryan finds himself staring at Chloe once again. Her eyes seem to glimmer in the sunlight as he can feel her hand start to shake with excitement. For some inexplicable reason, it makes him smile. When she looks back, she finds herself smiling too.

They approach the store with increased vigor as Beth requires Caid to hold her back less she break out into a sprint. Thankfully, Chloe doesn't need anyone to hold her back as she keeps pace with Ryan, though her eyes never leave the decorated letters over the door.

When they reach the front windows, Caid pulls Beth back and waves for Ryan to come closer, "We're going to make sure there are no dormant zombies in there."

Beth rolls her eyes as she crosses her arms, "Alright but make it quick."

And just like that, Caid and Ryan step inside the store with weapons in hand. 

The dark interior is lined with dense shelving and copious amounts of signage. He doesn't understand half the terms plastered on the walls and hanging from the ceiling but he assumes the girls will. In between the various displays is just enough room to walk through which becomes his main focus in making sure nothing is lying on the ground waiting for them. He can Caid walk through the store silently on either side, in the case that either of them stumbles on something, the other will know just by looking. 

They've developed a hand signal for times like these. A closed fist means stop: there's danger. One fingers means there's a dormant threat that can probably be taken out silently. Lastly a thumbs up means clear: no threats found. It's a system that he and Blake use primarily, but Caid seems to know it too as he holds up a thumb as they reach the end of the store. Ryan does the same.

They return to the front door and Caid opens it, stepping out of the way for Beth, "After you my lady."

Beth holds her chin up in a dignified manner, "Oh, how chivalrous of you."

Caid gestures with a small bow.

Chloe doesn't make a performative comment, however, as she quickly makes her way into the building and immediately starts inspecting the shelves. She picks up a particular bottle, reads the name and some text underneath, and then places it down only to grab another. If Ryan thought this was energetic, he wasn't ready for Beth's reaction. Beth, on the other hand, pushes half the products on a display to the side and proceeds to grab eight or nine small bottles and place them in the freed area. She then grabs tiny little slips of paper scattered around the store and proceeds to spray the strips with each bottom before holding them up to her nose. She repeats this process with all of the bottles she has and then elects to grab even more. All the while, Caid and Ryan just look at each other and laugh.

"Caid!" Beth exclaims as she smells the scent of a perfume from a translucent purple bottle, "Come smell this, it's amazing!"

Caid chuckles something under this breath as he heads over to try it. In the meantime, Ryan meanders over to Chloe who's calmed quite a bit and now holds a bottle in either hand as if comparing ingredient labels at a grocery store.

"You probably think we're crazy, don't you?" Chloe surmises as she sets on of the bottle down.

Ryan shakes his head, "No not really... just unexpected."

That is the simple truth too. Seeing the two girls overjoyed and excited has a way of lifting his spirits, he wouldn't call that crazy at all. Personally he doesn't understand the allure of scented sprays, but he'll be the last person to judge based on something as frivolous as that.

He picks up one of the bottle and inspects it with an untrained eye, "So what exactly are we looking for?"

Chloe narrows her eyes playfully as she grabs on to one of the odd strips of paper around the room. She then points the bottle in her hand towards the paper and sprays some of the contents. When the paper turns a sufficiently dark shade of purple, she holds it up to him, "Smell it."

Complying with the demand, he leans over and takes a whiff of the peculiar fragrance. It has a strikingly fruity aroma, one that he can nearly taste if it were pungent enough. Matching that fruity note is another more subtle one which he can only describe as flowery. Beyond that, he doesn't really know what he's smelling.

"I really like the lighter one," Chloe explains, a little beyond Ryan's understanding, "I'm not much of a citricy girl."

During his casual explanation, he notices that she's still wearing the red ladybug earrings. When they were in the prison, she tended to avoid wearing earrings exception on an odd occasion or two. After the prison, Chloe almost stopped wearing earrings altogether. This is probably why her ladybug earrings caught his attention so easily. It just so happens that Ryan distinctly remembers seeing these exact earrings before. 

"You're not much of a red girl either," he comments.

Chloe glances over with a bit of confusion, "Hm?"

Ryan points to his own ear, "You're earrings, you said red's not your color. I was just surprised to see you wearing them."

As if his words weren't enough, Chloe quickly touches her ear to feel the red, circular pieces of jewelry waiting there. Her cheeks grow a little red as she looks at the floor, "Oh, you remembered that. You see, these are the only earrings I've got."

"And you don't like them?" Ryan wonders honestly.

Chloe shakes her head, "That's not it. I'm actually thinking that red might suit me a bit more than I thought. I'm just a little embarrassed because I used to have a lot more of these sorts of things."

Ryan looks around at the hundreds of bottles decorating the walls and display shelves, "Things like this?"

The store is particularly large by any stretch of the imagination, but it's still the largest collection of perfumes he's ever seen before. Chloe looks around at the store as well and releases a drawn out sigh.

"I'm not huge into jewelry like some girls I knew," Chloe begins as she ponders the multitude of colored bottles, "I just liked it... the normal amount. Though, my mom did like jewelry quite a bit, so I ended up coming to these kinds of shops quite often. My mom was a bit more on the frugal side so she didn't buy things often, but when she did, she would buy me something too," she laughs to herself, "I ended up having a whole box full of perfumes I was a bit too young to truly appreciate."

Ryan picks up a bottle but ends up setting it right back down without even glancing at it, "It sounds like she cared though. You know, because she brought you alone."

Chloe rolls her eyes, "Bringing me along felt like a pretext to go shopping half the time. I suspect it was easier to convince herself to make a purchase when it was for me instead of for herself."

Ryan laughs lightly as he picks up another bottle only to set it down again. Chloe catches on to this method rather quickly.

"You don't have to pretend you're interested," she says.

"I am interested," he says casually as his eyes gloss over a few other random bottles, "just not in perfume."

The front door contains a wide glass window revealing the outside street with great clarity. He finds himself staring out at a snow drift across the street. Why it catches his eye, he has no idea, but it does provide for a brief distraction. When he looks back at Chloe, he finds her blushing and sorting through a few bottles without looking up.

"Is that so?" she comments when she notices Ryan looking at her.

With a quizzical look, Ryan shrugs his shoulders and decides to grab one of the little slips of paper. The bottle he eventually selects is rather arbitrary, only really being determined because it has a pretty picture of a tree on the front. He presses the top down and sprays some of its contents onto the paper. Then, without flinching, he holds it over to Chloe.

"Smell it."

Chloe recoils a bit, "Why? Shouldn't you smell it?"

Ryan shakes his head, "They all smell the same to me, so you'll have to tell me which one's good."

Chloe exhales in a humorous manner before pulling her bangs back and smelling the paper. While she does, Ryan can't help but notice the way she folds her hair over her ear. It's such a simple gesture that he finds it odd that he even noticed, yet... something about it draws his attention.

"I'm not a fan of citric but think this one is good," Chloe decides after taking a second sniff, "I think this one might actually be for men."

"How can you tell," Ryan looks around the room for any indication that it was a gendered perfume.

Chloe eyes widen a bit before she giggles, "You really don't know?"

He shakes his head.

She promptly points out the detail he was missing, and it makes him mentally slap his head. Plastered behind a set of three perfumes—or cologne—is a conspicuous image of a shirtless male model. The model has the sort of stereotypical masculine physique that you see in movies, and he holds the cologne as if trying to seduce someone with it.

"Oh," he says a little forcefully, "I didn't realize this store sold things for me."

Chloe giggles again, "This place is mostly for women, but there's still a little bit for everyone."

"I see," he says awkwardly as he sets the cologne back down on the shelf. It's annoying that he's managed to avoid staring at any of the suggestive female models, but the singular male model is completely transparent to him. 

That's the moment that Beth burst into the scene and grabs Chloe's hand, "You have to come see this, I didn't think they'd have it, but it's the same one my mother got me last year."

Chloe's eyes widen, "Serious?! We've never been able to find it."

Beth nods as she starts tugging her away, "Come on! Come on!"

With that, the two girl transition over to the far corner of the store. Ryan's solitude doesn't last long, however, as Caid meanders over to him.

"You bored too?" Caid wonders as he watches his girlfriend rant about a perfume with her best friend.

Ryan shakes his head, "I wouldn't say so."

Caid raises an eyebrow, "Shoot. Good for you. This isn't my sort of thing though."

"Maybe it doesn't have to be" Ryan realizes aloud, "can't you just enjoy the simplicity?"

After a brief moment, Caid nods his head a few times, "Yeah, maybe you're right. Gotta live for the small things."

The four of them go on to package nine distinct bottles inside the brand's signature bag. This would ordinarily signify the end to their visit—and the end of their double date, but Beth's plan wasn't so one dimensional.

As they departed from the store with Caid volunteering to carry the "haul", as the girls call it, Beth swings around and stops the whole group. She smiles broadly as her hands are placed confidently on her hips, "One more stop?"

"Where?" Chloe asks.

Beth points down the street towards town, "There's a library down there. I think we can end our date there. Besides, we've still got plenty of time before the sun sets."

"I don't know," Caid says hesitantly, "We did agree not to go too far into town."

Rolling her eyes, Beth nods, "Yeah I know. I'm not pulling a Max here, but I genuinely think it'll be worth stopping at. We don't have many books back home, and I'm not about to die of boredom."

Caid purses his lips as he glances back towards Ryan, "What do you think?"

Almost instinctively, Ryan shrugs. He actually does know where the library is—he and Blake had passed it multiple times in the past. As far as he's concerned, that's neither deep into town, nor a particularly dangerous part of it. So long as he and Caid clear the building first, it should make for a reasonably safe location.

"I'm fine with it," Ryan eventually decides.

Beth nods affirmingly, "I knew you'd understand."

Caid sighs, "alright then. Let's go."

As they head deeper into town, Ryan pays considerably close attention to their surroundings. This does, incidentally, stall any conversation between him and Chloe, but she doesn't seem to mind in the slightest. In fact, she flashes a stray smirk at him every once in a while as he looks around corners and through distant windows. If anything, she seems to be enjoying herself a bit too much by watching him keep guard.

When they finally arrive at the library, he and Caid quickly proceed inside with weapons drawn. They carefully scout out the building stocked with thousands of books and shelves as tall as their arms can reach. By the time they reach the back of the library, they wind up finding zero hostile threats. The girls enter shortly after that.

Now Ryan finds himself in yet another unexpected situation. He's never even read a mystery book in his life yet here he is browsing through a vast two shelves of rustic-looking novels. Chloe, standing directly beside him, fingers through a book he vaguely remembers seeing in class once. He never read the book, but he still remembers the cover. She holds an unreadable expression as she glazes over page after page at a pace he didn't know people could read at.

He reaches for a random book with a colorful cover when he notices Chloe standing a bit closer to him—close enough that their shoulders touch. She doesn't seem to notice as her attention remains fixed on the book in hand, so he brushes it off and pulls the book out. 

The selected book is startingly heavy for a book, but he doesn't realize this until the pulls it off the shelf and nearly drops it on the floor. Chloe giggles before going back to her book. Ryan proceeds to dust the cover off, though there isn't really much dust, and pops open the front page. It's a short list of dedications so he flips to the next where he's met with a brick of words. The first few sentences are fine, but then he notices a few words he, frankly, isn't familiar with so he decides it's not for him.

He closes the book and slides it back into place.

"Were you a reader?" he asks idly as he struggles to fit the large book back in amidst the falling books beside it.

Chloe looks up from her book, "Hm? Oh. Yeah, I love to read. Beth probably wanted to come here for my sake more than anything else," she laughs softly, "Beth hardly even reads to begin with. I nearly burst out laughing when she claimed she was going to die of boredom."

"Really," Ryan comments, "Could've fooled me."

She gives him a knowing look before returning to her book.

Once he finishes pushing the book back into its rightful spot, he decides it might be better to check out another section. He read a few popular action novels in middle school and those weren't that bad, perhaps he might find another here that he likes. Starting to take a few steps away, he searches for some signage that might indicate the desired location. He isn't able to get far, however, as he feels something tugging on his coat. When he looks back, he finds Chloe grabbing on to with one hand while the other comfortably holds the book.

Since he moved, she quickly takes notice and promptly lets go, "Oh, sorry... I didn't realize," she hides her eyes pretending to be looking at a different book.

Ryan studies her for a moment before coming up with a response, "I was going to go check out a different section... want to come with?"

Chloe looks up from the shelf with a shallow smile, "Okay."

It doesn't take him long to spot the sign indicating the adventure section—which he thinks is the same as action books. Wanting to peruse that section, he leads Chloe over only to stop right before stepping into the cramped aisle.

Further down the row of tall shelves is Beth setting on the third row of books with her legs wrapped around Caid's hips. Her arms lay atop his shoulders while he holds her steady. Luckily neither of them have their eyes open so they don't notice Ryan right away, but he steps back before investigating what they're doing with their mouths. They don't leave much for the imagination, however, as he can hear faint pleasureful exhales.

He turns back to Chloe and places his index finger across his lips while shaking his head. At first she's a bit confused by what's going on, but then her ears are hit with the subtle sounds occurring in the aisle and her eyes widen. Together they back off and return to the mystery section.

"They weren't..." Chloe trails off quietly.

Ryan shakes his head, "I don't want to think about it."

Setting the book down on the shelf, Chloe shifts uncomfortably in place. She tries to hide it by inspecting a nearby book, but it's clear that her thoughts are elsewhere.

"You know," she says softly, "if you want... we... never mind. Forget I said anything."

Ryan studies her carefully in silence as she refuses to look up from the shelves. Even the book she was previously absorbed in doesn't break her awkward stance and strange insistence on bouncing between covers. With no real direction to go off of, Ryan grabs the first manageably-sized book and gestures with his head.

"Come on, let's go find somewhere to sit."

Chloe finally looks up, though with a small delay, before grabbing onto her mystery novel and nodding her head. Together they head in the opposite direction of the adventure section and find a small open area with various lounging fixtures. Almost as a relief, he finds a perfectly place couch at the edge of the room and takes a seat at it.

While he did expect Chloe to sit on the couch with him, he didn't expect her to sit directly beside him—virtually no gap between them. Chloe takes this with stride as she calmly opens her book, leans back, and picks up where she left off. Ryan, however, struggles to focus on his book with so much space on the other side of the couch.

He glances across the room at the adventure section. He wonders what they could be doing no—he shakes his mind off. That's not an image he wants to get in his head. It's not that they were doing anything irrational, but the mere thought of making out feels a bit too... intimate to be around for. It's one of those things that are best done behind closed doors, at least, Ryan thinks so. 

Chloe continues to flip through page after page of her novel without a care in the world while Ryan finds himself stuck on the first page. Now that he thinks about it, Chloe always seems to be the one to initiate any physical contact when they're alone in their room. He doesn't have a problem with it, but this one of the rare occasions where they are together but she's not doing anything. Part of him wants to respect her desire for simplicity, but another—more mischievous—part of him sees this as an opportunity. 

His heart pounds in his chest as he musters the courage to try something he's never done before. It's a strategy he's only ever seen in movies, but he hopes it doesn't come off ill-mannered or strange.

He sets his book to the side and stretches his arms into the air. As they come down, one of them takes a wider angle, though briefly. Before they can come all the way down, he stops. His mind races at such a simple action and now he finds himself paralyzed. With any built up courage finally vanishing, he gives up and brings his arms together intending to return them to where they started.

Chloe stops reading and look over at him with a undecipherable expression. For one horrifying second, Ryan wonders if she noticed his half-baked plan. Then, after setting her book down, she falls into him while wrapping her arms around his torso.

"Is this what you wanted?" she asks as his arms have nowhere to go but around her.

He finds it hard to breath all of the sudden but he forces himself to do it regardless. His heart continues to pound in his chest as his joints all but lock up. They've been this close before, why does it feel different all of the sudden? He never got so flustered when they were in their room. What is going on?

While his head spins, Chloe releases tension from her embrace and pulls back a bit, "You can always tell me to stop if I make you uncomfortable."

Ryan knows he's at a crossroads here so he forces himself to take a deep breath and calm down. Although he can't control his heart rate, he still can decide what he does about it. 

When he doesn't respond, Chloe starts to pull away but Ryan won't let her. Instead of giving in and letting it end, Ryan pulls her closer to which actually takes her by surprise. The one who initiated the embrace quickly finds herself flustered as one of her arms gets trapped between herself and him. So with seemingly no choice, she too takes a deep breath and settles in.

A minute goes by with neither one of them saying a word. Eventually sitting becomes too much a chore and they sink further into the couch. Before long, Ryan's back is placed firmly against the couch with Chloe laying on top of him, her head rests on his shoulder. Instead of watching each other, they both keep their eyes closed for a moment—simply listening to one another breath and feeling their hearts beat. Chloe's coat quickly becomes to cumbersome to wear so she takes it off and lays it over top of them like a small blanket before returning to her seemingly comfortable position. 

"You're right," Chloe says softly, "I like this more than a book."

She traces his arm until their hands meet. She then interlocks his finger between his. He wasn't wearing gloves before but he never realized that Chloe had taken hers off too. The warmth from her fingers smooth fingers sends a chill down his spine which gives her a short lived laugh.

"Is this... what you normally do on a first date?" Ryan wonders.

"Don't know. This is my first."

With his eyes still closed, he rubs his hand across her back, "Would you say it's memorable?"

Chloe nods her head against his shoulder, "I'll never forget this. Thank you."

Ryan doesn't know why he's being thanked but disagreeing with her wouldn't be right. Instead he lets that uncertainty pass and elects to be present in the moment. 

They don't exchange words for quite a while. At some point, Chloe's hold on his hand loosens, though it never disengages. Her breathing steadies out along with her heart rate all the while, Ryan listens and feels carefully with his eyes closed. This tranquil moment is only disturbed when he feels someone's presence enter the room.

Opening his eyes, he finds Beth and Caid approaching. Beth steps out and looks like she's about to say something but then she notices Chloe's face. Ryan, who had his eyes close before this, also looks to finds a peaceful, unguarded form resting on his shoulder. He had seen Chloe asleep before, but never quite like this—without tension in her expression, without the faint suggestion that she might wake at the first sound.

He looks up to Beth who holds a wide smirk and gesture for Caid to be quiet.

"We should probably get going," Beth whispers, just loud enough for Ryan to hear.

Ryan looks down at the innocent girl in his arms. Part of him never wants to give this up for the world, yet another part of him knows it can't last forever. Either way, he's glad he made this decision.

"Alright," Ryan nods with a hushed tone. He then shakes Chloe just enough for her eyes to flutter open, "It's time to go."
