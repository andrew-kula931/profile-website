The following day brings with it a bright sun that melts enough snow for them to walk down the road with ease. The temperature is still quite chilly but the diminished snow drifts starts them off with high spirits. A line of clouds on the horizon, however, threatens their joy.

Ryan and Blake pass a small clump of metal scrap on the side of the road probably fallen off of a dump truck or something. From the pile of rubbish Ryan spots a propeller looking piece of metal. It reminds him of a drone propeller.

“Say, Blake,” Ryan starts.

Blake looks over with his hands in his pockets, clearly engrained in some deep thinking, “Hm?”

“Have I ever told you about that first zombie I ever encountered?”

Blake gives him a confused look, “What? In the school?”

Ryan shakes his head, “No, before that.”

Blake glances off at a nearby ditch as they pass on opposite sides of a broken down car. After passing, they reconvene together and continue walking side by side.

Talking about past events isn't something Ryan finds himself doing very often, but he feels particularly talkative today. Ever since their little date night he's felt quite hopeful for the future, and incidentally, he's felt more talkative.

“Are you telling me that you saw a zombie before the school incident?” Blake musses as he takes the opportunity to ensure his survival knife is still strapped to his belt.

Ryan nods, finding himself unable to remove the subtle smirk on his face, “Yeah, the day before the incident I saw this woman eat her dog. I was stupid enough to want to investigate so I used my little brother’s drone to check out the inside of her house. As you probably guessed, the zombie ended up finding me. Had to stab her in the back.”

His recounting of the story feels abysmally inadequate, yet for some reason he doesn't regret saying it. It's almost as if he wants Blake to know about what happened. What a strange feeling.

Blake raises his eyebrows, “Wow, it’s crazy to think that all of that took place three months ago. It feels like a lifetime since then.”

“Ain’t that right,” Ryan agrees, "Things really have changed."

They smile at each other and look off in the distance at the slowly approaching town. Their time of peace and quiet will soon be ending.

"I'm feeling hopeful about this," Blake says out of nowhere, "We may have heard terrible things, but I believe there's a rational explanation."

Ryan gives him an incredulous look, "Do you really think we'd join them."

On cue, Blake shakes his head with pursed lips, "No, not ever. I just think we won't have to run from them... or at least I hope so. I'm tried of being always being edge."

They continue in silence for a while after that.

As Martin explained, the West side of town—the side with the museum—remains as barren as it ever was. As they head East, they quickly notice a steep drop off in destruction. The town remained in relatively good condition considering how many zombies were in the museum, but that didn't save multiple shattered windows and broken down doors. As they go further, however, these occurrences all but disappear. The buildings remain as untouched as they day were constructed. 

They continue advancing down the road for a few more blocks before they spot something in the middle of the street. At first glance, it looks like some kind of trash pile. There is some kind of pole sticking out with strange discoloration but they can’t make out what it is at a distance. Only as they get closer the pile do they noticed that the structured rubbish is anything but trash. The closer they get, the eerier the surroundings seem to feel until eventually their fears are realized.

The pile before them reeks of the pungent stench of decay as the whole base of the ritualistic construction is created out of human bones. A wooden pole sticks out from the center of the pile frozen chunks of flesh nailed to it. At the top of the wooden pole is the most horrific part of it all: a woman’s head still twisted in pure agony. The expression of being ripped apart permanently cemented upon the poor victim’s face. Her features or frozen over and her skin is a light purple, but it's clear this was no quick death. He thought he had seen the worst of it, but then his gaze lowers and his heart skips a beat. While Blake covers his mouth to hold back vomit, Ryan freezes to the core.

Resting beside the satanic effigy is a small note in poor handwriting: Experiment #238 Biological Rejection.

Ryan walks past the demented scene without looking back, his gaze fixed on the ground. If this is what he thinks it is, blood is going to be spilt. Blake takes another moment to let the sight sink in before he too follows past it into the East end of town.

They turn a corner down a main street and are immediately met with human faces looking at them. Half a dozen people are scattered around the nearby area watching in silence as they witness two brand new faces enter the scene. All of the townsfolk look like civilized individuals going about their day, none would strike Ryan as people to do such horrendous deeds. Then, as if to further their strange behavior, they look away from Blake and Ryan and continue on their daily routines indifferent to the strangers. That is, except for a single individual who turns on their heels and hurriedly hustles down the street away from them. 

Ryan and Blake exchange confused glances before continuing down the street into one of the last bastions of humanity acting like the apocalypse never happened. Both of them keep a hand at the ready to draw a weapon if anything appears more suspicious than they're willing to accept.

Not a single person tries to strike a conversation with them or even ask who they are. To some degree, they might as well be invisible. The entire time, Ryan can't shake the eerie feeling that he's seem something similar before. What he's feeling, he has no idea, but he knows deep down that their indifferent dispositions are frighteningly familiar. 

“It’s like we’re barely here,” Blake comments cryptically.

"Yeah," Ryan replies.

They expected to be shunned from society as outsiders or maybe even attacked to defend their peace but they're being treated like tourists walking casually through the streets admiring the scenery. The only person that acted in an expected manner was the single person who ran off.

“So you must be the stranger?” says a rather eccentric voice from behind them.

They both snap around to find a shorter man with short unkempt scruff on his chin and messy hair. His skin is a bit wrinkly and bags hang under his eyes but he doesn’t look to be any older than his late forties. He has on a brown robe like a medieval monk but the sleeves are ripped off revealing thin, boney arms with long stick-like fingers. A black leather belt wraps around his waist with a few vials of dark shaded liquids within each. His eyes glow with a sense of crazy passion as he glances between the two much younger, taller men before him. 

Ryan gauges the fellow carefully but ultimately decides it can't possible be the Creep. Although their attire is rather similar, this person actually passes as human and is about six inches shorter, "Who are you?"

The strange man bows ceremoniously by folding one hand in front and sticking the other arm in the air, “My name is Experiment P, but you may refer to me as Pesh. I am the first secretary of the wise and honorable Doctor Crane,” the short man speaks matter-of-factly yet still sounds like a child explaining his imagination to their parents. Thought after he finishes, he exhales rather forcefully.

"Did you make that thing?!" Blake asserts drawing his knife.

The man doesn’t respond nor even look at Blake as his attention remains solely on Ryan.

“Hey, I asked you a question!” Blake demands a bit more forcefully this time.

“Silence peasant,” Pesh burst out aggressively, "Humans have no need to speak," he turns to Ryan, "But an experiment candidate... I..." he struggles to get the last part out, "implore you to answer."

Blake nearly explodes with another argument or maybe even a threat with his knife, but Ryan steps in front to block him off. He simply shakes his head which causes Blake to back off.

"Did you do that?" Ryan asks in Blake's stead.

Pesh half smiles but ultimately shakes his head while scratching his unkempt scruff, "Oh, no. I haven't the faintest understanding of it's purpose, but M-master Crane will understand."

For a split second, Pesh's head shakes while his lip quivers, not in a somber way, but an agitated one. Pain flashes through his face before immediately disappearing. Pesh's demeanor returns to that of an interested investor seeking out a business prospect. This is reinforced by the fact that he greedily rubs his hands together.

"What the hell is this?" Blake questions with irritation.

Pesh scowls at Blake with a look so menacing it must have come from someone far scarier than this small pipsqueak of a man. It almost appears as if he's witnessing some unjustifiable injustice occur before him. Blake even takes a step back at the startling sight.

Pesh's voice lowers to one far more sinister than his previously upbeat and energetic demeanor, “I don’t like to repeat myself. Do not test my patience again.”

Ryan looks back to Blake, “It’s fine, I can handle this.”

Returning to the Pesh, Ryan crosses his arms and gives him a much more serious annoyed look, "Who is Doctor Crane?"

Immediately, he's met with a confused look, as if the question itself is irrational.

"You do not know?" Pesh wonders, "My m-m-m" he stutters quite extensively on the word, "m-master assured me that you had met."

Ryan's eyes narrow as he draws his knife. All the signs are there, choosing to believe there is any future where his suspicious is not realized would be utter ignorance. They're dealing with a cultist alright, a cultist he thought was long since dead.

"I actually do know him," Ryan decides catching Blake by surprised.

"Seriously?!" Blake exclaims.

"Silence!" Pesh demands with a booming voice as he steps forward.

Blake tries to ward the man off by hold out his knife, but Pesh isn't having it. He places his palm right on the blade of the knife and proceeds to snap the metal in half with a twist of the wrist. The man then glares up at Blake, "You do not interrupt experiments. Do you understand."

Blake finds himself in utter disbelief as he looks down slowly at this shattered knife. The steel broken in half with a trace amount of blood left behind by the effort. 

Ryan prepares to retaliate by drawing his knife back, but Pesh beats him too it by grabbing onto his wrist. The small man's grip tightens around Ryan's arm and doesn't budge. Even though Ryan is physically larger than the man, Pesh's strength far exceeds anything Ryan has ever experienced. This must be the strength of ten men Mark had warned them about.

Throwing Ryan's hand back with enough force to make him stumble backwards, Pesh glares the abnormal knife, "That's not a toy. Be careful." 

Immediately afterward, Pesh claps his hands together and a wide and Creepy grin growing on his face. His boney hands impact one another and create the clanking sound of bones smacking one another. How this frail man doesn’t find pain in that, Ryan doesn’t know.

"Let's abstain from violence, shall we?" Pesh says, "My master is expecting you."

Pesh then pulls off a vial from his belt and pours a generous gulp of deep violet liquid into his mouth, swallowing it quickly. He wipes away some of the excess and releases a sigh of relief. Seemingly satisfied with the spontaneous action, Pesh turns around and begins to hobble—not walk—hobble off, "Right this way."

Ryan and Blake exchange glances, the latter clearly disapproving of the entire situation. From his perspective, that may be a justified stance, but Ryan knows that this Doctor Crane is not one to be trifled with. The important thing to do now is escape with their lives and get the hell out of here. Nowhere is safe when that Creep is around.

"Let's just go," Blake says quietly so only Ryan can hear.

"You should," he says simply, "he wants me. I doubt he'll let me leave."

Blake grabs on to his shoulder and points down the street they came from, "We can run, that guy's legs are too short to chase after. Heck, he can barely walk as is."

Ryan shakes his head, "Doctor Crane is supposed to be dead. If he's alive... then I don't think running will solve anything. You should go. I can do this alone."

Contemplating the situation, Blake takes a deep breath, "I'm not going to leave you behind. If we're doing this, we'll do it together."

Pesh leads them into a small bakery which turns out to be completely empty, not even furniture remains in the building. They pass by the barren room and head to a doorway in the back which opens up to a large staircase towards a dark and gloomy basement. Pesh halts in front of the doorway, "Only experiment candidates may proceed"

“We stick together,” Blake says simply without getting to riled up, “I won’t let him go alone.”

Pesh scowls at Blake but doesn’t overreact like before, "You may proceed if you desire, but be warned that Doctor Crane is in need of a subject for trial two hundred thilty-nine."

Ryan's blood runs cold. He turns back to Blake quickly, "Do not go down there." Blake opens his mouth but Ryan continues, "I'm going alone."

With that Ryan leaves his best friend with an old stranger and heads down into an ominous basement wreaking of uncertainty and death. 

The lighting of the stairs grow dimmer and dimmer the further he goes. Dust particles hang in the air casting a precarious atmosphere across the corridor which only growing in intensity as he gets closer to the basement. He expects a blood curling scream to ring out but that never transpires. The journey down the stairs feels like an eternity but he eventually reaches the floor and finds the last thing he wants to see. 

The basement is full of laboratory equipment scattered all around. Test tubes, scalpels, pipette, microscopes, and many he can’t name all litter a collection of tables around the dark, open room. Just like before, organic body parts hang on the walls, some human, some not. It seems like the laboratory experiments have expanded beyond human experimentation and now reached mutant studies. Ryan draws his knife ready to kill this Creep for the second time. A pungent odor of corpse mixed with bleach attacks his nostrils forcing him to cover his nose with one arm while the knife remains in the other. No living figure stands anywhere waiting for him so the likelihood of this being a trap only increases with each passing second. 

“It’s good to see you again Ryan,” spouts the demonic voice of a fallen adversary. 

Ryan slowly turns, his eyes full of fury, his body tense, his rage building. This is the voice of someone… or rather something that shouldn’t be alive. This Creep should have stayed dead.

A single dim light turns on across the room revealing a glass wall with a demonic figure standing behind. Fluorescent green ooze seeps out of the Creep’s mouth and eyes. It’s gray, wet skin reflecting the faint glow of the thick green liquid. Its white lab coat is now frayed at the ends and its hunched over body looking as abnormal as the day he decapitated it.

Ryan assumes the glass is not going to break by pounding on it so he doesn’t bother overreacting, he’ll kill this monster the first opportunity that he gets.

“How are you still alive,” Ryan scowls.

The Creep—or Doctor Crane—stands completely stationary, calmly watching his test subject, "If death were so convenient, don't you think we all would have done it?"

“Because... you're an experiment?” Ryan guesses, keeping his head tilted down with eyes of burning rage. He hasn't forgotten about Amber for a second.

The Creep pushes two fingers against his forehead in a disappointed manner, “I guess The Director didn’t fill you in on everything…”

“The Direct—” Ryan starts.

"Yes, yes," the Creep interrupts with a wave of his hand, "he is of no concern right now. I am simply appreciative that you have arrived. Are you prepared for the second dose? This one shall fundamentally alter the trajectory of this trial."

Looking around the room for something powerful enough to break this glass, he finds only large metal tables and minute lab equipment. With no noticeable points of entry to the other side of the glass wall, he decides it's best to focus on what he can control—understanding why the Creep's here and getting out alive. 

Begrudgingly, Ryan turns back to the monster, "You hardly got my consent the last time, why bother now?"

The Creep sighs and slouches his shoulders, "A true statement indeed, though you purged your freedom of choice when you regressed to violence."

Ryan tightens his grip on his knife, "Why?"

Previously fidgeting with something in his hands, the Creep suddenly stops. His body falls into a tense stasis as he stares at Ryan with a truly unreadable, inhuman visage. It's an expression that sends a chill down Ryan's spine.

"Astute observation paired with a hint a humor. All wrapped under the legitimate desire for clarification within the confines of my preferred vernacular," the Creep rambles to himself, "you are truly fascinating."

The Creep's unintelligible rambles don't interest Ryan, so he takes the opportunity to start drifting towards the door. If things take a turn, it might be better for him to simply book it. He fought this creep before and the fight somehow resulted in a draw. The likelihood of that happening again aren't in his favor.

"You never explained how you survived," Ryan points out, partially to buy himself more time while he slowly weaves between two tables.

The stairs are so close by this point. Whenever he feels it necessary, he's confident that he'll be able to make out of here. That sadistic scientist even has a wall between the two off them. With that it in mind, escape is clearly an option. Sure he still wants to properly avenge Amber, but he knows this is not the situation to do it. After all... he doesn't want to die here. He has something he wants to make it back to.

"I told you," the Creep begins as he picks up a syringe from a nearby table, "death is not an option for me. Physical attacks have no more effect on me than Experiment P. I'm sure you'll come to understand."

"I won't understand," Ryan says directly as he places his hand on the edge of the stairwell, "I want nothing to do with you."

This answer seems to disappoint the Creep as he sets the syringe back on the table and looks down, "so are you refusing to offer me your compliance."

Ryan places a foot solidly on the first step of the stairs, "the next time we meet, I'll make you feel what you put me through. What you put Amber through."

The Creep chuckles quietly, "No conjectures or hypotheses has ever led to a reliable understanding of why you maintain such a tenacity and will to live," he looks back up at Ryan with cynical expression, "I have resolved myself to gain your consent, but that does not hold for my subordinates."

He hears it immediately as a looming shadows covers the side of his face. Looking over, he finds Pesh standing a few steps up with a morbid look and clenched fists. Ryan prepares for a fight immediately, even going to far as to draw his gun, but his adversary doesn't react.

"I don't want to do this Ryan," Pesh says quietly as his eye twitches and his breathing grows unsteady, "Experiment V said that it warned you. Told you that you needed to die. Why didn't you listen?"

Ryan takes a step back and points the gun right at the short man's crooked frown, "Just let me out, I don't want to fight."

Pesh closes his eyes and looks down, "It's too late," as his eyes rise slowly, his lip quivers and his teeth grind together, "I don't have a choice."

It's the last thing he wanted to do, but Ryan finally has had enough and pulls the trigger. The bullet passes clean through Pesh's head but that does little more than leave an annoying whole in the man's head. As if boiling, his skin between to bubble up until the whole is completely filled in. Then the bubble effect subsides and all that is left is Pesh's normal head. This is the exact same trick that the Creep used. 

Falling the small spectacle, Ryan tries to rush forward and slash Pesh to pieces—a strategy that seemed to have some efficacy last time. Pesh, however, doesn't stand idly by anymore and promptly slams his fist forward, just to the side of the knife. Ryan tries to dodge but the attack clips his shoulder and makes him stumble into the wall. Impacting the stone wall he looses control and hits the ground.

He doesn't like the idea of being so close and prone so he immediately rolls over and pushes himself to his feet. Right as he's about turn around and fact the imp again, he feels to boney hands reach onto his hips. He instinctively slashes his knife in Pesh's direction and even makes a clean cut through his chest, but that doesn't prevent the imp from lifting him up and tossing him like a ragdoll across the room. As his head slams against the ceiling, he isn't able to stand back up right away. Instead, he fumbles to his knees and nearly collapses all over ahead.

The world spins while his head writhes in agony that would make a migraine feel like a inconvenience. His vision swirls creating a vortex of metal tables, gray walls, and a figure getting closer. The last thing he's able to comprehend is the figure stopping in front of him and shifting in place. Something swings into his face, but he's out cold before he's able to understand what it is.

When Ryan comes too his cheek if firmly places against the cold concrete floor. He looks off at the dimly lit surface weakly as he's only able to make out an empty syringe in front of him. At first he wonders why he's here, but then the memories flood back and he scrambles to his feet. The attempt to rise is halted as he finds every muscle in his body cramping and unwilling to budge. He eventually manages to regain control but not without the feeling that he just ran five marathons using every muscle group in his body.

He manages to rise slowly and inspects the reinforced glass across the room. The small hanging light has since been turned off and the Creep is nowhere to be found. A small amount of anger, or maybe frustration, arises in him but his splitting headache quickly pacifies those thoughts. Whatever got put into him is clearly has a stronger lingering effect than the last dose.

A glass bottle ahead of him falls to the floor and scatters against the ground. He blinks and then the glass bottle falls to the floor a second time. 

Any fatigue he was feeling before is quickly compounded with another brick of exhaustion which forces him to lean against a table for support. The haziness goes away fairly quickly but then the light flickers three times in a perfect rhythm. It returns to a steady brightness for a moment but then blinks in the exact same rhythm three times. His body feels weak once again only for the feeling to dull away just as quickly. 

Taking his hand off the table slowly, he takes a few steps towards the door only for him to feel weak once again and fall to the table and knock off a human finger. It lands on the ground with a soft bump, then a moment later, the exact same sound repeats as the finger seemingly blinks halfway up to the table and falls back to the ground. Immediately following the spectacle, his body feels weak once again, this time he collapses to the floor. The now dizziness takes a bit longer to fade but eventually subsides and he gets to his feet pushing himself towards the door with greater vigor. 

He takes step after step feeling only the lingering fatigue but then some six sense activates and his body goes limp once again. 

He blinks without closing his eyes and finds himself a few paces back leaning against the table watching the finger fall to the floor for the third time. His knees buckle in exhaustion.

What the hell is happening.

The world flashes once more and Ryan finds himself a few paces back watching a glass bottle shatter on the floor. A rhythmic blinking of the lights follows suit. Exhaustion strikes him like an unconscious envy as he stumbles. Falling over, he catches himself on a table—just barely—and tries to get back up slowly. His arms shake as if he'd done hundreds of pushups. 

His eyes don't budge yet he blinks. This time he finds himself laying face down on the floor staring at an empty syringe. He tries to jump up but his body gives out and he hits the floor hard. The writhing pain in his head blinding him from the impact with the floor. 

Upon standing back up with a long winded and careful effort, the glass bottle shatters on the floor and it is followed by the blinking of the light. He freezes.

This can't be real.

Looking around the room, he awaits the world to glitch out again. Surely he if just breathes and waits, he'll feel something and understand what's going on. The glass bottle should fall any second now. The light should flicker again, he knows it. Then the light flickers, though not in a steady patterned but a quick twitch before holding stead.

He looks down at the floor. What?

He blinks and an invisible sensation surges through his back. When the sensation ends, the world blinks, his face is against the concrete, and a tsunami of fatigue almost knocks him out again. The invisible muscle running from the tip of his head to the bottom of his feet offers him no indication of it's presence but he vaguely recalls how it felt when it surged. Simply out of curiosity, he attempts to flex that muscle. It works. He doesn't move, but the fatigue washes over him. Then, as he continues to lay on the floor, the glass bottle shatters and the light flickers.

It takes a considerable deal of energy to get himself back to his feet, but he inevitably manages that much. The room remains exactly as it was before which only fills him with more dread. His eyes land on the stairwell across the room and he wonders if he'll ever make it that far. Regardless, he has not choice but to try.

Each step he takes is presented with a unique challenge of avoiding another tumble. His legs shake and his knees threaten to buckle but he forces them to stay upright. The invisible muscle coursing across every fiber of his being tingles from time to time, but the world never flashes. At one point he can feel the onset of another blink, but is somehow able to manipulate it into staying dormant. It's not something he consciously decides but rather a general feeling he's forced to control. In a way, it's like walking but choosing to not sway your arms while doing so. 

With considerable effort, he arrives at the base of the stairs drenched in sweat. In his attempts to cross the room, he found himself suddenly appearing a step or two back and slapped with more fatigue. Each time he resists the urge to blink twice and keeps pushing forward. Now that he's here, his gaze falls to the floor at a round object resting beside the lowest stair.

A toothy grin smiles widely attached to a decapitated head. The face, lacking any features except a mouth, seems to be bidding him farewell with its smile. Small orange bits of chips remain in the pockets between the teeth revealing that it maintained its glutenous attitude until the end. He looks back into the room only to feel the slight and extremely subtle feeling deep within. The muscle tries to move, but he won't let it.

The stairs are just as they were before. He climbs them one at a time.

"Ryan!" Blake exclaims as his friend stumbles out of the basement door.

In the background, Pesh remains silent as he avoid eye contact altogether. Instead the short man stares out the bakery window with a melancholic look. 

"What happened down there?" Blake asks, "A moment ago a man wearing a mask walked by and told us to wait for you to come up."

"I'm not really sure," Ryan starts placing a hand against his forehead while finding his hair damp with sweat, "I... I think."

Pesh snaps his attention towards Ryan, "Careful now. Choose your words wisely."

Ryan struggles to remain standing but with Blake's help, he's able to, "That man did something to me..."

Pesh narrows his eyes.

"I have some sort of power," Ryan tries to explain, each word only barely able to make it out, "I can—"

Suddenly something flashes past his vision. At first he's too exhausted to comprehend it, but then he glances over at Blake. Only a stump of a neck remains as blood spews out across Ryan's face. Blake's body falls to the ground while waves off some blood from his hand.

With a menacing glare, Pesh simply shakes his head. He doesn't even say anything—only answering with a disapproving looking.

Ryan's eyes turn slowly to his friend's body on the floor, a wide pool of blood accumulating around his feet. He suddenly finds it hard to breathe, Jeremy flashes through his mind, Amber, Steve—

*Bang*

He collapses to the ground and slowly slides backwards. Blake's body remains completely unresponsive as the crimson pool seems to chase him across the room. With his hands scrambling backwards he slips and slams his head into the ground.

“What happened down there?” Blake asks, “A moment ago a man wearing a mask walked by and told us to wait for you to come up.”

Ryan looks at Blake, his head cleanly intact and his eyes full of worry. In disbelief, he slowly turns to Pesh who fixates his gaze out the window.

What the hell.

"Ryan?" Blake asks as he finds his friend seemingly in a trance.

What the hell. What the hell. What the fuck is wrong?

His hands lift up to his head as his voice leaks out as a whisper, "I already saw this".

*Crack*

Ryan's gaze rises slowly as he finds Pesh's hand inside Blake's head. Blood and brain matter oozing between the short man's finger before he casually throws Ryan's friend to the side. Pesh then glares at Ryan with a disappointing look.

Ryan's lip quivers as he attempts to formulate words but nothing comes out. His hand instinctively raises to his knife but then pathetically sags to his side. Is there even a point.

Making a conscious decision, tries to make the invisible muscle move. Nothing happens. He tries again. This time the scene shifts.

When Ryan regains clarity in his senses his gaze falls upon wet fingers in front of him. A bright red liquid oozes over Pesh's pale skin as he casually throws Blake off to the side. A disappointed scowl follows.

The world flashes again.

“What happened down there?” Blake asks, “A moment ago a man wearing a mask walked by and told us to wait for you to come up.”

"I—" Ryan tries as he falls into Blake's arms, nearly unconscious, "I just need a second."

"Ryan, are you okay?!" Blake exclaims frantically as he eases his friend to floor.

"Choose your words carefully," Pesh says in the background.

He just has to breathe. His head is throbbing, his hands are shaking, and he's covered in a sweat he hardly remembers, but he's still able to breathe. If only he could just sort though his thoughts and—and... his brain won't stop churning. Thoughts aren't making any sense.

"I..." Ryan starts before Blake can ask another question, he knows how that'll go even if he can't think straight, "I just had a chat. How long was I gone?"

Blake, not buying the obvious facade, takes a deep breath while place a hand on Ryan's shoulder, "I don't know. Maybe thirty minutes. The guy in the mask came by around ten minutes ago. Just tell me, what happened?"

Ryan looks up to find Pesh ominously glaring at him. On his knees, Ryan's eye level is actually lower the Pesh's which means the short man can actually frown down upon him which only works in his favor as Ryan shivers. 

"N-nothing happened," he stutters, "I just got spooked, really spooked."

The look of concern that carefully cements itself on Blake is nothing short of unnerving as Ryan almost feels guilty for not explaining things. If only... oh it doesn't matter.

"You're free to go," Pesh claims, as he steps over to the bakery door and turns the handle.

Both boys exchange glances before Blake helps Ryan up to his feet. Standing is extraordinarily difficult, but he manages—just barely. When Pesh doesn't waver in his claim and even waves for them to step out, they accept the offer reluctantly. With Blake's assistance, they depart from the empty store.

"Fret not," Pesh calls out to them, "We shall see one another again. Very soon in fact."

That's the last thing they hear before the bakery door swings closed.
