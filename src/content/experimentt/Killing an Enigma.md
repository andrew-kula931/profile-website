They end up taking a lengthy break in the convenience store two blocks down the road. This serves as both a safe, cleared shelter and a location without enough miscellaneous supplies to help them treat Ryan's wounds. It takes all the effort between himself, Chloe, and Blake to get him there, but he manages to fight through the pain and haziness without passing out.

When they finally do arrive to their destination, Ryan carefully set on the floor beside the checkout counter while Beth and Alison search the shelves for any serviceable supplies. Strangely enough, the store wasn't completely vacant as a few miscellaneous bags of junk food remained and the non-food sections seemed to be largely untouched. The multitude of salvageable items results in a rather calm search.

Beyond basic first aid—that same skill Steve taught him—Ryan knows absolutely nothing about first aid. As such, he assumed wrapping his arm and mentally tolerating the pain would be sufficient to keep himself alive. This line of thinking, however, becomes unapologetically flawed as Beth storms over with an armful of gauze and various other pieces of medical equipment he's only ever seen while skimming the drug aisle. She then proceeds to get into a conversation with Alison and Chloe about how best to treat the wound to avoid infection. He doesn't ask what that means but he assumes they know what they're doing—Caid even chimes in with some tips he learned after getting injured in basketball.

The next ten minutes are spent with a crowd around him and at least three separate pills being shoved into his mouth. Continuing to fight the unquenchable impulse to pass out and some mind numbing pain, he simply lets his friends do their thing. Even if there was something he could do, he decides staying quiet and taking it is probably the best thing he can do to put their minds at ease.

After the treatment is concluded and he finds his arm covered in a thick layer of white gauze, the crowd finally disburses through the building to collect some last minute supplies before heading back.

Blake leans against a nearby shelf and glances out the window as the snow wiping by. The white flakes constantly hit the ground but not a single one lasts on the ground before melting creating a wet environment without the glamor of persistent snow. 

"Crazy to think it's the first snow," Blake comments without taking his eyes off the scene outside.

"Yeah," Ryan agrees softly, his body still tired but he can feel a hint of control returning.

"Hey Ryan," Blake starts finally looking back at him. He hesitates for a moment before releasing a sigh and looking away, "Never mind."

Ryan deliberate decides not to pursue whatever was on Blake's mind. If it's important, Blake's the kind of guy to bring it up. 

Chloe suddenly appears from around the corner of a gift card rack, in her hands are a few water bottles and an athletic drink. She offers the former over to him, "You need to drink something, here."

Ryan tries to raise his right arm instinctively to grab it but quickly stops as the pain keeps the arm down. Instead he grabs it with his left hand and stares at the cap.

Without even asking, Chloe sets the water bottles down, grabs the cap of his drink, and opens it for him. She then tosses a water bottle over to Blake and takes a seat next to the injured guy.

He places the sugary drink to his parched lips and is immediately hit with a gluttonous, ravenous lust for more as he begins to chug it faster than his mouth can handle. Only when half the bottle is emptied does he pull it away from his lips and take a deep breath.

"Yeesh," Blake comments unscrewing the cap of his own water bottle, "Don't drown yourself."

Ryan wipes some of the spilt liquid on his chin with his wrist, "Sorry, I didn't realize how thirsty I was."

Chloe rolls her eyes but doesn't say anything.

Max and Alison appear from the snacks aisle with an armful of junk food in hand.

"All the name brands are gone," he comments as he waves a strangely colored bag around, "But these rip-off brands should be palatable."

"Who says palatable?" Caid comments as he and Beth appear from the opposite direction, "Just say it's edible."

Max narrows his eyes, "Chefs like me gotta be more... um... articulate with how we talk about food."

"I don't think junk food fits the bill," Caid adds as he takes a seat across from Ryan.

Beth sets various supplies on the floor. Nothing that she grabbed particularly catches his eye but he does recognize one thing from his freshman health class and decides starring at it would make him uncomfortable. Instead, he matches Beth and looks outside at the falling snow and sheering wind.

"We can't stay much longer," Blake comments as he notices everyone else looking outside, "If the snow does start to stick, we don't want to be caught here."

"What about Ryan," Alison adds, "He can't ride a motorcycle in his condition."

Blake nods, "Right, so I'll take his bike back. Someone just needs to drive him instead."

"Yeah, I'll get him, we'll even take the Corvette to get back sooner," Caid offers quickly receiving an incredulous look from Max.

Max crosses his arms and stares bullets into his friend, "Who said you could take my baby?"

Caid rolls his eyes, "Dude, who drives a rear-wheel drive vehicle when it's snowing outside."

"I didn't know it was going to snow," Max says timidly, suddenly a little embarrassed.

"Right," Caid nods knowingly, "That's why I'm not going to let you drive it. You'll end up in a ditch."

"Says who?" Max defends.

Caid shakes his head, "I'm not going to remind you about two years ago. If you really want to come, you can lay in the trunk."

Max crosses his arms, "That thing's way too small."

"Yeah, it's for a small person after all," Caid says quickly, "Now, I'd love to keep chit-chatting but take a look."

Everyone glances out the windows to see what is being pointed at. Having been snowing for almost half an hour now, the faintest bit of snow is starting to collect on the ground. Most of the street is still covered in cold water but where one flank survives, another can follow. 

"Alright, let's reconvene at the prison," Blake concludes looking at Ryan, "Do you think you'll make it?"

Ryan can't help but smirk at the comment as if the answer were blatantly obvious. He might be dizzy and quite tired, but his legs still move. Once he was able to regain his barrings with a break, he feels like he can move around no problem again. Heck, he might even be able to fight with his good arm, though he knows he'll get an earful if he even proposes that possibility.

"Yeah," he responds instead as he gets up slowly, using his left arm from most of the work, "I'll be fine."

With that they depart from the convenience store and head for their base.

---

The snow only picks up as they hustle back towards the prison. The temperature also steadily drops as the sun slowly disappears—though he couldn't see it through the clouds.

Caid and Ryan ride back silently as the truck and motorcycle behind them takes their time navigating the whitening roads. It's probably a bad idea to drive any faster than ten below the speed limit in such hazardous conditions, but Caid comfortably drives the speed limit despite being in a "rear-wheeled" vehicle. Ryan's not sure what difference that makes but Caid seems rather comfortable behind the wheel as he turns corners without a hint of worry.

They round the final corner and the prison appears between the trees. Their hearts collectively sink as they view upon the dreadful scene before them. The prison's gate is completely gone with only bent metal pools opening to a gap no less than twenty feet wide. The first and second exterior yards are littered with zombie and human corpses alike. The hole in the gate is mirrored almost perfectly in the front of the building as the concrete wall is completely missing in the same circumference. 

Caid parks the Corvette just outside the front gate with the nose of the car facing the street. They then both exit the vehicle while looking around for any sign of what transpired while they were gone. By the time they actually reach the hole in the front gate, the scene begins to grim.

Laying on the ground underneath the guard tower is an armed guard. Neither one of them can identify the poor soul, however, as his head is completely crushed with blood staining the concrete above his shoulders. His M4 assault rifle lays a few feet away from his hand.

Ryan reaches down to grab the rifle but Caid stops him. Instead, the guy with two fully operational hands picks up the gun and places the stock to his shoulder. They silently nod as Ryan pulls out his holstered pistol instead.

A few more guards line the path towards the main prison building with double that amount in zombie bodies. Inside the front entrance, just past the gapping hole in the wall, a small pile of bodies lie. Kneeling over the pile are two zombies stuffing their face with the human soup below them. Caid scopes his gun in and takes both out with a scary amount of precision before either can look up.

Behind them, Ryan notices the truck and bike arrive with the rest of their group. Their reactions seem to be quite similar to his, though Beth and Max appear a bit more pronounced than anything he would have done. Blake rushes into the guard tower as the rest of them hurry towards the main building.

Caid and Ryan continue into the first room of the prison just as a large form enters the scene from an internal hallway. The mutant stands around seven feet tall, but it's probably larger as it hunches his shoulders forward. Its arms look like they passed steroids a long time ago with four fingers that are essentially just long, protruding knives. The thing that catches his eye the most, however, is the elongated neck which attaches between the shoulders but winds down to a head which hovers in front of the chest. The demonic visage holds the ancient remnants of a human face with black eyes, buck-like horns, and just enough of a toothy frown to consider calling it that.

Ryan prepares to fire, unsure of what part is the weakest but guesses that the head is always a good place to start. He knows he won't hold out in a fight so he and Caid will have to end this quickly if either of them want to make it out alive.

The monster casually steps into the main room and inspects some of the corpses on the floor. It doesn't take notice of the two humans right away, but when it eventually does turn, it simply stands still. The mutant inspects them with an unreadable disposition as no one dares make the first move.

"Can you run?", Caid whispers, receiving a reluctant no, "Shoot, guess we don't have a choice here."

"Well if it isn't Ryan... and Caid" the mutant speaks with terrifyingly low tone, yet what's worse is the control of his enunciation.

Ryan's heart nearly stops as he recognizes the owner of that voice despite the intense demonic filter. It’s a voice full of confidence and awareness. A voice that could put even the most anxious or furious at ease simply by the amount of respect it commands with each passing word. It’s the same voice that convinced him to stay in school for one more grueling, awful day.

“It’s been a while. Hasn't it?”

“Mr. Heimes,” Ryan sneers, making Caid's eyes widen.

He wishes it could have only been them to discover this monstrosity waiting for them. He wishes the battle could have been avoided outright without dragging anyone else in. This hope goes sour as Chloe and the other suddenly appear in the hole of a doorway. It's evident by their expressions that they heard who they're looking at.

“Were you behind all of this?!” Max shouts, stepping further into the room until he's standing right at Ryan's side.

Heimes chuckles at the comment which comes out as more of a deep toned, short-lived cackle, "No, but you can think of it that way."

"I don't understand", Max argues.

The monstrosity before them shifts in place slowly as Blake appears from the behind. As the final teen catches up with the group, he's stuck with the same shock and hesitation. He, however, doesn't stay frozen as he lifts an AR he found and points it directly at Heimes.

"Ah, Blake," Heimes begins with the same assertive tone he carried when he was still human, "I haven't gotten to say this, but I commend you for leading your peers out of the high school. In truth, I expected all of you to die there, in fact, I had hoped it would be that way. Seeing you here now feels... bittersweet I guess. You all must have endured so much."

The monster looks between the remainder of his former students whom he hasn't yet named, his malformed visage shaking off some sort of distress in favor of a calm, relaxed look, "Chloe and Beth, it's good to see you're doing well. I hope you two haven't missed volleyball too much."

"Don't patronize me," Beth asserts with a menacing look in her eye.

Heimes moves on to the last two students, "It's... ah yes, Max. And Alison. We've never talked before, but I take pride in remembering the names of my students."

"What is this all about?!" Alison exclaims, "Where is everyone? And why are you here?"

Heimes chuckles once more, "Your confusion is completely warranted..."

“I still want to know something!” Max shouts overtop of Heimes, his voice shaking as he speaks, “You said you didn't expect any of us to survive... you didn't want it. Why? Why did you sentence all of us to death back in the school?”

“I’m sure all of you are wondering that,” Heimes explains, twisting his coiled head slightly to glaze over the students once more, “That school was locked down to make sure all of you became test subjects for a certain scientist. You see, this parasite that currently infects me is far from stable. Animal tests had promising results, but Dr. Crane was adamant about having live human test subjects—and a lot of them at that.”

Max shakes his head in frustration and disbelief, "Why the fuck would you go along with that?"

“It's simple,” Heimes says matter-of-factly, “You might perceive my actions as cruel, but... it was necessary. Either you died quickly serving a purpose, or you died slowly pursuing a meaningless struggle for survival. That being said, it did cause me great sorrow to walk out those doors that day. I have become a slave to what's inside of me, just as you all are slaves to your humanity.” Heimes' voice drifts off as his coiled head vertically shifts towards the steady snow trickling down through a crack in the ceiling. 

"I can't believe this," Beth says under her breath while averting her eyes.

Alison glances around at the corpses all around the entrance, "But... this isn't the school. Why are you doing this?"

Heimes matches Alison's gaze before glances at the corpses, "I never let anyone escape the _military_ checkpoints, so why would anyone be safe here?" The enunciation of the word military sends a chill down Ryan's spine.

Alison immediately stares, not at the monster, but at Ryan, "You said people made it out—escaped the city. You said my family could have gotten out."

Ryan hangs his head, "I didn't know."

Heimes watches the dialogue curiously without intervening.

"Does that mean that everyone we haven't found is probably dead?" Alison continues.

Ryan shakes his head, "I don't know."

An unadulterated horrific fear strikes Alison's face as she steps back with her hands covering her mouth.

Heimes sighs, "Rest assured Alison, your family has already moved on to whatever comes after this life."

Alison collapses to her knees.

"I still don't believe you!" Blake yells out, probably trying to reroute the conversation, “The four years we spent under your administration was just a build up to be slaughtered.”

Heimes frowns, a sudden onset of regret—or something similar—enters the monster's face, "No. I did care about all of my students. That was never a lie."

"Then why?!" Blake shouts, his voice growing hoarse as he holds something overpowering back, "Why do you call our survival meaningless?"

The monster stares directly at Blake for a long moment before responding, "Unfortunately telling you is futile, but out of respect for the trust you once gave me, I shall share the truth with you. This world is not to be survived. Letting you live felt like an unimaginable cruelty. You see, when dealing with experiments, death is not a punishment, it is a gift—one that was refused by one among you."

Ryan narrows his eyes. Perhaps being offered a knife in that bathroom meant more than he let on.

"I've wanted to know something too," Caid comments, much calmer than his friends, "I don't have any family left so I don't care about any of that, but I do want to know what's happening to the world? The school, the military, the zombies, all of it. What is going on?"

A demented smile crosses Heimes' face as he hunches forward just a bit more, "Again, you are asking the wrong questions, but as a parting gift, I shall indulge you with the little that I do know. The world as you understand it is headed to ruin, though this is just a transitionary period. What comes after... well... not even I know what that is."

Blake walks slowly over to the wall and places a hand against it. "You can't be serious right now. Are you saying that world is going to end? For good?"

Heimes shakes his head, "I don't believe so, but unfortunately myself and the six of you will never get to see what comes of that."

Ryan checks the group hesitantly but can't shake that foreboding detail. Blake, Caid, Max, Beth, Alison, Chloe, and him. That's seven. Why did he six?

"Then..." Blake says looking back at the monstrous figure of his former principal, "The people that we... that I saved in that school. What happened to them?"

Heimes gives him a morbid look, "You're standing on one of them."

Blake looks down slowly to find the white snow on his shoes stained red. The hand of a familiar face lays directly next to his shoe. His eyes widen.

*BANG*

Ryan places a bullet right between Heimes' eyes. The force of the impact causes the monster’s head to lurch back but when he looks back towards Ryan, there is barely a scratch made by the bullet wound. Whatever boney armor he had last time is clearly still present.

Immediately following Ryan’s declaration of war, Blake opens fire screaming violently as he tries to send his pain through each passing bullet that hits Heimes. Caid quickly joins the fray. With bullets now flying, Chloe grabs onto Alison's arm and drags her out of the room. Max follows suit but stays by the hole in the wall to watch the scene unfold. Beth simply stands in place in utter shock. When all is said and done, every bullet in their magazines is emptied and Heimes stands there completely unmoved by the mere annoyance.

“I don’t need to be the one to kill you,” Heimes says disappointedly, unable to look at any of them, “They can do it for me.”

A number of figures slowly walk out of the dark hallways with uneasy steps and a soft groaning with each step. Only three appear at first with a twitchy demeanor, but within seconds a whole crowd of at least twenty stand at the entrance of the hallway revealing themselves against the shadowy light. Stephanie, Michael, Janelle, and so many more. All of them standing completely dead in the eyes with streaks of blood and gashes littering their bodies.

In the meantime, Ryan looks down at the pistol in his hand and tightens his grip on it. He's not in any condition to fight a horde of zombies, much less corpses with names. Whether he likes it not, this where this battle was doomed to lead no matter what they did.

The horde collectively groans as each of the teens slowly start backing away. Ryan suspects that running might be their only option, but then he notices Blake dropping the magazine out of his AR and reloading it with another. Caid—without such forethinking—drops back to the hole in the wall while dragging Beth with him.

Heimes look over at the small horde of zombies, "You can move now."

Immediately the horde breaks forward in a mad dash towards the frightened teenagers. Ryan steps back but knows he's not going to be outrunning anything. This fact is validated as Blake starts unloading his magazine into the undead rushing towards them. With little choice left, Ryan lifts his pistol and hopes he can land a shot with only one arm.

His first shot misses, the second lands. He remembers to control his fire rate and attack in bursts, but that quickly goes out the window as the horde gets dangerously close to him. As adrenaline surges, he opts to just pull the trigger as fast as he can in hopes that it will stop them.

By the time his pistol runs dry, he only manages to down four zombies and promptly tosses the pistol in favor of his knife. Holding the weapon in his non-dominant hand feels foreign to be sure, but he doesn't really have the luxury to worry about that. With a zombified Michael barring down on him, he really doesn't have time to think at all.

Pushing all his nausea and fatigue to the side for a moment, he draws his knife back only to slap the point of the blade right into Michael's throat. The zombie stops flailing at him, but its momentum doesn't halt. Michael's undead corpses tackles him to the ground as its body goes completely limp with Ryan powerless to hold the weight. His body gives out before he even has the opportunity to brace himself. 

Ryan hits the ground hard and quickly pushes the bloody corpse off of him only to find Stephanie right behind her former husband. Her eyes are as bloodshot as the first zombie woman and her teeth protrude out of her face as if two rows of spider fangs. His former history teacher rears her head back preparing to take Ryan's neck off, but her action never succeeds as a unexpected gunshot completely erases her head from her neck.

In shock, Ryan stumbles backwards on the ground only to look over at the entrance to find Drew expertly taking out zombie after zombie with one bullet each. Each projectile released is accompanied by a snappy shift of the barrel only for another the next bullet to follow closely behind. Ryan's not sure when Drew showed up, but the entire horde of remaining zombies is completely dispatched within seconds. Blake, who had been struggling with two zombies on top of him, finds both undead to be true corpses before either can bite down on his arm.

All eyes turn to Drew as he steps into the prison entrance with a glaring attitude directed towards Heimes. He carefully pulls the magazine out of his gun and replaces it with another.

"And who might you be?" Heimes asks, almost indifferent to how quickly the horde was eradicated.

"So you're the cultist, Seth Heimes," Drew says, disregarding the question, "you have no idea how hard it was to track you down."

Heimes narrows his black, void-like eyes, "If you know who I am then it narrows down the possibilities quite substantially."

Drew points his AR at Heimes' face in a dramatic gesture of ill will, "Where is The Director?"

Appearing a bit bothered by the question, Heimes' head shakes from side to side originating from the coiled neck, "Even if I knew, I wouldn't disclose that to you."

Drew glances over at Ryan and takes notice to the prominent gauze wrapped around his arm. He looks as if he's going to make a comment but opts to turn his attention back to the threat at hand instead, "Why don't you speed things up and just tell me where I can find the next cultist. After you're dead, I'll need a new lead."

Heimes raises on eye, "You're quite demanding for someone who isn't even an experiment."

"And you're not one either," Drew responds unflinchingly.

The quick response causes Heimes to go silent for a moment. Maybe contemplating the comment or maybe simply in awe of such a timely and contradictory response. Eventually he allows his eyes to drift close for a brief moment with a sigh, "It seems you are familiar with the nature of experiments."

"What... what are you talking about?" Ryan asks, getting the feeling that he's the only one other than Drew that can ask a question at this point.

Drew shoots him a sideways glance but keeps his focus primarily on the monster before him, "Experiments are the furthest thing from human. Heimes here is just a cultist and a wannabe experiment. The only difference that it makes to me is that I can kill a cultist."

"Presumptuous aren't we," Heimes musses as his entire form begins to shift. Instead of remaining in a calm, upright stance, the former principal lowers it's slouched shoulders until his arm touches the ground. His knee bends slightly as if a runner preparing for a sprint, "Why don't I just kill you myself."

A smirk crosses Drew's face, "If I had a nickel for every time someone said that to me."

Immediately the standoff springs to life as Heimes lurches forward with his heavy arms drawn back to crush Drew in one swing. Drew, however, fires off two round into the monster's knee. If Ryan or Blake had tried that, their impact would have been negligible, but Drew's round does something unexpected. Upon impact, the bullet explodes into a small orange puff of flame. These same flames don't extinguish instantly and instead grapple the surface of Heimes' knee and continue to burn. Drew then jumps out of the way as Heimes goes barreling by.

Heimes releases a beast-like roar, as the small, insignificant flames begin to take to his skin quite easily. What initially started out as a single puff quickly engulfs half of the knee. In response, Heimes slams one of his claw-like hands into his knee to smoother the flames. It works, but not before Drew fires two more fire-producing rounds into the monster's coiled neck. Now with fire beginning to spread on his neck, Heimes turns to the soldier and charges at him without remorse.

A mighty claw, easily capable of splitting steel swipes right by Drew as he expertly ducks underneath the strike only to roll out of the monster's way as Heimes attempts to stomp on him. Drew then returns to his feet and pulls something off the bottom of his backpack. The object in question is no larger than a thick textbook with some sort of mechanical contraption on the top. The soldier holds it calmly in one hand as he inspects the monster turning around to face him.

"I've been on your tail since Pakistan," Drew comments as he eyes the monster, not an ounce of fear on his face, "Just tell me what I want to know."

Heimes scowls as he snuffs out the flames on his neck, "You should know that I can't tell you."

Drew sighs, "You don't need to be alive to tell me what I need to know. Your death will do that for me. A little conversation just makes thing easier."

Narrowing his eyes and lowering his body, Heimes prepares for another assault, "You're one of them, aren't you."

Drew raises an eyebrow, "I'm impressed you know."

"Everyone in the Organization knows about you," Heimes explains shaking his head, "I hope your eventual encounter with an experiment is... painful."

For the first time in this interaction—and one of the only times Ryan can remember—Drew smiles, "I appreciate that."

Immediately following, Heimes launches forward with both arms wide and low so that Drew can't dodge out of the way. Drew, without a hint of surprise, tosses the block in his hand into the air. As the block hits the peak of its momentum and begins to descend back to the ground, Heimes instinctively smacks the block out of the way which grants Drew enough space to slide by the outside of Heimes' arm. The soldier doesn't even collide with the monster's bulky body as he pulls something out of his pocket immediately after Heimes slides by.

Heimes doesn't slide too far past and quickly swings his other arm back at the man clearly disrespecting his dangerous appendages. The attack swings and just as easily misses as Drew takes two quick step backs and reveals a copper penny in his hand.

"Heads, I get the credit for this kill. Tails, I give it in honor of Grant," Drew then tosses the coin in the air, catches it with a closed hand, and inspects it so only he can see. He chuckles slightly, "Figures."

Heimes gives him an incredulous look as he draws his arm back and prepares for another slash, this one guaranteed to hit even if Drew tries to dodge. This preparation doesn't phase Drew, however, as he looks up from the coin with an indifferent look. The nonchalant solider than taps the penny between his index finger and thumb. This action immediately triggers orange liquid to erupt from Heimes arm—exactly where Drew had carefully slide by. This spontaneous liquid is far from showy as it sticks to Heimes' bony skin and begins to burn in a bright yellow color. The monster tries in vain to put it out quickly as the inferno begins to spread across his body like a coiling snake engulfing its prey. Seeing as the flame cannot be extinguished, Heimes reaches out for Drew in one last attempt at revenge, but Drew just turns his back on the monster and starts walking away.

The fire burns through the monster's arm before it can touch the back of Drew's head. The inferno continues to blaze until Heimes' entire form is engulfed in the bright flames. He doesn't scream, he doesn't roar, he simply burns as if accepting his fate. Only when Heimes' coiled head goes slack and legs crumble does anyone but Drew look away.

With Heimes no longer moving and the fire beginning to melt away the former principal, all eyes turn to Drew for an explanation. When they do inspect the man, they find him pulling a pin out of his gun. He then completely removes two components to the weapon, pulls a sizzling bullet shell out of the chamber, tosses it on the ground, and finishes by reassembling his weapon. After that, he pulls the magazine of fire round out of the gun and slides it into an empty magazine holster before placing a separate magazine back in the AR. 

The group continues to watch in silence as Drew walks across the room to the small box that Heimes had batted away. He inspects the dented gadget with a sigh before pulling the mechanical contraption free and tossing the ruined casing aside. He places the contraption in his backpack while he standing up to inspect some armor on his shoulder. A section of it has been cut clean through.

"Drew?" Ryan starts, not sure what to even say at this point.

The soldier glances up as if he'd forgotten that anyone else was nearby, "Right... I guess you wanted the finishing shot, didn't you?"

Ryan shakes his head, "Uh, no. I'm just. I'm at a loss for words."

Drew raises an eyebrow as he inspects the graveyard of corpses littering the room and seven brave teens standing overtop of them. He purses his lips slightly as though to concoct some mature, dignified statement to comfort the ill-prepared teens, but the thought process ends just shy of words. Instead, Drew pulls a band out of his back pocket and attaches it to both ends of his AR. Once both sides are connected, he strings the weapon over his shoulders to rest beside his backpack.

"Sorry kid," Drew says patting Ryan once on the shoulder. He starts walking away without a second thought, completely dismissive of the gazes on him. Right before he disappears into the snowy outside, he stops and looks back at Ryan, "Take care."

With that, he's gone. Max and Blake try to extract something from him, but their words are lost with the wind as Drew disappears into a truck at the edge of the prison gate. All seven teenagers are left in silence.

As the others linger without much conversation, Ryan notices Chloe breaking from the group and crouching down beside a few scattered corpses. She nudges one of them carefully with her foot before releasing a sigh of relief once she realizes it's not who she's looking for. The next ten corpses go by in a similar manner with her carefully inspecting them—just long enough to determine who the corpse used to be. This pattern ends on one particular corpse near the back of the room. The particular body in question is that of a man, that singular details tells him all he needs to know.

Ryan steps up behind her contemplating what he's supposed to say. What even can he say? This thought alone feels... strange familiar... like déjà vu. But where in the world would he have felt this way?

Oh.

Chloe crouches down in front of the corpse and slowly closes the eyes of the horrified man with agony petrified on his face. Beside him is the body of a much smaller male, his body battered in similar way—that being a single bullet through the head. The physique, the proximity, the corny slogan on his shirt, all of it screams a name he wishes he could never hear again. 

For the duration of a single blink, Ryan watches Steve kneel in front of Karmen and Charlie. The sight makes his knife fall out of his hands.

Chloe doesn't scream, she mutter unintelligible questions, she doesn't even cry. She simply stares at the bodies in front of her with an unreadable expression. Her shoulders slouch forward as her head hangs over them just a bit.

Ryan clenches his fist, takes a deep breath, and steps up behind her. It's not his place to say anything. It's not his responsibility to save someone if they aren't willing to be saved. That being said, the very least he can do is show her that, unlike him, she's not alone in this. Perhaps that's what he needed to tell Steve back then.

And so, Ryan places his hand on Chloe's shoulder without a word.
