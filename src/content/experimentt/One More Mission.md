Ryan stays in bed until around lunchtime. He catches a few winks of sleep here and there but nothing substantial. Unfortunately, sleep deprivation has always been a commonplace in his life, so pulling an all-nighter doesn't really throw him off much. Having snuck out of his foster homes, almost nightly, for years really conditioned him to a lack of sleep. That being said, the strange fatigue he feels when walking around the prison is nothing like he's ever experienced before. It's not a physical kind of fatigue—he would have noticed when he worked out—instead it's almost a mental fatigue that makes him strangely irritable. Noticing this trend, he makes the conscious decision to not appear grumpy, but he knows his limits will be reduced until he gets some proper rest. 

Alas, he finds himself in the cafeteria surrounded by half the camp all chowing down on a rather rare hoagie meal. Seated next to him is—what has quickly become—the usual crew. Chloe, Beth, and Alison all sit on one end of the table discussing something about a game that the children of the camp could play. Max gets shoved by Caid as he attempts to take a bite out of the larger boy's sandwich. Lastly, Blake sits at the end of the boys' row talking with a small group of teens whom Ryan hasn't the slightest clue of their names. 

This last detail isn't "typical" but he has noticed that Blake spends quite a bit of time talking to people. It's not just some sort of secondary friend group either, it's other teenagers right now, but a couple of days ago it was adults, and before that it was an entirely different group of teens who made it out of the school with them. It's not that Ryan's kept a particularly close eye on Blake's whereabouts, but looking around and watching people feels like the most comfortable thing he could do recently. Blake just so happens to be within line of sight each time.

"What are you thinking about?" Chloe asks after naturally disengaging from her prior conversation. Her demeanor feeling oddly comfortable as she leans towards him with her elbows on the table.

Ryan shakes his head, "Nothing in particular."

Chloe gives him a knowing look, "Sure."

Feeling almost insulted, Ryan recoils but immediately brushes it off thinking that it's just him being grumpy. Instead of displaying discontent, he hides it with a smile and leans over the table. Only after doing so does he notice that he's unintentionally gotten dangerously close to Chloe. They both jump back a bit startled by the closed gap.

"Sorry," Ryan immediately apologizes.

"It's fine," Chloe replies just as quickly, both of them avoiding eye contact.

Perhaps he could have resolved the situation internally if that were the end of it. This idea is shattered, however, as the one-and-only Max takes notice and decides to jump on the situation.

"No way," he comments louder than necessary, "Is it sailing?"

Almost immediately, he receives a rather forceful slap on the back of the head. He nearly face-plants into the table as Caid shakes his head disapprovingly.

"Leave them alone," he asserts.

As always, Max doesn't take the physical violence as an offense and promptly turns around with a sly grin, "You only say that because your ship has sailed."

Caid narrows his eyes, "You know, I could say no. You probably want me to. But I think it'll be so much better if I say yes. Yes, the ship has sailed. Now where's your ship?"

Max narrows his eyes right back, "Oh just you wait, I'll have a ship in due time. You know what, that girl over there," he points across the room, but Ryan can't specifically identify whom he's pointing at, "give me a month, and we'll be boating together."

Caid glances back and widens his eyes, "I didn't realize you were in to wrinkles."

"Shut up," Max complains, "I was talking about the girl next to her."

Caid looks back at the boy in shock, "Who? Her mom?"

Max shakes his fist at his friend, "I can't stand you."

"Right," Caid says disinterested as he takes a drink out of his water bottle.

In the midst of all this, Ryan finishes off his hoagie sandwich and proceeds to finish off his water bottle as well. He didn't realize until taking the first bite just how hungry he is. After finishing the first, he immediately looks towards the kitchen in hopes of finding a second serving available. Unfortunately, there isn't.

"Do you want mine," Alison says, surprising Ryan.

Alison has always been the third musketeer between Beth and Chloe, but his interactions with her have been quite limited. As such, when she asks him a question directly, he just about does a double take. Whether out of concern for him, or strictly her own lack of hunger, she offers up an untouched hoagie to him.

"S-seriously?" Ryan stutters.

Alison nods as she gives the hoagie a little shake.

"I'll take it!" Max exclaims reaching out for the sandwich, but Beth slaps his hand away and shakes her head.

Almost confused by the kind gesture, Ryan reluctantly accepts the gift. It's not just the person offering the sandwich that catches him off guard, it's also the sentiment behind it. In a way, it's the same feeling he got when Chloe decorated his room with all the children's decorations. He can't quite call it gratitude, but that seems to be the closest word he possesses in his vocabulary. 

"Uh, thank you," he eventually says as he pulls the clear foil off the top of the sandwich.

Alison just smiles as she takes a drink of her water.

After that, Ryan proceeds to dig into his second sandwich. He doesn't even bother to consider the fact that he might look like a glutton as he takes two large bites out of the top. After chewing and swallowing the soft bread and salty meat, he finds Chloe starring at him.

"What?" he asks, completely clueless.

Chloe smiles and shakes her head, "Oh, nothing. Am I bothering you?"

Ryan shakes his head, "No... you're not."

After finishing his own sentence he notices Beth giving him a knowing glance. Upon noticing, she immediately looks away, but by then the damage has already been done. He goes back to his sandwich to avoid dwelling on it any further.

Some additional idle chat is passed back and forth, primarily between Max, Caid, and Beth, but Ryan doesn't pay it much attention. What does rope him in, however, is Blake finishing off a conversation with the teenagers and falling back into the swing of things as he passes Beth a sticky note. She accepts it without batting an eye.

Caid, who's sitting right next to Blake, leans forward on the table and looks over at him, "Hey, I saw you talking to Michael this morning. What was that about?"

Blake gives him a cautious look, "Do you need to know everything I talk about."

"Daddy Caid needs to know everything," Max jumps in, "but I saw it too. You looked like you were super serious."

With a sigh, Blake caves, "Fine, he asked me if I wanted to start going to the meetings with the other survivor groups," he looks at Beth, "you got asked the same thing didn't you?"

Everyone looks at Beth who folds the sticky note and stuffs it into her pocket, she then addresses the group with a nonchalant demeanor, "Yeah, that happened."

"So you guys are going to start representing the prison in trade discussions or something?" Chloe wonders.

Blake bites his cheeks, "Sorta, but it mostly sounded like we were going to shadow the first few times."

"That's still really cool," Chloe adds, "Was there a reason why he wanted you two?"

Beth smirks at her best friend, "Are you jealous?"

"No," Chloe responds quite calmly, "I'm just curious."

"I assume it's because of what we did back in our old base," Blake explains, "Between Beth and I, we did most of the management work."

"I guess that makes sense," Caid decides looking mostly at Beth, "Don't overdo it though, I'm sure it's a really serious job."

Beth rolls her eyes, "Do I ever overdo it?"

Caid maintains a firm gaze, "Do you want a serious answer?"

For some reason this response doesn't get Beth's usual dismissive comment. Ryan wonders if there's something deeper he's missing, but to be honest, it's probably none of his business. For now, his sandwich is gone and his water bottle empty. It's probably best if he cleans up after himself.

He grabs his two sandwich wraps in one hand and crushes the plastic water bottle in his other hand. The noticeable crunching of the plastic draws the tables' attention as he rises to his feet. Someone is about to ask him a question when Drew appears beside him.

"Ryan, we've got to talk," he asserts starring only at him and dismissing the rest of the table watching.

"Uh... sure."

Drew gestures for Ryan to follow as he swiftly heads for the door. With one last glance at his friend, Ryan follows being sure to toss his trash in the bin on his way out. 

In the hallway, Drew waits with his arms crossed and his finger tapping his shoulder impatiently. The man gives a serious side-eye down the hallway to check for anyone nearby before turning his attention to Ryan. He gets the feeling that this conversation with probably revolve around what happened yesterday, but Drew doesn't seem particularly upset—only impatient. 

"I found him," Drew says directly, "It seems my informant came through before he died."

"Who?" Ryan asks, already knowing the answer, "Heimes?"

Drew nods, "I'm heading out now. After everything that's transpired, I think you deserve a spot to come with. That being said, I won't be coming back here afterward."

Ryan takes a step back and holds out a hand for the man to slow down, "Hold on, you still haven't told me who your informant was or why I needed to go to the stadium in the first place."

With the faintest amount of irritation, Drew checks a watch on his arm, "My informant was a former colleague of mine—from a long time ago. That's all you really need to know. As for the baseball stadium... well, you have a knack for surviving cultist encounters. If only my colleague could have been like that too."

Ryan narrows his eyes, "Yet you don't know who Jimmy is."

Looking at his watch again, Drew lets out an exasperated sigh, "I don't know a Jimmy, but someone by the name of Henry Baxter was inviting former convicts from this very prison to the stadium and promising some grand reward. My informant had a suspicion that this 'Henry Baxter' was somehow related to the Organization. And when you dig out one cultist, you can follow their trail to another. My informant discovered that trail before he died, and now I know where Heimes is."

Ryan places his hand against the wall as his thoughts begin spiral. Jimmy used the alias Henry Baxter back when he met Stephanie and the others. If what he says is true, then Jimmy was a cultist all along and that he and Heimes were on the same side to begin with. It's no stretch of the imagination to think that the creep was part of this plot also. In fact, he'd be willing to bet on it. 

"I just deciphered the coordinates, so I'm leaving," Drew continues, "Get whatever you need and let's go."

Finding his balance again, Ryan shakes his head, "Hold on, are you telling me to just leave? Leave everything here?"

"No," Drew responds quickly, "You can come back here afterward, I don't care. I'm just saying that _I_ won't be coming back here."

Ryan pinches the bridge of his nose. It's all so sudden. The last time he tried to track down Heimes, he was with Steve and Bill and everything went south—everything. Whether it be the bite mark on his shoulder or the scar running down his forearm, he can't overlook what happened the last time he tried "finding" Heimes. Sure, Drew might be a skilled, that doesn't change the fact that he ordered the search in the first place. He wouldn't even call it déjà vu, there's an explicit mistrust he's feeling towards this offer. Not only that, but he hasn't encountered Heimes a single time since that last search. As far as he's concerned, Heimes is at the bottom of his priority list. He'd much rather focus his efforts on something he feels that he _must_ do, like resolving whatever conflict remains in the stadium. Drew can have what he wants, but Ryan's not about to get dragged into another fiasco just because Drew orders it.

"You can go without me," Ryan decides as he takes a step back towards the cafeteria.

Drew gives him a dumbfounded look, as if the refusal was the last thing he expected to hear, "Are you joking? That cultist killed your father and got your school massacred. Surely you want revenge?"

Ryan purses his lips. Drew's not wrong, all of that did happened... though he's leaving out one important detail. Back then, he didn't care if he lost those things. Maybe he secretly did, but he didn't realize any of that until it was too late. This time is different, however. This time, he actually knows what it's like to lose things. Searching for Heimes cost him Steve. It cost him Bill. To some degree, it even cost Jeremy. He's done gambling with people's lives.

"Good luck Drew," Ryan says softly, "I hope you find what you're looking for."

Drew watches, awestruck, as Ryan heads for the cafeteria. Before he can fully leave view and end the conversation for good, Drew calls out to him which makes him pause in the doorway.

"Hey kid," he looks back as Drew returns to his calm, collected demeanor with a subtle smirk, "Take care."

With that, Ryan returns to the lunch table. He fully expects someone to pry and ask what his conversation with Drew was all about, but that never happens. When he returns to the table, he's only given a casual nod by two people while the rest find themselves engrossed in discussion. The discussion in question seems to be about some raccoon that appeared by the dumpsters the night before which captivates Max particularly. Ryan simply sits quietly and listens to his friends brainstorm absurd names for the rodent.

"Yeah, that trash panda's probably seen a lot of dumpsters in its days," Caid says at some point, "Speaking of seeing things. It's actually been a while since I've left the prison, I think we need to get another mission going, you know, with the Boys." Upon making this claim, he glances between Blake, Max and Ryan specifically.

"Enough with this 'Boys' stuff," Beth complains, "Why don't we all go do something—all seven of us. After all, isn't there safety in numbers."

"I agree," Chloe adds.

Max leans forwards on the table, "That kinda defeats the purpose of 'The Boys' don't you think. Then it'd have to be 'The Boys plus Girls'. Doesn't roll off the tongue."

Instead of revealing the faintest amount of annoyance to Max, Beth stares directly at Caid, "I didn't want to have to use this, but Michael can be a bit of a misogynist. He won't let us go because we're just three girls."

As much as Ryan would like to disagree with the statement, it would make sense given the fact that Stephanie came to him in order to leave. Whether or not Michael's belief is unfounded is another matter entirely. 

"So you're saying you need a chaperon?" Max points out a little bluntly.

Looking offended, Beth narrows her eyes, "Are you implying that we can't take care of ourselves."

Caid lets out a sigh, "No one's saying that, and I'm okay with you guys coming," he glances at Blake, "I know you have something to say about it."

Blake shakes his head, "I'm actually fine with it. Beth is right, there's definitely safety in numbers. The question is: where do go?"

"Dumpster diving apparently," Max answers giving Caid a cheeky grin.

"What if we went to Covington Plaza?" Allison offers drawing the attention of the group, "It's a strip mall. There's probably still a lot of stuff left to find."

"I like that idea," Beth adds.

"That sound find," Blake decides which seems to put a cap on the matter, at least that's what the silent consensus implies.

This idea, however, is a bit troublesome for Ryan. It's a shame that he'll have to take a stance so quickly after making a plan.

"You guys have fun," Ryan comments as he starts to slowly stand up, "I've got something else I need to take care of today."

All eyes are suddenly glued to him, making him pause. Surprisingly no one looks particularly upset by Ryan's declaration, instead the collective reaction is far more troubling—they seem concerned.

"What do you have to do?" Chloe asks directly, giving him the feeling that her intentions are more compassionate than anything else, "Is it about yesterday?"

"Yeah," Blake adds, "When you brought everyone back, Michael said he'd handled the logistics. There's nothing you should need to do, honestly you've done enough."

"It's..." Ryan struggles to say, "It's not about that."

"Is it dangerous?" Blake presses.

Ryan averts his eyes. He could lie and say he doesn't know, but something about that answer doesn't sit right with him. "Probably."

Blake nods his head, "Alright, that settles it. I'll come with you."

Snapping his head up, Ryan stares at the boy who seems a lot less like a former high schooler all of a sudden, "You—you can't."

"And why not?" Blake rebuttals.

Suddenly Ryan finds himself at a loss for words. Obviously it's going to be dangerous to return to the stadium, and he doesn't want to see anyone else get hurt. Ordinarily, he'd probably think himself crazy for even considering the idea of returning to such a hellhole, but he can't get Vincenzo's words out of his head. Every time that masked man appeared before him, he would also say or do something crucial. Vincenzo saved his life on multiple occasions, got him back on track in moments of true desperation, and even gave him gear that's kept him alive. The level of trust Ryan has for the enigmatic man is shaky to be certain, but unlike everyone else in this apocalypse, his direction has actually proven to be beneficial. That fact alone is part of the reason why he decided not to go along with Drew.

As for why he needs to go alone, well... he just doesn't want to risk anyone else's life. What happened in that stadium is something he feels partially responsible for, even if it was all a cultist's doing. He went along with Jimmy's direction, he just sat there when a serial killer took charge, and he failed to kill the malicious murderer. Those things demand his return, even if unwillingly, but pulling his friends into that matter is the last thing he wants to do.

At the end of the day, he has to make a choice and his choice will be to return to that place of death—alone.

"You know, at this point I don't really care what your reason for going is," Max begins after Ryan takes a long moment to think, "I'm coming too."

"But—" Ryan tries.

Beth sighs, "You told us not to come last time and came back looking like a ghost. We're not just going to sit here and let you do that again."

"Th-that's not," Ryan stammers.

"Ryan," Chloe says directly forcing him to look at her, "You can trust us. All we want is to help you."

Ryan purses his lips and looks away. His own resolve suddenly being tested in a way it never has before. Yesterday the decision to go alone felt so obvious, so instinctual, but now it feels completely backwards. Part of him refuses to entertain the idea of putting anyone at this table in harms way, but another part... is grateful. Whether he accepts or declines their resolves is no longer a simple question of who's going. He kicks himself for getting stuck in such a muddy decision.

"It's..." Ryan starts slowly, "Not going to pretty."

"That's fine," Blake immediately counters, "We'll be ready for whatever happens. Besides, it wouldn't be our first time experiencing a little chaos."

"And you're sure," Ryan finalizes internally as he glances around the table at each one of them.

Blake and Beth seem resolute in their decision without a shadow of a doubt. Chloe and Max echo that sentiment but seem a bit less forward-facing with their ambition. Perhaps that discrepancy is part of the reason Blake and Beth were selected to represent the prison. Caid silently shrugs his shoulders and nods his head clearly agreeing the premise but refusing to make it obvious. Allison is the only person who doesn't seem fully on board with the idea of going out to a potentially dangerous area, instead she sits quickly at the end of the table and allows the momentum of the conversation fill in her schedule for her. She doesn't necessarily seem troubled by the determination but, out of everyone, she seems to be motivated by the atmosphere than her own freedom. Whether that is a bad or neutral thing is not for him to decide, however.

Ryan releases a drawn out sigh, this is probably a terrible idea—yet he doesn't hate himself for it. Therefore, releasing the tension in his shoulders he addresses his friends, "Very well, I'll be leaving in an hour."
