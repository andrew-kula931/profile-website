The truck pulls up to the farmhouse quietly as the sun hangs low in the sky. Blake's truck remains parked beside the barn. Inside that distant structure is Ryan's motorcycle and Max's Corvette. Maybe one day they'll be able to ride them again.

The two of them exit their vehicle and head towards the door, though before they can get there, a figure exists from the front door. It's a man that seems familiar though Ryan can't determine who it is. Drew, however, widens his eyes as if seeing a ghost. The confident, controlled man's face grows pale as shakily raises his gun.

"It's good to see you again Wilbur," the man says in a deep, confident tone—not unlike Drew's.

"H—How," he stutters, "There's no way."

The man shrugs, "Did you really think I'd die so easily."

Drew shakes his head as he aims the gun slowly, "No, you were the strongest man I've ever met. But you're not him."

The man's face grows sour. Immediately afterwards, the human features around his body fade away into a metallic gray. S-019 sighs, "You clever people are no fun."

"Give my friends back," Ryan says, not in a yell nor in any demanding tone whatsoever. He simply possess the thought and assumes S-019 hears it. The entire exchange is so devoid of emotion that Drew glances over with confusion.

S-019 rubs the back of its blank head, "Yeah, before we get to that, there's something important that needs to happen."

"What?" Ryan asks directly and unflinchingly.

With a simple look around gray humanoid figures begin to appear from behind every tree, building, and old bale of hay. Within seconds a small army of gray figures surrounds them with a slowly encroaching presence. Many of these figures begin to morph their arms into weaponry. A sword here, an axe there, and many more weapons he couldn't name if he tried.

Drew turns towards the truck but humanoid figures have already since slashed the tires and begun climbing overtop the vehicle.

"What is going on here," Drew asks carefully as he continues to look around. His face isn't frightened but a single bead of cold sweat drips down the side of his head, "My questions were supposed to be answered."

S-019—or rather the one standing on the homestead porch—shrugs its shoulders, "Does this not answer your questions?"

Drew takes a deep, begrudging breath, "Unfortunately it does," he looks directly at the experiment before him, "I'm not part of the plan, aren't I?"

S-019 chuckles to itself, "I'm glad we're on the same page."

That is the moment that the large crowd of fake humans brakes their foreboding positions and begin an all out charge towards Drew and Ryan. 

Drew mutters something under his breath, and at first, Ryan doesn’t register it. Then as the enemies get closer and closer, the words form into a coherent sentence in this thoughts.

"How about that Grant. You always said curiosity killed the cat."

The closest experiment develops a giant lance out of nowhere and thrusts it directly at Drew. He sidesteps the attack and fires a three rounds burst directly at the combatants face—a technique he explicitly taught to Ryan. The experiment manages to dodge the first two rounds, but the third strikes its temple and it backpedals a few steps without so much as a grunt of pain. 

That’s when a separate experiment verges dangerously close to Ryan forcing him to stop his observations and confront the foe. S-019 swings a sword a mere inch from his face as he evades and stumbles back. Ryan then counters with his own knife right to the experiment’s head. It twists its head to the side in order to dodge, but Ryan wasn’t expecting it to hit anyway. The bullet from his pistol underneath his other arm manages to land in the neck of the false person. Experiment S collapses on the ground twitching as its body succumbs to liquification. 

Two more experiments arrive just after that, both attacking with a bow and scythe respectively. Ryan dives out of the way of an arrow and then rolls on the ground to avoid the scythe. He kicks the scythe wielder back a few steps and fires a round at the bow wielder. Misses to the left, he blinks back a fraction of a second and predicts the shot, this time hitting it on the mark. 

Drew, a little ways away, fires a barrage of bullets while parrying a mace with his bare hands. 

Focusing once again, Ryan does a backwards somersault to his feet and confronts the scythe wielder who now is being backed up by four other additional experiments, each with a unique weapon. He prepares to parry a short sword but feels a piercing pain in his abdomen. The impaling object lifts him into the air and tosses him like a rag doll. As if a reflex, he goes back in time a second and sidesteps the attack swinging his own knife backwards and landing it into the shoulder of the assaulting experiment. 

From there he draws his gun and begins taking out experiment after experiment. Every shot he misses is immediately corrected with a few milliseconds of time jumping. Every body that liquifies is replaced with another threatening presence. Realistically, only about three seconds go by before his magazine runs dry, though for him, he cleaned out that mag at least five times. Dodging a lethal slash and no time to reload, he drops his gun and prepares his knife to do the rest of the work. 

The feeling occurs to him that he should run, but looking around he only finds an endless sea of monsters appearing out of every nook and cranny in sight.

He spots Drew hacking away at an experiment with blood and sweat covering his face like he just took an aggressive swim through a crimson pool. He’s no longer holding his assault rifle and instead has resorted to a survival knife like Ryan. A staff impacts his back knocking him forward a few feet only to whip back up and stab another experiment right in the jaw before following the slash up to the brain. His accelerated breath is causes his chest to heave as he slashes another experiment while rolling to dodge a war hammer colliding with the previously slashed enemy. 

Ryan returns to his own problem only to receive three throwing knives to his leg. With a quick time jump he avoids them but instead gets a mace smashed into the side of his head. After another reset he dodges both by diving away and standing up quickly only to receive yet another killing blow from a source he isn’t even aware of. He avoids death yet again but is struck not with a tangible weapon but a sudden overdue wave of fatigue by too many jumps. 

Drew, on the other hand, is barely able to stand, yet continues to slash and stab everything within arms length. A pike impales into his shoulder but is broken in half as Drew mashes his hands down on it. Then with a pike blade still in his shoulder, he uses his other hand to slit the throat of the defenseless pike wielder after a incredibly fast dash forward. 

Ryan gets to his feet and rushes forward towards his combat teacher. 

He hopes that maybe they can work together to open a whole for them to escape. Ryan may not be able to die, but Drew can and he owes him that much.

Ryan decapitates an unsuspecting experiment from behind and brushes off a small claw scrape as he passes by. He rams into an experiment holding a shield before breaking through and ending by Drew's side.

“There’s too many,” Ryan exclaims with a panting voice as he turns his back towards the bloody comrade.

Drew pants equally as heavy, “Ryan… I hate to say it, but you may be on your own.”

“What?” His words don’t make it to Drew as the man takes down two more enemies with an acrobatic flip while slashing midair. He receives a large gash in his leg but doesn’t even flinch as he lands cutting a battle-axe user’s weapon in half. Then if that wasn’t enough, he throws the disintegrating ax right into the next line of enemies killing at least one before it disappears. 

Drew pauses for a brief moment taking deep breaths as blood literally drips down his face and off of his chin. His clothes are completely soaked in that dark red color. He looks out to the sun about to peak over the horizon just as a sniper bullet knocks his right arm clean off. 

Time blinks back over and over in an effort to warn Drew about the incoming bullet but whether it be the loud noises of the battle or his own lack of hearing, he can't seem to get the man's attention. In desperation, Ryan pushes past an experiment and tackles the man right before the bullet pierces his arm.

“Drew!” he shouts, pushing himself back up as quickly as physically possible.

Drew’s face is as red as a bottle of wine and a smirk lays across his face.

“Those who live by the bullet die by one…” he coughs, “Ain’t that right…” Drew’s head shifts slightly to see Ryan leaning over him, his voice lowers to that of a whisper, "Take care kid." 

With that, the fierce warrior and assassin Drew Wilbur takes his last breath under the weight of a morning star mace. No amount of subsequent restarts are able to save him from his fate.

Ryan remains on his knees slowly looking at imminent death slowly encroaching towards him. 

Is this it? Is this where his immortality meets an unstoppable force? Is this... hell?

A tear slowly begins to roll down his cheek. Contained within that single tear is a message he can't quite explain aloud. He doesn’t know what hits him or what stabs him or even what shoots him, but whatever happens, the world goes dark and this time he doesn’t wake up a second before. 

--- 

When he comes too, he's not in the a truck or surrounded by experiments, instead he finds himself starring up at a familiar ceiling. Upon pushing himself off the ground, he spots all four of his remaining friends unconscious and tied down to the dining room chairs. The single light source in the room directly overtop of them looks like it were made for an interrogation room. Notices that he's free of any ropes binding, he stands up slowly. 

That's when a familiar gray figure appears from behind his friends.

“You woke up quick,” S-019 says in its monochrome suit of armor. Its menacing slit eyes instill a deep unsettling feeling within Ryan’s bones. “We’ve finally reached the final step.”

Ryan doesn’t respond as he glances between his unconscious friends and then back at the monster before him. Going along with this entity was probably the worst mistake he could have made, yet... what was he supposed to do to avoid it?

S-019 leans over the chair with Max underneath, “You are probably wondering why. Why did you obtain that ability? Why did you have to witness so much death? Why did the world have to screw you over like it has done so many times in the past?”

Ryan's gaze falls to the floor as he slouches his shoulders and hangs his head a bit. He'd be lying if he didn't admit that he was curious. Why has his life sucked so much? What was he supposed to do when his mom died? How was he supposed to respond when families threw him around? Did Steve's death mean anything?

“You don’t seem to be very talkative today?” S-019 taunts in a tone of superiority. Instead of a playful expression and personality, it now seems to want to present itself as a being that stands above humans. That being said, ever time it looks at Ryan, it doesn't seem to be looking down, it's mostly featureless face looks straight on with its entire body turned towards him—it's what Ryan can only describe as an equal. 

"Does it even matter?" Ryan responds aloud. It's not strictly a point of futility, but rather a culmination of partial answers that he's struggle to grapple with. If anything, he truly believes that S-019 won't have a clean answer for it—no one does.

S-019 crosses its arm with a thoughtful look in its eyes, "It's not because you're special, if that's what you were thinking. It's because of your choices. No one is to blame for this except for you." The monster thinks for a moment, “I have an idea,” S-019 stomps forcefully onto the ground, shaking the whole floor and waking up all four of Ryan’s friends from their tranquil unconscious states. 

“Ryan!” Blake and Beth both exclaim at the sight of him. 

“What is going on!” Max exclaims trying to break free of his restraints.

Ryan simply watches with a cold, dead expression as S-019 continues its plot, “I will kill three. You can have whatever is left.”

Ryan glances at all of his friends slowly as they all begin to panic. Blake studies his restraints for anything he can exploit. Max haphazardly starts shaking in hopes that something will slip free. Beth shakes sporadically as if hoping something will shift just enough that she can take notice. Chloe, however, sits silently in place with her head hanging.

“What the fuck kind of logic is that!” Max screams after realizing that he can't break free.

Blake takes a look at the scene and stops resisting for a moment. He goes into his own little world for a moment before looking directly at Ryan, "You know what you have to do."

Ryan shakes his head slowly. To be honest, he doesn't have the faintest clue what is happening, nor how he's supposed to respond. 

"You have to take Chloe!" Blake demands as if the statement were obvious. His resolve more resolute than anything he's ever said.

“This can’t be real,” Beth says to herself as her eyes open so wide that it makes her look insane. A gloomy aura of both despair and confusion expands around her as she begins to laugh quietly to herself.

“Ryan!” Blake repeats, “As your best friend, please do this one thing for me… choose Chloe. I know you can't make a decision, so damn it, let me make it for you! I'll shoulder the burden!”

“It was all an illusion...” Beth chuckles to herself.

Max shoots a strange look at Beth, “Come on man, this isn’t far. Choose me or Blake, we’ll get you through this apocalypse—we have the best chance right. We took all those trips into town together, remember…” his eyes fill with tears. “I’m not ready to die yet man. Please, I don’t wanna die.”

Blake’s face remains stable yet shaken, “I’ve failed too many times, but you have to make the right choice here and choose Chloe.”

“What about me?!” Beth spontaneously exclaims, “I’ve done my absolute best for all of us the last few months. Don’t I deserve a chance to survive?” she quickly loses her cool and breaks out into a fit of laughter and sobbing.

“Please Ryan,” Max attempts once more, “I’m... I'm scared. Scared to see my family again.”

Chloe remains silent with her head hanging and her gaze fixed on her feet.

Ryan switches between them all slowly unable to decide what to think or even feel. If his brain were a building, it would be a pile of ashes and his friends are trying to sift through it to find a corpse that isn’t even there. He wants all of his friends to live. He wants to go back to those peaceful days in the farmhouse… no… he wants to go back before this whole damn apocalypse began and just… apologize… apologize for ever existing.

Ryan blinks and finds himself staring up at the clouds once more, the miraculous blue sky calming his nerves. His gorgeous mother lay next to him and his real father on the other side. This was another false memory; his father never did this with them… no… this was real. This was a memory he had long forgotten; a memory he chose to forget when he turned his back on his biological father.

“I wish we could stay like this forever,” his mother says in her real, authentic, warm, comforting, and soothing voice. A voice that sang him lullabies to help him sleep and a voice that always told him how amazing of a son he was.

“I want nothing more than to make that wish come true,” his father agrees in a hardened yet caring voice; A voice that he forgot he even knew. A voice that is stern enough to know by second nature that he means business, yet soft enough to know he loves you.

“If only that were possible,” his mother continues with a slightly disheartened tone.

“One day,” his father begins, “When the war is over and this world is at peace. I will come back here and we can all do this again. We’ll do it every day and when its rainy we’ll do it in our living room. I promise that I will come back and we’ll live in peace together. Just you, me, and our boy Ryan.”

Both his mother and his father turn and look at him with the most warm and loving smiles parents could offer. He sees them vividly. The slight curve of his mother’s face with her brown bangs that occasionally cover her eyes. His father’s scar next to his eye doesn't make him look scary but rather authentic. His mother’s deep blue eyes are like an ocean in which someone could get lost in. And his father’s smile that always held back immense pain yet only spoke words of kindness and love to him even when he messed up. These were the features that Ryan had long forgotten in his life of trial. Why was he remembering them now?

Ryan returns to reality to find tears streaming down his face. He reaches up and feels the dampness of his face where the streams of liquid had passed. He feels the warmth of his cheek despite the cold outside. Crying was not an emotion he has ever recalled feeling yet right now it is the only thing on his mind. Not a single ounce of hatred or grief or even anger resides in his being. All he wants to do is cry.

“I’m sorry,” he says, closing his eyes and covering them with his forearm to hide his tears.

“Don’t be sorry,” Blake says as his own tears begin rolling down his cheeks, “Don’t apologize to me damn it! You gave it your all and didn’t back down despite insurmountable odds. You know what Ryan, as much as I hate to admit it, I’ve looked up to you these past few months. No matter what happened, no matter how bad the situation, you fought tooth and nail risking your own life just so we had a chance to see the sun rise in the morning. I'm sorry that I couldn't help you more. I'm sorry I couldn't be there when you went to save the hostages with Jeremy. I'm sorry I didn't come with you when you broke out of the school. I'm sorry that we've all been asking so much from you without ever giving enough back.”

Tears fall out of Ryan’s eyes like a waterfall as Max begins to speak.

“Yeah. Caid was always my best friend, but sometimes I felt like there was distance between us... I never felt that with you. I never told you this, but that woman in the freezer was my older sister. Despite what you saw, you lied to me—told me her death was painless... Honestly, knowing that you'd shield me from that should have pissed me off, but it didn't... it was about the only thing that got me through it. I barely knew you, all the while, you did so much—not just for me but for all of us..." he looks left and right at his friends, "You guys tasted my cooking and even laughed at my jokes. For the first time in my entire life I felt like there were other people out there who really understood me. My family never could, my peers never could, but you all welcomed me like a member of… of a real family,” Max laughs through tears, “If I die, at least I can be glad I got to be happy at least once. Right? Is that so bad?”

“Ryan,” Beth says, taking a deep breath to cool herself, “I may never get to see my dad again, but thank you for always reminding me of him. He was such a stoic, kind man who always looked out for his family. I miss him so much… so thanks for... giving me that comfort even if I didn't realize it until now.”

“So Ryan,” Blake reiterates, “Grant us this one selfish desire and allow us to finally give back to you. Please take Chloe and go find a life for yourself, away from all of this chaos and strife. Away from suffering and loss of loved ones. Do that, and we can all die happy deaths. And remember, don’t ever live in spite of us, but live because of us. That's the only think that has kept me going, so maybe it'll work for you too.”

“Is it settled then?” S-019 questions as it stands menacingly behind all of them.

Ryan glances between all of his friends and despite their fear, they all look at him in agreement. 

He made a promise to himself that he'd save them all—not just one—all of them. Here he is being told to cut his losses and run. For God's sake, how many innocent lives have been lost just to get here? How can he accept loosing so many? There's no way in hell he'll give up on everything he's lost to just lose some more.

With hatred burning through his eyes, he reaches for his gun but finds nothing there. He follows through for his knife but finds an empty sheath. His gaze searches his surroundings only to find it empty. 

Is this it? Does he have to choose?

When Jimmy gave him the choice with the gun, the options felt obvious, but this isn't obvious at all. Where is the option to risk his own life for the safety of others? Why does he have to choose who dies!

He slams his fist into the ground, his arms shaking so severely that his wrist twists and he ends up hitting his wrist on the ground instead of his knuckles. The subsequent pain doesn't even make an entrance in him mind. All he feels is the inevitably of that demonic six letter word: "Choice". It's the same word that Vincenzo was obsessed with and it's the same thing Heimes and Jimmy forced upon him. Maybe S-019 was right, he did make choices to get this far. He can't blame everything on those around him.

There is no clean options—there never has been a clean option. So maybe... maybe for once he's just going to abstain. He'll abstain from choosing. 

What else is he supposed to do?

“Okay,” Ryan mutters, his chin quivering as he whips away a few more tears.

“So be it,” S-019 says, growing his hand into a blade and cutting Chloe’s restraints off. 

At first she doesn’t react as her head remains hanging low. Then after a moment’s delay, she rushes off of her chair and into Ryan’s arm. They grasp onto each other tightly unwilling to ever let go.

“I’m sorry...” she says to him as she buries her head into his shoulder.

“It’s okay,” Ryan comforts.

She hugs on tighter, “I’m sorry for… this.”

A sharp pain bursts through Ryan’s abdomen. In the heat of the reunion he doesn’t notice it at first, but then it becomes the only thing he can feel. Slowly he lowers his head to look at the source of the pain and finds a dagger stabbing out of his gut coming from Chloe’s hand. The only problem is not that he was stabbed, but that the dagger isn’t from Chloe’s hand—it is Chloe's hand.

He steps back, placing a hand against his gut as blood quickly seeps out dousing his shirt. Chloe’s face is cold without an ounce of remorse as the dagger reforms into a hand.

“Why couldn't you protect me?” the fake Chloe says, "I thought you'd protect me."

Ryan’s vision shakes along with his body. His head trembles and his core throbs. Every ounce of his being shakes violently between the fine line of sanity and the lack thereof. He lets out a blood curdling scream letting out every single speck of built up stress and pain. Every last particle of emotion that he had bottled up for so long is let out in that scream. Every single last bit. Only when his scream concludes does he reset time to just before Chloe is freed.

He falls to his knees in the new timeline with his sanity and conscientiousness still shaking.

“Kill three and leave me the remains…” he says to no one in particular hopelessly.

“So you understand then,” S-019 says, a dark gray smile forming on its face.

Ryan’s shaking head raises slowly to look at the cruel adversary, though cruel doesn’t even begin to describe it. He can’t even calm himself as he stares into the eyes of a being that enjoys his suffering.

Kill three. He only has three friends left.

“Welcome to the world, Experiment T.”

With that, his remaining friends leave him behind.

--- 

Ryan trudged down the street of the town stepping over countless bodies and bloody remains. The golden sun slowly raises itself over the horizon as he pushes himself straight towards it. As he walks out of that small town on the far reaches of a major metropolis he brings nothing with him. No hope, no despair, no purpose, not even his sanity. Nothing lies ahead for him, not even death.
