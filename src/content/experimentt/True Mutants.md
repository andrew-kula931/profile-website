The blue five gallon bucket sloshes to and fro in Ryan's hand as he overlooks the stadium once more. Beside him stand Blake, Max, and Caid all holding similar containers with similar fluids inside.

After a deep discussion, their plan fell together—although begrudgingly. They found four five gallon buckets in the back of the convenience store probably used for janitorial tasks. Max then climbed into the ceiling panels and cut a yard worth of rubber tubing for seemingly no reason. This odd assortment of materials confused him at first, that is, until Max continued to impress the group with another odd skill—gasoline siphoning. Using the tube and the buckets, he manages to drain gallon after gallon of gas directly from cars and transfer it over to their buckets. This operation results in a sufficient fuel source to burn such a massive pile of flesh.

The second matter that gave them pause was who would be partaking in this operation. Ryan was willing and prepared to do the entire task himself, but it quickly became evident that no else even considered that a possibility. As such, they underwent a quick verbal battle on who would be dumping gasoline on dead bodies and who would be lighting it. Beth initially thought it would be something they'd do together, but Blake quickly countered her with yet another fear for caution. In the case that these "corpses" weren't all corpses, then they'd need an escape route as quickly as possible. As such, it became evident that they'd need a get-away driver, or rather, two of them. 

Queue a series of seemingly trifling argument, and it ends up being another boy vs girl thing. It's not that anyone intended it to be that way, but Alison started the get-away driver roster by vehemently opposing the very notion of stepping foot into the stadium and Ryan naturally took the position of doing the dirty work. After that, Blake, Caid, Max, and Beth all volunteered themselves to partake in the work and Chloe—noticing the lack of a second get-away driver—opted to fill that role. Things were about to be set, but then Caid asks Beth directly to stay with the vehicles. Given Beth's personality, she instinctively objected to such a request, but then Caid dropped a bombshell that even caught Ryan off guard. He told her that if things went south, he needed someone he could trust to make the difficult calls—no matter the consequences. Max didn't understand this point, but Beth did, and that's all that mattered.

Now, Ryan stands before the horrific scene with gasoline in one hand and his pistol in the other. His friends initially all share an authentic feeling of dread and shock. The sheer horror of blood and gore makes them collectively nauseous and likely rethinking their positions. After that first wave of emotion, their unique demeanors begin to take form. Blake closes his eyes for a moment before returning with a look of determination. Max makes a sour face at the vile smell around them before shaking his head as if having some heated internal conversation. Caid stares down at the center pile unwavering, anything passing through his head is perfectly masked by a frighteningly composed poker face.

"It stinks" Caid comments dryly as he twitches his nose, the pungent odor fringing all of their nostrils.

"Yeah, it's awful," Blake adds, "To think, this is what the school looked like."

Caid shakes his head as he starts down the stairs towards the field, "Please, don't put that thought in my head."

They walk quietly as they all take turns visually inspecting the singular mass of flesh in the center of the field. Meanwhile, they also sporadically look around the stadium for any sign of hostile movement.

As the plan stands, if even a single monster appears—one that Ryan can't dispatch quickly—then they'd run without a second thought. Likewise, Beth would wait just outside the stadium and honk at them if something happens that they can't see. One honk if the threat is coming in, and two honks if the threats are outside. Lastly, a sustained honk would be the worst case scenario and result in the get-away drivers leaving entirely. No one liked that idea, but Caid wasn't going to agree to anything unless that piece of knowledge was incorporated into their planning.

"So Ryan..." Max starts looking up at the powered off lights, "Did this happen before or after you were ordered to come here?"

Ryan glances over suddenly not wanting to answer the question, "What do you mean?"

Max shrugs his shoulders, "Never mind, I guess it doesn't matter."

They all reach the bottom of the stairs and the pile doesn't budge from its static position. Staring at it now makes each of them feel like dwarves by comparison. A mountain of corpses is truly and adequate description as the pile rises to at least eight feet tall with both human and partially mutated corpses within it. Seeing disfigured limbs should disgust Ryan to no end, but it honestly relieves him a little to know that some of the mutants that stormed in the night before now lay dead in the pile. It's one less thing to worry about.

"Let's start dumping... I guess," Max says while pinching his nose.

"Right," Blake comments, "I'll make the trail, the rest of you get to the corners."

Without complaint the team disburses across the field, taking positions on the four corners around the pile. Meanwhile, Blake creates a light trail of gas from the base of the stairs to the foundation of the pile. Once there, all four of them begin making a trail of gas at the base of the pile moving clockwise around. Only when they all reach the starting point of the purpose to their right do they stop with their specific section. After that, each of them splashes the remainder of their bucket into the pile. 

Max is the first one done, and as such, is the first one to splash the bucket's contents onto the center of the pile. As he does, the mountain suddenly shifts causing all of them to step away quickly. The undulation begins again, just as before, while each of them stare in horror praying that the mountain doesn't grow legs of its own and charge them. The sound of rolling flesh and cracking bones echoes throughout the amphitheater-like structure for a solid twenty seconds. None of them dare move while the mountain rumbles.

Eventually it does stop, however, and they all release a collective sigh of relief. They then proceed to finish their mission with one synchronized splashing of their buckets. Just as before the pile rumbles, but it thankfully stops in time. The final step is for all of them to return to the base of the stairs and light the decaying mass once and for all. 

Ryan sets his bucket down and proceeds towards the stairs with shallow breaths—to avoid breathing the repulsive air as much as possible. When he makes it back, he finds Blake staring off into the distance with wide eyes and an indecipherable expression. Max, standing next to him, has the same strange look while staring in the same direction. Ryan starts off by waving his hand in front of their face to see if they were in a trance or something, but when they don't react, he opts to simply turn around look at what they're staring at. When he finally does turn back, he understands the situation far too well.

Standing behind the fence at the edge of the field is a fourteen-foot bony behemoth. Its arms, spiked and long, rest on the top of the fence. Its talon-like feet dig into the ground with a wide circumference. Bone sutures sew together a grotesque torso of old, mangled organs. Of all facets of the behemoth, the thing that truly shakes Ryan to the bone is the monster's ghostly visage. Its face has no definable features whatsoever save the remnant of two scarred eyes, neither ever properly healed.

They all get the collective urge to run, but Blake holds out his hand telling them to wait. The monster leans forward and proceeds to crush the fence under its mighty claws before stepping onto the field. Its head begins to scan the surrounding area as if searching for something, but it's at that point that Ryan realizes the truth. It's not searching: it's listening. 

Blake and Ryan exchange glances silently while the former takes a step onto the stairs as quietly as he can. Max and Caid slowly converge together as Max's trembling hands pulls out a lighter from his pocket. The boy shaking boy inspects the translucent trail on the grass before crouching down overtop of it. In the meantime, the rest of them slowly start creeping up the stairs as the behemoth stalls in the outfield.

Max looks back with the lighter hovering an inch over the end of the gasoline trail. He silently mouths "do I?" as he motions the lighter towards the ground. Blake nods his head slowly before mouthing "then run".

With the plan basically decided, Max strikes the lighter with his thumb and sticks the subsequent flame into the fluid. Almost immediately, the trail takes to the flame and fire erupts down the track towards the pile. Once the flame gets to the mountain, the gallons of gasoline staining the sides of the pile burst into a blazing inferno, and with it, comes the screams of the damned. A pandemonium of mutant shrieks erupt as the mountain of corpses is engulfed into flames. Mangled limbs and bloated flesh begins to undulate violently which cause the entire pile to rock slightly. The behemoth steps over to the inferno but stops just shy of the flames as it inspects the pile with its blind eyes.

Despite Blake's silent command, no one dashes away at the onset of the fire. The portal to hell captivates them, or rather, places them in a trance that shackles them in place. The inhuman cries of corpses makes the whole stadium freeze as everyone and everything waits for the shrieking to end. Their desire for silence is only met after what feels like minutes of screaming. 

Only when the crackling of flames finally overtakes the dull groans of undead, do the four guys recede up the stairs at a careful pace. They each watch the behemoth closely as they ascend the stairs. Ryan particularly keeps his hand on his knife just in case anything happens. All four of them make it halfway up the stairs when a single demonic scream beings only... it's not coming from the pile. 

All eyes dart to their right finding a zombie stuck underneath a stand bench reaching out for them. Its legs are completely absent, and it's missing one arm, but that doesn't prevent the painfully loud screams from echoing across the stadium as it uses its singular arm to reach for them. The next place to look is not at the trapped zombie, but at the behemoth, and that is where the true horror sets in. A once stationary behemoth is now lowering its center of gravity and staring directly at them. 

Without thinking, Ryan steps over to the trapped zombie, stabs his knife into its face, and proceeds to draw out his pistol.

"Get out of here!" Ryan commands as he rushes down the row of stands. As he runs, he fires off two rounds at the behemoth.

He hears the vague comments of his friends in the background as the behemoth begins charging in his direction. All he intends to do is draw the behemoth away so that everyone else can escape. He's escaped this monstrosity before, so he's the most likely person to do it again. That being said, his hope begins to waver as the monster completely ignores the stairs and begins stomping up the stands with ease.

Ryan flips around and sprints up a different row of stairs making sure to traverse three or four steps as stride. It's surely the fastest he's ever ascended a flight of stairs, but the rumbling behind him makes him believe that it may not be enough. Despite that nagging doubt, he arrives at the top of the stairs and elects to dive out of the way. As he does, the behemoth's massive claw slams down onto the concrete where he once stood. 

He scrambles to his feet and looks back—or rather up—at the behemoth as it towers over him. His blood runs cold but warms a little as he notices the monster staring about two foot to his left. This is quickly confirmed as the monster slams its other claw down on that spot and narrowly misses Ryan's leg. He thinks about running, but something causes him to wait—not because he freezes up, but because the mutant clearly can't see him. Running might provide just enough noise to cause his death. 

The behemoth withdraws its claw finding that it struck nothing but solid cement and lifts its head. Despite not having any visible ears, he can tell it's listening for Ryan to screw up and make a noise.

There has to be some way to take this thing down for good. When he and Steve fought it off the first time, they took out it's eyes and it ran away. If that pattern holds up again, he'll just need to locate where this monster's ears are, and then he can potentially scare it off. The only problem is that the monster's blank head has no noticeable indentions where ears could be. Only two scared holes are visible upon it's face that now prevent it from seeing and indirectly keep him and his friends alive.

An idea begins to bubble up in his mind, but then someone shouts something and the monster snaps its head.

"Hey ugly!" Blake shouts from the other side of the stadium, "Over here!"

The behemoth immediately leaves Ryan behind and tramples multiple rows of stands to reach the location of the shouting. In the meantime, Blake rushes into the indoor section of the stadium.

"No!" Ryan screams, though the monster heeds him no mind, "Come back!"

He tries to get back to his feet to fire off another round, but he's too late. By the time he gets his pistol into place, the behemoth has already rammed into the wide stadium doors and created a slightly larger entrance for it to fit into. The monstrosity disappears as Ryan searches for what he's supposed to do now. It doesn't take him long to find out that the interior of the stadium is constructed in one wide half circle. As such, he scrambles towards the nearest door and rushes inside.

The inside of the stadium is a hollow husk of what it once may have looked like. That being said, the empty spaces are perfectly clear for Ryan to rush by as loud echoes of bone hitting concrete reverberate down the hall. With his gun in his left hand and his knife in the right, he sprints forward determined to end this monster once and for all—somehow.

When he arrives to the end of the stadium with the greatly damaged doors, he finds the other side of the interior harboring a very similar scene. The entrance doors, surrounded by a wide glass wall with old posters, is now but an empty frame of metal beams with chips of glass. The doors and a wide arch around it are completely replaced with air while most of the glass on the remaining wall is shattered and on the floor or desperately clinging to the frame. Beyond that door and down a few steps is the behemoth towering over a white van, it's claw partially crushing the roof. On the other side of the vehicle, sitting on the ground with his hands over his mouth, is Max.

Ryan frantically searches the scene to find Blake and Caid but doesn't spot them immediately so he opts to focus on the task at hand. He could try rushing the monster outright, but there's a good chance it would hear him approach. A diversion might be an alternative method, but that will just lead to the same situation as before. There has to be some way to take the behemoth down or at least incapacitated it enough for them to escape.

He's about to default to the diversion route, but at the last moment he notices something important that warrants a second thought. The monster's hind legs, while covered in sporadic bone plates, still vague resembles a human leg in formation. The subtle joint at the knee and a vague familiar muscle formation near the foot... or rather tendon. A particular myth comes to mind that just might help him.

The behemoth stomps down on the vehicle underfoot and crushes it with Max falling forward to avoid any debris. The boy manages to keep his lips sealed through the process but not before face planting into the concrete due to his hands being preoccupied with controlling said volume. With a dipping head, the creature gets dangerously close to Max—waiting for the final signal to locate its victim.

In the meantime, Ryan carefully creeps forward towards the monster, trying to move as swiftly as possible while also avoiding enough noise to draw its attention. This attempt is quickly made futile as a car horn rings out across the stadium parking lot. All heads snap to find Beth slamming her hand on the truck horn as she slowly cruises by.

It's now or never. Forgoing any need to remain quiet, Ryan dashes forward hoping he can make it to his destination before the monster takes off. The behemoth lowers its center of gravity as it prepares to show off it impressive speed. Beth puts pedal to the metal and zips the down the street. Ryan takes one step after crucial step. Then, right as the behemoth cracks the concrete underfoot, Ryan slides his knife cleanly through the back of the monster's "ankle". Just as hoped, the monster trips up and crumbles to the ground after finding it's left talon almost completely inoperable.

"Get out of here!" Ryan commands to Max as he jumps back to avoid a stray swing of the monster's claw. 

After evading such a sudden attack, Ryan proceeds to take a few more steps back to watch the monster swing its arms around wildly. It quickly finds that nothing but a crushed car is within range of its arms and eventually decides to give up on that idea. Instead, it slams its arms into the ground and lifts its body off the ground with its now three legs. The monster then rotates around and faces off with Ryan just a few yards away.

Should he run? Take his chances in a chase? Or is there something he can do from here? Uncertain choices swirl through his head as his arms shake and his heart pumps so loudly that he can hear it.

The two advisories have a silent standoff for a brief moment before two new faces enter the fray from behind Ryan. He only notices in his peripheral vision, but he swears Blake and Caid appear in the smashed open glass doorway. This thought is only confirmed as Caid's voice quietly passes by his ears.

"Damn," he says.

The voice is all that's necessary for the monster to rush forward in an unorthodox dash while dragging its unserviceable leg. The sudden onset of motion and a fourteen-foot behemoth barreling towards them is certain enough to get the two boys to sprint away, but's it's also enough to get Ryan running after the monster.

Within seconds the behemoth clears the parking lot and smashes a fresh new hole in what remains of the glass entrance. It releases a muffled roar as if a bony plate were pressed up against its vocal cords restricting any reverberation. The damped volume is both appreciated and necessary to allow Ryan to close in on the monster without it hearing his footsteps. So, from the behemoth's perspective, it likely doesn't even notice Ryan rush right up behind it and until he cuts right throw it's other back leg.

The monster roars much louder this time and begins to flail around just as before. As such, Ryan ducks out of the way to avoid a stray claw that would easily cleave him to pieces. The maneuver requires that he tuck and roll, but he manages to escape without a single massive gash on his body. He then rises back to his feet, being sure to remain completely silent so that the monster can't pinpoint his location in the case that he tries to run. This decision turns out to be the right choice as the monster quickly settles down and begins listening to its surroundings in utter silence. 

The standoff begins once more, though this time the monster is unaware of where he is.

Blake and Caid poke their heads out from around the corner of a concession stand and immediately notice Ryan waving them off. It's not that they can't help, but volume is currently their enemy and trying to formulate a plan in silence is bound to lead to misunderstandings. It'll be safer and easier for him to just handle this himself—no one has to be put into danger this way.

Now having virtually no control over it's legs, the behemoth lifts itself up as if doing some sort of yoga position. It's featureless face glances around as a deep growl echoes through the concrete corridor. 

No pronounced weak point is visible across its mangled form but Ryan trusts his impossibly sharp knife can procure as vital region with enough strikes. The only real issue is avoiding a critical hit from its hefty arms. Even a single backhand would undoubtable end the fight if not kill him. He'll have to pick his strikes very carefully. Maybe if he's agile enough, he can sever some sort of tendon in an arm and remove it from play. Heck, he did it with two legs, so what's stopping him from doing so?

A hint of conscious courage fills his veins as he tightens his grip on his knife. This giant monster which nearly killed him and Steve before is now literally on its knees before him. If it can kneel, then it can't chaise, and if it can't chase, then maybe it can killed. For the first time, defeating this inhuman monstrosity doesn't feel so far fetched anymore.

Ryan reaches into his pocket and pulls out his motorcycle keys as quietly as possible. Then, while the behemoth is still listening intently, he tosses the keys over to the other side of the monster. Upon impacting the ground, the keys emit a distinctive clinking sound which causes the monster to snap it's head in that direction. Following this reaction, Ryan lurches forward and slices his blade right through the monster's right arm. He doesn't just aim carelessly and hope lady luck to grace him, instead he targets the inside of the arm, exactly where the joint bends inwards like a typical human. Dragging the blade as deeply as possible he can't help but marvel at how little he feels in the process. The splitting flesh, separating bones, and snapping tendons are but little vibrations in the cold metal that passes through it. Ryan simply slides the blade clean through and releases it out the other side. And as simple as that, the arm falls away hanging on by a thin layer of muscle and bony skin. 

Completely awestruck by how easy the attack went, Ryan doesn't notice as counter attack arriving from his blind spot. For a split second, he can feel an invisible force churn inside of him, like a wide muscle spanning from the top of his head to the tips of his toes. The seemingly imaginary muscle tightens, but any further investigation in the matter is violently interrupted by a large claw striking his side and flinging him back through the glass hole. 

He sees the world spinning, the stadium entering and exiting his vision. Then, in a episode of black flashes, he finds himself staring at a patch of green grass. The small leafy pillars flow back and forth slowly as a small white, crystalline substance lands on one of them. That same grass pokes the side of his face so he quickly rolls over and lays on his back. Only then does the onset of pain begin.

His body gasps as it realizes he has no air left in his lungs. He tries to breath but his diaphragm only drops a fraction of the way down before it hits a physiological wall. Attempting to breath again, he finds it to be a futile effort as his lungs refuse to operate as they're supposed to. The normal, oxygen rich breaths he's accustomed to are quickly replaced with quick, shallow breaths which hardly satisfy his need for air. In addition to this pain, he looks down at himself to find three laceration lining his right arm, cutting perpendicular to his old scar. As he winces, he tries to reach over to stop the bleeding but finds his fake leather jacket slowly closing in on itself, repairing the hole swiftly. Whether out of fear for the jacket or the pain itself, he frantically pulls his arm out of the sleeve only to lay back down as the pain surges through his body and causes his arm to shake in pain. 

Although he initially diagnosed them as laceration, a quick second glance lets him know that the claw marks aren't actually as deep as he initially presumed. A great deal of blood drips out of each wound but he doesn't see a bone underneath and the bleeding that is occurring seems to be gradual rather than pumped. He's not really sure what the deduction tells him, but he convinces himself that the cut isn't vital. His thoughts are then once again interrupted by a burning sensation in his eye as everything on the left becomes red. Closing his left eye and touching his forehead he's returned with a crimson palm. 

Through his entire ordeal, the pain, the disorientation, the shock, and everything else, he finds himself subconsciously comparing it to the serum he was injected with. He wouldn't call it a rational comparison, but the cuts and dizziness feel so insignificant to what he felt then. His arm still shakes and he can't stop wincing with a shock chambered in his thought, but his mind isn't spinning quite as fast as it probably should. 

He looks at his arm one more time and finds something peculiar on it. A small white substance lands right beside a trail of blood and immediately disappears. He wonders what it could be but then an identical substance lands right beside where the first hand, and then another, and another. Looking up, he finds countless flurries gracefully descending towards the ground. Then a gust of wind picks up and carries them away while simultaneously chilling Ryan in a way he hadn't felt before. Watching the small white crystals fall, for a moment, feels the same as watching the clouds roll by. It's the only thing he cares to do or think about at the moment.

This illusion is shattered as a car door slams shut behind him and blonde hair waves in front of his face. The blurry visage of a familiar person stares right into his eyes as they shake him forcefully. He simply watches the curious figure move as it lightly touches his arm before saying something he doesn't quite understand. Perhaps a few blinks would clear up the confusion and he could get a good look at the person in front of him, but his attention is immediately drawn to the stadium as the behemoth appears in the wide hole.

Ryan points out to it, still mostly in a daze, as he searches for where his knife went. He finds it but only when looking a few yards away on the pavement so close yet so far away. Maybe if he had fallen just a bit closer to the stadium he would have landed with it—right on the unforgiving concrete—as a bloody smear.

Without even thinking he tries to stand up, to retrieve his weapon and finish off the monster, but the person before him shoves him back onto his butt. He finally looks up and finds Chloe staring at him with tears welling up in her eyes. Her lip quivers as she tries to simultaneously assess his arm and his forehead.

"I...I—I" Ryan mouths finding his voice to take a considerable effort his his lungs continue to struggle, "It's coming."

Chloe immediately looks back at the one armed monster dragging itself out of the stadium directly towards them. A river of greenish blood following it as it's arm acts as more of a pump than an appendage. Perhaps Ryan's own arm may have looked like that had the lacerations been a bit deeper.

Looking back, Chloe studies Ryan's blood face as she searches for an answer. Her eyes then lower a bit one a solution finds her. 

She reaches overtop of him and clumsily pulls his pistol out of its holster. She then fixes her hand position on the weapon while nearly dropping it twice. Finally, with a shallow stance, she aims the gun at the slowly approaching behemoth. Her arms and legs shake together as her finger tightens on the trigger—the behemoth completely oblivious to what stands before it.

The behemoth slows as it approaches but Ryan knows it'll get to them in time. Seeing this to be true, Chloe strains her eyes as the decision becomes an inevitability. Then in the blink of an eye, a Ford F150 rams straight into the side of the behemoth causing it to roll over twice and take it's last breath. 

The truck, now decorated with a broken bumper and fresh blood all over it, reveals a serious looking Beth from the driver's seat. She quickly exits the vehicle to inspect the monster she just ran into.

In the meantime, Chloe turns around and falls to her knees, the pistol shaking in her hands. Ryan reaches over to it and easily extracts it from her hands. He inspects the weapon briefly but pauses as he notices the small knob near the trigger—the safety is still on. Shaking his head, he stuffs it back into its holster and makes a face as the icy wind and nauseating pain in his arm clash at the same time.

"Ryan!" Chloe shouts as he falls backwards.

It's weird, he's lost blood before but this time is making him surprisingly faint. He almost feels tired. 

"Ryan!" Chloe exclaims as she presses her hands against his chest while glancing between his arm and his forehead, "Don't close your eyes, everything is going to be alright."

The snow continues to fall all around them as Chloe's hair makes a sort of tunnel around her face. For a moment it feels as though she's the only thing he can see. Her blue eyes and lips that always seem to arched upwards. Her tendency to... to look at him. She's always looking at him. She's like a mother. No. Just... compassionate. That's what a mother is. For some reason, looking up at her makes his pain feel further away.

Beth appears in the corner of his vision. Max and Blake follow closely behind. While multiple voices echo in the background, Ryan can't take his eyes off of Chloe as a warm tear strikes his cheek. This small droplet of water isn't his, however, as Chloe whispers, "Please don't close your eyes."

A deep seated fatigue washes over his body as the pain seems to subside just a bit. His eyes flutter closed, but he manages to pry them open a bit. Just a bit longer. Chloe's cheeks grow flush and red. She's... crying? For him? He can't look away. Why? His eye keep coming back. Every time they open... they're looking back at her.

Maybe this isn't so bad. Friends. Her. Perhaps... he can feel comfortable closing his eyes—just for a moment.

Just... for... a moment.

No... No? No. No! NO!! 

His eyes burst open, his heart speeds up a bit, and he forces himself to a seated position. Beside him Beth wraps the shredded remains of what looks like a shirt around his arm. Behind her, Blake paces back and forth with a worried look until he notices Ryan sitting up. Caid, Max, and Allison all sit around him with a similar expression which transforms to shock as Ryan matches eye contact with each of them. Lastly, Chloe—sitting behind him—supports his back to prevent him from falling backwards. Her face is the only one that doesn't look worried as tears occupy too much to call it simple worrying.

"Ryan!" Blake exclaims dropping to a knee beside him, "Are you alright?"

"Of course he's alright, a little blood won't stop him," Max comments receiving a disapproving look from the group.

All eyes focus on him. He can't match any of them.

The world still feels faint and his desire to rest feels even more potent than before, but at least he's still here. He's still... he's... still... here. 

He lowers his head and hides his face.

He's still here.

Beth looks around, "The snow's picking up, we need to take cover before heading back," she turns back to Ryan, "Can you stand?"

Ryan clenches his left fist and grits his teeth. No, he doesn't think he can stand... but he'll force himself to.

With that, he leans forward and drags his feet below him. With Chloe and Blake's aid, they move to take shelter from the worsening storm.
