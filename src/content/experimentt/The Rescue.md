The only door to the next warehouse is locked shut with three locks and a wooden board across it. A subtle convex indent in the door protrudes out right around shoulder level. Beyond that, nothing out of the ordinary can be spotted along the creaky metal building. He's about to try the key out, but then he hears something, something soft and brief. If he's not mistaken, it sounds like a whisper coming from the other side of the door. 

Immediately he reaches for the first lock and shoves the one of the two keys inside. The oddly shaped key doesn't resemble the key hole in the slightest and gets stuck unable to fit into the lock. In light of this, he flips the ring upwards and grabs the second key attached to the ring and tries that one—it fits. Upon twisting, it doesn't budge which causes his grip to give out and his hand to slip off the key. He then stares, not at the key, but at his hand which continues to shake subtly. He clenches his hand into a fist and finds that the shaking stops—strange. With that out of the way, he twists the key in the other direction and is relieved to find the lock snap open. He promptly pulls it off the hook and as the lock clangs he hears the faint whispering immediately die away. Despite this, he drops the lock on the ground and raises to the next lock—it's a combination lock.

Without even realizing it, he attempts to stick the key into the same spot on the lock but is prevented as the metal key comes in contact with the numerical pad. Almost confused, he pulls the key back and studies the small mechanism. The key... doesn't work? Oh, this lock doesn't come off with a key. He immediately stuffs the keys into his pocket, pulls out his knife, and cuts the remain two locks off. It's a good thing he had a key for the first one... wait... why did he need a key in the first place?

He stops himself and studies the door with a blank expression. What... no, why?

Shaking his head, he decides to pull the wooden beam off the door and drop it on the ground. He never realized that he put his knife away to do so. Finally, he reaches for the door handle and notices his hands completely bleached with red. Not only that, but all the way up to his elbow—underneath a completely clean jacket—is the same sticky substance. He attempts to wipe it on his jacket but nothing comes off as if oil on water. Instead, he tries wiping it on the metal wall of the building but finds that a considerable amount of it has already dried to his skin. Feeling a bit disappointed, he takes a deep breath, finds his gun and knife returning to his hands, and slowly starts pushing the door open.

Inside any voices that may have broken the eerie silence immediately vanish into thin air. All eyes turn to him, not with curiosity, but with a deeply rooted dread. Many of the densely packed crowd crouching on the floor flinch at his arrival, particularly the children. Perhaps the realization that he isn't the creep would ease their worries and clear up any misunderstandings, but that relief never comes. The entire crowd, hungry, neglected, and terrified, watch him meekly as if any attention to themselves would get them selected to be the next experiment. A child beside him cowers in fear by stuffing their face into their mother's side, an old man lowers his head in defeat, a middle-school aged boy clenches his fist in rage, but his father holds him back. None of them understand that beneath the blood, imposing weapons, and distinct leather jacket, is just another one of them wanting to save... to save anyone.

After a quick count, he determines that twenty-five unique individuals are huddled up inside this room no larger than a small classroom. When he doesn't make an initial move and finds himself just glossing over the crowd indecisively, he gets the awful feeling that he might be doing the exact same thing the creep does with this poor crowd. That thought alone causes his chest to tighten, but for some reason that doesn't get him to stop. Instead, he continues to look around searching for something... but what was he searching for?

Suddenly, someone moves and he glares right at them. What he finds is not a threat but a face he's stared at for hours whether he liked it or not: it's Stephanie Grail. His former teacher's face is struct with shock as her eyes hold nothing back–his entrance evoking an intense bitter-sweet relief. Then, to second her surprise, someone else in the crowd makes a move. He turns slowly which results in a trembling body pressing up against his with two delicate arms wrapping around him. His arm instinctively rotates to stab the body off of him but right be can his arm stops. For some reason his arm refuses to puncture the object no matter what he does—the hand holding the gun is no better.

"Thank you, Ryan," the person wrapped around him says softly as if to make it a message for him alone to hear.

His arms begin to shake, and before he knows it, they seem to relax on their own. Both hands fall to his sides as he takes notice of her blond hair and face tucked against his shoulder. 

"Are... are you alright?" Ryan asks, his voice shaking for some reason.

No response is given right away, instead a middle-aged man stands up from the crowd looking directly at the two by the door. Ryan glances at him warily.

"Who are you?" the man asks aloud. The brave soul in questions is covered in dirt and grime, slight bags hang under his eyes, and his air looks oily enough to be untouched for days. It's not just him, however, everyone in the room—save Chloe and the others—looks as if they haven't seen sunlight in some time. Many of them hesitate to make eye contact with him as they hang their heads in despair. The few who do seem to have lost any fighting spirit a long time ago.

"That's Ryan," Stephanie Grail explains before Ryan can formulate a response, "He's a former student of mine. H-he was with us when we got captured."

The man's mouth drops as he turns to Ryan with disbelief, "Does that mean... we're saved?"

Ryan closes his eyes, Chloe's warmth somehow calming his spinning thoughts, not entirely, but just enough to get a coherent idea out, "The doctor... is dead. Is everyone alright?"

The man looks around at the group, seemingly taking the authority to speak on all of their behaves... or at least that's the way it looked. Instead, he simply hangs his head and shields his eyes. It's clear that news of the doctor's death is quite relieving to him, but perhaps news alone isn't strong enough to alter his despair outright. 

Chloe steps away from him and looks him in the eyes—one of the few people in the room to do so, "Everyone here has... has been through a lot."

Ryan looks at her, his thoughts blanking once again, "And what about you."

This question seems to take her off guard as her response is grossly delayed and proceeded by a quick glance over to Stephanie. Unlike the rest of the people here, she doesn't look exhausted and distraught, instead she appears more apprehensive than anything else. 

"That doctor," Chloe starts quietly, the entire room freezes at the mere sound of his unofficial title, "he selected us one at a time... and we often heard the screams," her composure shifts to a more rushed one, "what about Amber? She just got taken out not long ago. Is she okay?"

Amber. Jeremy. The inevitable facts flood of him so quickly it makes his chest tighten and his knees wobble. If it weren't for the stray glances around the room, perhaps he actually would have collapsed at that very moment. Then his gaze lands on Chloe who silently asks for an answer and his symptoms only escalate. Finally, as his body nears the precipice of collapse, all the lingering emotions suddenly vanish and the exit door becomes the only thing he can think about.

"It's time we get going," Ryan concludes as he steps towards the door and finds no handle present to pull it open. 

The realization of a one-way-door becomes an intellectual barrier immediately, yet his hand still reaches for a handle in a futile effort to escape quickly. When his grip lands on empty air, the reality of the situation finally sinks in. He inspects the door again searching for any hidden mechanisms that he missed the first time but unfortunately finds nothing, only a flat, blank sheet of metal presiding over the inside of the door. 

An ordinary reaction would probably be shock or anger—the realization should fire him up. That being said, such thoughts never occur to him as he raises his knife and stabs it through the crack in the door. He then pulls the blade down and cuts straight through the metal block locking the door in place. Immediately afterward he pries open the door just enough to get his fingertips on the edge and proceeds to fling the door open exposing the outside air. The action is met with the eyes of the entire room in utter disbelief, not directed towards him, but towards the possibility of freedom.

 "The prison outside of town is safe," Ryan directs, finding these logistics to be the only thing he can think to say, "Make it there, and you'll be protected."

Stephanie steps through the crowd and approaches Ryan directly, "Did no one come with you?"

Ryan studies her slowly before gradually coming to the determination to shake his head, "No."

Only after his response is given does he realize that it was, in fact, a lie. A lie he'd have to take to the grave.

Her eyes widen but she accepts his answer. 

He looks out the door into the dark night beyond, "Are there any vehicles nearby—ways to get everyone there?"

Chloe steps forward beside Stephanie, "We were brought here in a van, I don't know where it went afterward, but maybe it's still here."

"Some people here aren't from the prison," Stephanie points out, "They might want to go back to the groups they were a part of."

Still looking out the door, Ryan contemplates his options to little success. No matter how hard he tries to concentrate his thoughts feel so clouded and unclear, as if a thick fog has been enveloping his mind dampening everything. Then he notices his heart racing for a reason that still eludes him, "I can go look for something..." he turns to Stephanie.

Without even needing to say anything, Stephanie nods her head, "Go, I'll make sure everyone here gets out."

Nodding in agreement, Ryan heads out the door looking around the warehouse for any indication of a mode of transportation. With it being night an all, surely they can't all walk to their destination. A car is mandatory.

He gets a few paces away before someone's presence behind him causing him to turn around. Behind stands Chloe with some sort of determined look masked behind the dim lighting of the night.

"What are you doing?" Ryan asks directly.

"I'm not going to let you go alone," her voice full of resolve as if the matter has already been decided.

A compulsive urge to resists falls over Ryan in a way he strictly can't process, instead he just gives in to it, "It—it's not safe."

Chloe doesn't waver, "I know, now let's check the other side of the building."

She starts off past Ryan, but he catches her arm. If she peers into the wrong window, she might find that lab, find Amber. No one should ever have to lay their eyes on that sight ever again—ever. 

Chloe resists by pulling her arm away, "Ryan, I'm not asking, let's go."

"You... you can't," he says unconfidently.

Immediately she grabs the front of his jacket, her force surprising Ryan a bit, "My father and brother are in there right now. I... I can't sit by and do nothing. Not after my mother got taken."

"She was taken?" Ryan gasps weakly.

Chloe nods, averting her eyes, "Yeah, she was taken away before I got here. It's been three days now."

Ryan's fist clenches at the mere thought of what could have happened. If she was taken by that creep then... it's probably... he can't take it anymore.

"Come on," Chloe asserts letting go of Ryan's jacket, "Let's go find some vehicles."

"But—but," Ryan stutters, the knife in his hand shaking at how much his hand refuses to remain still, "your mother!"

She turns her back and takes a single step away, "Don't you think I know that?! Of course, I want to look for her, but if I do that, then it'll put everyone else at risk. If the doctor comes back... then..."

For some odd reason, Chloe's exclamation seems to have calmed Ryan down. His hand stops shaking and his heart returns to a more comprehendible pace, all because of a single outburst. It perplexes him, yet understanding it feels trivial compared to what needs to be done. Instead of dwelling on it, Ryan walks forward and catches up with Chloe, "Let's go."

The two of them proceed further down the warehouse only to arrive at a small parking lot near the gate. Chloe notices the motorcycle sitting in the gate opening but doesn't comment on it as she turns her attention back to the warehouses in hopes of finding some stow-away vans. At first glance, limited by the darkness, they both find nothing but concrete walls and metal roofs. 

"Of course it's not going to be easy," Chloe complains, a hint of worry leaking into her annoyed front. He's not sure why he noticed that.

Suddenly a very dangerous thought enters his mind. He quickly disregards it as far too risky and dangerous. The idea alone is absurd. Not to mention the fact that he'll have to see... oh God. 

His chest suddenly tightens and he feels nauseous. The effect is so intense that he stumbles to the side and barely remains on his feet. This reaction, while dampened as much as Ryan can manage, still causes Chloe to grab onto him.

"Are you okay?" she asks quickly as she grabs his arm and wraps it around her shoulders to support some of his weight.

Ryan promptly pulls his hand away and steps back, "I'm fine. I just... I don't know what came over me."

Chloe doesn't buy his lackluster explanation, but for some reason she doesn't press the issue. Instead, she starts off around the corner of the warehouse and immediately freezes. Then, looking to Ryan with wide eyes, she waves for him to follow as she rushes forward. Ryan dashes around the corner after her and falters too when he notices what she was referring too: a long black van.

Rushing after her, they arrive to the van and Chloe immediately tries the driver side door. Unsurprisingly it's locked. Chloe releases an exasperated sigh as she lightly punches the door.

She looks back to him, whatever face she's making is lost in the darkness, "I don't suppose you know where the key is?"

Ryan shakes his head. How in the world would he know where the keys to this vehicle are. Keys don't just lie around, they... wait.

Out of curiosity, he pulls out the keys he stashed in his pocket and promptly hands it over to Chloe, "Try this one."

She looks at it hesitantly before looking back at Ryan, probably incredulously. That being said, it doesn't stop her from turning to the door and pushing one of the keys into the lock on the door handle. To both of their surprises, it works. Chloe immediately pulls open the door, throws herself inside, and stuff the key into the ignition.

"Hop in," she commands as she starts the van with a disloyal chug. Regardless of how it sounds, it'll have to do. 

Chloe proceeds to reveal herself as a rather competent driver as she seamlessly finds the headlights switch and flips them on to the brighter version. She then carefully navigates around a trailer, past the front gate, and right in front of the door where the rest of the hostages wait. Afterward, they both hop out and Stephanie directs the weary crowd to start packing into the van. Twelve or so people stuff themselves into the back of the van, and only then do they realize the bitter truth: one van is not enough. 

"Are you sure there wasn't another van back there?" Stephanie asks.

Chloe shakes her head, "No, I looked, but this was the only one."

Stephanie bites her lip, "Well then... we'll have to make two trips. But the prison isn't very close, I don't like the idea of leaving everyone."

Chloe crosses her arms as she stares at a middle-aged man and a teenage boy on the other side of the room. Their faces feeling rather familiar to Ryan for some reason, as if he's seen them but never learned their names, "I don't like that idea either, but we don't have much choice."

The two women go silent as their only option becomes a rather precarious decision. That's when Janelle steps forward rather reluctantly, "What about Ryan? How did he get here?"

All three women look to the boy who stands at the sidelines a quiet as a statue. Their gazes demand a response.

"I came here on a motorcycle," he explains which doesn't alleviate any of their tension. He debates trying his terrible idea, but it dawns on him that he still doesn't know how to drive. Even if he doesn't risk his life for some stupid vehicle, there's no guarantee he'll even be able to get it back. But... does he even deserve the luxury of not trying?

"I—uh," Ryan starts again, catching the attention of the ladies, "I might know how to get another van."

"How?!" Chloe exclaims stepping into his face, "Where is it?!"

Ryan averts his eyes, "It's nearby... I can try and go get it."

Stephanie releases a sigh a relief, one far to calm for the situation, "Okay, while you do that, the rest of can search for Amber and the other people that were taken."

The final comment petrifies Ryan down to the deepest depths of his heart. They can't. They can't search. What's waiting for them is far too dangerous. Amber's... dead. She's dead... they just need to leave.

"Don't," he blurts out unaware he even did it until everyone looks at him. Despite the fog in his head, his heart still pounds at all the unexpected attention. 

"What's wrong?" Stephanie prompts reaching out grab Ryan's shoulder comfortingly.

As if paranoid, Ryan dodges the hand and steps back. It wasn't his intent to be disrespectful but, yet again, his body took command before his mind could, "Just stay here and wait."

Immediately he turns to leave, a decision he didn't properly plan out as he does it. Some deeply rooted desire strikes him and tells him to run, before he can resist it, he's already outside the bright light of the van. Once his mind catches up, he stops in his tracks. The eyes of the entire crowd beating down on his back. He wants to go, he wants to shield everyone from knowing what happened, but there's just one problem. One unavoidable problem that he's pushed off for so long he probably deserves to be spited by it at this point.

"You need someone else to drive the van, don't you," Chloe comments before the tension can get any worse, "I'll come with you, I think everyone else just stay put in the meantime. It's late and no one is in a position to fight back if things go south."

One of the men from the crowd—the same one who questioned him earlier—steps forward and grabs onto Chloe's shoulder. Turning her around, he looks into her eyes sincerely, "Are you sure about this."

Without a moment of hesitation, Chloe nods her head with amble resolve, "Yes dad, please just trust me."

The man contemplates the request very deliberately, despite his worn demeanor. In the meantime, Ryan comes the realization that this stranger is, in fact, the same man from the photo he found. It's relieving to know at least one thing worked out in his favor.

"Okay," the man says slowly, "I'll trust you."

Chloe smiles warmly before stepping over to Ryan. With one last glance around, he departs into the darkness knowing full well he's headed to the last place he ever wants to see again.

Their walk to the front gate is bitterly silent. Ryan expected Chloe to offer some idle conversation—like she usually does—but that never comes. Instead, their march through the darkness remains as eerily quiet as possible with only their steady footsteps against the concrete producing any sound. Silence is typically a comfortable position for Ryan, yet at this moment, he can't help but feel unease in its midst. What is she thinking? What is he thinking? All these questions eat away at him as he consciously suppresses any contemplation on where they are headed. 

When they arrive to the motorcycle, Ryan hops on right away and starts the engine. Chloe takes a seat right behind him and wraps her hands around his waist. The feeling of her hands on his hips sends a strange sensation through his body, but seeing how Chloe doesn't seem uncomfortable, he avoids addressing it. 

The ride is brief and met with the same bitter silence as before. Ryan notices Chloe watching certain buildings as they pass, but she never comments on a single one. As they pass by the silhouette of some sort of niche stop, he can feel her grip tighten against his jacket, but she still retains her calm composure.

Just as before, the ride between the warehouses and the stadium is short. Honestly, Ryan wishes it could have been at least an hour longer to give him time to prepare, but alas he pulls up the edge of the parking lot and shuts off the engine. He stares at the looming walls of the sports arena with a cautious gaze. Inside the massive structure is a low growl like large machinery twisting its gears at a steady pace. He has no intent to find out what's inside, but the presence of such a noise makes him far more weary than before.

"Stay close," Ryan commands without looking back.

Chloe nods, "Of course, I'll be careful."

For some reason that response wasn't good enough for Ryan and his grip on the handle bars tighten far more than he wanted, "No, stay right next to me."

He doesn't quite look back to notice her reaction, but he gets the feeling that she's slightly taken aback by the sudden demand for compliance. That being said, she doesn't seem upset by it.

Without further ado, they both step off the vehicle and Ryan leads the way deeper into the parking lot. His initial concern is whether zombies and mutants may be lurking around the parking lot. With it so dark out and no street lights to illuminate any potential threats, they're essentially walking through a minefield. He doesn't spot an awkward looking clumps on the ground, but the various cars around the parking lot provide ample space for a surprise attack. As they proceed, Ryan draws his knife with his right hand and grabs Chloe's hand with his left. He doesn't even think about it as he does it and finds Chloe a bit surprised by the action. He can feel her stare in the back of his head, but then, for some strange reason, she relaxes—completely.

"It's okay Ryan," she says softly, "Everything will be alright."

He glances to the side, just enough to see her with his peripheral vision. What could have caused her to say that after how quiet she's been? After all, he's the one who demanded she stay close to him. If anything, wouldn't it be his job to comfort her? He's as calm as he's going to be, but her... 

It's around that moment that he notices how still Chloe's hand is. He remembers vividly how often he's had to stop his hands from shaking, but he thought he was relatively stable at this particular moment. Then, before he knew it, he finds Chloe's soft palm anchoring his as it slowly trembles. He closes his eyes briefly to conceal any contempt he may be showing for himself, though in the darkness, it probably doesn't matter regardless.

Keeping a close eye on the passing vehicles, he finds nothing hiding in the spaces between jeeps and SUVs. He even goes so far as to make a small clanking sound against a car to prompt any lurkers out—none come. With that in mind, they finally arrive at a destination Ryan thought he'd never see again. The small minivan is exactly where he remembered.

Chloe inspects the vehicle closely, particularly the license plate, "Isn't this..."

"Yeah," Ryan responds as he lets go of her hand and steps around the van to the other side.

He proceeds to walk a few more paces through the dark, foreboding lot before crouching down in front of a black blob against a curb. Taking a deep breath, he reaches out to flip the object in search of the car key, but stops as he finds the trembling in his arms has escalated to such a degree that he can barely hold them straight. His arms shake as breathing becomes quickly becomes a conscious effort. 

"What's wrong?" Chloe asks, unable to see what's before Ryan clearly. In light of that, she starts walking closer.

"Stay back!" Ryan exclaims a bit too suddenly, his body making the decision for him.

Chloe leans back in shock, "Seriously, what's wrong?"

Turning back to the black blob against the cement, he forces his shaking arms onto it's squishy surface and ruthlessly flips the blob over. Underneath the mask of darkness, Ryan finds the a distorted visage with a gapping hole in the middle. The copious amount of blood already dried across the face and ground.

Chloe gasps softly at the sight of it.

Memories flood into his mind. Of Jimmy. Of the stadium. Of the masked man... of Jeremy. Yet, despite how heavily his heart pounds and the tightly his teeth grit together, he can't break the fog laying over his mind. Despite reliving the entire sequence of events intellectually, he finds himself largely unaffected by it. That fact alone terrifies him and that terror being the only thing he can properly discern amongst the sea of swirling, dampened thoughts.

He reaches across the surface of the corpse and sticks his hand into the only pant pocket he can find. Inside the pocket he finds two oddly shaped objects. The first feels very similar to the key, but the second is much more foreign... well on second thought, it's not foreign at all. He recalls vividly how often he would lift this teardrop shaped object to his ear. He also remembers the awful music Jeremy would listen to a bit too loud. If only he could hear that niche music just one more time. The second object never comes with him, instead he retrieves only the key.

Rising to his feet, he relaxes his shoulders and closes his eyes. The churning inside the stadium being the only sensation he truly can comprehend. Then, after a sort moment of silence, he outstretches his arm to Chloe with the key. She accepts it without resistance but, unlike Ryan, she doesn't take it as an indication for action. Instead, she simply stares at him.

"Ryan..." she hesitates as she holds the key close to her chest, "...is that?"

Ryan shakes his head, "It's time to go."

"But—" Chloe attempts, stumbling over her own thoughts.

He begins walking back to the motorcycle, "Let's... let's just go... please."

Her eyes never break from his back as he silently advances towards his motorcycle. As the minivan starts up, he can feel her concerned presence even at a distance. 

When he buried the Hughes, he was so weak, he assumed the trembling was his body on the verge of collapse. This time he knows he's not as fatigued, yet his body trembles just as much. The only difference is that he's not... he's not... he's not really sure how to say it, but this time feels different somehow. Not that it's easier, but that he's oddly distant from himself.

He can't bring himself to look up as they drive the two vehicle back to the warehouse. This time the driver following him seems much more cautious and in control... if only she would have accelerated a bit more aggressively, perhaps he could convince himself—even if only for a brief moment—that Jeremy was still here.

---

When they get back to the warehouses with an empty minivan, spirits seem to sore. One of the middle schoolers in the group even cheers as they pull open the van door before Chloe can even come to a complete stop. Ryan grinds to a halt a short distance away while everyone seems to be preoccupied with cramming into the minivan like sardines.

Unlike the rest of the former hostages, Stephanie breaks from the crowd and confronts Ryan.

Half of the woman's face is covered in a dark shadow while the other half is lit by the headlight of the motorcycle, "I hate to ask this of you, but I still want to search for Amber. I'm not certain I'll find her, but I have to check the other warehouses at the very least. I can't leave without even trying. My conscience won't let me."

It's a good thing Stephanie is mostly blinded by the light and that no lights shine on Ryan to illuminate his face. If they had, she may have caught sight of his morbid expression and lowered eyebrows. All he wants right now is just to leave.

"Just get in the car and let's go," Ryan tries very softly, almost in an exhausted manner.

"But," Stephanie argues, "We can't just leave her here!"

Ryan glances up and finds Chloe standing off to the sidelines like a ghost spectating the scene.

"Please, let's just go," Ryan halfway pleads.

Upon the subtle shift of tone, Stephanie hesitates to respond. When she does, it's far more uncertain, "Just... just give me five minutes, that's all I'll need."

"Mrs. Grail," Ryan starts, finding that honorifics are more habitual than her given name, "Amber..." he chokes on his words unable to say a single thing further—whatever face he makes is consumed by the shadows. He tries again to clarify, even vaguely, some sort of reason, but nothing manages to make any further than his mind.

"Mrs. Grail," Chloe jumps in, "I think he's telling us to go."

Stephanie looks back at her other former student with a distressed face. As much as he'd like to forget it, she is the one who invited Amber onto the shopping trip. In a way, she likely feels responsible for what happened. Ryan can't blame her one bit, but that doesn't change the fact that no one should ever step inside that warehouse ever again.

With one last glance, Stephanie hangs her head in defeat, "fine, we'll go."

Afterward, Stephanie squeezes into the minivan, Chloe retakes the driver's seat, and all three vehicles pull out of the warehouse lot. Ryan leads the way back to the prison. Although he's alone while he drives and constantly looking for threats that might be lurking in the darkness, he can't help but notice how exhausted he feels, yet his eyes don't droop—not even once.
