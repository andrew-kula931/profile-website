Reign's office is dimly lit with only one lamp left on to illuminate the bookshelves, desk, and most importantly the windows with the best view of any faction. The boss himself and the vice president both stand over the maroon carpet in front of the windows discussing something very serious based on the tension he arrives to. Reign's face seems to have gained a wrinkle since the last time he saw him which honestly doesn't surprise him in the least. He also takes notice of the confident aura around the vice president. He can't imagine Elizabeth being any older than him, yet she possesses wisdom far beyond her years. Her hair always seems to be in some functional style to remain out of the way, but she never seemed to cut it short. Her blonde hair commonly stuck out the back in a ponytail but for the first time it rested down by her shoulders in a much more casual way. He almost gets the feeling that he's interrupting a personal family conversation.

"I'm not interrupting, am I?" he starts off with.

Reign takes the lead as they both notice his intrusion, "No not at all, we were actually waiting for you."

Markus raises an eyebrow as he makes himself a bit more comfortable in the room. He finds the couch across from the desk to be a suitable location.

Reign continues, "First off, I want to congratulate you on preventing a war by saving the hostage from the Eagles, Jaylin I think her name was." Markus nods as Reign keeps talking, "Now then, I really don't have much to say, but Elizabeth here is the one that summoned you."

Elizabeth takes the queue to join in the conversation with her ever diligent tone, "Yes that's right, I have been battling complaint after complaint to try and prevent war from breaking out, but it gets harder and harder each day. A league is beginning to form between the Eagle, the Nurse, and a few other factions. Nothing is concrete but if we don't get any results here soon, bad things will happen. Really what I wanted to ask is…" she pauses, looking Markus straight in the eyes, "Do you have any information on the Mayor's bunker?"

The hair on the back of Markus' neck stands up. It's a reasonable question given what he said back at the meeting, but at this point, Harvey and the bunker have dropped on his priority list. Not only that, but the Eagles are now working with the Nurse faction. That can only mean this Doctor Crane escaped and is back at work. Hunting that cultist has superseded some potential cultist that may be lurking in an old, forgotten bunker.

Markus sighs and crosses his arms defensively, "I'm getting close, but a lot is going on right now."

"Tell me about it... though," Elizabeth continues pushing forth an invisible pressure like an interrogator pressing for answers, "If and when you find it, what do you plan on doing with it?"

Reign, surprised by how the conversation is going down, leans in intrigued. 

Markus glances out the window briefly looking at the streets shrouded in shadows, "We both know information like that's not free, so I'll tell you if you tell me everything you know about the bunker... and about this new coalition forming."

"Deal," Elizabeth responds without hesitation.

Markus closes his eyes to find a moment to think. What should he say? What is the best way to get out of the situation without drawing them into his personal vendetta, or worse, putting them in the crosshairs of that hooded enigma? The Reigns are trustworthy and won't spread any information, but they are also smart and could use what he says against him if push comes to shove. For now, he will have to settle with the most likely scenario.

"I'll break in, get information, and then probably leave the city. What happens with all the food inside is not my concern… Now tell me what you know."

Elizabeth looks at her father with a flash of concern on her face but quickly adjusts back to a calm and collected posture. "I've had thoughts about the bunker for some time now. After the first few months, everyone gave up on looking for it. Don't you find it so weird that a massive bunker could just be undetectable? We know for a fact that it's in the city because the mayor was giving a speech right before the city was quarantined and locked down. Also, a few weeks after the Fall we found a note in the Mayor's office saying that the bunker was just finished under the city and a few unnamed individuals could join him. So, we know it's in the city and most likely in one of the wealthier residential area where construction was commonplace."

Markus nods, Elizabeth was right and he didn't have much more info than she did. That being said, Regin takes the opportunity to share his own thoughts.

"This bunker has the capabilities to feed the city for more than a year, at least allegedly. Even if that's only partially true, it's an avenue that we must pursue."

Markus hears the sincerity in his voice and understands that they share a viewpoint. None of them want war. 

"I'm getting closer every day," Markus explains, "I actually have a pretty strong lead too. I believe it has to do with this new coalition forming."

Reign and Elizabeth exchange a glance briefly. Reign speaks up first by asking, "And what might that lead be?"

Suddenly, a knock on the door makes the room go even more silent than it already is.

"What is it?" Reign calls out in his typical, authoritarian voice.

A man walks in and immediately solutes; a sight that Markus can't help but look away from as he remembers his own time in the military.

"At ease," Reign commands, "what's going on?"

The man responds, "We've just got word that another meeting between each faction has been called for tomorrow at noon."

"Already!" Elizabeth blurts out.

Reign turns and looks out the window to the street waving his hand to dismiss the soldier. He puts his hand on his chin. "I can't say I'm surprised for another meeting to be called, but so soon? We could use this to maintain peace…" he pauses but only briefly, "We're going to need some leverage if things go south." He pulls out an access card from his desk and holds it in view.

From the confused look on Elizabeth's face Markus can conclude that this key is a guarded secret.

"What–?" Elizabeth starts.

"This card goes to a military checkpoint in an underground parking garage under the capitol building. No one has checked it out yet because it's a dense hive. I haven't met anyone skilled enough to explore down there until you Markus, but I respected you too much to ask you for something so life-threatening. So, I'm going to give this to you and let you decide what to do with it, but I have a feeling I already know your decision." Reign hands the card to Markus.

"Wait!" Elizabeth stands up, "If that's really the checkpoint under the capital it'll have priceless files about government plans. It's practically a field operation headquarters of the city. I thought it was destroyed during the Fall?"

"He's right," Markus joins in, "I know that place he's referring to. There's at least ten high class parasites, going in would be suicide. Even the Death Order has labeled that area to be off limits. No one can make a round trip there alive."

"That's what I thought," Reign says, lowering his head a bit.

"But," Elizabeth and Reign both snap back to Markus, "I might be able to figure something out."

Elizabeth, without a hint of hesitation, says, "I'm coming with you."

Reign takes a double-take by the remark before quickly adding, "No, he just said it would be suicide why would you even think that?!"

"Isn't it obvious, if we get access to that information we could solve more than just a bunker issue. That place has the potential to find a way to retake the city… I'll admit it's a bit of a stretch, but if there's an auto-deleting function I need to be there to get the information before it's gone." Elizabeth is now leaning against Reign's desk with conviction written across her face.

"Absolutely not, this kind of thing is way too dangerous to be worth it. Back me up here Markus." Reign is clearly playing the protective parent role and with good reason so why does Markus have to pick a side.

"That parking lot may be one of the most dangerous places in the city. Untrained people wouldn't last a second down there." 

Elizabeth tries to protest, "I'm not untr–" but is cut off as Markus continues.

"But, she's right about the auto-destruction of information. After the government found out there were spies within their ranks they began destroying classified information if the wrong people tried to access it. I came across it recently when a laptop exploded after attempting to operate it… For that reason Elizabeth would only be able to see those documents for a short period of time."

Markus has a newly adopted countermeasure for the meltdown, namely Jenks, but he still realizes that if he doesn't take Elizabeth she might try to go on her own. In his mind, it's better to baby sit her than risk someone deliberately putting themselves in harms way.

"Exactly," Elizabeth says relieved.

"That being said, she doesn't actually have to come down there with me to see it, I could use a short range camera to broadcast what I'm seeing while she stays at a safe distance."

Reign raises an eyebrow and considers the proposition; it's clear he doesn't want Elizabeth to go, but he understands the importance of this mission. Finally, he gives a deep sigh and agrees with a simple "Fine."

Elizabeth grins at Markus.

"Meet me in front of the capitol building two hours after sunrise."

"I've managed to find out quite a bit," Jenks explains as he and Markus walk down the street towards the capitol building, "This coalition that's going on is clearly being orchestrated by some sort of puppet master. It's the same sort of thing we saw in South America with El Niño, the gang leader. I haven't tracked down this cultists whereabouts, but I think I'm getting close."

"Thanks," Markus says sincerely, "Your work is helping a lot."

Jenks nods, "Sure, also I've been looking into that fungus that's been popping up recently. It's proven to be extremely toxic and widespread. I don't actually think Volker started it though. There are too many differences between the chemical makeups."

Markus raises an eyebrow at his comment, "Seriously? When did you become a chemist?"

Jenks offers his signature smile casually, "I'm not a chemist, but when you work in tech you've got to learn fast. Had to whip out some high school ball knowledge."

"Okay, then where did this fungus originate?" Markus wonders.

"I don't think it matters," Jenks counters assertively, "There might be a way to cure it."

"What do you mean it doesn't matter?" Markus' words are cut short by the realization that they made it to their destination with a single girl waiting for them with anticipation. Elizabeth spots them almost immediately and jogs over to them.

"It's honestly weird seeing you without some kind of guard force," Markus calls out as they all meet up.

"I know, it's weird for me too. My father wanted me to bring guards, but I thought that you would say to keep it small. Also, just because I'm a leader of a faction doesn't mean I can't hold my own in a fight," she gestures to a pistol at her hip and a machete on her back. 

Markus rolls his eyes as he draws a parallel to Cleo. Elizabeth's focus then turns to Jenks.

"Oh, this is my friend Jenks," Markus introduces, "he's going to make sure we can save as much information as possible before the documents melt down."

Jenks extends his hand to Elizabeth in an oddly formal manner. She accepts it with a smile. 

Jenks' signature smile becomes a bit awkward by Markus' standards, "Markus has told me a great deal about you. He described your beauty in much detail, but I say his words didn't do you justice."

Elizabeth glances at Markus with a weird look while he rubs his forehead. He forgot that Jenks can be like this sometimes.

Markus ends the small talk by heading towards the entrance of the parking garage. Below the infamous capital building is a small road blocked off with a fence and a toll. The parking garage isn't the largest in the world as it's meant only for government officials and guests. The one vehicle entrance a floor below the ground radiates an ominous and foreboding feeling of decay. 

His plan requires a bit of trial and error as he isn't certain his camouflage still works. To be honest, he hasn't encountered a parasite since the Revenant which means the nature of his strange ability is up to chance. In a perfect world, he's still invisible to basic parasites and will be able to sneak on by. Volker proved that it can lay traps, which is another point of consideration—one that he does not take lightly.

"I'll set up on the top of that office building over there and connect to you when you're ready," Jenks says walking away.

Elizabeth stays behind for a moment staring at Markus. "So what exactly is your plan?" 

Markus gives a half smirk, "Stealth." 

He enters the parking lot.

As expected, the parking garage is as dark as a moonless night which causes him to turn on his night vision goggles as the last remnants of sunlight disappear. A couple of Infecteds stand dormant against the walls here and there, but none of them open their eyes as he passes. Surprisingly, no cars are actually in the parking lot which allows for plenty of visibility and even more escape routes. 

He makes his way deeper into the parking lot without any problems. Five minutes of slow deliberate walking pass before he comes to the gate of the checkpoint. 

This is the moment of truth. 

He pulls out the access card but realizes there is no power to the gate. He promptly pulls out his wire cutters to get through the obstacle. The slow and methodical cutting is relatively quiet, yet that doesn't prevent his heart from skipping a beat every time the cutters click together and sever a metal link. When all is said and done, he realizes that the power to the checkpoint isn't on, which ordinally wouldn't be a problem, but his access card won't open any doors if there's no power. He decides that he needs to find the backup generators before going any further.

He passes through the hole he made in the fence and prepares his gun for anything. His escape route is still clear so he can ditch the operation if he's discovered. 

The further he walks into the checkpoint the higher the density of parasites becomes. After only twenty paces past the gate, he spots a Screamer class, a Slender class, and a Deformity class. Each passing parasite seems to be asleep as they don't even look over when he creeps by. With his escape route slowly closing, he swallows hard and continues going deeper with his AR at the ready. 

He searches the place for another ten minutes before finally arriving at backup generators. He finds five in total with four completely unworkable after some sort of impact left large, concave dents in the metal. Markus lowers down to the remaining one and presses the power button—nothing happens. 

He pulls out his camera, turns it on and puts an earpiece in; Jenks' voice comes up immediately, "Connection successful."

Elizabeth's voice mumbles in the background saying, "Does the camera have night vision capabilities?"

"Quiet," Jenks asserts, "I'm talking to him now. Okay, Markus. Without power it's gonna get more difficult. So according to a blueprint I found of other checkpoints, you should find some kind of portable trailer that can be accessed with a keycard. Find it."

Markus looks around attaching the camera to his night vision goggles so Jenks and Elizabeth could see what he sees. From what he could see, the checkpoint is covered in dormant parasites varying in size from a small child to a car. 

"Good God," Elizabeth whispers in the background.

Multiple tents sit scattered around the area. A line of trucks sit against one wall and the last truck has a large metal box connected behind it. That has to be the trailer he was referring to. Once he gets to the doorway of the box, Jenks starts talking again.

"Okay so now you'll have to use the device I gave to you."

"If those things wake up, he's screwed," Elizabeth mutters.

"Stay quiet," Jenks says in a hushed tone.

Markus pulls out a small black box with wires haphazardly wrapped around it. To an outside viewer, it would look like a mix between a computer and a recently collapsed building. 

"Place it directly to the right of the cardreader."

He puts it up the card reader and promptly hears a noise. It's not one of the eerie ambient sounds of sleeping parasites but something more mechanical. Almost like the near silent hum of a generator. Jenks keeps talking, but Markus tunes him out and simply swipes the access card through the card reader. The locks of the door release with a click. 

"Well, I guess that works, I thought there were going to be more steps involved, but what do you know?"

He glances back briefly at the army of sleeping parasites behind him. Not a single one bothered to wake up as he snuck by, but that could change at any moment. Markus' hands grow sweaty underneath his tight gloves.

Perishing the thought, he enters into the trailer and closes the door behind him as quietly as physically possible. Just to be safe, however, he keeps one eye on the door at all times and an ear open for whatever lies beyond.

The trailer looks to be a mini lab with tables attached to each wall and beakers laying all over each table. Test tubes of a variety of colors label themselves with numbers ranging from 1-76. Jars with what looks to be infected mice sit next to the beakers, though the mice have been dead for some time. A stack of papers sits in one corner and the most important thing sits right in front of the entrance, a laptop. 

"Place my secure generator device next to the laptop and stick the USB in to counter the meltdown process," Jenks instructs.

He does as he's told and is relieved to see the laptop turn on with no signs of overheating. Instead of opening to the usual login page, it instead opens to the desktop courtesy of Jenks. The only caveat is that the battery percentage is zero percent—and charging.

"One hell of a device," Markus mutters to himself.

"Thank you, this thing's my pride and joy," Jenks comments as Markus places his hands on the dusty keyboard.

Markus opens the file window and browses through. Most of it is in codewords and scientific abbreviations. After getting halfway through the file list, he spots something of interest; it's a file by the name of "Log 203: Volker". The file opens up to a document, looking to be a report of some kind. Elizabeth and Jenks talk in the background with their volume turned down. The document starts off with formalities of addressment before getting to the meat of the page. The document reads:

_We've discovered that Specimen 002 is being controlled by Specimen 001 as a hive-mind system. The systematic destruction of important military facilities is no coincidence. After a series of tests, there is no way to sever the communication without killing the organism. Fire and extensive physical damage are the only two weaknesses to Specimen 002._

By Specimen 002 it means day to day parasites and Specimen 001 is Volker himself. At least, that's what Markus surmises.

_Specimen 001 is a sentient entity calling itself Volker. Its true intentions are unclear but seem to have some correlation to world domination and the eradication of homo-sapiens._

The rest of the document doesn't really say much more than that, so Markus moves on. The government did manage to get that far which Markus already knew. During Allegiance One's last operation he found out most of the information he knows now, the government apparently didn't spread the idea of Volker around when he was debriefed. It makes him wonder how much of that last mission was actually unclassified during the Fall. 

Markus moves on to browsing through the logs and files for something else. He arrives close to the bottom of the file list when he finds it - Log 470: Pearson Leadership. Elizabeth starts talking loudly but is still being muffled by the almost muted mic. The document starts off similar to the last but then picks up.

_After connections to nearby cities were cut off, all essential government officials of Pearson escaped to a nearby bunker for safety. The bunker is owned by the mayor of Pearson and the location was not disclosed to the government under the pretense of private property. Several squads searched for the bunker and managed to find the mayor's personal car outside 39123 Maple Ave. The house was not searched due to extremely dangerous Specimen 002 occupying the space._

_With the absence of government, the city of Pearson has entered into a state of Martial Law under the government…_

That was it… The location of the Mayor's bunker is on Maple Ave or at least this could be a huge lead to the true location. 

After skimming through the rest of the documents he notices the very last one which is a video. Upon double clicking it a warning error erupts to the forefront of the screen. It reads: Notice! Video was stopped unexpectedly, would you like to play what was saved? Out of curiosity, Markus clicks on it.

A woman dressed in a white lab coat sits in the very same spot that Markus now stands. She seems to be sleep deprived while fear visibility plagues her face. She begins talking quickly and informally for a scientist of such a high position.

"I don't have much time," her face filled with panic and tears nearly falling from her face in fear, "Volker is not the only danger, the government was not brought down by the parasites. The parasites are just a means to clean the world of any stragglers. I repeat the governments of the world were infiltrated and destroyed by a different entity entirely, it is assumed that Volker and this other entity are working together. This accomplice is—"

The scientist is cut off by the door swinging open and her screaming at whatever opened it. This scream is quickly followed up by a long sharp knife or spear like object impaling into the scientist's head and she falls to the floor.

Markus looks down only to find an old, dried trail of blood below him. The video doesn't end there, however.

He continues to watch as something... or someone appears within view. They seem to be a middle-aged man with an indifferent look to him. Upon looking down at the body, he simply blinks once before noticing the laptop.

"Oh," he says to no one in particular, "So that's what you were doing."

The man steps over to the screen and looks around trying to find where the camera is. When he does eventually find it, he stares directly at it as if watching the person on the other end.

"The Director doesn't want you to know what this woman was going to say," the man says very slowly and methodically. 

Then the impossible happens.

In the blink of an eye, the man's face seems to ripple in place. It almost looks like a stone being dropped into water as his face literally begins to shift and contort until a moment later, he looks completely different. The man's new appearance sends a chill down Markus' spine. The new appearance is the former mayor of the city.

The man smiles a twisted smile, "And I'll make sure it stays that way."

What comes after is a silent scene of the mayor slowly dragging the woman out of the room with a single hand clamped around her leg. The remaining seven hours of video consist of a single screen pointing at the wall with no movement at all.

Markus takes a deep breath as he hears Elizabeth say some pretty concerning things in the background. Jenks tries to hush her up.

"Will I be able to unplug the computer without any serious consequences?" Markus asks Jenks.

There is a moment of silence before Jenks responds with a near whisper, "It should work now. Go ahead."

Elizabeth in the background starts talking again with a serious tone. Jenks goes quiet. Finally he whispers into the microphone.

"We've got company. Center J going dark."

He hears mic cuts out and notices the faint light on the camera go out. Quickly unplugging the computer, he stuffs it into his bag. 

Navigating back through the dormant parasites is a gruelingly slow process, one that requires two separate detours as a stray parasites stumbles into his path. Regardless, he manages to escape without drawing any attention. It pains him to know that his stealth skills haven't diminished since back then. If anything, they're only stronger than before.

Outside the parking lot he is met with a barrage of guns pointed at him. Over twenty men, each wearing a mask, stand unwavering and spread apart. Markus studies the masks only to realize the unexpected: it's the Death Order. Behind the line of men Jenks and Elizabeth kneel on the ground with ropes bounding their arms.

"What are you doing here?" Markus asks quickly, glazing over the formation of parasite hunters.

Two people walk forward removing their masks. Behind one of them is the familiar face of an aged man: Darek. The other man is the notorious leader of the Death Order: Dimitri. Dimitri is a man in his late thirties with a deep scar across his forehead. He was once the most infamous hitman on the continent, but changed his ways when parasites looked to be a larger and more challenging threat.

Darek speaks first, "Let me start off by saying that the weapons are only here to reinforce our statement, we don't intend to hurt anyone."

Markus narrows his vision and makes it clear he needs a better explanation.

Darek continues, "We have, for the longest time, been a neutral party devoting ourselves to making the city safer. Though after my apprentice died at the hands of an enigma, it's become clear that our prior presumptions may have been misguided and naive."

Markus glances back to Jenks and Elizabeth. Elizabeth has her head down, clearly knocked out and Jenks looks the same. Then he notices that Jenks is twitching his eye. He repeats an inconspicuous pattern of a single eye twitch with a second pause in between. It's a sign saying that Markus has less than a minute before he breaks free of his constraints. 

"We want the information you found down there. The famine will hit us just like everyone else, but if you're willing to do something reckless like this, then it means you have some important information in your possession," Darek maintains a calm composure throughout.

Dimitri pipes in, "Let's just cut to the chase, join us, and we can use the information together or else we'll have to take it from you. I see you have Elizabeth with you, so Reign probably wants that info too. Hell if we join them though."

"Let me start off by saying that I'm here to prevent war," Markus explains, dropping backpack to the ground. "I have some info right here I can give you," Markus secretly pulls the pin on his flash bang, "Also, for the record, I won't hold this against you as long as you don't hold this against me."

As a cloud's shadow drifts away, it causes many of the Death Order members to squint under the light. That's the same moment Markus throws the activating flash bang a few feet in front of him and dashes to the side trying to make it to cover. Right as the flash bang begins to activate, the Death Order opens fire at the sudden commotion and Markus takes a bullet to the leg. He grimaces in pain but only for a moment. He dives off the ramp and into the grass to the side of the capitol building. Behind him, bullets begin to fly.

Before any bullets can hit him, he crawls behind a marble sign. With nowhere else to go, he may have been screwed, but then he hears the men shouting as they realize Jenks has already made it a nearby building with Elizabeth on his shoulder. Three dead bodies lay in his path as Markus peaks out from this cover and takes down a Death Order member.

"Fall back," Dimitri shouts as steps into a car and starts the engine, "Get out of here unless you want to die."

With the Death Order disbanding, Markus gets up and limps around the building. He makes it a few blocks before he looks back to find a rather plentiful stream of blood following him. Upon looking down, he notices blood gushing out of his legs.

Great he thinks, as he rips out a tourniquet from his bag and begins to wrap his leg. It grows tight, and the bleeding stops, but that doesn't prevent the faintness from starting. Within seconds, he's so fatigued he can barely walk. A few paces more and he's forced to take a seat in an alleyway. 

He clutches onto his gun as his eyes struggle to stay open. Passing out in the alleyway is a surefire way to get eaten by a stray Infected so he must remain conscious. His head dips over and over, but he absolutely refuses to close his eyes.

Then, if things weren't strange enough, he gets a strange sensation in his leg. The open wound, which had been bleeding profusely before, suddenly stops bleeding. Blood clotting is a real thing, but that doesn't occur as suddenly as it just did. The edges of the bullet wound slowly begin to fold in on themselves as the gapping hole begins to fill like a small stream of material slowly filling a pit. It's a sensation unlike anything he's ever felt bef—actually he has felt this before.

Back when he met Volker for the first time and nearly died fighting the horde of Infecteds, he woke up to a very similar sensation. Whatever is inside of him, it's been there a while. He can only conclude it's from his childhood. In hindsight, this unusual ability is probably the cause for his reckless behavior. Back in the Near East, he could get away with dangerous stunts because he brushed off the consequences without a second thought. These effects though, are new—like the inconsistent camouflage. Whatever it is, maybe the Bunker will answer some of his questions.

The dizziness and faintness subsides slowly, but when it does, Markus rises on his soar, but functional leg, and departs swiftly.
