On his way back to his motorcycle, Markus finds Darek in his path. The aged man doesn't seem to be waiting for him, rather they coincidentally crossed paths out in the street in front of the HoV. This unexpected encounter immediately triggers a conversation as Darek approaches him directly.

"I'm glad I ran into you," Darek says with a jovial smile, clear wrinkles appearing in the corners of his eyes.

"So you're not going to point a gun at me this time?" Markus criticizes in a stand-offish manner.

"Oh, right," Darek says hesitantly as he rubs the back of his head, "Sorry about that, it wasn't my call to make."

"I figured," Markus says dryly, "Who should I be pissed at then? I got shot."

Darek gives Markus a once over trying in vain to find where the bullet injury is. Of course he doesn't know that said injury healed almost immediately, but it still hurt. To be honest, he forgot about that entire confrontation until Darek appeared in front of him. Right now he's feeling more spiteful than anything else. No one got hurt, and he threw a flash bang in the Death Order's faces, but it still bugs him that it happened in the first place.

"Allow me to apologize," Darek says directly with a little head bow, "I advised Dimitri against it, but he made the call in spite of my thoughts. It was never our intent to hurt anyone, we were simply looking out for our own. Besides, we didn't end up getting any information in the end."

Markus sighs, "Right. Anyway, why are you all the way out here?"

Darek gestures to the HoV, "In light of recent attacks, the Death Order has been especially busy trying to track down who or what is wiping out entire faction bases overnight. I thought I'd come back to where this whole fiasco started and maybe reverse engineer how these attacks are being orchestrated."

Transitioning over to his nearby motorcycle, Markus finds himself staring down the street in thought, "I take it you're pinning these assaults on Experiment T."

"Experiment T?" Darek wonders aloud.

Markus shakes his head, mentally kicking himself for letting that slip.

"Oh," Darek laughs, "Is that what we're calling him now? I have no quarrels with that, but after he killed my apprentice this entire situation has become a bit personal."

That's right. Amber, Darek's apprentice, was killed during their first attempt at tracking Experiment T down. The plan was rather shotty to begin with, but their execution wasn't bad. Despite their efforts, however, Experiment T placed a bullet clean through Amber's head before anyone could intervene. Markus coincidentally found himself talking to Experiment T later that day, but he completely forgot that Darek has been out of the loop since that event. Perhaps it's time to catch him up.

Markus takes a look around to ensure there are no ease droppers, and only when the coast is clear, does he turn back to the Death Order recruiter, "There are actually three threats. There's Experiment T—the man who attacked the HoV—and then there's a Revenant on the loose," Darek's eyes widen, "The last one is a bit... complicated. Just assume that last threat is another Revenant."

Darek pinches the bridge of his nose as he begins walking in a little circle, "That definitely complicates things. Not only one but two Revenants, this 'Experiment T', and all three could be independent or coordinated. Just when I thought I was starting to get somewhere."

Feeling a bit bad that he laid it down so hard, Markus pats the guy on the shoulders, "Take heart, this is problem for us all, not just you."

The supportive comment was intended to lighten things, but this seems to have the opposite effect as Darek purses his lips and looks at the ground, "That doesn't change the reality of the situation. And a famine is on the rise. The future of the Death Order and the city is on precarious soil."

"The Death Order too?"

Darek nods slowly, "Yeah. We're hurting for food just like everyone else, but we're also stretched thin as half the city is on high alert right now. Two settlements were wiped out in the last four days and odds are, the next one is tonight."

"What makes you so sure?"

"Call it intuition," Darek comments as he looks down the street—namely towards the north, "I intend to camp out a particular settlement tonight. Catch the culprit in the act if my suspicions come to pass."

Markus undoes the chain locking his motorcycle to a light pole, "And what suspicions are those?"

"The two settlements that got wiped out were relatively close. This third settlement is the only one for a few miles around. By proximity alone, it's the obvious next target. I hope I'm wrong, but you can never be too sure."

"How many people are you going to take with you?" Markus wonders, genuinely curious as a Revenant nearly took him down. Any less than five people won't stand a chance against a Rev. This isn't even considering Experiment T nor Harvey.

Darek lets his hands rest on his hips while he thinks, "I know of one or two that might join me... regardless, probably not many. Dimitri already denied my idea under the pretext of internal affairs. I'm more worried about the city than the Death Order at the moment, so I guess it's a conflict of interest. Regardless, I'll go even if I go alone."

"I don't think that's a good idea," Markus warns, "This isn't something you can take down alone."

With a quick nod, Darek smiles, "Which is precisely why I'm glad that I ran into you. I was hoping that you'd be willing to give a hand. We can consider it a continuation of our last engagement."

That's what he was afraid of. If he doesn't go, Darek will be observing a Revenant or worse by himself. To be honest, Markus isn't sure he can convince the man to rethink his position as info gathering is the bread and butter of the Death Order. On second that, Darek's probably only hoping for information anyway, but that still doesn't rest easy with Markus. He'd offer to go alone in his stead if he were healthy, but that simply isn't the case right now. Perhaps the best thing to do is info gather and Markus wouldn't want to miss out on that. This might actually be the best option available.

"Alright," Markus admits, "Just tell me where."

Markus had never heard of the Crafter faction before arriving outside the gated community. Despite their namesake, they aren't actually crafters in the blacksmith or cobbler sense. They are actually a faction of caretakers for orphaned children. The name allegedly only came about because they commonly export unneeded children's toys—many of which they 'crafted' themselves. In a way, this small faction on the far north side of town is a pseudo-orphanage. The only distinction being that it contains entire families in addition to the orphaned children.

The gated community's perimeter is... functional, and that's all the credit he can reasonably give it. The front gate is manually operated with no additional reinforcement. The subsequent fence looks exactly the same as the day it was made. How this place survives parasite attacks, Markus can't guess, but he'll admit that a metal fence is better than nothing.

Watching over the entire complex, Markus finds himself naturally on top of a nearby water tower. The community is far enough away that he needs binoculars to keep watch, but this part of town is severely lacking in tall buildings, so this position will have to do.

Darek has the same idea as he appears by the ladder.

"So you really came alone," Markus comments as he pulls the binoculars off of his face.

With a light-hearted chuckle, Darek takes a seat beside Markus, "I had hoped one or two people would answer my call, but I underestimated how spooked normal people are of what's out there."

"Are you saying that I'm not normal?" Markus asks incredulously, "That's rich coming from a Death Order recruiter."

Darek laughs a little harder this time, "You forget that I'm here too."

They wind up sitting on top of that water tower for a few hours. Their conversation doesn't reveal much as it sticks to small talk mostly, but Darek does a good job of being pleasant without being fake. Every time Markus connects one of his stories, Darek will follow it up with one of his own. This process keeps them busy as Darek learns a little bit about what the war looked like and Markus learns about him.

Apparently Darek used to be a coal miner in Kentucky back when he was young. Eventually the coal industry began to diminish, so before he lost his job, he wound up moving to Pearson to attend the university for a degree in communications. Seeing him now, the decision fits quite well as he's much more a talker than a miner at this stage of his life. Anyway, he wound up getting a decent job in the city and settled down.

"Did you have any kids?" Markus wonders after receiving a detailed explanation of how he taught his wife to stop burning bacon.

Darek shakes his head, "No. It's a bit of a sad story, but my wife battled cancer as a child and ended up sterile. We simply decided never to adopt."

Markus takes a look through his binoculars to avoid looking at the guy, "I'm sorry to hear that."

"Psh, it's never bothered me."

"I don't know," Markus comments, still watching the base from far away, "I think you would have made a great dad."

Darek pauses for a moment as if contemplating a comment made by someone a third of his age, "...not back then. But I appreciate the thought regardless."

The conversation reaches a pause the sound of metal crashing against metal rings out from far away. They both look, but the point of origin can't be less than a mile away. 

"I do wonder," Darek ponders, dismissing the background noise, "Did you have any family, Markus?"

With a quick shake his head, Markus prepares to dismiss the question entirely, but then he hesitates. Grant, Carter, Jenks, Wilbur, and Nox enter his mind and he can't quite sit still thinking that he'd deny ever knowing them.

"No in the traditional sense," Markus eventually decides.

"Hm?"

Markus sets the binoculars down, "My comrades were my brothers. And I think that's enough for me."

"Is that so? It still amazes me that someone so young could partake in a war like that."

It's at that moment that Markus phone begins to vibrate in his pocket. Knowing that it could only be one person, he's quick to stand up and take a few steps away from Darek.

"What is it Jenks?" Markus asks, pulling the phone to his ear.

"I checked it out," Jenks reports on the other side, "The supplies are... sparse. Everything that's still edible only accumulates to about three months of food."

Markus' jaw nearly drops, "That's terrific news."

"No it's not," Jenks counters fiercely, "It's three months for one person."

Markus' heart sinks at the thought. The one cache that was supposed to offer them good news results in extraordinarily lackluster results. Unless something changes, Cleo's prediction about the famine is all but assured.

"I did however find evidence of that cultist," Jenks explains, "It's quite late, but I'm going to follow the lead."

Markus looks up at the moon overhead. Not a cloud in the sky dare obstruct the perfect view of the waxing gibbous, "Okay, stay safe."

Jenks laughs on the other side, "I should be telling you that."

"Sure thing."

The call ends and Markus returns to Darek who patiently counts the stars.

"That bad huh?" Darek wonders.

Markus glances over as he picks his binoculars back up, "So you were ease dropping?"

With a simple shake of the head, Darek smirks, "You were not very subtle."

Rolling his eyes, Markus takes a seat with his back against the thick metal tower. His back sends an irritated shock through his arms as he remembers how long it takes third degree burns to heal. After sitting up here for three hours now, he's beginning to wonder if this place will ever get attacked. To be clear, he doesn't want these innocent people to die, but he's itching to do something and three hours of idle chatter are not on his to-do list.

"I do fear for the city," Darek comments without prompting once he discovers that Markus isn't going to elaborate on the phone call, "We've all been through some tough stuff, but this famine has me worried."

Markus nods with his eyes briefly closed, "Tell me about it. It's these sorts of things that make me glad I fly solo."

Darek raises an eyebrow, "You're quite lucky to be able to say that. Very few are bold enough to tackle this broken world by themselves."

Pulling the binoculars to his face, Markus checks in on the faction one more time. When he finds nothing, he debates calling it a night outright, so he simply places his binoculars on the ground and stands up—rather slowly. Darek watches him struggle to stand up, but doesn't comment on it.

He turns towards the ladder but then stops in his tracks as his peripheral vision catches something moving off in the distance. Upon turning back, he only finds an empty street and the dark, moonlit shadows of houses. Then the movement shifts his attention closer to the faction base. The small blurry figure he manages to notice makes his blood run cold. Amidst the darkness, he can't quite tell what color the entity is nor its general body composition. What he does notice, however, is four inhumanly thin limps attached to a frail frame.

By the time he actually locks onto the target, the parasitic creature has already reached the front gate. Its sheer velocity paired with its tiny body easily crash straight through the gate leaving a small hole behind. Unfortunately for it, the impact causes some severe damage as it collapses onto the ground with one of its arms twisted backwards. Then, after only a few seconds of laying on the ground, the dislocated arms contorts back into place and the parasite stands back up and begins looking around the inside of the faction base.

Markus and Darek are already sliding down the water tower railing by this point. Darek likely wants to help out of a sense of purposes and duty—one that isn't circumvented by the sheer danger. Markus, on the other hand, is sliding down the ladder despite his injuries for another reason: he was the one who let Harvey out.

It takes them about a minute to reach the front gate and by that point they arrive to serious damages to the faction. The front gate aside, three nearby houses already have broken down doors, a vehicle in a garage has a giant hole through the engine, and two people lie unmoving outside broken windows.

"Where did it go?" Darek asks swiftly as he slides through the hole in the gate.

Markus observes the surroundings carefully knowing exactly how fast Harvey can be. Nothing stands out as the entire block is as still as it was just three minutes prior. Then a scream cuts through the tension like a butcher's knife through bone. The worst part is not the scream itself, but the location from which it came: the small community center. The voice isn't of a terrified woman either, it's from a child.

The two of them are quick to arrive to the small rectangular building which currently is missing a door. Luckily there are no bodies outside, but that remains a low concern as they both rush inside without a second thought.

Inside is one open room with seven or eight children curled up in a corner. Each child is covered in colorful pajamas, one even holding a teddy bear in his small arms. Against a nearby wall is presumably the caretaker who now resides in two separate spots on the floor. Finally, in the center of the room is the lengthy, pale form of a cultist. His slender hands covered in dark blood which becomes especially pronounced as Markus raises his AR and flashlight towards the creature. 

Once the beam of lights hits the cultist, he snaps his head back with two obsidian eyes glaring into him. The round vortex of teeth slowly begins to embed itself as a smile across his cryptic face. As Harvey slowly turns around, he reveals a sobbing child in the palm of his hand which he lifts into the air like a human shield.

"Markus..." Harvey growls before releasing with a short chuckle, "It was you, or th-th-them. This is w-w-what you chose."

The child in his clutches covers their eyes as tears stream down their face. From the looks of things, this child is likely the same one who screamed earlier. Their voice must have gone mute.

"Let them go!" Markus demands, "If I'm the one you want, come get me!"

Harvey shakes his head slowly, his simplistic visage casting a ghostly shadow on the wall behind him. Amidst the darkness of the room, Markus can barely see anything but the cultists horrifying face illuminated by his singular flashlight.

"I won't get w-w-what I want from killing you anym-m-more," Harvey admits, lowering his head just slightly, "That opportunity is g-g-gone."

"Then put the kid down!" Markus shouts with as much authority as he can, "I won't ask again."

Harvey raises his head just enough for his void-like eyes to scrutinize Markus' threat, "Or what?"

"Or..." Markus hesitates, "I'll ensure you never get what you want?"

Despite the lack of eyebrows, Harvey seems to narrow his eyes into a disappointed frown, "W-w-we only get what we w-w-want by doing what we h-h-hate. I want to stop killing."

With a scowl of his own, Markus sends a bullet straight through Harvey's arm—only that doesn't happen. Whether it be the soreness in his shoulder, the fact that he's hunched slightly over, or maybe the darkness itself, the bullet misses and only scraps the thin bone. Markus never misses an important shot. What the hell just happened?

Harvey growls and, using his immense strength, closes his fist. Immediately following Markus and Darek are splashed with a crimson mist that temporarily forces them to step out of the way—Markus' flashlight moves with him. 

When he gets the light back on the threat, he doesn't find a pale, white form. Instead, he finds nothing. It's only when that thin silhouette detaches from the ceiling does it appear within his line of sight, but by that point its too late. 

Markus tries to dive out of the way but winds up landing hard on the ground. His chest explodes in pain as he can feel cracks in his back opening again and his rib fracturing slightly. He groans as he tries to get up, but Harvey has already learned his lesson. Slamming his fist down on Markus, he expects to get crushed immediately but instead gets grabbed and chucked out a window. A few bullets pass by him as he soars through the glass and into the street outside.

He struggles to get back to his feet as his shoulder completely gives out forcing him to lift himself with only one arm. The damage is only furthered as he finds significant scraps on his good forearm from the sliding on the concrete. Even a few shards of glass remain inside his shoulder pad, but luckily those don't make it to his skin.

From the vacant front door, Darek appears and sidesteps out of the way just in time to avoid Harvey diving on him. The pale parasite lands a few feet outside the community center and immediately turns towards Darek.

The aged, experienced man doesn't hesitate to pull a sawed off shotgun from his lower back and releasing two rounds into the monster. The first lands true to Harvey's chest, but the second one does the real damage as it completely detaches the parasite's arm. Harvey stumbles as his arm gives out which offers Darek just enough time reload and take the other arm a done leg off. Each shotgun round easily severing the two inches of limp. 

Markus forces his feet underneath him despite his body nearly collapsing three separate time. Mentally subduing the pain, he manages to trudge over to the incapacitated Harvey.

Without a hint of remorse, Markus slams his foot into the side of Harvey's head—exactly like Experiment T did to him, "Answer me. Why do you want to stop killing?"

Harvey shifts agitatedly but is unable to make any meaningful movement as he only possesses a singular leg at the moment. The remaining limbs are already starting to foam over and regenerated, but Markus assumes he might be able to get a few words out before addressing the obvious question.

Markus increases the pressure on Harvey's head, threatening to crack it at any moment, "Are you a cultist or an Experiment?"

Only a small growl forms in the monster's throat as he refuses to bring light to the question. Out of curiosity, Markus drives his foot a bit harder down and can hear the bones beginning to fracture. This impending damage causes Harvey to cry out in a deafening shriek.

Darek covers his ears, "Shut that thing up!"

"If you won't answer me, then tell me what Doctor Crane is!" Markus demands, "Answer me!"

Harvey tries to shake his head and slam his singular foot into the ground but is unable to break free from Markus' restraint, "No! I can't tell you."

The stumps of his limps begin to expand away taking the form of a bicep and thigh respectively. Their time is running thin.

"If you want to stop killing, then tell me how to kill you."

Harvey's ink-stained eyes open a bit wider at the thought before his eyebrows lower back into the same scowl, "I d-d-don't know."

Calculating that he only has a few more seconds, Markus lifts his fit off Harvey's head only to smash it back down with all his might. The bones underneath crack and snap as his shoe breaches through the skull about an inch. Without question, he does it again and slams his foot into the same indent over and over; he only stops when he reaches the concrete on the other side.

With no identifiable head, Markus steps back and recoils from his handiwork. Harvey's body has finally regrown its limbs but now all the regenerative effort has transferred to the neck.

"What the heck is this thing?" Darek inquires as he points the sawed-off shotgun at the reforming head, "I've never seen a parasite regrow like this—nor talk, but I take it that we don't have time for an explanation."

Markus shakes his head, "I have no idea how to kill this thing. It just keeps coming back."

"Have you tried burning it?" Darek thinks aloud as he pulls out a lighter from his pocket.

Instead of answering, Markus simply gestures towards the regenerating carcass. How he lights the thing without some fuel source, he doesn't know, but Darek seems determined to try out his methodology and who is Markus to stop that.

Instead of engaging with the carcass, Darek jogs over to a nearby and promptly finds a backup generator resting beside the building. He promptly unplugs the generator cables, lifts the heavy thing with a swift exhale, and carries it over to the scene. Then, untwisting the cap, he tilts the generator on its side and lets pure gasoline leak out onto Harvey's body. The final touch is to watch the show as Darek strikes his lighter and holds the flame over to the soaked carcass—the fire lights immediately.

Together, they watch in silence as Harvey's body begins to shrivel up into a pathetic, charred skeleton. In the back of his mind, Markus hopes that this be enough, and he can finally cross one issue off his to-do list. That thought is misguided, however, as the flames slowly subside and Harvey's entire body begins to bubble up.

"What a troublesome monster," Darek comments before turning to Markus, "We need to move it somewhere else. We can't keep killing it, eventually we'll run out of tools."

Markus nods finding it strange how the old man can make such rational calls back to back. Heck, Markus is still trying to grapple with the fact that this cultist exists. Perhaps the age gap really does have some validity to it... at least in Darek's case.

Reaching down to pick up the cultist, Markus finds it remarkably light to lift. It's probably due to the fact that most of its body was just burned to ash, but that doesn't change the fact he is able to lift the entire body with ease despite his injuries. 

Speaking of his injures, they suddenly knock on his consciousness' door as his attention is diverted from his suppression technique. He immediately drops the body and grimaces as his hand falls onto his side.

"Are you okay?" Darek asks as he falls down beside the corpse and grabs it himself, "You did take a pretty serious hit back there."

Markus shakes his head, "I'm fine. We need to focus on this thing for right now."

"Sure."

As they carefully slide through the hole in the gate, Markus looks back at the carnage that just unfolded. He has no idea what those children in the community center are thinking. He has no idea whether there are any adults left here. It's feels irresponsible to leave them alone, but Harvey takes priority right now. If they don't dispose of him properly at this very second, this entire fiasco can and will occur again. Whether he likes it or not, he can't help these kids at the moment.

Darek stumbles as he pulls Harvey's carcass through. Then, as the carcass falls out of his hands, it begins to squirm as the brain begins to reform. The danger is quickly averted as the stump of head is blasted away with a buckshot round. 

"We have to think of something quick," Darek comments as he reloads the expended round, "This thing will run me dry before we get anywhere."

Markus looks around at the dark street wondering what their options even are. Harvey heals so quickly that continual damage is only prolonging the inevitable. They can't seem to kill him so the only option they really have is to contain him... exactly like the bunker. 

There's no telling how long Harvey was down in that bunker before Markus came along, but evidently he couldn't escape until Markus conveniently lowered the elevator and opened the maintenance hatch. If it weren't for his intervention, Harvey would likely still be stuck down there, alone and in the dark; away from anyone he could kill. While the bunker may not be an option any more, surely there is a replacement for such a mechanism. The question is where they can stash Harvey—even if temporarily—that he won't be able to regenerate out of. 

"We need to contain him," Markus thinks aloud.

Darek nods as he pulls Harvey's carcass onto his back, and they start walking away idly, "I was thinking something along those lines. If bullets won't put an end to it, then we need to cage it like the animal that it is."

They continue walking as Markus rubs his chin. 

Looking over his shoulder, Darek takes notice of Harvey's exceptionally stringy limbs, "I wonder if we can't find a sturdy chain and tie it up. Even if it regenerates, sure we can restrain it."

Markus shakes his head, "What's stopping him from dislodging an arm and simply regrowing it."

"Him?" Darek wonders before promptly shaking his head, "None the matter. What other options do we have?"

Raking his brain, Markus searches desperately for the forgotten corner of his memory that might give them an actionable solution. They don't really care whether Harvey dies, they simply need to ensure that he can't hurt anyone else.

_Grant shoved Markus' face into the dirt with content expression._

_"Go ahead," he says, "Try and break out."_

_Markus shifts swiftly hoping to slip the man's grasp, even if only a little. The effort fails completely as Grant only pushes him harder into the dirt._

_"You're not getting out," Grant finally admits, "Even if you dislocate your shoulder, I'll still just use my foot's weight to hold you down. The only way out is with a knife but I won't make it easy for you."_

_Markus grimaces as Grant twists his arm a bit further._

_"Tap whenever"._

The empty environment doesn't promise Markus much, but a clever solution does begin to form in the outskirts of his mind. Even if the analogy isn't one for one, he can just add a bit of baggage to the burden. 

"I've got an idea," Markus finally says which draws Darek's undivided attention, "How many bullets do you have left?"

Darek inspects his belt briefly, "Five total."

"Alright," Markus says as he looks around and spots exactly what they need, "We'll have to make this quick. You make sure that thing doesn't come back. I'll explain the rest as I set it up."

Without further explanation, Markus limps over to a large SUV parked in the driveway of a house. He suspects that the car is locked so doesn't even bother with it and fires three well-placed AR rounds into the driver side window before smashing it in with the butt of his gun. This inadvertently causes the car alarm to begin wailing so he promptly pops the hood, locates the car battery, and uses a bit of Nox's skill to cut the alarm's fuel. From there, he pops the truck, pulls out the bottom cushion, and finds a carjack, spare tire, and wrench. 

Darek fires off around round into Harvey's head before sliding the carcass under the car under Markus' order. Then, while Darek stands guard with his shotgun at the read, Markus proceeds to remove the car wheels in a rush order of all rush orders. 

Using the car jack, he's able to get each tire off without much issue. His chest screams in pain as he looses his grip on the wrench and slams into the side of the vehicle. He'd love to agonize over it, but then Darek fires a bullet under the car and Markus elects to persevere. 

Two tires fall completely off with only two left. They both move to the other wide and Darek takes two shots. From a new angle, the first one didn't fully sever the head, so he is forced to use another round—only two rounds left.

The third tire successfully falls off the car while Darek expends the second to last bullet. Markus proceeds to set up the jack for the final wheel with his hands sweating. The final wheel is going about as well as the rest, but then he notices a tenny tiny problem—one of the previous shotgun rounds hit the tire rim and dented it. No matter how he maneuvers the wrench, it won't into the socket to reach the bolt. 

"Shit!" Markus exclaims as he tries one more time to get to the last bolt but fails. 

Darek fires off his last round into the cavity below the car before casting the sawed-off shotgun aside, "That was the last one."

"It's stuck!" Markus shouts as he steps back with sweat dripping down his forehead.

With his hands now free, Darek steps over and inspects the situation. He sees the tire, and then he looks at the very narrow cavity. By this point Harvey is already partially crushed by the underbelly of the car, but there is still enough room for his head to regrow and an arm to reach out and pull some part of him away. 

Perhaps the issue with the bolt should worry him, but Darek doesn't even flinch as he drops to the carjack and lowers it. When the weight of the vehicle is returned to the wheel, he throws the jack aside, stands up, draws his pistol, and fires a round into the rubber of the wheel. Immediately the wheel deflates and the rest of the car's weight lurches downwards and crushes Harvey's head. His skinny arm isn't fully flattened, but the underbelly is low enough that it might as well be considered pinned.

Whether Harvey is dead doesn't really matter. At the very least they've bought themselves some time—some time to breath. This car isn't going anywhere anytime soon.

Markus struggles to get back to the Crafter faction, not because the walk is inherently difficult, but because he wants to prove to Darek and himself that he's alright. The old man, who has arguably done more taxing work, offers him some assistance, but he waves it off and walks on his own. He got hit due to his own negligence, so it's his responsibility to take care of himself. 

When they return, all the faction occupants have come out of hiding. Before the attack, Markus guesses there were around twenty adults and however many children. Now, he counts seven distinct adult figures outside the community center with about fifteen children standing around crying. It doesn't take a genius to know what the adults are thinking when they take a look around—their faces say it all.

He's about to head inside to talk with the faction leaders but Darek cuts him off.

"I'll take care of things here," Darek announces as the craws through the hole in the gate, "I don't have any supplies to assess or treat you, so you'll have to do that yourself. I'll have Dimitri send over a few people to help the Crafters out—God knows they'll need it."

Naturally Markus gets the inclination to stay and help more, but over the course of the last hour, Darek has proven himself to be trustworthy and effective under pressure. In all honesty, he'll probably do a better job talking to the Crafters than Markus would. Groveling a bit under his breath, Markus branches off from the base and heads back to his motorcycle. 

It's going to be a long drive home.

With his freshly cracked rib, the shaking of his bike is excruciating. He'd love nothing more than to walk, but with the moon out, parasites could be anywhere, and he's not outrunning anything in his condition. As such, he manages to top off at a fourth of his normal speed and takes turns so slowly one could question whether or not it can truly be considered a real turn.

About halfway back to his house, his phone goes off. If it were Reign, he'd disregard it entirely, but pulling the motorcycle to a halt does alleviate some of the pain in his chest so he welcomes the relief. The only thing he really considers is where to park the bike and that just so happens to be in the empty parking lot of an elementary school. 

"What's up Jenks," Markus says with the phone to his ear.

"Sorry if I woke you," he whispers through the microphone.

"It's fine," Markus responds, "I was already up."

Jenks voice comes back quickly and even more hushed, "Perfect. Come to the Eagle faction base right now."

"Why?" Markus asks incredulously.

"I'm walking the scene from afar... Experiment T is here."

Markus goes silent.

"He just wiped out the entire faction."
