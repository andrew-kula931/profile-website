The following day is quite cloudy, yet no rain arrives to Markus' appreciation as he carefully walks through an abandoned apartment building. The first through fifth floors were perfectly clean and also thoroughly looted. The sixth floor, however, presents an interesting case as he is hit by a pungent odor of rot as he steps through the stairwell doors. The typically light brown hallway is considerably darker as dried blood paints the walls like abstract art. Small rotting chunks of flesh occupy the corners of the walls like cobwebs in an addict.

Markus quickly pulls his gas mask on to avoid the vile stench and any potential contaminants in the toxic environment. He unclips his pistol at his hip and adjusts the AR on his back while simultaneously drawing a long combat knife from the side of his left leg. His sword is great and all, but the apartment hallways presents an enclosed environment which is not conducive to wide reaching weapons. A small, compact slashing weapon is suited much better for this situation.

He carefully creeps up to a door resting ajar with his knife in hand.

_Jenks slid a gadget back into his pocket and nodded to Markus indicating that the room is clear of any remotely operated devices._

_On the silent count of three, Jenks kicked the door open to the apartment building room while Markus rushed in. He scanned the room with expert level efficiency, looking for any hostiles and thankfully came up empty-handed._

_"Clear," Markus acknowledged as Jenks followed in just behind._

_The man then kneeled down next to the couch of the living room and opens a brief case. Through his shaggy black hair, a few beads of sweat dripped onto the keys of the device which causes a shallow curse as he brushes his bangs back._

_"This place will have to do," he admitted as he started typing away at the computer hidden within the case._

_Markus closed the door behind them as he checked on the hallway outside. Once the door closed, he let out a deep sigh._

_"You were holding it in the whole time?" Jenks laughed, "Active combat stopped twenty minutes ago."_

_Markus eyed the man. Jenks may have been the youngest member of the squad, but he was still technically an adult which puts him a few years older than Markus. Even so, the guy acted way too nonchalant given how precarious their situation was. Only Grant could pull off that kind of routine and get away with it. _

_"I was just alert," Markus defended._

_"I hear you," Jenks responded as his eyes remain on the dim screen before him, "I just pretend I have asthma sometimes which forces me to breathe."_

_"I wasn't holding my breath…" Markus muttered, "Wait, does that actually work?"_

_Jenks twirled his finger in the air before landing it on the enter key. The black terminal then begins running a long list of commands as he steps away and takes a seat on the couch, "What? No… no I just made that up. I just saw that you were working overtime on monitoring our surroundings so I prioritized running through a few algorithms in my head. It paid off too! My program here should retrieve the data we need in a few minutes… gosh I love fuzzying insecure inputs."_

_"What?" Markus asked as he leaned against the door to listen for any movement on the other side._

_"Don't worry about it," Jenks concluded as he drew his pistol and peered out the crack in the curtains, "You're really good at room clearing, and I'm good at clearing test suites: we just stick to our guns."_

_Markus studied the man for a moment, "Has anyone ever called you a nerd?"_

_Jenks smiled, "Thank you."_

_"It was a complement too," Markus added, "Glad to have you around."_

_Jenks placed a hand over his heart, "That's so kind, I'm not used to getting praise from teenagers."_

_Markus sighed, "Forget I said anything."_

Markus blinks and finds the apartment room empty with an army of flies hovering over a small pile of rotten flesh. He takes a deep breath with his mask and transitions over to the kitchen to search for any leftover food.

He's in the midst of placing two cans of mushroom soup into his backpack as he hears something fall from another room. His knife is held at the ready before the distant echoes stop. His bag is on his back, and he's out in the hallway shortly after.

The epicenter of the commotion seemed to come from a room about two doors down, but he couldn't be sure as all of the doors were ajar with blood leading in and out of each. His primary concern isn't that the parasite hiding here is dangerous, rather he's concerned that the moment he finds one, there will be another right behind him. Because of this, he checks backwards in his limited vision countless times as he steps over a shriveled up intestine and arrives at the door he suspects the movement came from.

One would likely expect a solo scavenger to be extra careful when opening a door to an unknown enemy, but Markus finds that line of thought dangerous as it gives the parasite more time to react. No door opens silently so he opts to swing it open abruptly and check the other side. 

What lies on the other side of the door is no parasite, no living one rather. A collection of parasitic body parts lay scattered across the floor as if surgically removed and thrown about haphazardly. The former body of the slender class parasite is impossible to decipher as he finds no less than ten unique sections of the fresh carcass.

Markus checks the rest of the room in search of what killed the parasite but finds nothing. Instead, he steps out of the room and rechecks the hallway.

His heart skips a beat.

Standing at the end of the hallway is a mysterious figure draped in a dark blue hoodie with the hood perfectly concealing their face. The man's hands rest in his pockets as he stands so still that a statue would dare move before him. Markus' blood runs cold as he notices two bloody footprints leading away from his current room and heading straight to where the man currently stands. The hallway darkens as Markus' vision instinctively narrows. His grip on his knife grows so intense that his fingertips begin to go numb. All the while, the mysterious figure just watches him in absolute silence.

How long has he been there? Why didn't he hear him? Not even professional assassins can be that quiet–that effective. He struggled to kill two slender class parasites with one slash each. So the scene from this room… How?

Markus debates drawing his gun to prepare for a physical confrontation. If his movements hint at hostility, he'll gun him down where he stands. If he tries to negotiate, he'll remain wary, but that's the problem: the enigma doesn't react. Markus forces himself to breathe after the long game becomes too oppressive to bear and this acts as a strong enough catalyst to enact motion in the enigmatic statue. 

The man turns and walks through the stairwell door as if asserting Markus isn't worth his time. It's an aura that can only be achieved by someone who's abandoned the need to prove themselves. Markus listens but doesn't hear any steps going up or down the steps as if the man vanished like a ghost; the second line of sight was broken. 

Markus takes a deep breath and rechecks his surroundings once more. The bland walls haven't shifted from their unimpressive states as he blinks through a bit of glare on this gas mask. 

That's when a door from the opposite side of the hallway bursts open and three Infecteds flood out. Markus takes the initiative and draws a throwing knife from his bag and readies it. 

The Infecteds, having trampled over each other and collapsed on the floor, regain their footing and spot Markus. Their subsequent stampede towards him is swift but stunned as one of them falls with a five inch chunk of steel protruding out of its cranium. The other two push past the fallen parasite and continue rushing towards Markus with zero consideration for anything else. 

Part of him hopes that the strange man has been waiting in the stairwell the entire time and will jump out to clean up the last two Infecteds, but that's wishful thinking. Instead, the first parasite reaches within melee range of Markus and attempts to bite down with an elongated mouth. Markus sidesteps and slices his combat knife right across the side of the Infected's neck. He then proceeds to throw a powerful sidekick right into the abdomen of the following parasite. His foot makes contact with the rotting skin of the Infected and completely halts its momentum. Then, to accentuate the blow, he slams the point of his knife right into the eye of the parasite before kicking it away with his conveniently placed foot. The first parasite groans as blood guzzles out of its neck like a cracked hose before finally throwing in the towel. 

Markus waits for three seconds before continuing—taking extra care to ensure that no other parasites will follow and these three are truly dead. When it becomes clear that this floor is officially clear, he rummages through the corpses to find one bloody identification. He pockets it hoping that Misty will find it worthwhile before moving back to the room with the slaughtered slender class parasite.

He inspects the handiwork more closely of the mystery man by analyzing the scene like an authentic detective. 

Off of pure speculation, he concludes that the Slender wasn't far from a humanoid figure as all of the butchered body parts closely resemble that of a human's though quite elongated and thin. From the locations of each part he surmises that the entire confrontation was near instantaneous—none of the dismembered limbs had made it from the center mass. Two arms lay on either side of the head with two split legs resting perfectly symmetrically from one another. 

Each slice through the parasite is so clean he honestly doesn't believe it was done by a real human. Flesh, muscle, and bones all have completely smooth edges along each slash mark. His level of precision is difficult for anyone to accomplish, much less on the fly and so quickly.

He steps out of the room and inspects the shoe prints engraved in blood along the carpet of the hallway. The marks closely resemble that of a tennis shoe indicating that the man is wearing something light and mobile. 

Whoever that man was, he's dangerous and nothing like anything he's ever seen before.

He clears the rest of the building without any issue and loots a few more apartment rooms. The seventh through twelfth floors were mostly picked clean but the sixth floor—with all the rotting corpses and blood—manages to provide a decent haul for him. His backpack offers a significant burden on his back as he returns to his motorcycle parked outside.

As he arrive there he stops in his tracks to find the hooded figure crouching over his motorcycle.

Markus raises his AR and prepares to shoot, "Hey! Who are you?"

The mysterious man doesn't look back, nor even seem to pay attention to him.

"Hey!" he shouts one more time.

This time the hooded figure stands and slides his hands back into the hoodie pockets. While in transit, Markus notices dark bruise marks on the man's hands with fresh cut on one of them. These injuries don't seem to bother the guy as he turns his back towards Markus and begins to walk off.

"Hold on!" Markus demands as he keeps his gun aimed and begins to advance closer to the guy, "What were you doing to my bike?"

The man doesn't even bother to glance back, instead he continues on towards who knows where. Markus genuinely considers firing a warning shot, but decides that it's not worth the bullet. If the man sabotaged his bike, then maybe there will be a fight after all, but unless that's the case, there's not point in escalating.

Just to be sure, Markus rushes over this vehicle and inspects it. The parts are all in the same state as they were before and no additional contraptions seem to have been added. It's almost as if the man was just casually inspecting his bike. The idea of something so mundane feels more far-fetched than if he had been trying to damage the motorcycle.

When Markus finally checks back in on the man, he finds nothing but an empty road. In the three or four seconds he looked away, somehow the man disappeared into one of the nearby alley ways.

The cunning spy.

At that moment his phone beeps with a message. The "No Caller ID" makes Markus sigh as he reads: "I realize it's sudden but the board is meeting this afternoon at 4." 

Meetings aren't particularly interesting to Markus, but he deems that it might be worthy to eavesdrop a little. After all, he might be able to gain a bit of information on the rising famine crisis and maybe even something about Harvey. He just hopes Cleo doesn't spot him right away and wrap him into the city's drama. Either way, it'll likely be to his benefit to go.

The meeting is held in the usual spot: the Laurson Arena. It's an old basketball stadium in the center of the city which makes it convenient for all factions to get to. Nowadays it's a sort of meeting place for all of the faction representatives. The place is not ordinarily guarded but when the meeting is called, every faction gets to bring four guards and their leader. Some people might call it a place of tension, but it serves its purpose and has proven to prevent wars in the past.

At the main doors, Markus is greeted by a group of men with large weapons and even larger egos.

"Sorry, but this meeting is closed to the public," one of the men in front says, motioning his gun for Markus to beat it.

Markus, a little surprised that his reputation hasn't preceded him, nods his head, "Since when were meetings closed to the public?"

The man gives him a disapproving look before checking a clipboard, "This particular meeting has sensitive discussions. I'm not really sure myself, so I'm just following protocol."

Someone behind him suddenly calls out, "Wait a minute I've seen him before."

The first man finally looks back up from the clipboard and asks, "What's your name?"

"Markus," he responds like it should have been obvious. He doesn't particularly like leveraging his reputation but in this case he genuinely has no official right to be at this meeting. He notes to himself to tell Reign to get him official clearance next time he has to do this sort of thing.

"He can't be the Markus, right?" he says sarcastically back to his group behind him. 

"Maybe not," Markus responds very politely, "Either way, 'll go through this door or find another one."

The men exchange glances before the first finally speaks, "I don't get paid enough for this. Either he's the real deal or he's an imposter. Anyone with enough gall to impersonate Markus doesn't have long anyway."

The second laughs and turns to Markus, "That's fair. You've convince me, but you'll have to convince the guards inside too. Head on it."

Markus nods as he passes through the small group of armed soldiers and climbs the stairs to the stadium entrance.

Finally inside, Markus finds a single large hallway wrapping around the perimeter of the stadium. A large entryway sits in the middle opening up to the arena itself. Despite what the guards outside said, he doesn't find anyone waiting for him in the entryway which allows him to advance further in without issue. 

Inside the arena, bleacher seats cover all floor space except the court itself which currently has a large circular table with chairs placed around it. Occupying the seats are members of different factions from all across the city. Cleo at one end of the table, Elizabeth Reign across from her, and the rest of the leaders filling in the gaps. The headmaster of the meeting, Judge Darius as people called him, sits in a slightly larger chair overlooking the meeting. Darius isn't associated with any particular faction but as a certified judge from before the Fall he is best suited to mediate these meetings.

Markus takes a seat in the back row of stands and kicks his feet onto the seat in front of him. If he had popcorn, he'd probably pull it out right about now. The drama typically doesn't get that intense, but he still finds them drawn out and slow paced.

"I understand your concern Mr. Logan, but that doesn't change the situation at hand," Darius explains, slightly frustrated. "We still have to find a way to grow more food first before we worry about future decisions." 

"I propose we ration food based on population, it would prevent some groups from monopolizing food," Elizabeth announces. 

"You only say that because your people have the highest population, it's practically giving our food to you," a man from Scavenger faction sternly exclaims.

"On the contrary," Elizabeth promptly counters, "the Radio Tower has a cache of more than enough food for all of us, I'm proposing we give some of that food to smaller communities, as long as the rest of you participate."

Markus has to agree with Reign, his daughter is a force to be reckoned with when it comes to debates. Her quick wittedness and ability to prepare puts her far above most other people when they try to fight with words. Almost half of all of General Reign's land came from Elizabeth making deals and agreements to secure land peacefully. In fact, The Radio Tower had only ever "gone to war" with one faction ever and that war didn't last very long.

"That's a notable idea, but are there any other ways we could go about this?" Darius asks.

"Ms. Reign's idea is a good short term strategy but what happens after food runs out?" Cleo points out.

"Agreed," a few voices all mumble.

"I'm glad you asked that," Elizabeth starts, "the truthful answer is… I don't know, we still have yet to figure out the properties of this new fungus, but rest assured I personally have a group researching the issue at this very moment."

"That's supposed to make us feel better?" One guy shouts out.

"I wasn't done," Elizabeth counters, "We just have to work together to buy a little more time and then once we know more, we can make an accurate decision. The last thing we need now is to make a hasty decision that leads to more problems. No one is starving right now, right?" Elisabeth says rhetorically as she waits for a few heads to shake, "So we don't have to do anything rash."

"So you're saying we should just do nothing?!" A guy says standing up. It was Brian Stewer from the Raven faction. 

The Raven faction is a group of mercenaries who do all the hired work for practically every other faction. If a group couldn't defend themselves from a larger group who had a grudge, they turned to the Ravens.

"That's not at all what—" Elizabeth tries.

"Then what then, my people are about to find out that there won't be food at the table in two weeks; they'll turn to me and then what. What do I say to a bunch of hungry people? I can't do that, I need some kind of action—some kind of assurance," Brian argues.

"So what are you proposing then?" Darius urges.

"I say we make the Retainers give up their food stockpile so everyone can be even. Only then would anyone listen to their terms so easily," Brian explains with a demanding tone.

Elizabeth waves her hands to the sides, completely baffled by the demand, "That was the plan, as long as everyone else joined along with us."

"I also agree, the Retainers are too powerful to demand others to give up food, we have to see them take the first step," another member adds.

"I agree," another joins.

"Me too," yet another follows suit.

"This is starting to sound like everyone wants to see the Retainers fall," Cleo comments, growing subtly irritated, "It doesn't sound very productive."

"If anything, it's the Retainers' fault, they were the first ones to find this fungus, they should have quarantined those plants and then none of this would have happened," Brian accuses. 

"Order!" Darius yells.

"You're right, this is all the Retainers' fault," someone yells.

"They must pay," another adds.

"ORDER!" Darius shouts, slamming his fist on the table, "We must stay civilized or nothing will get done."

Everyone in the room goes silent and takes a seat if necessary. 

The room is commonly split in situations like these, Darius never ceases to impress Markus with his patients and control. An ambassador of a powerful faction, commonly Elizabeth, on one side, everyone else on the other, and Cleo always playing the neutral route. The Retainers are the strongest powerhouse in the city and no one hides from that, but most still are jealous of that fact. 

Another reason Markus runs missions for Cleo is her neutrality, no one typically thinks he's picking sides if he does that. Though he has to be careful whenever Reign calls him or else all of those efforts would be meaningless.

As for what Markus himself thinks, well, it's complicated. He'd be lying if he said he didn't mostly agree with Elizabeth's perspective, but at the same time, Brian's hesitant towards him has some validity. After all, the solitary war the broke out among the city was in large part to the Retainers.

"So, what is our consensus in regards to our current usage of food?" Darius finally asks after a moment of silence and rubbing his own brow in exasperation.

"I think we should all share food, after all my group is running low with no way to get more and I'm sure there are many others in the same position," Lauren, the leader of the Nurse faction, finally voices.

There is no reply but quite a few heads nod. The meeting is finally about to make a productive turn, but then Cleo glances around the stadium and spots Markus seated at the very top.

"Markus!" Her eyes light up at the sight of him, but her professionality keeps her from doing anymore. This comment is closely followed by an entire room of eyes darting to him.

"Oh, what a pleasant surprise. Who knew our city's top mercenary would honor us with a visit," Darius quickly comments.

Someone else in the group promptly chuckles at the comment saying, "You're lucky Mr. Dorsekki isn't here to hear you say that."

"Good grief," Markus whispers to himself before raising his voice, "I'm just a spectator."

Darius gestures towards the bottom row of stands, "Please, if you're here, why don't you come down and join us."

Markus shakes his head, "I'm not a faction leader, I fear my opinion would be to biased."

That darn silver tongue never ceases to amaze Markus as Darius chuckles pleasantly to himself, "I'm not asking for your opinion, I'm asking for your expertise." 

The room remains completely frozen as Markus contemplates the notion for a moment. Then, begrudgingly, he makes his way down the impressive flight of stairs to the table. There isn't a seat for him so he stands at the base of the stairs leaning against a hand railing. Everyone's eyes hold their gaze oppressively on him, not long ago Markus would be dripping in sweat at this scene, but now he doesn't care enough to get worked up about it.

"So I take it you know the dilemma," Darius breaks the thick silence with. 

Markus nods as he notices a guard disappear in the corner of his eyes. Continuing to use his peripheral vision, he notices a few more guards all following suit and exit the perimeter of the arena to go somewhere else. He takes note of this as he proceeds with the unintentional meeting presence. 

"What are your thoughts on the situation… uh, Markus?" Elizabeth asks. Despite her mostly successful attempt to remain formal and respectful, she, along with everyone else here, doesn't know his last name. They all silently conclude that Markus will have to suffice despite the context.

"This crisis will affect us all," Markus explains quite obviously, "I'll leave crops to you guys, but there are a few caches left in the city we could exploit."

"So you're saying there are caches of food left in the city?" Parker Collins from the Rogues faction insists on getting interested. The Rogues are also the same faction that Markus purchases his gasoline from so he feels slightly more obligated to respond.

"Absolutely, there's plenty, but most of them are in the middle of hives. We all understand the inherent danger of trying to burn out a parasite hive, but it might be a necessary risk if we are going to find enough food to last us through the incoming famine. I, for one, am currently hunting down the largest cache of them all," Markus knows those words are loaded but proceeds so say them nonchalantly. He gives a little pause as everyone gets to the edge of their seats. He waits one more moment as the footsteps running to the door get louder and louder. "The mayor's bunker."

The room bursts out in an uproar. People talk to each other or even yell in complete shock. Darius yells 'Order' over and over again, but the talking persists. It is at that moment that a guard from the HoV faction bursts through the door and announces, "We've got a Colossal outside! All of you need to evacuate!"

Finally something more his pace.
