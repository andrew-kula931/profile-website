Although it takes a toll, Markus picks up speed to reach the Eagle faction—the same one he infiltrated just a few days prior. He finds Jenks at a nearby vantage point, and they make their way towards the base. They wind up lurking into the first floor of a nearby building overlooking the entrance of the Eagle faction base.

The gate was once sturdy and electrified, but now it lies open with a single man standing in front of the factory. A few lights remain left on inside the factory, but the entire environment is quiet—too quiet. A location that once harbored over a hundred people is now completely void of any ambient murmurs.

He and Jenks exchanges glances briefly as they remain silent as they hear the Experiment T speak.

"No," he says despite there being no one around him, "It's not like that."

Ryan goes silent for while as he slowly turns his head as if tracking a phantom in the forefront of his vision.

"Shut up," he grumbles, "I did all that I could."

Suddenly his head snaps to the side as if another unexpected phantom had entered the fray. He narrows his eyes but simply listens to the wind blow by.

"I told you that I tried!" Ryan exclaims spontaneously, "I tried so many times. What in the hell was I supposed to do?!"

Silence. Ryan looks around for a moment.

"What exactly happened?" Markus whispers over to Jenks, trying to as quiet as physically possible.

Jenks, ensuring that he's out of Ryan's line of sight, whispers back, "I'm not sure. I heard screams in there, and they all went silent a few minutes ago. Ryan is the only one who came out, and he's been standing there since."

Markus looks back to the empty scene to find Ryan's fists clenched as he scowls at the nothing.

"I haven't forgotten," he says slowly, "Not for a second."

Intrigued, yet hesitant, Markus latches onto his AR and gives Jenks a nod. His friend, however, doesn't share in the intention of moving in. Instead, Jenks disputes the notion and holds out his hand to prevent Markus from going.

Suddenly another figure walks into the scene. A well-dressed man covered in dark robes, leather gloves, a black bolero hat, and a mask steps through the open gate. The man's mask is hard to make out in the dark, but it clearly resembles that of a crow or a raven. Whatever hides underneath that mask isn't even hinted at through the eyeholes.

"It's nice to see you again Ryan," the man states in a very formal, composed tone.

Ryan doesn't snap like he did with the phantoms, instead he turns around slowly. Any emotion that may have been displayed during his little scene has completely vanished only to be replaced with the familiar, cold demeanor he uses so often.

"Vincenzo."

The masked man, or Vincenzo allegedly, folds his hands behind his back, "I'm so glad you remembered my name. I knew disclosing that to you was the correct decision."

Ryan lets his arms hang down by his sides as he remains a still as a statue, "Why are you here now?"

Vincenzo shrugs his shoulders, "Is it so wrong to check up on an old friend?"

"Don't call us friends," Ryan corrects swiftly.

The other man cocks his head to side briefly as if amused by the comment. He then paces around to find a parked car to sit on. 

"I'm sure it'll come as no surprise," Vincenzo begins, "but I have not stopped thinking about you these last three years. You are, after all, very important."

Ryan remains still and silent—his eyes glaring coldly into the man's mask.

Vincenzo continues with a small nod of his head, "You're likely looking for direction right about now. Natural this is just a suggestion and is offered completely at your digression—your choice. I simply believe that doing this will not just resolve problems for you, but also for everyone in this city."

The conversation halts allowing Ryan some time to answer or even rebuttal, but the stoic man doesn't take it. The discussion resumes only after ten seconds of silence.

"I see how it is," Vincenzo comments to himself, "Very well then, I believe it would be within your best interests to hunt down a particular individual in this city. You're likely thinking of a particular doctor, I can't blame you given what you found in there, but he is not the target that is most detrimental to this city."

Ryan's gaze moves away from Vincenzo and stares off in the distance. Even when Markus traces the line of sight, he doesn't find anything there but empty street and forlorn city. The man then sighs and says, "I see."

"If you understand," Vincenzo initiates, "I'll be leavin—"

Markus pushes through both the pain of moving and Jenks' grasp to storm through the open gate into the scene. All eyes turn to him as Jenks begrudgingly follows behind.

"I want to know too," Markus demands. He doesn't wave his gun around just yet but the thought does cross his mind multiple times.

Vincenzo takes note of the two new faces and promptly slides off the vehicle he was resting on, "Ah, I'm always open to visitors."

"That doctor you were referring to, it was Doctor Crane, right?" Markus asks, specifically singling out the black clothed man.

With a humored chuckle, Vincenzo nods his head, "That would be correct. I'm sure you would recognize his handiwork if you stepped inside this facility."

Markus' gaze falls on the Eagle factory.

"Don't," Ryan asserts swiftly without making eye contact, "Don't go in there."

"Wait," Markus says doing a double take, specifically looking back at Jenks, "I thought you said it was Experiment T's doing?"

Jenks holds out his hands defensively, "I didn't see everything, but I'm certain that Ryan killed the Eagle faction."

"Oh, Ryan most certainly killed the majority of the faction," Vincenzo answers before turning to Ryan himself, "Do you deny it?"

Despite the accusations, Ryan maintains a cold, empty expression. He neither shakes his head nor speaks to defend himself—he simply takes it silently.

Markus inspects the surroundings briefly to ensure he has an escape route and thankfully finds ample of them as there is literally no one else here. The wall provides cover in case Ryan opens fire and Jenks has his back in a similar situation. This Vincenzo guy's abilities are still a mystery, but he doesn't seem hostile at first glance, in fact, he actually feels familiar for some reason.

"Okay then," Markus prompts, "Who is the person we should be looking for. Are they a cultist? Another Experiment?"

Vincenzo laughs at the comment as he looks at the ground in a laughing manner. When he raises his head, takes a deep breath to collect himself, "I commend your motivation to search for members of the Organization. I'm sure you will be pleased to know that there remains but a singular affiliate remaining in the city. Mind you, I am excluding Experiments as you do not possess the ability to dictate their actions. This affiliate, however, could be eliminated as swiftly as you draw your weapon."

"Then who?" Markus presses, his words cutting short as his lungs poke his ribs the wrong way.

Ryan glares down Vincenzo momentarily before moving to Jenks, then Markus, finally to an indeterminate location beyond the group. 

Vincenzo shakes his head, "I don't need to say. If you want to know, you can always ask him," he gestures towards Ryan, "He knows."

Markus takes a calculated step forward as he singles Ryan out with a finger, "Tell us who we need to track down. The city is at stake here."

Having been watching the scene unfold silently for a while now, Ryan closes his eyes and averts his gaze as if conflicted by the question. When he returns to the conversation his eyes land on the two Allegiance One members.

"Drew Wilbur told me something a long time ago," Ryan begins capturing the two mens' undivided attention, "He said that when you meet a cultist, only two things can happen. Either you kill them... or you become one. Which one are you?"

Taking a step back, Markus shakes his head instinctively. Surely he's not insinuating that he's a cultist—he's spent the last three years hunting down these bastards, why in the world would he join them. That idea alone is so absurd that he does a double take. 

"So you really knew Wilbur," Jenks poses.

Ryan looks off into the distance, likely to avoid eye contact once more, "Yeah. I did."

Jenks glances at Markus briefly, "I heard that he died three years ago. How? Wilbur isn't the kind of guy to go down easy."

"He..." Ryan stammers as the words struggle to get out. Eventually he sighs, "It's my fault. He died because of me."

An intense emotion, Markus can only describe as anger, builds up in Jenks' eyes as he clenches his fists and takes a few steps closer towards Ryan, "So it's you. You're responsible for letting him die."

Ryan refuses to look the man in the eyes, "Yeah."

Jenks runs a hand through his hair, "A dozen guards, sure, a hundred innocent people, it happens, but Wilbur?" his lip begins to quiver, "Why would you do that?"

In the background, Vincenzo strokes the chin of his mask with amusement as he mutters, "Interesting."

As if all reason has left his body, Jenks storms over to Ryan and grabs him by the shirt, "How many people have you killed? Huh?! How can you even say that with a straight face."

Suddenly Ryan's eyes snap open as if some grand realization had occurred. He studies Jenks' fuming face for a brief moment before some unexplainable emotion washes over him, and he averts his eyes once more. Without resisting in any way, he allows Jenks to shake him once before shoving in backwards.

Maybe that could be the end of the confrontation, but there's still one more thing left in store for Ryan. Jenks unholsters a pistol and holds it a fist length away from Ryan's forehead.

"We tried to kill you before, but you evaded every single thing we threw at you," Jenks explains, "Why don't you try and dodge this."

"Jenks..." Markus tries, not fully on board with how the situation is unfolding.

Suddenly the sound a gun shot rings out across the landscape with Jenks' hand raising up slightly due to the recoil. What stands on the other end of the barrier is the same Experiment T though with his head cocked to the side—the bullet having grazed his earlobe. Jenks follows it up by adjusting his aim firing off another round. Ryan snaps his out of the way at the exact same moment that the trigger is pulled.

The strangest part is not that the bullets are dodged, no. To Markus, it's the idea that Ryan isn't presenting any superhuman abilities. He's not regenerating from any potential damage, he's not moving at an inhuman speed, he's not manipulating anything. Every time Jenks pulls the trigger he simply moves out of the way at the perfect time, as if the entire scene were choreographed. With each dodged bullet, Ryan's face expresses an unimpressed notion as if insulting the bullet itself for being so slow.

Five bullets escape the barrel and five bullets land in the cement foundation of the factory without carrying anything with it. Ryan remains completely unharmed as Jenks takes a step back. In an ordinary confrontation, Ryan would have retaliated—at least Markus would have—but this enigma of a man simply stares at Jenks with a cold, dead expression. He doesn't even seem surprised as the would-be assassin backs off. 

"Are you satisfied," Vincenzo asks as Jenks starts pacing away with his hands in his pockets.

"Yeah, whatever," he calls out dismissively, "Markus, I'm trusting the rest to you."

They all watch as Jenks disappears around the corner of the wall, but Markus knows he's not going far. He's willing to stake his life on the fact that Jenks is simply repositioning to another location—to get an off-angle if you will.

"Now then," Vincenzo concludes as he glances between those remaining, "With him out of the way, I can finally disclose precisely what I came here to say."

Markus waits patiently to hear what this strangely knowledge man has to say. Just a few moments ago Vincenzo was about to leave, but now he seems to have other priorities. Somehow Markus must have complicated things—or expedited them.

Vincenzo steps back over to the car he used as a bench and reuses it. When he looks back at the two men before him, he does so with a raised chin, "You two make a very interesting bunch, don't you think? Both shaped irreversibly by the Organization and have developed a sort of grudge because of it. One has power while the other knows how to use it. Originally, I only anticipated one of you to be alive by this point but... plans change."

"Change how?" Markus prompts, trying to break down any filibustering that may be occurring.

"Right," Vincenzo gives with an open gesture of his hand, "I intend for both of you to head to this country's capital after the situation here has been resolved."

"No," Ryan asserts directly, "Not with him."

The masked man glances at him, seemingly unsurprised, "I figured you'd be hesitant."

Markus inspects both of them but can't figure out what could have caused such a direct refusal. A few moments prior, Ryan seemed like he would listen without conflict, but now he's flipped his position. Could the perception have changed simply because Markus is involved... also, why is Markus involved in the first place; all he wants is answers.

"Why should I even consider doing that?" Markus inquires.

Vincenzo doesn't stall, "To defeat Volker of course. Save the world in way."

"Volker..." Markus echoes, "Are you saying that Volker—that thing—is in the capital."

"No," Vincenzo corrects, "At least not in the way you're expecting. What you will find in the capital is simply a means of defeating Volker. That is why you must go—to get revenge on the Cult, no?"

Markus takes a step back and looks away. He's not trying to be defensive, but when his intentions are put so plainly out there, it makes him wonder if he's being conspicuous about it. Wait a minute. It doesn't matter if people know, he's going to get back at the Cult no matter what. If this masked outsider is willing to aid him in that mission, well, a lead is a lead.

"Maybe you could start by asking so of my questions, and then you'll offer some assurances," Markus demands, "I'm not in the business of following stranger's directions without reason."

Vincenzo chuckles as he glances at Ryan, "I guess the flare was an exception, but I digress. Please, ask away."

The backhanded comment gives Markus pause, but he shakes it off, checks to see if he spots Jenks on any nearby buildings, and prepares his questions. He doesn't actually find Jenks, but his trust still remains that he'll get supportive fire if these questions come out more insulting than he intends.

Markus clears his throat and holds out a single finger, "First. Volker appeared to me twice recently and told me to die. I haven't seem it since. Likewise, I was also invisible to parasites for a time. Do you know anything about these events?"

"It's because—" Ryan starts before cutting himself off.

Vincenzo waits to see if Ryan will volunteer any more information, but when none comes, he elects to tackle the question himself, "That's rather simple, and I'm likely finishing Ryan's sentence here: Volker appeared because events were aligning towards an Experiment Genesis, that is, an Experiment was about to be born."

"But that didn't happen," Markus guess, "Experiment A was stripped from me."

"That is correct," Vincenzo agrees, "Instead of an Experiment being born, it was simply being extracted and returned to the custody of the Organization. I wouldn't expect to encounter Volker itself anytime soon. Volker's priorities are likely... elsewhere."

"How do you know that," Markus interjects fiercely, "Both about Volker and about how Experiment A was stripped from me. I never told anyone about that."

Well, he did tell Jenks, but there's no way that this Vincenzo character ever had a discourse with him. There's some underlying info network going on and Markus needs to establish whether or not Vincenzo and Doctor Crane are on the same side. That's the crux of his questioning after all.

"My info network is vast," Vincenzo answers simply, reading Markus' thoughts exactly, "Besides, I already knew you possessed Experiment A. I was the one who let you out of the compound after all."

Markus' breath leaves him as the memories come flooding back. That masked man from ten years ago—the one who helped him escape the cult—that's him. Does that mean he owes this man his life? Does that mean this man is heavily involved with the Cult? Does he trust him or not?

Shaking the confusion off, Markus returns to his focused composure, "I hope you realize that I'm only more skeptical now."

Vincenzo leans forward and rests his forearms on his knees, "And risk you finding out that I lied to you. I value choice and honestly above all else—you are no exception. Whether you trust me and whether you heed my advice are two entirely separate questions. Ask Ryan, he should know better than anyone."

Markus shifts uncomfortably, his aching joints, sore muscles, and agonizing rib makes him crave an opportunity to end the conversation early, but the wealth of information here cannot be understated. He needs to learn more before it's too late.

"Okay..." he trails off, "If your information network is so vast, what am I supposed to do about the famine?"

"Track down the one who started it," Vincenzo responds immediately, "I am willing to wager that the one who started it, has a cure for it."

"And who is that?"

Vincenzo turns to Ryan, "He knows."

All eyes turn towards the black jacket wearing, intention withholding, ill spoken, self-proclaimed Experiment. Markus doesn't mince his words on this one, "Tell who it is. People are dying."

Averting his eyes like usual, Ryan takes on the demeanor of shy high school student for a split second. It's likely just Markus' limited exposure to genuinely shy people, but he hasn't seen someone so unwilling to speak since that part of his own life... maybe that's projecting though... regardless, the man doesn't formulate a response very quickly.

"It's... better if you don't know just yet," Ryan eventually answers with.

Markus immediately shakes his head, "Don't give me that. If we have the chance to stop this person—or thing—we ought to do it now. As I said, people are dying every single day. We have to do something."

Ryan shakes his head, "No, we can't do anything yet."

Feeling a bit resentful and incredulous, Markus narrows his eyes, "Yet you went ahead and cleared out an entire faction because there's time for _that_. Maybe I was naive to think you're even on my side." 

Looking up slowly, Ryan's eyes return to the dead, apathetic looks he usually holds, "Maybe... but—"

"Cut the shit," Markus interjects before turning to Vincenzo, "If you know, please tell me. I just want to save as many lives as I can."

"While eliminating the Organization," Vincenzo adds.

Brushing the comment off with a shrug, Markus nods his head, "Yeah... I guess."

"Very well," Vincenzo decides as he takes a few steps towards the entrance of the former faction base, "You will gain the most success by investigating the source of the fungus."

Markus crosses his arms with mild frustration boiling into his tone, "Yeah, you already said that. That doesn't give me much to go off of."

Vincenzo touches the forehead of his mask briefly as if dissatisfied with the response, "Markus, you have experience in investigation. Go back to the first person who informed you about the fungus and backtrack from there. I shouldn't have to explain that."

"Or just tell me who the person is," Markus argues, "Sure I can find him, but time is of the essence right now."

The masked man shakes his head and takes a few more steps away, "If I give you the answers, you'll never learn. I value your choice more than your actions. Furthermore, what would you gain from a name if you don't recognize it."

Markus opens his mouth.

"And no one else does either," Vincenzo answers quickly, "Now. Run along. I've determined that resolving this famine will convict you of my efforts. You will agree to head to the capital in due time; there's no need to rush."

Following this statement, Vincenzo departs around the corner of the gate. Markus doesn't feel quite satisfied with the conversation, so he presses forward while groaning to reach the wall before the man completely vanishes. To his dismay, however, Vincenzo is nowhere to found on the other side. Whether he actually disappeared or simply found a hiding place Markus doesn't see doesn't matter—Vincenzo is gone for the moment he doesn't seem like the type of person to be found easily.

Damn, he still had so many questions.

Jenks is unfortunately not nearby, so Markus makes the difficult decision to return into the compound. Still in his former spot in the center of the courtyard, Ryan waits with his head down. Their conversation isn't over, per see, but Markus is done talking. His intent now is to investigate what happened with the Eagles and plan his next step after that. If Experiment T wants to give mixed signals and offer help conditionally then so be it, he's not going to waste him time anymore expecting an answer that will never come.

Markus storms across the courtyard towards the faction base intending to go inside, but Ryan breaks his stagnant position and cuts him off.

"Get out of my way," Markus demands as he tries to sidestep the Experiment. Ryan shifts to prevent his pathway once more.

"Don't go in there," Ryan says avoiding eye contact, "Just don't."

"What?!" Markus nearly shouts as he steps back and waves his hands, "Don't want me to see your handiwork? I've seen awful things before—whatever is in there won't be new. So do me a favor and get out of my way. I don't have time for your games anymore."

Ryan doesn't budge.

Markus' fists tighten as he restrains the urge to punch him outright, "I'll ask you one more time. Get out of my way."

Ryan finally looks up and what he sees is not the cold expression of a killer, but the scared look of a boy—it vanishes before he can speculate. What happens after is a hollow man lacking all emotion simply shaking his head.

Fury builds up inside of Markus to such a degree he's tempted to forgo the pain in his chest simply to smack this guy around. Sure he can dodge bullets, kill Demos with two strikes, and scare of Revenants, but if Markus strikes first, he might be able to get one over. All he'll have to do is bait out two or three jabs and strike after the guy tries to dodge those fakes—it's a tactic Grant used to pull that worked on him every single time. 

Inconspicuously pulling his fist back, Markus prepares to try his luck, but then Ryan moves. 

In one quick motion, Ryan pushes Markus back lightly, "Get out of here. Something is coming, and you're not in a position to fight it."

"Like hell," Markus spits out spitefully.

Ryan doesn't respond to that he steps around him and starts off towards the gate. Just as Vincenzo did, Ryan breaks light of sight, and suddenly it's as if he never existed in the first place. Markus doesn't rush forward to check on him, however.

Spinning on his heels and mumbling something unintelligible under this breath, he decides to head towards the factory once more. Ryan's warning be damned, he'll see for himself whether the aftermath of he and Doctor Crane's work is worth discarding. It's honestly not like Markus to get so heated, but his patience has been consistently running thin recently and Ryan's evasive behavior is pushing the wrong buttons.

He arrives to the side door of the factory and nearly gets inside, but then he senses something behind him so he looks back to find Jenks.

"It's not worth it," Jenks says with a sigh, "I poked my head inside while the rest of you were talking and..." he runs his hand over his mouth, "it's hard to digest."

Markus gives him an incredulous look before placing his hand on the door once more, "Spare me, you know what we saw during the war. This won't be new."

Jenks doesn't take that as a sufficient answer as he slams his hand on the door right as Markus tries to open it.

"Jenks, I'm running thin on patience right now, please get out of my way."

His friend doesn't budge as he stares bullets into the door, his expression exuding a look of fury he's never seen on the man's face, "That strange man said that this was Doctor Crane's handiwork, but also that Ryan contributed, right? You also said that Ryan always cuts through his enemies in perfect slices."

Markus nods, but doesn't back off from the door.

Jenks sighs, "That's what I was afraid of. A lot of those bodies had perfects cuts through them..." he then spontaneously slams his fist into the metal door, "Damn it! We need to get rid of that monster as soon as possible. Our last attempt failed, so we'll have to get creative, but we can't let him live—he's far too dangerous."

"I can understand your concern but let me see inside first," Markus counters, "I can't know what's in there if everyone is trying to prevent me."

Begrudgingly, Jenks steps back and gestures towards the door. His friend's face is weary and uncertain, but he's conceded to let Markus experience the horrors within for himself. Without having to fight anyone else, Markus is finally able to enter into the former Eagle factory... that is until he finds it locked.

Markus looks back at Jenks before recoiling his foot to kick it down but holds himself back just before the surge of pain. He keeps forgetting that he has a cracked rip, if he exerts such extreme force, he'll probably collapse. Breathing alone is hard; he can't let his habits get in the way of objective reality.

"It's metal," Markus comments as he looks towards Jenks, "I can't kick this one down. How'd you get in?"

Of all the reactions Jenks could give, he actually sighs in relief, "Thank goodness, I climbed up the wall."

The comment warrants a quizzical look, that is, until Jenks cheekily pokes Markus' chest which causes him to groan.

Jenks chuckles, "See, you can't get in."

"I hate that you're right," Markus says, disparaged. 

With another sigh of relief, Jenks slides his hands into his pockets and starts walking away, "Alright then. Let's get you some bed rest, and then we'll figure out where to go from there."

The intimating factory looms over Markus as he inspects it one more time. Whatever secrets it's holding is likely something he'll need to know, but if everyone is dead, the place won't be touched. He can afford to heal up before breaking into the Eagle faction for a second time. Besides, Jenks already saw inside and he determined that it wasn't worth investigating—he can be at ease that at least.
