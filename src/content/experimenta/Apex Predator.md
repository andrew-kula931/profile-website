When mass panic erupted throughout the city, most stores selling anything of value were completely raided. However, because of the mass rush of people to popular areas, they often became feeding grounds for some pretty threatening parasites. Over the last three years, no one had been able to loot the Pearson Mall due to the dense parasite population, but Markus was determined to be the first.

The following day Markus drives to the neglected Pearson Mall. The iconic glass display overlooking the main entrance has been reduced to nothing but a skeletal frame of metal poles with a yard of glass. Not far before that sits an impressively large parking lot. The mall's parking lot is surprisingly full for the fact that none of the cars had been touched in quite some time. 

A few traffic accidents litter the lot on his way in, and force Markus to take an alternate route in order to arrive at the main entrance. Once there, he doesn't even worry about protecting his bike as two Infecteds stand there to watch over it. 

He grips his knife and gets off his bike, preparing to take on the parasite at any moment. To his benefit, however, the parasites don't pay him any attention and instead hobble over to the motorcycle and inspect it with whatever intelligence a parasite might have.

"Good morning, how are you two fine gentlemen doing today," he says, politely greeting the twitching Infecteds surrounding him, "I said, how are you two today?!" One of the Infecteds looks over to him and then off to the distance. Markus takes this as another confirming sign and promptly heads inside.

The building's power has been off for quite a while with the only light source being the windows on the roof of the main plaza. Thick vegetation has seemingly started growing up there in great quantities leading to a pretty interesting dynamic between the human construction and mother nature reaching through the cracked windows. Each store within the forlorn mall is eerily dark and hides a number of twitching parasites. 

Markus turns his flashlight on and shines it around the area. His light lands on a humanoid parasite standing on all fours—its gray skin and lack of facial features creating a phantom-like complexion in the dark. Upon feeling the unnatural glow of the light, it hisses and runs off deeper into the mall.

It's no wonder this place has been all but abandoned for years.

Markus makes his way to the main plaza in order to stand underneath the ceiling windows for some natural light. His plan is to find the pharmacy first, everything in that shop is worth its weight in gold nowadays. It may not produce any leads on the cult by itself, but medicine is a strong bargaining chip nowadays. He might be able to use it to get information out of the people he does know.

He continues to search the mall and quickly comes across a dirty, cracked map of the building's floor plan. The map offers little to him as various cracks through words and splatters of blood cover the majority of its surface. He resigns himself to search the place the old school way.

The halls of the mall are, for the most part, completely unchanged from before the fall, other than blood everywhere. The familiar crimson red has honestly edited itself out of any previous stigmas he may have attached to it as he finds it to be the most common color in places like these. He doesn't even realize how fortunate he is to be able to simply look around, that is, until he catches the grotesque smell of rotting corpses. 

Instinctively, he slides on a gas mask from his bag, just to be safe. Despite the high grade filter, however, that doesn't stop the pungent stench from making it to his nose.

Due to the copious amounts of corpses, the food court had been turned into a feeding ground for parasites. The human part of the corpses was long gone, but plenty of wildlife has intermittently found its way into the pile of rotting flesh. Some piles of mostly bone even rise to heights taller than Markus which sends a chill down his spine. The stench is so pungent that it would have made any normal person pass out.

About six Infecteds feed in the room as Markus walks by. Additionally, smaller Insect class parasites litter the feeding ground. Insect class is just a fancy name for small parasites, commonly having multiple limbs and lacking a pronounced head. This category is quite broad, but so too is the diversity of those tiny nuisances. 

He looks around for an escape route in which two reveal themselves to be easily accessible. An Infected crouches in front of him so he takes this opportunity to pull out his knife and puncture it right in the temple. Immediately after puncturing the skull, he pulls out his knife and puts it back in its sheath. A dark red, almost black, blood oozes out of the wound as the Infected goes limp on the ground. Markus lifts his assault rifle to a firing position scanning around for any disturbed parasites. If anything decides he's a target, he'll book it out of here, but if his hunch is correct, this camouflage may prove to be a very handy tool.

After scanning across the entire room multiple times, he finds that only a single parasite with a wide abdomen takes notice. It stands up from the pile it was eating and stumbles over barely able to walk. It stares Markus down and stops when it is a mere three feet from him. Markus' finger remains set firmly on the trigger waiting for the parasite's next move. It sways slowly in place for a moment before dropping to the floor, it's mouth opening to the hand of the recently killed parasite.

Markus relaxes and checks behind him to find nothing taking notice. It's then that the faint red sign catches his attention from down the hall: the sign of a pharmacy. Needless to say he goes straight over to check it out.

The small, white walled shop has its inconvenient wire cage barring the windows with the only door inside locked. Markus expected this and had prepared a counter measure. He pulls a state of the art piece of technology for the job—a lockpick. He learned quite a few handy tricks from his time with Allegiance One and lockpicking just happened to be one of them; he thanks Wilbur sincerely in the back of his mind. It only takes him thirty seconds to bypass the lock. 

The pharmacy is lightly looted: some shelves are completely empty while others are completely untouched. It looks as if a single person had come through and taken what they could before running off. With what remains, Markus could consider himself a rich man. Ibuprofen, Adderall, you name it, it's here. He empties the trash can on the floor and uses the bag to collect each capsule of medicine. He finishes looting the pharmacy quickly and exits out the same way he came in. There he is greeted by the back of an Infected.

"Oh, damn!", he stumbles backwards, "Caught me off guard there… well, don't mind me," he says as he passes the Infected with a light nudge on the shoulder. Suddenly he is stopped in his tracks by a cold, moist, dead hand on his own shoulder. He immediately spins leading with a kick to the torso of the Infected and pushes it back into the door of the pharmacy. It stands up slowly and turns its eyes to Markus.

"I knew... you'd think... you're special" Volker's voice shakes like its struggle to stay intact, "now I... have you... where I want."

**Bang**

By the time Volker's puppet hits the floor, Markus is halfway down the hall. 

Volker made it seem as though it knew about his camouflage, which is concerning, yet most of the Infecteds continue to mind their own business in the shadows, save one parasite in particular that makes an appearance.

He rounds the final corner reaching the final stretch of the mall labyrinth but arrives at a chilling sight. Right at the entrance doors is a seven foot tall, muscular parasite. Its body is plated with smooth organic armor that fits perfectly on the agile body. Its face is almost entirely covered with said plates, save small openings for the eyes and a wide crack that opens with each low-toned growl. The only imperfection in its intimidating form is cut across it's torso like knife had glazed through it.

In response to the surreal sight, he drops his sack of drugs and aims his rifle. He doesn't have any damning evidence yet, but at first glance he already knows what it is. 

A couple of months ago some scavengers started spreading a chilling rumor. They said a monstrosity among monstrosities prowled across the city unopposed by any force. They called the aberration a Revenant. It was supposedly a beast that had been mutated beyond comparison but then brought back from the brink only to compare much more closely to its former human ways. For the longest time Colossals were seen as the most threatening parasite that walked the streets, but Markus knew that something like this had to exist. 

What makes the Revenant so terrifying? Well the answer to that is actually quite simple. In addition to truly terrifying strength, dexterity, and coordination, they possess the most dangerous quality of them all: intelligence.

This fact is only reinforced as the Revenant lowers it's head a bit as if studying Markus and his weapons. The parasites even goes so far as to tilt its head in a speculative manner.

Markus takes a shot at its face and hits straight on. His attempt, however, barely affects the Rev as it gets deflected off of the facial armor plating. Markus sighs—of course it couldn't be simple.

The Rev crouches down and prepares an attack by placing one hand on the ground. It then cracks the floor underfoot to gain traction before launches itself directly at Markus. Markus on the returning end, dives left and rolls out of the way. The Rev pivots and dashes towards Markus again who, at this point, is making a tactical reposition.

All he can focus on while he runs is where on this Rev a point of vulnerability could be. As far as he's concerned, this is his first combat with a seemingly intelligent parasite—one that is not falling apart at the seams, that is. How tactical this Revenant is, he doesn't have the faintest clue. Can this Revenant communicate with other parasites? It's a possibility. To many variables, not enough time.

The chase continues with the Rev gaining ground and then losing it with every sudden turn Markus makes. He makes it back to the home stretch but the Rev cuts through a store and intentionally cuts Markus off leading to the chase continuing. 

Eventually the pursuit leads back to the food court. He's about to vault over a large pile of filth but notices the sounds of footsteps behind him, or lack thereof. Turning around, the Rev remains stationary next to a pile of corpses. It makes a clicking noise with its mouth and points at Markus. 

This is the contingency he feared the most.

Every infected and parasite in the room stands up and slowly turns to face Markus. Only a moment of silence exists before a stampede of parasites tramples over each other towards their target.

It seems his "camouflage" may not have been a real boon after all.

Two minutes later every parasite in the room lays on the floor looking identical to the corpses they ate. Only the Rev persists through the fight, watching the battle go down with an indifferent disposition. Markus, now out of bullets in his assault rifle, and halfway empty in his pistol, finds himself hesitating to fire. Beneath the Rev's rough facial plating, it seems to close its eyes and make a long deep growl, not in a threatening way but one of annoyance and exhaustion.

Markus reaches into his back pocket to grab a DIY gadget he made but is stopped by the sharp tendrils of a parasite behind him. The rough, moist tendrils wrap themselves around his arms and legs and pierced his skin for better grip. He attempts to rip his arms free but their oppressively powerful strength puts his efforts to shame. He doesn't even have time to reach for a weapon before he is completely incapacitated. 

Attempting to resist, he's met with a proportional resistance of the razors on his forearms. The Rev takes the moment to advance within inches of Markus and stares menacingly into his eyes. As their eyes meet, Markus finds something he's never seen before. Instead of white eyes with black pupils, this Revenant has black eyes with white pupils. The mechanics of how it works seem irrelevant as the eyes narrow on him.

It growls in his face with a vile odor reeking out of its mouth. Calling it a rotting stench would be a demeaning to the smell of a corpse.

The Rev growls once more while Markus glares straight at it completely unfazed by its attempts to scare him. It then flicks its hand open, extending sharp claws from its hand and rearing it back to strike. Markus quickly glances to his bindings in search of some way to free himself, but the razor blades only cut deeper with every passing second.

Then, right before the Revenant can take its finishing blow, the echo of a bullet cuts through the situation. Said bullet clips through, not one, but two tendrils holding Markus hostage. With his arms finally free, he slides a knife off his belt and slices through the two remaining tendrils before hitting the ground. Immediately following he rolls and gain distance between himself and the two parasites.

Looking back, he finds the Revenant standing with, what he can only describe as a scowl. A blob of mangled flesh and four severed tendrils tries to advance towards Markus, but the Revenant holds out it's hand causing the other parasite to halt. 

With a swift glance and a shaking of it's head, the second parasite recedes back into the various piles of similarly looking lumps of flesh.

Markus studies the Rev carefully as he puts his knife back in its sheath and grips onto his sword. He's not sure if fire is capable of besting this thing so easily, but it might be able to buy him some time while he preps his gadget.

Feeling blood dripping out of his wrists, Markus debates checking in on the injury, but the conflict at hand feels far more looming of a threat. This fear is never realized, however, as the Revenant remains stationary. The strangest part is that it doesn't seem to be starring at him anymore. If anything, it seems to be staring past him.

The Revenant's eyes narrow as its claw traces the mark across its chest. It seems to be recalling a vivid memory, but that is likely conjecture on Markus' part. 

Then, without further prompting, the Rev turns on its heels and rushes down the hallway of the mall into the shadows beyond.

Markus recollects his thoughts as he stands back up and scans his surroundings. It doesn't take long for him to realize why the Revenant spared his life or what saved him from the other parasite. 

Standing in the center of the hallway leading to the main entrance is the mysterious figure whose identity is concealed by the hood of a dark blue hoodie. The man faces away with his hands in his front pockets yet he can tell he's being watched. The man doesn't bother to show his face as he stands over the blood stained hallway with a foreboding presence. A pathway of parasite corpses leads up to his location along outskirts of the hallway. 

Markus knows those corpses weren't there when he raced through it. Furthermore, each corpse is cleanly cut through with a single attack which splits them in half.

"I take it you scared that Revenant off," Markus starts with a bit of gratitude edged into his tone.

The silent man doesn't grace him with a voice right away. 

"If you don't mind me asking, how'd you do it?" Markus inquires.

The man rotates his head just enough for the most miniscule trace of face to show. Nothing more than an eye to see Markus. He then looks away once more and shakes his head slowly. At first Markus believes this to be a response on its own, claiming that he doesn't deserve to know the answer. Then, in the back of his mind, he wonders if this is a recreation of just a few minutes earlier. If that were true... then... this man is the real deal.

"What am I doing?" the man whispers to himself as he looks down, "I'm no better than he is."

"...uh," Markus stutters, trying to think of something to say as the man begins to walk away, "Thank you."

Out of respect for his actions, he allows the mysterious person to leave unopposed as Markus recuperates himself mentally and bandages his wrists. He is even able to apply some sterilizer to the wound with his newly acquired medical supplies.

Who could that man be? How does he scare a Revenant? How did Markus not notice his entrance the whole time? 

So many unanswered questions swirl through his mind. 

With so many thoughts but no answers he decides that worrying about it won't do him any good. After all, he didn't know a Revenant even existed until a few minutes ago and now there's yet another layer to the hierarchy.

Could that man be an even more intelligent parasite? ...A cultist?

He's a bit shocked to say the least, but for now he needs to stop being so reckless.

To summarize what he knows about the last twenty four hours: an old friend has returned, apparently Markus is being talked about, he has camouflage that can be revoked at a moments notice, there's an intelligent parasite floating around, Volker is actively hunting him to come capacity, and finally there's this strange man who, quite frankly, could be a foe just as much as he could be a friend. So much to think about and that's not even the half of it.

God, he needs a break.

When he comes to terms with the fact that his 'camouflage' is apparently back on, he returns to his bike. 

His next destination should hopefully prove to be a lot less life threatening than a Revenant... though bartering is probably his least favorite thing to do. It's a shame Misty isn't his prospective customer on this one.
