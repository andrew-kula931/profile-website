Markus sprints out of the stadium before even the guard warning them can leave. As he runs, he curses himself for not asking the council about 'Harvey' but resigns himself to inquire about it on his own time. 

Once he reaches the street he makes his way to his bike and re-arms himself with his sword and AR which he had previously left to avoid potential confiscation. A Colossal class is a monstrosity that he can't normally take down on his own, but with guards from all the primary factions it should be a cinch… or at least that's the hope.

Once his gear is in place he makes his way around the building to the main entrance with a fifty foot tall beast standing there with over thirty men of various factions pointing guns at it. They all take turns firing off precise shots at the Colossal trying to make each bullet count.

Colossal class parasites get their names from their extraordinarily large size. Pair that with their thick skin like a Demo and it's an absolute recipe for disaster. Colossals are no Godzillas, however, as their size does not hinder their dexterity. They typically are versatile, adaptive, and extremely aggressive. The singular saving grace of Colossal parasites is that they are extremely rare. In fact, this sighting is only the third ever with the first two being within the first year of the apocalypse. The first two were both about forty feet tall, so this current one sets a new record. 

The beast in question has a lean but muscular form with an organic plating covering its entire body, its talons each contain six powerful claws, and its feet are similar in nature. It also possesses a sharp barbed tail with a club-like weight at the end of it. Lastly, its eyeless head is almost entirely a toothy snout. The creature stands back on it's hind legs, but judging by the position of its limbs, he assumes it's no stranger to four legged movement.

The Colossal whips its tail around and almost catches a group of guards, but they skillfully dodge at the last moment. It then lifts its hands and smashes down onto the road below, cracking it and throwing a few nearby guards off their feet. Extra shots are fired to try and stall the inevitable, but it's too late, the beast lunges forward and crushes two of the unprepared men who had lost their footing. 

Markus analyzes the monster over and over in search of an obvious weak point, but every inch of the parasite is covered in that inconveniently thick exoskeleton. The only solution to big parasites like this is fire or heavy artillery. Both are solutions that would be incredibly difficult to utilize given the current circumstances.

Markus quits analyzing and takes action with a sudden dash to the front lines next to a few men. 

"What have you tried against it?!" Markus yelled over another car being flipped followed by a series of bullets being fired.

David, the captain of Reign's guard unit, looks over briefly and yells back, "Nothing worth mentioning, we're trying to draw it away from the arena!"

"Why?!" Markus protests, "Just finish it off here before more soldiers die!"

"We can't!" David returns as they both duck under a large chunk of concrete flung by, "We have to protect the beacon above the arena!"

"Beacon?"

Neither one of them gets the opportunity to finish the conversation as a corpse flies into David knocking him off his feet. He lets out a faintly audible grunt as he struggles to get up. Markus dives out of the way of another piece of debris threatening his own life. The Colossal seemingly decides out of nowhere to alter its tactics and begin throwing anything it can get its hands on. A car here, a chunk of concrete there, nothing that doesn't already pose a serious threat to anything it hits.

A few yards away a soldier trembles behind cover with his gun wrapped tightly in his arms. He glances around irrationally as his cover doesn't seem to be quite adequate enough for his entire body. As such, he makes a dash for another spot only to be met with a bookcase-sized chunk of reinforced concrete to displace him by at least twenty yards. 

Markus doesn't need to watch anything further to know that these soldiers aren't built for this kind of combat. In fact, no one is, not even himself. Colossals are so rare that you'd be hard pressed to find anyone that has experience taking one on and living to tell the tale. There is no training to prepare a soldier to match these odds, there is no contingency plan that could predict a dire situation like this. The first two Colossals were only brought down by tanks, but unfortunately firepower like that has been out of commission for over a year. 

If Grant were in charge he'd have some sort of plan, right. Think damnit! He has to find a way to save lives and get rid of this thing for good.

Another soldier who Markus vaguely recognizes from the HoV pulls the pin on a pipe bomb and lobs it at the Colossal. More than a few soldiers watch the Hail Mary with anticipation as all of their futures ride on it. For a brief moment, the level of hope in the air is nearly tangible, that is, until the monster slaps the bomb like an irritating bug. Instead of a giant parasite getting the blunt end of that explosion, some unfortunate souls inside a building get the honor of a quick death.

Markus grits his teeth as he stops thinking and rushes off the battlefield. He arrives at his bike and wastes no time testing out the engine's limits as he accelerates right towards the Colossal. He might not be able to kill it right now, but he has to get it away from the arena as to prevent any more needless slaughter. He'll just drive it to the edge of the city and then shake its pursuit. From there, he can return to the arena and help formulate a plan of action. 

The Colossal throws debris at a group of guards and then proceeds to reach for another soon to be projectile. It does this process in a seemingly repetitive motion which Markus notices immediately. Without need for a proper count, he concludes that he has about two seconds in between each cycle where the parasite watches the mayhem it produces. Using this knowledge he plans out his attack for that opportunity. 

He rides dangerously close to the Colossal, draws his sword, ignites it, and right as that two second window opens he rides right past its leg leaving behind a shallow cut blistering with an infectious fire. The parasite roars in discomfort—at best—as it notices Markus a few yards out of reach. He slows down briefly hoping that his efforts got its attention.

The Colossal proceeds to drop down to four legs and screech so loud nearby glass shatters. It then launches itself in Markus' direction like an olympic sprinter on heavy steroids. Without a second thought, Markus slams down on the gas and the chase ensues. 

He reaches maybe fifty miles per hour before looking back at the thumping behind him. The Colossal, which started off at an impressive pace, only continues to get faster as its velocity exceeds Markus'. Turning back to the road, he speeds up and prays that his custom vehicle's acceleration is higher than some oversized track star. 

About three blocks ahead is a dense traffic jam of abandoned cars which he hopes is enough to slow the monster down. The Colossal, however, takes this distance as a challenge and speeds up even more; each step shakes the ground all the way into Markus' bones. Right as he pulls up to the jam he makes sure to find an open lane between the cars and narrowly scraps his way by; a side mirror on his bike sadly finds its final resting place there, however. The Colossal thankfully finds it more difficult to navigate over the cars, but still keeps a consistent pace with the motorcycle it pursues. 

Markus takes that opportunity to finally top off on speed and gain enough distance to make a sharp turn around a corner. Needing to slow down to avoid a crash, the Colossal closes the previously formed gap between them and attempts to crush Markus in one strike of its mighty talons. Markus swears that he loses a few hairs as the claw narrowly misses his head.

Another five blocks pass and the Colossal catches back up only for their speeds to equalize once again. A bit further is the next obstacle: a downed building which had collapsed due to a missile attack during the Fall. Normally no vehicle could pass, but because his motorcycle had lost some weight recently, namely a mirror, it should be just small enough to fit through a crevice. 

The two occupants of the chase reach the mostly collapsed office building. Markus finds the gap and takes note of the light at the end of the sorry excuse for a tunnel. He ducks his head down and braces for the most dangerous motorcycle stunt this city has and will ever see. 

The building passes like a fever dream: he isn't sure what happened during the transition but when his mind catches back up with the situation, he's on the other side. He slows to a halt and he looks back, analyzing the building for any chance that the parasite stopped pursuing. Taking full advantage of the lull in the action, he pulls out his phone and make a call to that pesky 'No Caller ID' number. The number dials, but he can't even put the phone to his ear before the Colossal appears standing atop the crumbling building. 

Sometimes he wishes these Collosals would stick to stereotypical fire breathing or whatever else Hollywood thought up for them. Why do they have to be fast and agile?

Markus flips around and enters into the chase once more. He goes ten, one hundred, two hundred yards and expects to hear that familiar soul shaking rumble of the Colossal's steps, but surprisingly he doesn't find it. Glancing back, he finds that the Colossal has quit its pursuit of him and redirected its attention to a location nearby. 

Markus made a stupid mistake, there is a small settlement down this street. 

By the time Markus can turn around and go back, the Colossal has already breached the wall of the settlement with ease and is terrorizing the people inside. He watches as ill-equipped men and women try in vain to fight off the beast only to be crushed under its massive claws and tail. The ensuing massacre dwarfs that of the one at the arena.

The beast thrashes about in the center of the settlement which was built in between two skyscrapers. People flood out of the front gates as entire floors of smaller buildings crumble under powerful strikes of the parasite. Screams ring out across the settlement which pail in comparison to the shattering of glass and complete annihilation of everything this settlement had built up. 

Markus enters through a hole in the wall and grabs his secret weapon on his bike, a single block of C-4. He glances around and then his eyes fix on his plan, a large can of gasoline and a cable wire connecting the two high rises.

The Colossal's grotesque snout stretches into what could be described as a smile as it smashes through wooden structure after wooden structure, stomping on anything that moves. By the time Markus' plan is set up, most people have already evacuated and remaining monster stands quietly over the pile of corpses and debris looking around for another target. 

If this plan is going to work, he's only got one shot.

The Colossal takes notice of Markus standing behind a broken window on the seventh floor of one of the high rises. It bares its fangs and growls as it charges forward with its impressive speed. 

Markus swiftly pulls out a short wire he found in the rubble and connects it between the cable and his belt. At that moment Markus detonates the C-4 he strategically planted, which happens to currently be directly underfoot of the Colossal. As planned, the C-4 is enough to knock the Colossal off its feet directly beneath the cable running between the two buildings. 

Markus then jumps out the window letting the zipline carry him right over the Colossal with his tank of gas in hand. The Colossal recovers from the shock of the explosion and begins to regain its footing. Being just high enough that the Colossal can't reach him, he confidently draws his pistol and fires it once at the head of the Colossal. 

What happens next is the win condition for this battle, a long and anger filled roar. Its mouth opens allowing Markus to rip open the gas can and release the spilling liquid into the Colossal's mouth.

"You bastards never learn," Markus sighs as he pops a special round into his pistol's magazine and slides the whole thing back into the grip. He promptly fires it into the Colossal's mouth as it attempts to spit the strange liquid out. The Colossal's snout bursts into flames and it shakes its head violently trying to extinguish the pyro attack to no avail.

Parasites can be killed in one of two ways: either you deal enough physical damage to a vital area or you burn it alive. Markus' sword catches on fire because it uses a fungus that is extremely susceptible to fire. That fungus is just a variation of the same fungus that killed billions of people and turned them into these things.

After a minute or two of riving in agony the parasite collapses to the ground. Markus reaches the end of the zipline which conveniently opens up to the other high rise on a lower floor. Almost like the people who lived here had built it that way.

He returns to ground level and inspects the carcass of the beast. Small flames litter its snout and surrounding areas, furthermore places near its abdomen shake as internal flames struggle to make it out of it's boney exoskeleton. Bringing a goliath like that down fills Markus with a subtle sense of pride. Thought, this pride is quickly extinguished as he looks around at the completely ruined settlement that had nothing to do with this confrontation. He'd say he regretted his decisions, but that wouldn't do his true feelings justice. He's been here before, many times in fact. Yet every time is just as difficult to stomach as the last.

Then he notices a particular corpse underneath some rubble nearby. The former person, no older than a teenager, didn't do anything to deserve this fate. He just... Markus turns away. High school is over—there's no point in dwelling on it.

"Congratulations," calls a deep, hoarse, voice that reeks of nothing but death and decay.

Markus flips himself around to come face to face with a hoard of no less than fifty Infecteds. The speaker is a lightly mutated parasite who still retains the visual of humanity. Semi-straight blond hair, a not so ragged nightgown, and even white teeth flash inside its mouth. The only distinguishing feature between this parasite and a human girl is its eyes… or the lack thereof as both have been brutally gouged out—or rather, so contaminated by blood that they appear gouged. 

It continues with a shaky, demonic voice, "I'm surprised you killed it... so easily. That one was... hard to make."

Hard to make? This couldn't be. Could it?

Markus struggles to prevent his jaw from dropping. Not only is this parasite talking, but it's acting like it has dominion over other parasites. He'd be stupid to try and define this thing as another class—parasites don't talk. He's talking to Specimen 001 himself, Volker. The center mind of all the parasites; the original parasite. 

The creature talking to him now is just a host, but Markus is utterly speechless. He has only heard of Volker through the cultists he interrogated, meeting it in person makes his heart skip a beat and his hands instinctively reach for a weapon.

"The last time I tried to warn someone," the parasites says with dark liquid dripping out of the corners of its eyes and mouth, "they didn't listen... Will you be stubborn?"

Markus, still caught up in his spinning thoughts, manages to choke out, "What?"

Volker's voice only seems to get more vile and disfigured the longer the host speaks, "You are in possession of something very powerful—very important..." it coughs out some coagulated mucus, "It's coming for you... you can still escape," its voice cracks as the host stumbles forward, "Let me help you. You must die."

"Like… hell," Markus lashes out as he bites down on every last word, "I don't negotiate with cultists."

"Hm..." Volker grumbles quietly with each passing word seemingly damaging the host body further. The small streams of dark liquid now becoming consistent flows of waning vitality, "I'm not a cultist."

Markus is about to propose another question but he finds himself to be too late as the parasite woman collapses on the ground with a green fluid leaking out of every orifice of its body. Following this, the stationary horde of infects no longer finds reason to continue their diligent practice of patience. The ensuing charge rivals the roar of the Colossal as all fifty rush to be the first to rip Markus apart.

Markus promptly pulls up his AR and fires a burst of three rounds taking four Infecteds down. He then shifts his aim and shoots at another. Shot after shot explodes out of his barrel as the hoard gains yard after yard towards him. By the time his magazine runs dry, it appears as if his efforts have barely made a dent in their numbers. With the hoard nearly on top of him, it forces Markus to make a break for his bike. 

Another group of Infecteds stand in front of his escape route blocking him in. Markus unsheathes his sword and tries to light it but finds that the fungus is already burnt out. Despite that, he prepares to use the weapon anyway. 

Body after body gets within striking distance as he makes every swing count. He decapitates one, dodges another, stumbles into the next and has to kick its body off. He shoves a shoulder into a smaller bodied one which manages to get a scratch on his back as it falls. He parries a strike from the next but has to jump and roll out of the way from two coming in from opposite directions. Focus is key, every enemy within range has to be accounted for or it's all over.

He finds a gap in the horde and launches himself onto a pile of rubble. Quick-drawing his pistol, he dispatches another ten parasites before even that weapon runs empty. 

The parasites reach him by that point causing him to return to his hacking and slashing. Liquids of various colors splatter across his face as bones crack under his strikes. A body dives towards him in which he rolls backwards allowing it to fly past as he flips back to his feet and prepares for the next three incoming parasites.

His breath grows heavy, wheezing even, but his kicking, thrashing, and swinging doesn't slow even one bit. Rumbling of vehicles echo in the distance but he offers none of his focus to the distraction. 

An indeterminable amount of bodies lay on the ground now, so many in fact, that repositioning begins to become increasingly difficult as he stumbles over both debris and corpse piles. His clothes stick to his skin as its drenched fabric struggles to hold all the fluid splashing on it from outside and within. The Infecteds around him begin to become blurry. Using the environment to his advantage becomes an afterthought.

As his flurry of blows begins to slow and his tactical repositioning begins to prove ineffective, he notices an overturned bus in the background. With one last effort, he rams through a thin layer of parasites and makes a break for the high ground. A different parasite grapples him from behind so he draws a knife from his hip and slices the arm off before vaulting over another parasite diving low. He rolls between two more narrowly avoiding a lethal slash from their abnormally long nails. Finally, he spins around a short and stubby one before reaching the bus and wall-runs up its side. 

By the time he stabilizes himself on the roof, a small pile of squirming parasites is already scaling the side behind him. His vision feels like he's looking through a windshield during a microburst, but he refuses to let that stop him.

The roof he stands on is full of shattered windows but thankfully enough of the surface is a large yellow sheet of metal so he is able to avoid falling in. With his lungs now wheezing, all he can manage to do is take the parasites down one-on-one as they climb over the side of the bus. 

The first parasite in line is that of a former nurse who swipes her gray arm right at his face. Markus leans back just enough to dodge before stabbing his sword right into the woman's neck and kicking her body off the edge. The next one up is some larger body that he can't quite make out at this time. He repeats a similar action, though decapitates it instead. He prepares for the next Infected but given his blurry vision he fails to notice the one that had climbed up from the side. His realization is too late as the parasites dive at him, sending the two of them flying off the edge of the bus. They hit the ground hard with Markus using the parasite as a crash pad. He, of course, pays for the thoughtfulness with a knife logged in its throat.

Markus rolls off the body as blood squirts out of its neck splashing him across his already bloody forehead. His hands reach the cement ground before giving out. He doesn't quite know how he collapses but when his eyes open back up he is pressed against the ground and he's staring off at the bus. Somehow the parasites scrambling up the side of the bus haven't noticed his departure but he knows it won't last long.

In the brief respite, he feels some strange, complex emotion. Something akin to regret but filled with nostalgia. He regrets countless things like getting into this situation in the first place. Another side of him, however, is laughing out of his mind at how familiar this situation is. Death is an old lover of his, despite how compatible he is, they can never find it in themselves to come together in union. As the parasites disengage from the bus and rush towards Markus' new position, he knows deep down that Death has not yet come for him. It's cocky to say, which is fair, but he knows he's not done yet. He can't afford to die until he's finished the mission Allegiance One set out to do—the mission he set out to accomplish.

Markus slaps his hand into the ground shoving himself up and slides the knife out of the parasite's throat. He searches for his sword but doesn't find it. Instead he simply rises to his feet and watches the relatively small mass of ravenous flesh charge right towards him. 

What happens next is a bit of a blur as his mind was already leaning towards unconsciousness. 

A figure appears in front of him with a strangely shaped knife in hand. The man cuts through parasites after parasite with ease as if his movements were perfectly choreographed. The knife dances through the undead corpses in an awe-inspiring display of finesse. Parasite after parasite tries and fails to reach the man but every single one falls short the second they get into range.

Markus collapses to a knee as the mysterious figure dispatches the remainder of the horde without changing position even once. The feat itself should be miraculous to see, though Markus can't focus on that as his eyes struggle to remain open. He can feel the blood on his face as his consciousness wanes. 

Finally, when the horde is dead and gone, the man turns around. Markus can't see his face as the shadow of his hood blocks the little bit that he'd otherwise be able to see through his blurred gaze. The man then reaches underneath his hoodie and pulls out some pistol-like object. Perhaps it is a pistol, but Markus can't quite see it clearly.

The man lifts the pistol-like item towards Markus and holds it there. He isn't offering it over as he's too far away, but why does it feel like he's pointing it at him?

Nothing happens for a moment as the two men stare at one another in silence. Then the man begins to tremble slightly.

"I can save you," he whispers just barely loud enough to hear.

What happens next, Markus doesn't know. His eyes close shortly after and everything remains dark for some time.
