The first bullet pierces straight through Harvey's forehead, but he doesn't even budge as he lunges forward. Markus narrowly rolls out of the way before firing two more bullets into Harvey's side.

The sentient monster screeches as one of the bullets cracks one of his ribs and makes him stumble forward. Before Markus can punish this, however, he darts into the darkness, one again using the fog to his advantage.

Markus' body still doesn't feel one hundred percent so he can't afford to take needless risks. That's not even accounting for what could be different with Experiment A flowing through him. For all he knows, this poison might come back with a phase two, and he won't last a second incapacitated.

He listens around very intently, waiting for any noise to tip him off. When nothing does, he makes a break for the door. Last time he got close to the exit, Harvey pounced on him, so this time he's prepare in advance.

About two paces shy of the doorway, Harvey enters his vision like a flash of light. As the monster's claws attempt to impale Markus complete, he falls to his back and slides right underneath the lunging parasite. This slide is not simply to dodge, though, as Markus skillfully leaves a little gift behind for the hostile monster as it flies. By the time Markus returns to his feet and steps out of the door, he can hear the explosive go off and an inhuman shriek echo through the storage room.

It wasn't as powerful as C4 or a grenade, but a careful mixture of firework gunpowder, a pressurized canister, a bit of enough shrapnel to make a statement. That little gadget of his should be enough to give him a head start at the very least.

He covers around ten meters through the fog, using memory alone to navigate him back towards the entrance. After that point, Harvey bursts out of the storage room with a few residual flames glowing on his skin. He screams something in a foreign language before darting down the hallway towards Markus at twice his speed.

Ordinarily, he'd never win at a footrace, but Markus has one factor working in his favor: he has a gun.

Right before changing hallways, he turns around and fires a single round at Harvey. Instead of brushing the bullet off, the creature jumps onto the wall, clings to it briefly, and then leaps forward intending to take Markus down. The valiant effort nearly succeeds, but Markus uses a bit of aikido to parry the lengthy arm and force Harvey to slam into the wall behind him. Markus then dashes down the other hallway.

He arrives to the cafeteria and slams the door shut behind him. Just to be certain, he inspects the room one more time and quickly identifies that there are no other exits or entrances available.

Taking a deep breath, he casually walks over to the garage style door that supposedly leads back to the elevator. He probably should rush out and use the time to his advantage, but knowing how fast Harvey is, it probably won't matter. He'll need to bait Harvey into another hallway while he closes the elevator door. It's no stretch of the imagination that the elevator will take a hot second to close and the last thing he wants to do is get trapped inside that small box with a flailing monster.

Just as before, Harvey never tries to penetrate through the closed door and instead lurks outside his eventual departure. Out of curiosity, Markus pulls the garage door open just slightly to try and take a peek outside. He falls to the floor and takes the risk of peaking out into the hallway.

The other side of the door is mostly masked by fog, but what he can see is a clear runway towards a light at the end of the tunnel: the elevator. The lamp of the fake room glows brightly against its ominous surroundings. Then a bloodshot eye falls in front of him and a deafening scream breaks out. Harvey reaches his arm underneath the door.

Markus rolls backwards and reaches for his sword but finds nothing. In light of his blunder, he pulls a knife off of his belt and slams it into Harvey's hand which had nearly grabbed on to him. Harvey quickly retracts his arm as the blade cuts straight through his bony hand—the knife goes with it. 

"Stop!" Harvey screams on the other side as Markus rushes forward and slams the door shut, "Let me kill you! You must die! Please, you have to be the last one! If you don't die, I'll have to kill more! More! More!"

"No!" Markus shouts back as he assesses the situation.

As he sees it, they're at a bit of a stalemate. If Harvey wastes the time to try and break into the cafeteria, Markus will make a break for the elevator. Likewise, if Markus makes a break for the elevator without distracting Harvey then he won't make it there in time. With his gadgets running thin, his sword still in the storage room, and his bullets largely ineffective, he really doesn't have many options.

"You know t-t-to much..." Harvey's voice echoes through the room as the ventilation shaft begins to rumble.

Markus reaches for the door and prepares to open it if the monster pops out of a vent.

"Kill. Kill. Kill," Harvey continues as a door across the room rattles.

Markus slides his fingers underneath the door.

"Die. Die. Die," this time his voice echoes from the other side of the garage door which causes Markus to stumble backwards in surprise.

Stabilizing himself, he pulls on his AR's strap to swing it off of his back and into his hands. He promptly unloads the magazine and swaps it out for his singular incendiary magazine. If Harvey survived an explosion, these bullets probably won't stop him, but they'll likely do a bit more than regular bullets, and he could use any advantage he can get.

The constant shuffling suddenly draws to a halt and the bunker goes eerily silent. Markus scopes out the room, waiting for Harvey to make the first move. That move doesn't arrive for a full sixty seconds. Another thirty seconds more and Markus is still waiting.

"Where'd you go," Markus says to himself.

Suddenly a thin arm busts through the garage door and wraps around Markus before he can react. Immediately following, the arm constricts and slams Markus' back into the door. He gasps as the air leaves his lungs.

Harvey screams on the other side of the door with a shriek that nearly ruptures Markus' eardrums. Instead of waiting around, Markus uses the painful second to reposition his gun. Then, with a quick three round burst, he breaks right through the bone in Harvey's thin arm. His hand, Markus' knife, and Markus himself fall to the floor.

Markus gasps for air as he rolls forward, grabs his knife out of Harvey's severed hand, and aims at the garage door.

"Please, please, please," Harvey nearly sobs, "DIE!"

If things weren't bad enough, Harvey's next move is like a cherry on top. With the speed and horsepower of a truck, the agile monster bursts through the door. Only one of Harvey's arms remain attached to its body, though the other stump seems to be bubbling up with some sort of green liquid. At the rate of an inch per second, the arm looks be to regrowing. This observation is only reinforced as Markus notices that the previous cranial injury has completely healed leaving Harvey's pale visage as good as new.

Harvey turns towards Markus and opens his circular mouth to release another glass breaking shriek. Markus responds by diving out the hole Harvey made in the door and firing a barrage of bullets towards the creature. The shriek does manage to happen but only after two incendiary rounds take out the parasite's bony leg. By the time Harvey's destabilized body hits the ground, Markus is already halfway to the elevator.

He sprints into the small pseudo-office space and looks around for a switch to close the door. To his dismay, there is no conveniently place controls on the inside of the door which forces him to vault over the desk and pop open the keyboard.

"Harvey" gets typed into the keyboard and enter is pressed by nothing happens. In the meantime, the real Harvey appears out of the cafeteria door with his one remaining arm, remaining leg, and a growing stump of each. 

Markus types "Harvey" once more and hits enter, growing desperate this time.

Nothing happens.

Feeling a bit frustrated, he turns to the ceiling to find maintenance door camouflaged as part of the room's ceiling. Markus pulls out his pistol to conserve his incendiary rounds and fires two bullets into the handle's lock. He then runs up a bookshelf, jumps off, and rips the maintenance door open by the broken handle. 

"Come back!" Harvey screams as Markus glances down the hallway one last time. 

The pathetic parasite trudges through the hallway at half a normal person's sprinting speed.

"Sorry," Markus says aloud, "I can't die today."

Following this snide remark, Markus jumps up to the door and climbs out. What lies above is four floors of elevator shaft with no ladder. Instead, he finds only a cable and a long climb. The situation presents no other options, so he gives his arms a quick shake before grabbing onto the cable and putting nearly two decades of training to good use.

Around the two stories up, Harvey catches up and attempts to climb out the maintenance shaft. Markus had closed the hatch, but that posed little obstacle to Harvey as he easily bust it open and easily fit his slender body through the opening. The difficulty arrives when he realizes that he can't confidently climb with only one arm and leg. His stumps had made considerable progress in regenerating over the past few seconds, but they weren't progressed far enough to use.

Markus continues to climb as quickly as he can as Harvey fails to make it up more than one floor. When he eventually arrives at the top floor, he notices that the door isn't the standard sliding elevator doors, but rather an ordinary wooden door. The problem now is that the central cable is around two meters from the door. This distance is long enough that he'll have to make a jump for it, and he's not confident he'll make it either.

Underneath the wooden door is small lip, not large enough to stand on but just enough to grab, if he could just jump that far, he'll be able to—

His thoughts are suddenly interrupted by the cable jerking back and forth. Four floors down, Harvey makes short, scratching noises with his teeth as he yanks the cable back and forth. This annoying action nearly causes Markus to lose his grip and fall.

"Come back!" Harvey shrieks from down below.

With his grip about to fail, Markus takes one last look at his target before swinging his entire body in that direction. All is well for the first two meters—the last few decimeters are the problem. The tips of his fingers land on the lip, but they don't hold as his body weight drags him down. In a bit of a panic, he kicks out his legs into a metal support beam and pushes his hands into a separate beam on the opposite side of him. To his relief, he's able to catch himself.

While he's no longer falling, he does quickly find that this completely horizontal position is far from comfortable as it takes all of his strength to not give out. Between his shoulder injury and the immense strain this position takes on his core, he imagines that he can only hold it for a few seconds. The obvious next step is to start walking back up to the door—and quickly.

The first few steps are fine, and he manages to arrive back at the lip at the base of the door, but then his shoulder decides to cause a problem and spontaneously gives out. As his arm slips, his feet go with them. His last-ditch effort consists of his good arm reaching for the lip and praying that his grip strength will hold. His finger cry out in pain as all of his body weight falls onto them yet they hold.

He quickly maneuvers his other hand onto the lip and braces his feet against the wall. From there, he pulls out his knife—the same one that stabbed Harvey—and sticks it into the crack of the door: the wooden door lacks a door handle so he'll have to improvise. With a little finagling, he manages to crack the door open just enough to wrap his fingers into the crack and rip it open.

"No!!!" Harvey screams.

Markus looks down one last time at the parasitic abomination with the look of disgust. That poor bastard will have to suffer for a bit longer.

Pulling himself out of the elevator shaft, he lies on his back for a moment to catch his breath.

He hasn't had a fight like that in a hot second... well he has, but not in such a confined space. Those walls really gave him a run for his money. Naturally he'll have to come back here to clear Harvey out lest he hurt other survivors, but that time can wait. For now, he needs to get clear of here to regroup at a safer position.

While he has the time, another thought comes to his mind. The elevator was still stationed at the bottom floor which means that cultist never used it. Does that mean the cultist is still down there? Should he take precautions in case the cultist does try and leave? Or maybe there's a second exit to this bunker. Either way, he doesn't have the resources to take on another fight, nor capture a cultist. All of this is only compounded by the fact that Experiment A is no longer inside of him and the effects of such an extraction is largely unknown to him.

Markus pulls his feet underneath him only to find a horrifying form waiting for him at the base of the grand stairway. With two menacing eyes and bony plating all across its humanoid form is the Revenant.

"Oh come on," Markus whines as he pulls his AR into position from his back.

He likes to think he's quick, but the Revenant is another breed. Using speed that any normal human couldn't dream of, the Rev lunges forward and crushes the AR barrel with its bare hand. Immediately after crushing it, the Rev rips the weapon backward which pulls Markus with it as the strap is still wrapped around him. Being careful not to hit the Rev directly, Markus stumbles to the side as the AR strap flies off of him and the whole weapon starts fumbling down the stairs.

Markus doesn't have time to recuperate as the Revenant immediately closes the distance. Before he can react, the Rev slams a fist into Markus' side. He does manage to brace for impact, but he can't employ any defensive maneuvers as he breaks through the railing beside him and falls right in front of the grand staircase. The marble floor hits back hard.

"Damn," Markus groans as he drags himself to his feet and draws his pistol. 

He looks back up in the nick of time to find the Revenant midair and falling. Rolling to the side, he narrowly misses the parasite's foot from crushing his head. After that, Markus is on his feet, ramming into the front door with his shoulder, and making a mad dash towards his motorcycle.

As it currently stands, he has a pistol and a combat knife to defend against the most dangerous parasite known. Escape would be preferable, but he'll settle just to grab some equipment from his bike. He may not be able to win this fight, but at least he'll be able to cover his escape with a little help.

As he takes a few steps out the door, he quickly realizes the error in his ways. On his way in, the parasites disregarded him so he left them be. Though, as he emerges from Harvey's house, the typical bloodthirsty disposition of the Infecteds return with a wrath. All seven or eight parasites turn his way with an enraged desire to consume.

With no time to think and a Revenant right behind him, he sprints through an opening in the crowd and vaults the fence before anything can even touch him. He lands on the other side only to be met face to face with a parasite and a large sack underneath the parasite's twisted chin. That same sack begins to undulate as Markus dives out of the way to avoid the Spitter's venom. Just to be safe, Markus expends one of his bullets through the parasite's head.

Neither the Revenant nor the parasites have given up by this point as he looks up at the Rev agilely climbing the tall fence. Behind it, three Infecteds follow suit in the fence climb, two head left, and the last two head right. The Revenant makes a sort of clicking noise as it looks around at the parasites and points to Markus.

Rolling his eyes, Markus flips up to his feet and sprints away from the parasites. His motorcycle is currently stationed in front of the house, which also happens to be where two of the parasites have conveniently parked out. Parasites don't normally do that, so he can only assume this Revenant is giving very explicit commands. 

Despite the danger of two waiting parasites, Markus rushes right up to them and takes one out with a well-placed bullet. The second ducks out of the way to evade a killing blow, but Markus follows it up with a knife to the parasites neck. Then, before the parasites can start flailing, he kicks it back and reaches for a gadget on the side of his motorcycle.

That's the moment that a sudden burning sensation erupts across his back. His shoulder already hurt, but he had consciously mitigated that pain. This new sensation causes him to groan as an entire inferno seems to erupt from the nap of his neck to his tailbone. In an ideal situation, this would be a symptom of having Experiment A extracted from him, but when he eventually glances back he notices green acid chewing away at his shirt—the exterior armor already completely gone. He doesn't have time to contemplate how it happened, but he can only assume that Spitter coated the ground in acid. When he dived out the way to dodge the spitting attack, he must have landed right where it wanted him.

Markus rips the acid covered clothes off of him as fast as he can and throws it off to the side. Now shirtless and completely unprotected, he raises the weapon he retrieved and watches as the Revenant approaches.

Instead of preparing for another lunging attack, the Revenant stops just outside attacking range. Its subsequent stare doesn't land on Markus, however, as it instead investigates the gadget in hand: the flare gun. It's a gamble for sure, but if that man could scare this Revenant off once, he might be able to do it again. At this point, he either takes this gamble or plans on fighting a Revenant shirtless.

Markus watches the Revenant slowly begins to circle him as if weary of the device. Perhaps that should give Markus some breathing room, but he has a sinking suspicion that there is more about to happen. 

Regardless, Markus lifts the flare gun and sends the blazing project high into the sky. Now all he has to do is hold on until reinforcements arrive and pray that the mysterious man doesn't pick a fight when he finds out that there are no cultists present.

As the flare continues to burn in the sky, Markus genuinely wonders whether this Revenant is going to enact some sort of plan. This question is quickly answered as if karma were mocking him as a Demo class parasite appears from the garage of a nearby house. At that point, Markus realizes why the Rev started circling him: the bastard was flanking him. This realization comes at the cost of the Demo appearing on one side, the five Infects appear on another, and the Revenant itself holds out the third corner of the triangle.

Markus raises his pistol and sporadically shifts between each group, waiting for one of them to move. The pain in his shoulder makes the knife in his off handshake and the burning all across his back makes his vision blurry. The worst part of it all is that none of those injures seem to be healing. He had taken it for granted—healing from every wound rapidly—a factor he really hadn't even noticed until he nearly died a few days ago. This realization that such an affinity is no longer a factor makes him quiver. These pains aren't going away, and the fight has only just begun.

The standoff ends with the Revenant lunging forward. Markus rolls out of the way and lands with four bullets landing into the crowd of Infected—only two fall. He tries to reposition, but the Revenant has already recovered by that point and swipes its claws right at his head. A nail scratches his face as he leans back, kicks both legs into the Rev's chest, and backwards somersaults back to his feet. 

The Demo is up next as it's large, burly form stampedes forward with its shoulder lowering like a football player about to truck someone. Markus tries to dive out of the way but gets a foot caught up in the path which knocks him off balance.

The gang of Infecteds capitalize on this fault as one of them tackles him as he tries to get up. Rolling out of it, Markus manages to break the Infected's grasp and kick it away but not before receiving another light scratch across his already tender back. He places a bullet in the Infected's head as the last two Infecteds attempt to dog pile on top of him. The fatal position is only avoided as he performs yet another backwards somersault.

He arrives to his feet once more only for the Revenant to appear in front of him and landing, not a claw, but a fist into his abdomen—a rib cracks as he keels over. This action is immediately followed up by the Demo ramming into him, knocking him off his feet, and rolling into a mailbox post.

Markus groans as he tries to get up as quickly and pathetically as he can. Pain he can deal with, he can accommodate for misbehaving limbs, but when every single movement and breath evokes enough pain to make a lesser man pass out, he realizes that this isn't a winnable fight. Shoulder gash, intense back burns, broken rips, and multiple scratches all across his shirtless torso. Each wound manageable on their own, but compounded... he has to run.

No parasites block his path behind him so he promptly turns tail and sprints away cradling his chest along the way. The Demo and Infecteds trail him for a few meters but don't seem to be able to catch up, the Rev, however, is significantly faster than him and gains ground quickly. 

The Rev isn't the fastest enemy though. As Markus tries his luck to get to the front door of a nearby house, another entity appears from the ashes of Harvey's house. That pale, bony entity with skin that could melt at the mere sight of the sun, emerges with void-like eyes and a bloodlust that puts the Revenant to shame. With his limbs fully regrown, Harvey covers the distance at twice the speed of the Revenant and threatens to reach Markus before he can make it halfway through the front yard.

Markus tries his luck with a bullet, but it doesn't even faze the cultist as he scrambles forward on all fours. Within three steps, Harvey jumps into the air with enough velocity to catch up with Markus. A quick calculation determines that there's no dodge he can make to avoid this.

"Die!" Harvey screeches as his claw-like hand latches onto Markus' torso and slams him into the dirt. No less than two ribs crack under the immense weight of Harvey's attack.

By the wayside the Revenant stops its pursuit as it finds Markus incapacitated. It quickly notifies the Demo to stop its charge as well but the two live Infected don't get the memo. Without proper direction, the two Infecteds rush forward towards Markus only for Harvey to turn his black eyes towards their noisy approach. Without a second though Harvey waves his stringy arm and slices both Infecteds in half.

Markus tries to capitalize on this moment slicing his combat knife into Harvey's arm but finds the Ulna in his arm is two dense to slice through. It doesn't help that his wounded shoulder prevents enough movement to finish the action anyway.

Harvey looks down with his circular, teeth filled mouth quivering, "Y-y-y-you must die."

"Why?!" Markus shouts as he tries to engage his pistol welding arm but finds one of Harvey's long fingers skewering his bicep.

"If I d-d-don't kill you," Harvey explains briefly as he rears his head backwards, "I have to k-k-keep living."

It's at the moment that Harvey's head falls forward and bounces off Markus' chin. The head then rolls off to the side as the body slumps off to the side. The decapitation was so swift and clean that a perfect circle is formed at the base of Harvey's neck which quickly begins to bubble up. The cultist's head, remaining wide-eyed with a slender tongue slumped out of the mouth, stares directly at Markus with subtle twitching movements.

Markus looks up slowly to find a man standing over him. What he may have expected to be a hoodie wearing enigma is now a black, leather jacket wearing man with a knife in one hand and a pistol in the other. He glances at the Revenant and the Demo and then at Markus.

Almost relieved to see such a dangerous individual, Markus tries to get up, but the man stomps down on his chest. Pain flares across his entire body as three cracked ribs make his wheeze pathetically.

The man doesn't look down with sympathy, no. This man glares down at him with the most cold, apathetic looks Markus has ever seen—even colder than the last time they met.

"You get one word," the man says as he puts more pressure on Markus' chest, "Are you human?"

Markus grits his teeth as he struggles to even breath, not out of pain, but because his rip is actively threatening to puncture his lung. Even if he wanted to speak, he's not sure if he'd be able to.

The man's foot doesn't budge, but when Markus doesn't promptly return a response, it seems to warrant more drastic measures. While the knife holding arm hangs at his side, the pistol flows over towards Markus with the barrel pointed as his head. The man's finger tightens around the trigger. No reminder is given, just the implicit statement that this response is not optional.

Mustering up all the vocal strength he can, Markus wheezes out, "Hu—man."

The pressure on his chest immediately lessens as the man pulls his foot off and sets it on the ground. The pistol, however, does not shift from it's fixated position. With a cold, poker-face look, the man says, "Do you want to die?"

Markus takes in a forced breath as he leans off the ground, just a hair.

"No," he says sternly and directly.

The man, how hadn't wavered in mind nor face a single time throughout this encounter, finally sighs. He lowers his pistol into its holster underneath his jacket and turns to the Revenant. No words are exchanged, no commands are given, the two entities simply stare each other down—the same two entities that clashed back in the Mall.

The Revenant grows as it lowers its hairless head and curls it's claw-like hands. One such hand quickly touches the mostly healed gash on its chest. With a quick glance, it makes a clicking sound towards the Demo which causes it to start lumbering forward.

The man's gaze falls away from the parasites and notices Harvey slowly regenerating his head. The base of the neck had already regrown into a stub of a chin. 

"It's just like Pesh," the man says slowly to himself before looking at the two parasites in front of him, "And Heimes brought his Behemoth."

Markus tries to wonder what the statement could mean but then his lung flairs with pain and his thoughts are sidetracked once more. 

Then the Demo begins its charge, the Revenant curves around from the other side of the man. The pincer formation would have forced Markus to move, but the man doesn't see it that way. As the two parasites close in on opposites sides, he simply stands still observing them. Finally, as the Demo's shoulder verges towards the man's head and the Revenant's claw aims towards his leg, he does something completely unexpected—he steps back.

Timed so perfectly that it can only be described as a choreographed movie scene, the man evades both claw and shoulder by stepping out of the way and dodging both attacks by millimeters each. If he had moved even a fraction of a second sooner, the Revenant—at the very least—would have redirected its attack. If he had moved in a fraction of a second later, the Demo would have hit him. Yet neither of those things transpired as the man dodges both and watches as the Demo nearly takes the Revenant out. The Rev only avoids being crushed as it quickly jumps out of the way—canceling any subsequent attack.

While this is impressive and all, what the man does next surprises Markus even further. 

The man casually steps up to the Demo which had slowed down to turn around and, before it can turn, slashes the back of its thick leg. The Demo's knee caves immediately and as the Demo falls forward, the man positions the knife directly below its head. The Demo's weight alone is enough to sever its neck upon contact with the blade. 

How the man knew where the head was going to land, Markus has no idea. How he cut through three decimeters of leg in one swoop, Markus can't even guess. The result of the two moves—a complete defeat of a Demo class parasite.

The man then turns to the Revenant with a look in his eyes of indifference. The cold, callous disposition remains unbothered by the events as the next target enters his sights.

The Rev, an unemotional parasite through-and-through, stumbles backwards as its eyes widen. It falls into a steady stance and grows at the man as if to intimidate him—the man doesn't waver.

Then, with a single step forward, the man scares the Rev enough to cause it to start sprinting away. No chase, no curses, not even a concerned look. The man simply turns back to Markus with an unreadable look.

That's the moment that Markus' adrenaline stops and he looses consciousness.
