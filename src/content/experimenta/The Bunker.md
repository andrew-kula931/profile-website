_Markus lifts himself off of his cot in that dark and shabby room. Calling it a prison would be an injustice to all those with the luxury of knowing what tomorrow might bring. This crooked torture house was nothing more than a sorry excuse for a mad scientist's research lab. He was never allowed to learn about the screams on the other side of the wall nor did he ever want to learn. Ever since he was abducted when he was just four years old, he's been forced to digest countless combat classes, though even that would be a disservice to the word. It was neither combat nor a class, but simply a method of survival that threatened to kill him without remorse unless he adapted and survived. The cultist bastards that kept him here have been training him for years turning him into a human weapon with one purpose. To kill. Not just humans, but everything that he touches. If it breathes, he's been instilled with the ideal of preventing that breath. If it moves, he's forced to stop it. If it can resist to any capacity, he can and will snuff out that vigor. The light of the sun is granted only to those who succeed at their missions. Many things had been instilled into his young mind through this conditioning. The most notable thing was that failing was far scarier than even death. Success came at a cost, but injuries were more acceptable than defeat. Winning was the single and only objective in his life._

_The dark, damp, steel walls around him lay cold and dormant, that is, until the door swings open and a figure stands looking down on him._

_"A-13, are you prepared for your next mission?"_

_Markus is hardwired not to look up or even react other than a subtle nod indicating his futile existence and triviality of such a question; his opinion doesn't matter._

_"Stand up and follow," the voice says as Markus can feel it turn and begin heading out the door._

_This is where he makes his final stand. He's done suffering in these decrepit catacombs living in a fate worse than death. The sight of his mother's open arms still burning in his mind even though his mother's face and voice are completely forgotten in the deepest corners of his mind._

_Markus lifts a small piece of metal he smuggled in and stabs the back of cultists making sure to slash it up and through the back of the man. Every cracking sound of shattering spinal bone ensures the ultimate fate of his adversary._

_Then, without a moment of hesitation, he dives out the door, kicks off the wall and slashes the guards neck. They aren't even quick enough to raise their gun or walkie-talkie before the large gash in their neck overcomes their conscious mind._

_Markus now stands in an empty, white hallway, or at least he thought it was empty._

_"I figured you would try something like this," a voice from down the hall echoes as Markus' intense, driven eyes dart to it._

_An adult figure stands calmly alone in the hallway wearing a black cloak covering their entire body. A white mask covers the person's face concealing any features that may lie below. Two black upside down arrow points are placed in the locations where the eyes of a normal person should be. Just below that, in place of a real mouth on the mask is a black crescent facing up in a smile. The mask feels like a kid's toy with how simple of a design, but the person's presence is so calm, controlled, and intimidating that Markus can't help but take a deep breath silently._

_"They were going to take that metal from you when you arrived, but I told them to leave it be," the person's voice is deep like an overly mature man or a bass singer of an acapella group._

_"Are you the leader of this Cult?" Markus says menacingly as he lowers his center of gravity preparing to dash at the drop of a pin._

_The man laughs quickly and waves his hands side to side, "Oh no, not at all. Now, head down this hallway until you get to a doorway labeled with stairs. Go up them, and you will find a security card on the top floor. Once you leave, you know full well that there is no coming back. I wish you luck and enjoy the next few years."_

_"I can't trust you…" Markus trails off not believing what he said but having been conditioned to think that way, it became an auto response._

_"Naturally," the man laughs, "I wouldn't trust me either. But take heart in knowing that the power inside of you is important—extraordinarily so. Do me a favor and enjoy the next few years—you never know which day will be your last."_

_Those were the last words Markus ever heard within that compound or within that Cult in general. He never got a name as he rushed out of that building onto an island in the middle of nowhere. A speed boat waited for him to make a quick escape to civilization a few miles across the ocean. Before he knew it, nine years of abuse, torture, and unconsenting conditioning finally came to an end, but that didn't mean his fight was over._

_Grant found him a few weeks later._

Maple street is in a rich part of town. Large manors cover almost six square miles of land with golf courses around that. This part of the city has never been looted due to a prevalence of dangerous parasites and the expensive home defenses the rich owners deployed. The last time a faction tried to loot a house, five people died from a sentry turret set up to shoot down intruders and Infected. Food eventually ran scarce so those rich dooms day preppers had to leave which allowed this place to become a dead zone. Markus never cared for this place because it was on the outskirts of the city and wasn't worth the drive when gas was limited. The dangerous parasites also made the travel less desirable, but now that Markus is somewhat invisible to most parasites he can get there with no problem.

Markus pulls up on his motorcycle, a few Infected and parasites follow behind but lose interest by the time he cuts line of sight. The manor of 39123 is protected by an open gate and an intimidating metal fence. A black car sits in the entrance loop with lush overgrown bushes lining the driveway leading to the house. Half a dozen Infected stand idle in the yard with dead rodents littering the premises. 

Stepping up to the gate, Markus bangs on the fence to try and draw some of the Infecteds attention. A few turn to look at him but none of them move. It seems like he'll be taking another risk today.

Markus passes them, even brushing shoulders with one, as he walks up to the front door unopposed. The large black wooden door is only partially closed; it looks as if the door was swung shut in a hurry.

Carefully, he swings the door open and steps out of the way to avoid a possible defense which never comes. After a quick inspection for any possible defenses, he takes the opportunity to enter the manor. 

The main entrance is so stereotypical rich that it almost feels cliché. A grand staircase is placed directly in front of the main doors with a hallway to the left and right. The primary color of every surface in the room is white with highlights of golden paint and a few accents of dried crimson. Three decomposed corpses lay on the stairs, two are laying on their backs and the highest of them on its front. He checks the wall around the door and finds what he suspected, bullet holes in the wall behind the skeleton corpse. The ancient fighting scene indicates that his first place of examination will be upstairs.

Past the stairs, another dead corpse lays down the hallway right at an open door. Inside the room is a small study/office space. The dubious room obviously holds some secrets, yet the desk is cleared, the bookshelves are empty, and there is no signage on the walls.

Markus searches the area for any sign of a passage or secret note. The desk in the room doesn't move, neither does the bookshelf, nor the lamp… Wait a moment. Upon trying to move the lamp again he realizes it doesn't shift - not even a little. The table and the bookshelf are the same way. He stomps on the floor out of curiosity and is returned with a loud clanging sound of metal. Despite the appearance, he can only conclude that this room is not made of wood or marble. 

He attempts to pull open the top desk drawer and finds it as stiff as the rest of the room. Then, in an impulsive decision, he pushes it, only for it to click like a button and pop out of place. Inside is a keyboard mounted to the drawer with no monitor to speak of.

Markus steps back and thinks, what could be the code to… wait. He recalls the events of the last few days. The only reasonable answer is that the passcode has something to do with Harvey. His clue from the port said "Bunker - Harvey's house". Could that be the code? He tries it and waits. Nothing happens. He tries just the Harvey's house part and yet again nothing happens. Slightly frustrated, he inputs 'Harvey' hoping for some luck. Then, to his relief, the door slams shut and a swarm of clicking noises fills the room. He braces himself and grips his knife just in case as he feels the entire room slowly start descending. Markus quickly checks his phone to find that he has no connection to the beacon or the Radio Tower. For the time being, he is completely on his own. 

Anticipating begins to bubble over inside of him as the elevator comes to a halt. He's been waiting for this moment for years. The door to the room opens and Markus' heart skips a beat.

Outside the door is a hallway. Corpses decorate the corners between the walls and floor while a moss-like fungus decorates everything else. If he didn't know he was in a bunker right now, he would have assumed this is some sort of jungle as the fungus coats everything with a greenish, brown tint. Whatever happened here not only killed the people but didn't even try to infect them. 

He reaches into his bag and pulls out his military grade gas mask before slipping it on his face. He then steps into the hallway with his gun and flashlight ready as he listens to the silent vibrations throughout the bunker. It's almost inaudible, but a soft scuffling reverberates down the hallway leaving him alone to feel the tension of its origin. Markus creeps down the hallway one step at a time analyzing each corpse to check for any small parasites hidden underneath the fungus spores. 

This hallway is not only vile, disgusting, and completely toxic, but it's also a dense hive. This is no ordinary feeding hive however, this here is a mutation hive. The distinction is fairly substantial as one serves as a place for parasites to feed while the other allows parasites to mutate beyond the Infected class. Demos, Slenders, and probably the Rev too, all originated from places like these. The size of the mutation hive varies greatly with the largest ones hiding in stadiums or other large, enclosed spaces. Being completely underground with limited access to organic material, he can't imagine it will be able to produce anything large, but something about it feels very wrong. There are no parasites laying dormant in the hallways.

Markus is practically tiptoeing down the hallway when he feels eyes watching him from behind, he flips around and finds only the grim, forlorn hallway. As he gets deeper into the bunker the lights begin to flicker more and more; eventually a few of them shut off entirely. 

He goes a little further and arrives at a cafeteria. The doorway is garage style with the door most of the way shut; a small gab about a foot and a half high remains open. 

He makes his way down the eerie hallway checking each ajar door he passes. The sound of the scuffling feet starts up again, this time behind him. There's nothing there. A door closes in front of him. The hallway remains empty. The lights begin to flicker, faster than before. 

Markus makes a break for the cafeteria hearing the soft footsteps behind him grow into a full force stomping noise. He doesn't waste the time to turn, he just keeps running. With every one step he takes, he can feel the entity behind him take two. Five paces from the door, and he can feel the cold, musty breath behind him. With one last effort he drops to his hip and slides underneath the door, grabs the interior handle, and slams it closed. He expects there to be a violent impact with the door, but that never comes. Only silence emerges from the other side of that door.

Markus exhales slowly as he stands back up and shines his flashlight about the room. What was that thing? It was stalking him, but he couldn't catch sight of it. Granted he didn't waste the time to look back, yet the events leading up to the chase gave him little to go off of.

Returning to the topic at hand, he finds the cafeteria is small but unobstructive. It looks to have housed no more than thirty individuals during its zenith. The same fungus coats almost every surface with some stains of blood here and there, but no corpses are present. At the edges of the cafeteria he finds three other doors—all shut. As strange as this bunker is, it only gets worse when a light mist rolls in out of seemingly nowhere. The visibility of the dark room only lowers as the fog gets thicker and thicker. Whatever is in here... it's watching him. 

He walks over to another one of the cafeteria doors and opens it. A wall of fog tsunamis into the room from the newly exposed hallway. Then to make matters worse the lights go out—all of them.

With only the aid of his flashlight through the mist, his vision becomes limited to only a few feet. Because of this, he attaches his gun to his backpack and unsheathes his sword. Escape is the best option, but there is no guarantee that this won't happen again when he returns. If this is a parasite, he needs to understand the threat before he retreats, and if it's a cultist... he'll just have to kill them here and now.

Markus recalls the hooded figure who told him to use a flare gun, but he parishes the thought as he looks up at the restrictive ceiling and over twenty feet of dirt. Not only that, but he doesn't even have the flare gun with him right now.

He creeps forward slowly maintaining silent footsteps. Ambient sounds echo down the halls seemingly originating from every direction. Eventually, Markus reaches the end of the hallway which divides into two pathways again. A sign points to one direction saying 'storage' and the other direction reads 'control'. 

The sound of something dropping echoes down the storage side. As much as he wants to go to the control room, he's either going to be the hunter or the hunted. In his mind there is only one option.

The storage room is a massive room judging from the fact that he can't see any walls in his near vision. Shelves are aligned in rows throughout the entire room. Each shelf has a plethora of supplies ranging from cloths and towels to cans of food. 

The scampering of footsteps tramples to his right but nothing reveals itself amidst the fog. The footsteps start up again behind him and he flips around. Noises begin all around him coming from multiple directions simultaneously. Then they stop. The fog conceals the cause with a bitter, dreadful silence. In all of Markus' operations over his life he's tried to maintain a professional composer no matter what transpires, but this time… this time a bead of sweat leaks from his forehead as his hands shake just slightly. With his vision impaired and his sense of smell dampened with a smokey mist mixture, he decides that only his hearing will be able to save him despite the tricks this parasite is pulling on him. The scuffling shifts around the room like a centipede scrambling over every surface. He imagines an agile creature shifting around the room with every metallic clinging of the shelves. Despite the irritable echoing this room provides, he manages to focus on the single point of origin right as the sound stops entirely. He allows about five hundred milliseconds to pass, give or take a few, before he reacts.

He ignites his blade and slashing the air behind him. The fully combusted blade illuminates the thin skeletal structure of some sort of unfamiliar-looking parasite. Its razor sharp claws barely miss Markus' face as it gets hit back with a large gash on its abdomen. Shining his flashlight through the fog, he notices the cryptic visage of a pained, almost white, face with pitch black eyes and a fang filled circular mouth. Its body stands nearly eight feet tall with long, lengthy arms looking like the stage of degeneracy beyond starvation. Said arms nearly reach the floor as its long legs mimic the arms in every horrific aspect.

The horrifying beast twists its head inhumanely and aggressively as it lets out a low pitched screech before leaping into the air disappearing into the fog. Moments later, it comes down directly on top of Markus in which he responds by rolling forward out of the way. Both combatants are back on their feet within milliseconds. Markus' burning sword collides with the parasite's claw as the creature continues its offensive. He manages to block on claw but finds that the monster is competent with both. He shoves the claw away from him and attempts to dodge the other but still takes a large gash in the shoulder—the strike completely bypasses his armor. The parasite releases a freakishly high pitch screech that rattles Markus, so badly his vision goes blurry. The parasite quickly disappears back into the fog.

When Markus comes to his senses, he clips his flashlight onto his shoulder and draws his pistol in his free hand. He then begins to carefully look about the room for where the parasite might attack from next. His heart pounds as he creeps towards the hallway hoping to put himself at less of a disadvantage in that environment. This, however, turns out to be a terrible idea as the parasite lunges out of the smoke knocking Markus back into the storage room sliding a few feet with the parasite on top of him. He struggles to aim his pistol as he presses the flaming edge on the slender torso of the parasite. It shrieks as its skin begins to sizzle and lunges back, taking a single round from Markus' pistol in the shoulder before dodging the rest by contorting in unnatural motions. 

Without even realizing it, he finds that the monster had scratched up his arms with its lunge but thankfully nothing serious was injured as his armor managed to take the brunt of the damage. Once Markus ceases firing, the parasite lunges at him once more only this time Markus counter-attacks. Instead of diving out of the way, Markus ducks and stabs his sword right into the parasite's torso as it passes overhead. He isn't able to stop its momentum, however, as the parasite rag dolls into a shelf behind him knocking a bunch of glass jars off.

Markus pounces at the opportunity as he rushes over to the temporarily immobile parasite. He lifts his sword to finish it off but for some reason his arm locks up; the muscles tighten and resist any command he gives them. He attempts to move his legs but finds that a similar fate has befallen them as well. The only answer he can find for his perilous situation is the burning sensation he feels in his shoulder.

The parasite promptly stands up and lunges at Markus putting him to the floor with a claw impaling through his good shoulder. He grimaces in the sudden pain and tries to reach for his pistol holdered at his hip but finds that his muscles are still on strike. The parasite opens its mouth wider than before revealing multiple rows of shark-like teeth with a horrid breath that could kill a flower. 

The pain of death is not something Markus has ever experienced, obviously, but he always assumed it would be a spark of nerves that quickly fades to nothingness. He also expected his life to flash before his eyes… but neither of those came. The parasite doesn't bite down, instead it stops with Markus' head completely in its mouth. Then it backs off away from Markus freeing him from the impalement in his shoulder. The parasite crouches down into a reserved, fearful position.

"I-I-I-I-I'm sorry," the parasite says with a twisted and broken voice. Its voice is filled to the brim with a demonic undertone, similar to Volker's, and its voice constantly cracks with moments of near unintelligibility. With whatever noises it lets out of its mouth, it becomes apparent that it hasn't spoken in years. Despite all of this, the voice is not one of Volker, but a real intelligent parasite.

"You can speak?" Markus says attempting to move his body only for the same paralysis to keep him locked on the ground.

"I am H-H-Harvey G-G-G-Green," it says timidly yet so disturbingly paranormal.

"So you are one of the cultists of the Organization!" Markus grimaces at another bout of pain rushing through his body.

The parasite slowly retreats into the corner of the storage room and curls itself into the smallest entity it could muster. "I was t-t-t-told to k-kill everyone in this bunker."

"Did Volker turn you into that thing?" Markus prompts.

The parasite hesitates and simply shivers as it holds itself in a fetal position, "No…"

Markus hesitates, "Are you an Experiment?"

"Please…" the parasite—or whatever it is—begins to beg, "Kill me... before it's too late."

"What?" Markus gasps softly under his breath.

"That won't be necessary," a voice says from the darkness.

At first Markus is unable to identify the speaker, but that question answers itself when two glowing green eyes appears in the foggy depths of the bunker. The voice is one Markus has heard many times, but only recently remembered. Although his muscles refuse to move, he is able to adjust his eyes enough to watch the cultist emerge into the light of his flashlight.

Doctor Crane is no longer the shabby, unkempt man he was before. This time his eyes glow with a green bioluminescent hue. His face is pale—not as far as the parasite in the corner, but pale nonetheless. Finally, his mouth which had been stitched together is now rebroken as glowing green ooze drips out slowly.

The cultist looks over at the parasite in the corner of the room, "Well done, Markus has a predictable temperament to run headfirst into danger. Exploiting it was trivial."

"You," Markus sneers as he remembers how he had this same cultist in custody just a few days earlier.

The cultist looks down as his eyes narrow, "Yes, it is I. Now, before we proceed, I shall allow you one question. You see, I value curiosity, so please, ask away."

Markus tries to budge but can only muster a twitch of his arm, "No, not until I kill you."

The doctor's eyes light up, even more so than they were before, "Is that so?! It seems confidence is a sort of coping mechanism for you. How intriguing. It's a shame your usefulness is coming to an end."

"What are you planning?" Markus questions with a frown.

"So now you want to ask a question," the doctor comments with a light chuckle, some of the green ooze dripping out of his mouth. The cultist then pulls a syringe out of his pocket and taps it with a dirty finger nail, "Alas, we still have a few moments until this homogenous solution has settled."

Markus waits begrudgingly as he tries and fails to move.

The doctor crouches down next to Markus' head, "I'm sure you've noticed, but your body doesn't function like a typical human's. How does it feel to have a taste of an Experiment's power without actually being one?"

He tries to clench his fist and manages to drag his fingers together, but he still can't raise his arm, "I knew you did something to me back then, so what exactly is inside of me?"

Doctor Crane lifts the edges of his torn mouth as if trying to smile, "It's called Experiment A. The Experiment of Augmentation. It seems to have accelerated your immune-responses, mitosis cycles, and muscle density. It's no wonder you're able to survive bullet wounds so readily."

"So an Experiment doesn't have to be a monster?"

The doctor shakes his head, "I call it Experiment A, but it's more a remnant as the original Experiment died a long time ago. We've simply been trying to recreate its power. For the longest time it seemed like an exercise in futility... that is, until you came along. You've proven to be compatible with it. Over the past fifteen years, you've nurtured it to a point where it can finally be sustained without a proper host. For your efforts, I am truly grateful."

Markus uses all his strength to draw his arm back and reach for a knife on his chest, but his arm fails halfway, "Why?"

Doctor Crane stops mid-thought and looks down slowly.

"You asked the right question," he says intrigued, "You've finally asked the most open-ended three-letter word. I am overjoyed to finally hear you say that."

"Then tell me why," Markus demands.

The doctor sighs as he lowers the syringe to Markus' shaking arm, "We trained you to ensure you would not die because we need Experiment A to achieve our goals. Take heart, boy, we are doing this to save the world. And everyone is part of that world, both you and me. It's unfortunate, however, because you are no longer needed in _this_ world."

Following his statement, the doctor slides the syringe into Markus' arm.

Immediately, pain surges through Markus' body as he can feel some sort of internal liquid start to flow towards the injection site. His body trembles under the immense agony, yet the paralysis keeps him in place. All of this pain is overseen by the surgical precision of Doctor Crane slowly pulling the end of the syringe and mixing the internal blue liquid with a new crimson one. 

The pain continues to course through every fiber of his being until eventually it stops. Once the mysterious sensation completely exits his body and enters into the syringe, the pain subsides completely—except for the claw mark on his shoulder.

The doctor stands up and inspects the new vial of Experiment A, "Very good. Truly magnificent," he glances back at Markus, "You have my utmost gratitude for your contributions. Now..." the doctor looks over at the parasite in the corner of the room, "Please get rid him."

With that, doctor Crane walks out of the room with vial in hand. At the same time, Harvey—the pale parasite—slowly stands up, it's lengthy limps trembling as the doctor exits from view.

"You cultists are all the same. You blindly follow a sadistic leader," Markus grunts as he finds that his arms are now operation. They're extraordinarily soar and trembling under their own weight, but at least he can move.

"N-n-n-no", Harvey mutters as he trudges forward, "We do not f-f-follow. We are forced."

Markus manages to roll over to his knees and outstretches his arm for his gun, "What's forcing you? You don't have to do this."

Harvey shakes his white, inhuman head in two jerky motions, "I-I-I do. I was promised th-th-that if I kill you, I won't have to k-k-k-kill anyone else... ever."

That's the moment that Markus lifts his gun to fire while Harvey dives forward with his toothy mouth forming into a grin.
