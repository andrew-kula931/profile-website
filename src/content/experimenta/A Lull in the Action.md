There is no second destination after the hospital, instead Experiment T has Markus direct him back to his house. Letting anyone know about his humble abode is a strict no-go for Markus, but seeing as how his life has already been saved multiple times, he decides Experiment T is not going to kill him. Begrudgingly, he points the man in the right direction. 

When they arrive to Markus house, the obvious next question is whether he'll have to fight to keep his modified motorcycle. Once again this anxiety proves itself unnecessary as the man promptly steps off the bike and begins walking away without a word. Markus, who remains on the bike for a few moments, watches the man depart, almost expecting him to turn around and threaten him for his silence. That doesn't happen either as the two break apart as if their conversation had long been concluded. Markus doesn't budge from the bike until Experiment T disappears around the edge of the block—he never looks back once.

From there, Markus carefully steps over the tripwire between two bushes, ducks under the mounted turret's radius, and types the pin into the door. 

He doesn't leave his house for the next three weeks.

Over the next few weeks, he received a plethora of message from Reign and everyone else he administered the phone number to. The thought to respond was there, but his healing ribs, burns, and muscle damage required rest time. He considered going to the Nurse faction—despite any grievances he had with them—just to ensure he was adequately caring for his injures. This decision was decided for itself when the tried driving the bumpy motorcycle over uneven terrain and genuine thought his life was flashing before his eyes. That thought died hard, and he elected to stay in bed a bit longer.

He had never told anyone where he lived, so no one actually visited him for the first few days. The expectation was to never be visited—unless Experiment T came back—but that assumption was adapted when Jenks appeared at his doorstep.

Jenks, being a strictly superior info-broker to Misty, kept Markus up to date on the current events around the city. 

Apparently, people assumed Markus died, so Jenks had to carefully send in a tip to Reign just to let everyone know that he was still kicking. Why everyone took such interest in Markus, he has no idea, but alas he became a pretty popular topic even if no one actually did anything about it.

Furthermore, the hostage situation and subsequent HoV security break became public knowledge. Additionally, people somehow discovered that they were hiding a dangerous person and allowed them to escape. Markus never told anyone but Cleo about the cultist, so it begged the question of how anyone figured that out. The reality of the situation, however, was that Cleo's authority and the House of Vegas' position was in question. People still wanted to gamble, but with the threat of a monster on the loose that could wipe out an entire security detail in one go, the HoV's business took a major hit.

This hit came at the worst time as the famine too became public knowledge. The farmers growing the crops couldn't hide their lackluster yields and suddenly the Market stopped selling food altogether. The existing prices on food skyrocketed, and the Market had to make a strict ban on selling food items lest a fight break out. Weapons, tools, and supplies were still sold, but with no food, the center of commerce became even more barren than the HoV. 

While all of this was going down, Elizabeth and General Reign tried their best to keep order, but their efforts weren't enough. The Eagle and Nurse factions banded together to hoard rations while discriminating against any outsiders. Thankfully no fights broke out, but word has it that a single bullet could start an all out war—at least, that's what Jenks thinks.

These info sessions were immensely helpful for Markus, but the fact still remained that he couldn't do a thing about it. For all intents and purposes, he was incapacitated, and the city needed a fighting Markus, not a poor excuse for a diplomat. 

Jenks stopped by every few days to keep Markus in the loop about the city and about Doctor Crane's whereabouts. The cultist moved around a lot and, as such, Jenks couldn't properly track him down, but he could confirm that he hadn't left the city yet.

Another important detail were the rumors. According to Jenks, parasite attacks were become less frequent but extremely dangerous when they did occur. People were initially afraid of the monster that attacked the HoV, but then a small faction on the North side of town completely disappeared overnight. When some people went over to check it out, they only found corpses—all sixty of them.

"This isn't an isolated incident either," Jenks explains as he leans forward on Markus' living room couch, "A few miles from there, an outpost of the Scavenger faction was completely wiped out too. What's important about this one is that the outpost supplied fifty percent of all oil to the city. Tentatively, gas and oil is completely unattainable for half the people in the city."

Markus folds his hands together, "That includes me..."

Jenks nods, "I actually checked out both locations and let's just say... it wasn't pretty. Though none of the remains were desecrated which means—"

"It wasn't a parasite intending to eat them," Markus concludes.

"Right," Jenks confirms, "Truth be told, I don't know what it was. The aggressor simply appears at dusk, kills everyone, and disappears before anyone can catch wind of who or what did it."

Markus narrows his eyes, "Do you think it was...?"

Jenks shrugs as he leans back, "It could be Experiment T, it could be Harvey, it could be that Revenant. Perhaps it might even be something else. Each attack didn't provide much evidence of retaliation which means the attack was decisive and overwhelming."

Not quite overwhelmed but definitely disheartened, Markus tries to stand up to grab a glass of water. As he rises to his feet, his healing injuries come back with a wrath that nearly causes him to collapse. He manages to survive with only a grimace, but it's obvious enough that Jenks takes notice.

"Let me help you."

Markus shakes his head and waves Jenks off dismissively, "No I've got it. Legs just fell asleep."

Jenks doesn't accept this answer and takes a few steps towards the kitchen, "What did you wa—"

"Stop it!" Markus demands as he limps past Jenks into the kitchen, "I can handle it myself."

"I'm just trying to—" Jenks tries.

With a quick glare, Markus turns back, "Well stop. Things are bad enough, you don't need to treat me like a baby."

Jenks recoils at the comment, "The fuck? A baby?"

Markus rolls his eyes as he stumbles into the kitchen and retrieves the translucent glass of filtered water. When he comes back, he finds Jenks casually scrolling through something on his watch.

"Sorry about that," Markus admits trying to clear the air of any tension that might be lingering.

Jenks looks up from his watch a little confused. The realization takes a moment to sink in, "Oh that?! Yeah, whatever. Anyway, I'm hoping that I can get us a lead on Crane before you heal up. If we play our cards correctly this time, we might be better off taking him out entirely."

Markus takes a deep breath and nods, "Yeah... you're probably right."

_Knock. Knock. Knock._

All eyes dart to the door, with Jenks even drawing his pistol. Through the translucent glass, Markus identifies the silhouette of a person. They stand completely still, almost expectantly, as they wait for someone to answer the door. Markus and Jenks exchange glances before one of them advances towards the door. At first it's Markus, but Jenks quickly pulls him back and start off towards the door with his gun at the ready.

He gets to the door slowly, ensuring his footsteps do not produce creeks in the floor. Then, with one swift motion, he flings the door open, steps back, and inspects the mysterious visitor on the other side.

The individual on the other side wears a familiar black jacket and blue jeans. His facial expression has taken a step away from cold and unapologetic in favor of a much more neutral, observant look. Experiment T studies Jenks and the gun before glance over to Markus.

"Who are you?" Jenks asserts immediately.

Experiment T maintains the silence for a moment, "...you don't know?"

Jenks glances back at Markus but doesn't wait long enough for him to respond, "There are three traps leading up to this door. How did you avoid them?"

The man raises an eyebrow slowly as he looks around the yard at cleverly concealed traps. Jenks avoided them the first time due to his experience and familiarity with how Markus sets traps, but this guy doesn't have the same exposure—at least that's what Jenks is probably thinking.

When he doesn't receive an answer, Jenks narrows his eyes and tightens his hold on the trigger, "You seem familiar. Do I know you?"

Experiment T purses his lips as he takes a deep breath. That's the moment that Markus decides intervention is necessary.

"Jenks, step back that's—"

Experiment T cuts him off, "Ryan."

The whole room goes silent as the answer catches everyone off guard. Jenks hones in on the response with a scrutinous gaze, Markus takes a step back hesitant to accept it, and Experiment T simply stands unwaveringly in the doorway.

"So... Ryan," Jenks begins, still pointing the gun, "You never answered the question. How'd you find this place?"

Experiment T—or Ryan—points towards Markus, "He showed me. When I dropped him off."

Jenks looks back, "That true?"

Feeling a tad bit overwhelmed, Markus stutters to get a smooth answer out, "I mean, yeah, kind of. He got me the meds that probably saved my life. But this guy, he's—"

Markus isn't cut off this time, instead he stops himself for some reason. He knows that he should say that he's the monster the slaughtered the HoV, that he's almost as dangerous as Grant, and that he can't be trusted. All of those answers are pertinent, yet some strange notion in his head prevents him from going through with that explanation.

"I didn't realize you had a visitor," Experiment—Ryan says as he averts his eyes, "I just wanted to make sure you're alright... I'll be leaving."

Ryan turns to leave, but Jenks seems to want a little more out of this interaction. Jenks promptly holsters his pistol and pushes the door all the way open.

"I can tell you're not here to hurt anyone, so why don't you come in and say your peace," he gestures towards the living room, "We don't have to be enemies."

Feeling considerably uncomfortable, Markus creeps over to a nearby table and inconspicuously slides a knife tapped underneath into his pocket. Ryan doesn't seem to notice him do this as Markus returns to the center of the living room with Jenks.

"No it's fine," Ryan comments as he turns his back and takes a step away, "It's better if I leave."

No one stops him as he departs, instead Jenks and Markus just watch carefully as the man steps over every tripwire and ducks below each invisible laser. A few seconds later and Markus' home has been once again reduced to only two occupants.

When the coast is clear, Jenks promptly turns to Markus, "Who was that really?"

Markus falls into the cushions of the couch with a long sigh. He's still not entirely sure why he hesitated back there.

"That was Experiment T," Markus finally concludes which drains the color out of Jenks' face.

"Seriously?!"

With an affirming nod, Markus leans forward a bit and folds his hands, "Everything I said was true... he saved me and killed all of those innocent people."

Jenks steps over a wall and leans against it as he scratches his bearded chin. Back in the Near East, he always shaved himself clean, but nowadays, he seems to have foregone shaving altogether. His short, black whiskers have since created the prelude to a respectable beard, though as it stands, it looks more like someone being lazy.

He touches his chest as if checking something but then looks to Markus before crossing his arms over his chest. Whatever it was, it doesn't seem important at the moment and Markus doesn't pry.

"Let's not forget how he killed Amber," Jenks admits.

"Who—" Markus cuts himself off. Amber—Darek's apprentice and Death Order member, "Right."

Taking a few pacing steps across the floor, Jenks slides his hands into his cargo pant pockets, "What do you suppose we do about him?"

Markus quickly shakes his head, "Nothing for now. If we find out that he's been massacring entire factions, we'll have to try our hand at stopping him again, otherwise it's better if we leave him be."

"Right, though I do wonder whether he's really an Experiment."

Markus falls back into the soft cushions behind him, "He is. Just take my word for it."

Jenks purses his lips briefly, "Alright then. I think for now, I'll go check out the bunker. I've put it off while I info gathered for you, but given that Harvey likely isn't there, I should definitely take stock of what's available. It might help some of the problems in the city."

"It's good to see you caring so much," Markus comments with a playful smile.

"What?" Jenks shrugs, "Just because we're after the remnants of the Cult, doesn't mean we have to leave everything worse off than we found it."

Markus' face grows grim for a brief moment, "Sometimes it does."

Jenks waves it off as he transitions towards the door and peers outside, just to be safe, "What's your plan then? Going to take another week or so off? You're in no condition to fight."

Trying to prove a point, Markus rises to his feet and rolls his shoulders back. He clenches his fists and two confident steps towards the hallway without flinching, "I'm going to go pay the Retainers a visit. See what Reign thinks of all this and make decisions after that," he turns back to Jenks, "Just let me know when you find Crane. He's our top priority."

---

The journey across the city is extraordinarily tedious as he has to carefully weave through every obstacle on the street—including cracks and potholes. Normally he wouldn't mind off-roading down a dirt path as a shortcut, but anything more bumpy than a smooth, paved road made him crave morphine. He does make it to the Radio Tower without running into trouble, but it takes him almost triple the time it normally would. 

When he arrives he's greeted by a few shocked faces and guns pointed at his head.

"Back of," one of the guardsmen commands with his AR pointed, "We're not accepting visitors."

Markus uses half of his energy to process the scene and the other half to ensure he doesn't groan the bike wobbles a bit slowing down, "I'm here to help you guys. Just let General Reign know it was me."

The guard shakes his head, "Sorry, we're under strict orders. Even faction leaders aren't allowed in."

Rolling his eyes, Markus places his hands on the handlebars, "Just give him a message then. Tell him that I'm ready to be back in action."

The two guards behind the gate exchange a weary glance before one of the nods, "Sure."

---

With that lead closed for the moment, Markus makes the heavy decision to go check on the House of Vegas. After everything he's heard about their situation, he half expects the place to be abandoned, though he quickly finds this to be incorrect.

Guarding the exterior is a set of new guards Markus has never seen before. Whether they're new recruits or members that have historically worked in other departments is hardly relevant. What's important is that Cleo has already filled in the void of a dozen guards with people who have likely never done guard duty before. This conjecture is only reinforced as the two people manning the front gate don't even see him coming until he's basically on top of them.

"HoV's not open right now," one of them call out as they nonchalantly wave their hand dismissively, "Come back in like four hours."

"I have an appointment," Markus claims as he steps up to the gate, "Just let Cleo know I'm here."

The two guards exchange glances as if searching the other for an answer before one of them shrugs, "Sounds legit. Come on in."

The gate quickly opens and Markus steps inside with a careful eye on the newbies. It's not that they did anything wrong, per se, rather something about them felt... like a novice. Maybe they recognized him, or maybe not, the interaction simply left him worrying about the security of the gates—even if he did get exactly what he wanted.

He arrives to Cleo's office and finds a familiar face about to walk in.

"If it ain't Markus," Brian comments as he steps away from the door, "I heard that you got seriously injured. Aren't going to miraculously recover like you did last time?"

Markus shakes his head and shrugs, "That was... a fluke, I guess. I'm as human as they come."

Brian shakes his head knowingly while holding back a smile, "Sure. Whatever you say."

"What brings you here?"

Crossing his arm and looking around the room, Brian clears his threat, "Oh, you know me—just being a nuisance."

"I don't think you'd be allowed in here if that were the case," Markus surmises as he thinks about to the guards out front. He may actually be wrong about that on second thought.

"Okay," Brian comments with a roll of his eyes, "I see how it is. But no, I'm here to offer the HoV some assistance. From what I've heard, their security detail is rather lacking nowadays. Heck, you saw the guys out front."

Markus purses his lips and slowly nods his head, "Yeah, it's a bit concerning."

Brian takes a step back towards the office door, "Anyway, times are tough and even if the HoV is on thin ice, I still see it as an opportunity."

At that moment the office door swings open on its own and nearly smacks Brian in the face. He only narrowly dodges the door by swinging his head and taking a full step back. On the other side of the door Cleo emerges and locks eyes with Markus.

"Markus!" she exclaims, "How many times are you going to make me worry?" she finally notices Brian standing on the other side of the door, "Oh, hi Brian."

"Yo," Brian comments casually.

Cleo promptly steps out of the office and begins to inspect Markus like a mother checking whether her daughters outfit is acceptable to wear outside. In Markus' defense, he always wears a leather with light sparse padding. It makes for a high maneuverability outfit that also provides vital defense from basic dangers. 

"Are you really okay?" she asks him after a thorough inspection.

"Yes, I'm fine," Markus asserts.

Brian glances between them, "Man Cleo, you remind me of my ex-wife. She micromanaged like nothing else."

Cleo shoots him an exasperated look, "It's Cleopatra to you."

Brian rolls his eyes as waves his hands defensively, "Is this because I offered you a bad deal last time? I'm here to lower the price."

Humoring the man, Cleo steps up to him with her arms crossed and looks him directly in the eyes. Despite him being about seven inches taller, their height disparity seems to be the least of Cleo's worries as she stands as intimidating as caged tiger, "That won't be necessary. I know have a mercenary that supersedes any deal you'd offer me."

"Please," Brian laughs, "Markus is one guy. He's not going to solve your perimeter problem."

"Doesn't matter," Cleo decides quickly, "I'm still not looking for your help."

Markus finally decides that he's heard enough and steps in between the standoff. The last thing any of them want is bad blood that might lead to further conflict. Despite the informal demeanors, these are two leaders of quite large factions. 

"Let's keep this civil," Markus warns as he places a hand between them, "Thing are rough enough as is."

Cleo shoots him a glance while Brian smiles and steps back.

"I'll drop the initial offer by twenty percent, but that's as low as I'll go," Brian explains, "I've got a faction to run and so do you. I think we both know that this is the best for all parties involved."

Cleo glares at him with a hint of malice, "Shut up. Just because our security detail is a little stretched at the moment does not mean we're looking to outsource labor. We can handle security ourselves."

Seeing as how the discussion is getting nowhere, Brian starts walking away, "Suit yourself. If these mystery monsters find their way back here, just know that I warned you."

"What do you mean mystery monsters?" Markus interjects, "Is this about the attacks in the north of the city."

Brian turns back, "Yeah. You've heard. Those stories have everyone freaked. Heck, I don't blab, but my money is on that Revenant learning to coordinate parasites."

Cleo glances between both men, "Revenant? Are you serious?"

Brian shrugs as he turns his back and waves as he walks out, "Good luck explaining that one."

Once the other man has completely departed, Markus finds Cleo impatiently waiting for an answer. He's hesitant to address the underlying issue of a Revenant, but seeing as how she doesn't already know, it's probably important to clarify things.

"It's exactly as it sounds," Markus explains, rubbing the back of his head, "There's a parasite out there that's gained intelligence."

Cleo purses her lips with a flash of concern, "How intelligent are we talking?"

"Intelligent enough to command other parasites."

Cleo's eyes widen. She promptly grasps her forehead as she starts pacing away, "I need to change of scenery, you mind tagging along."

"Sure."

Markus is led through the House of Vegas to the tallest building on the street. It's a fifteen-story hotel that used to be rather high end, but now it's as functional as the apocalypse will allow. That being said, the first three floor are still quite nice as the HoV has taken special care of those floors for visitors who get too drunk to walk home or simply need too quick getaway from it all. Everything above that, however, is HoV barracks which means its more homey than luxurious, but he's sure none of the occupants mind the repurposed laundry rooms and kitchens over another cookie cutter hotel bedroom. 

Their journey thankfully doesn't stop within the barracks and continues all the way up to the room top. From there the entire city becomes crystal clear with the bright lights of the HoV illuminating it all just fifteen floors below.

"I feel like I've been on roofs a lot recently," Markus comments as Cleo walks over to the edge and leans over the railing.

She glances back as Markus catches up, "What makes you say that?"

With a shrug, Markus leans over the railing as well, "Oh people just keep needing me to go to high places. Honestly though, I can't blame them. These views are amazing."

It isn't particularly late at the moment, so he's not getting a late night city view, but it still is impressive how much he can see at fifteen floors up. All of the skyscrapers are centralized downtown and the HoV is located about twenty blocks away so he gets a spectacular view of the skyline without taller buildings obstructing his view. The remainder of the city isn't quite as impressive, but he can spot almost every single faction base within the downtown region of Pearson. Many of the factions were easy to spot like the conspicuous Radio Tower or the oil refinery a few miles away, but others are only identifiable because Markus has actually been to those locations.

One spot in particular does catch his attention. That being two high rises that are particularly close to one another. While he doesn't see anything now, he remembers that a faction used to live between those two buildings—before the Colossal attack. It's a shame they couldn't rebuild there. 

That place is also where Markus met Volker for the first time... he wonders if Volker will try and corner him again. It's become pretty obvious that his "invisibility" is only a fluke, so he intends to operate on the assumption that he doesn't have it, but it does make him wonder why Volker didn't try even more extreme methods to kill him. The first attack was a simple ambush and the second round was more or less the same thing with a Revenant involved. Did Volker give up after Markus made contract with Doctor Crane? Maybe there's something he's missing.

"I like to come up here when I need some air," Cleo begins, as if monologue for her own sake, "No one's asking for help. No one needs a problem to be solved. I'm not responsible for anything while I'm up here. I can just breathe."

"Yeah," Markus agrees, not that he has the same responsibilities, but taking it slow every now and then is surely a good thing.

The conversation falls into a lull as they both look off across the city with a slow rolling breeze floating by. Markus' shaggy hair gets into his eyes so he promptly pushes it aside—he'll have to cut it at some point.

"So is your name really Cleopatra?" Markus wonders. After all, she introduced herself as Cleo three years ago when they met.

"That's correct," another voice says from the stairwell beside the helicopter pad. Both of them look back to find Jaylin walking over, "Sometimes I like to call her Patra when she's trying to be smart. She hates it."

Jaylin's claims is reaffirmed as Cleo shoots her an irritated look, "What are you doing up here?"

The cheeky sister shrugs, "I saw you guys walking into the hotel and said, damn they're actually going to do it. But then I was like, no way, in the middle of the day? Anyway, I decided that you were probably coming up here. I know you always do _Patra_."

"Well good, now you can leave," Cleo asserts as she points towards the door, "I want to be left alone for a moment."

Jaylin raises an eyebrow as she looks at Markus, "Right. Alone."

Cleo doesn't budge.

Eventually Jaylin gives up on whatever suspicious activity she came up her for and starts off towards the stairs, "I just came by to say that Marvin and his team are demanding more rations."

"Tell them no!" Cleo exclaims quickly.

Jaylin reaches for the door with a knowing look, "I told them to fuck off and get back to work. Marvin's been slacking so he doesn't deserve any of our sympathies."

With that, Jaylin closes the door and descends the fifteen flights of stairs. That just leaves Markus and Cleo alone on the roof.

"So is your name really Cleopatra?" Markus questions incredulously, "Like the Egyptian Pharoah?"

Cleo—or Cleopatra—or just Patra—rolls her eyes, "Yes is it. My father wasn't one for naming babies so he always left it up to his wife. My mother was a bit of a history nut and eccentric to boot, so I got a funky name."

"It's not that weird," Markus consoles, "But I take it that you find it more bizarre than Jaylin."

She tosses some of her brunette hair over her shoulder, "Yeah, when I was a baby my family got in the middle of a shootout and my mother took a bullet. I don't remember any of it, but my father's remarriage resulted in a much more typical baby name—Jaylin."

"Brutal."

"It is what is," Cleo comments, "As I said, I was a baby, so I don't remember a thing. I consider Jaylin's mother my mother anyway. She was good to both of us."

With nothing in particular to respond to that, Markus takes another look at the horizon and the city skyline. He has to admit that he's becoming a sucker for immaculate views.

"I'm glad you came up here," Cleo says softly, "It's so easy to talk to you?"

Markus raises an eyebrow with a hesitant look, "Me? I think you've mistaken me for someone more charismatic."

Cleo rolls her eyes, "Oh please. It's because you're a free agent. I don't need to be diplomatic like I do with other factions. I'm not your boss. You're not my sister. And above all else, you don't make things difficult."

Choking on his words amidst the praise, Markus coughs to cover it up which quickly becomes a terrible idea as it strokes his ribs in just the wrong way. He covers it up with another cough which is hopefully not seen as a wheeze.

"Sorry," Markus asserts quickly, "I wasn't expecting to hear that."

She offers him a knowing look, "It's true though."

"Anyway," Markus comments trying to change the direction of the conversation, "It seems food's becoming an issue for the HoV too. Like everyone else."

Cleo turns back to the railing and basically falls on it with her arms dangling off the side, "Don't remind me. That's just the nature of a famine."

Markus, being satisfied with his analysis of the view, leans backwards casually to look in the opposite direction for a bit, "So what's the HoV's strategy?"

Raising an eyebrow but not budging from her slumped position, Cleo sighs, "There's no strategy. Every faction is devoting all of their spare resources to finding a treatment for the fungus, but it's futile. Every crop in Pearson is infected and even if we do find a treatment, it won't be soon enough to grow any new crops before winter," she looks Markus over briefly before making a mental note, "Between you and me, I can't imagine more than half of the factions surviving the winter. The rest will either take their chances outside the city or die in the subsequent conflicts."

"And where does the HoV fall?"

Cleo hangs her head over the railing in defeat, "Not good. I tell everyone that we've got a month's supply—we've got a week."

Markus purses his lips but says nothing. Then a thought pop into his head and he elects to pursue it, "What about livestock. Surely the fungus hasn't spread to them."

She strokes her chin briefly in thought but ultimately doesn't seem to pleased with what she concludes, "Livestock was sparse to begin with and if you recall, they eat the same plants that we don't have. Even if we killed everything to eat, we'll not only be screwing ourselves over for the future, but it doesn't change the reality that there's not enough meat to go around."

He nods his head affirmingly, "Right. Meat was expensive to begin with."

Finally pulling herself off the railing, Cleo takes a step towards Markus as if to throw herself in his arms, but then that playful demeanor falls flat, and she leans back against the railing, "Sorry to lay that all on you. It's really none of your business."

"It's fine," Markus answers honestly, "It actually puts the entire situation into perspective for me. I've been held up in my house for the past month."

Cleo bites her bottom lip as she turns back around and folds both arms along the top of the railing, "Yeah. About that. What happened and why did you get hurt so bad?"

Markus chuckles at that thought of everything that went down. Where does he even begin?

"I found the mayor's bunker," Markus decides bluntly which causes Cleo to bolt up, "It wasn't safe at the time, and it nearly cost me my life, but I've got someone I trust going back there. It won't solve the city's famine, but it should help some if we can clear it out."

"How much are we talking?"

Markus shrugs, "It's really hard to say, but I'll make a decision once I know for sure."

Taking a deep breath, Cleo steps away from the edge of the tower and takes a few steps towards the door. She stops for a moment in thought before turning back to Markus with determined look.

"I don't know what the solution is, but I've got to keep trying. I came up here to catch a break, but really it was to mentally prep for a meeting. Myself and all of my administrators are going to have a long chat about what we want to do."

"Do you think your advisors will pull through?" Markus questions, particularly intrigued.

Cloe shrugs, "I don't know. I halfway expect them to attempt to usurp my position. I won't let that happen, but if it does..."

Markus pushes himself off the railing and walks up beside her while facing towards the door, "I'm sure it'll be fine."

"Right," Cleo says unconfidently.

Clearing his throat, Markus slides his hands into his pockets, "I stopped by to say that I'm here to help. If you find anything that needs my... expertise. Just let me know."

Markus takes about three steps away before Cleo's voice makes him stop in his tracks.

"Did you ever find out who attacked the HoV? Darek wouldn't tell me what he found out."

That's right. He hasn't told her about Experiment T. Trying to explain what an Experiment allegedly is would take hours, so maybe he could simplify the answer... how does he even feel about it? Ryan put Cleo into this position, yet he also saved Markus' life. What side should he be on?

"I did find him," Markus eventually admits, "He's not dead, but at least I know his name."

Cleo looks over her shoulder awaiting some sort of answer.

"If someone in a black jacket appears and responds to the name Ryan, know that you've found your guy."

With that, Markus departs from the roof.
