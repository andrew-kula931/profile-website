As darkness descends over the Eagle Faction headquarters, the spotlights flicker on. The location is fairly small for such a large faction: only one old factory building, a small power station, and a brick wall in total. Recently constructed guard towers sit in each corner of the factory but only two of them actually contain guards. 

One notable thing that Markus particular enjoys about situations such as these is a big factions propensity for a false sense of security. Factions expect random parasite sightings and the potential for an armed attack. What they never expect, however, is a single infiltrator sneaking through their blind spots. 

As the spotlights routinely patrol the dark grounds, a small group of Slender class parasites frolick their way to the gates of the factory. Some guards shout out commands and all attention is brought to the front gates to quickly dispatch the seemingly random appearance of parasites. 

"You ready?" Markus asks Jenks as they both crouch over the ledge of a nearby building.

Jenks nods, "I'm a bit rusty so I'll follow your lead."

They both nod as they lift black cloths over their face to conceal their identity to some degree.

While the guards are distracted, they stealthily makes his way up to the south wall. He tests the barbed wire on top for electric current by spraying a small bit of water on the metal wires—it sizzles immediately. Nodding back to Jenks, the man pulls out an insulated tarp from his bag and tosses it over the metal links. Jenks then bends down and cups his hands, ready to boost Markus whenever he's ready.

"Make sure you stick to this side of the building so I can provide cover fire," Jenks reiterates.

"Right," Markus agrees as he accepts the boost and gracefully vaults over the barbed wire.

Immediately after making it over the wall, the insulated tarp is tugged backwards and pulled off the wall as if it were never there.

By the time the Slender problem is resolved, Markus has already ducked into an unlocked door.

The inside of the factory looks like one large barracks. Cots line the wall with a cafeteria to one corner. A few doors to one side of the room lead elsewhere but given that each door is propped open, he assumes no hostages would be kept there. He quickly notices a few footsteps heading in his direction, so he backs off and exits the facility. 

His contingency plan for this isn't the most elegant, but it'll have to do if he'll be spotted anywhere inside. 

He pulls out a particular gadget he acquired from the military: a small drill with a ring handle. He approaches an exterior wall in a blind spot of the guard towers and places the gadget against the wall. It makes a soft rumbling noise as it spins the drill and inserts itself into the brick of the factory. Then, he pulls himself up and locks his shoe into the conveniently shaped handle. It takes a lot of upper body strength and balance to pull himself up with only one hand hold, but he manages. He repeats this process four more times before reaching the third floor windows. As he gets higher, he begins to use the windowsills as handholds to maintain balance.

When he arrives at a good spot, he promptly pulls out a precision knife and cuts the insulation around a window before popping it out of place. Not wanting to drop it and risk alerting anyone, he pulls out some ultra-strength adhesive, applies some to the wall, and finally sticks the glass window to the brick wall. It doesn't look pretty but it'll have to do.

Upon entering inside, he finds a table in the middle of the room with a few documents resting on it. Being careful not to creek the untrustworthy floor, he sneaks over to the table and inspects the papers. Each document is printed in an Serif font with formal heading, a lengthy body, and no visible identification of writer. 

He begins to reach each document but only gets a few words in before he hears footsteps coming up to the door. 

Two men walk into the room and go straight to the map. Meanwhile, Markus hangs from the window, industrial blinds hardly swaying, while he remaining as silent as a ghost.

"I still hate this," one of the men says with an irritated tone.

"Don't we all," another man with a deeper, more authoritative voice comments, "You think I want anything to do with that bubbly girl? We already made this decision, we're seeing it through."

"It's just..." the first trails off, "that guy gives me the creeps, how do you know he won't betray us?"

"He definitely will," the second decides, "and I'm accounting for it. That being said, we already lost twenty of our best men, we can't afford to go up against him again."

"Yeah, but it still feels like we're betraying the city by doing this. We're going to be hated by everyone for this."

"Don't care," the second counters, "it doesn't matter if we're loved or hated if we're dead. I'm just looking out for number one. You can tag along too if you fall in line."

"Right," the first confirms.

As the pain in his hands rises it finally reaches its breaking point and one hand slips. He barely keeps hold with the other as he looks down. The closest climbing point he had used to get up had seemingly malfunctioned and is now taking a little rest on the ground below—he should have taken their maintenance more seriously. Regardless, the closest foothold he could use is a floor down and he knows he can't stick that landing. 

With only one option remaining, he decides to pull himself back in the room only to find it empty once more. After a careful glance around, he creeps towards the door.

Outside the meeting room is a catwalk overlooking the factory as a whole. The catwalk splits into two different directions, one leading to an indoor stairwell and the other leading to another set of rooms. Underneath people walk around the "barracks" area completely unaware of his presence.

Behind a particular door on that floor, he faintly heard the muffled scream of a young woman. He approaches a door he believes to be in the correct direction and finds it surprisingly unlocked. The other side opens to a sizable armory. 

It's his lucky day. 

He planned to go shopping at the mall but got caught up in a heated debate with a Revenant so he had to put a raincheck on those plans. Now it seems like he'll finally be able to get the ever-important shopping done and this time there'll be guns. 

He passed the various gun racks and shelves of ammunition making sure to swipe a few things here and there. Once he reaches the other side, he moves on to the next room which he discovers to be their food storage area. The sight is depressing: only two out of ten shelves have anything on it and neither is full. It seems that the Eagle's irrational measures were somewhat justified as they were on their last leg. That's not even considering what those men were talking about earlier.

Markus passes that room and peaks through a door sitting slightly ajar. Three men stand inside huddled over someone sitting in a wooden chair. Upon further inspection, the person tied up in a chair reveals herself to be none other than Jaylin herself. Fury rages in her eyes as she futilely attempts to bite through the gag in her mouth. 

He's about to make a move, but then the men start talking and a small amount of curiosity strikes him. Given that none of them look particularly hostile towards Jaylin and that she's not injured herself, he determines that he can spare some time for organic information reconnaissance. 

"You're one fucked up doctor, you know that?" a man with a pistol holstered at his hip comments aggressively as he crosses his arms and leans back.

The man the comment was directed towards, keeps his back facing Markus as he stares at Jaylin, "Don't patronize me. If you had any idea what I was doing, you'd have more respect for my craft."

The third man, completely unarmed, glares at the 'doctor', "Don't make us remind you that you forced our hand. We're not doing this willingly."

"And neither was the last batch of humans," the doctor says dismissively as he leans in towards Jaylin. The girl's eyes fill with fear as the doctor gets closer to her.

"Please remain calm dear," the doctor says in a calm tone, "I'm not here to hurt you. You are simply going to be key to consumable materials for these people. You will also serve as an excellent decoy for a very, erm, evasive individual."

The first man sighs, "How do you know this will even work? We want some results."

The doctor goes still for a moment.

"Yes... yes, yes," he mutters to himself, "You're in luck humans. He's here."

Markus isn't sure whether he's the topic of discussion, but he knows that the information gather has concluded. So, in one swift motion, he swings the door open and places his knife against the throat of the guy with the gun. At the same time he draws his pistol on the two other men in the room, "If you scream, it'll be the last thing you do."

The man at knifepoint jolts in surprise, almost moving beyond the threshold, but then stops as he realizes the situation. He slowly and reluctantly puts his hands up. The third man does the same with surprise on his face. Jaylin stops making noise and watches the scene unfold with her gaze deadlocked on Markus. The doctor, however, does no such thing and slowly turns to the new face in the room without so much as a ounce of concern.

"Wait! Don't shoot!" the man in front of him says stiffly.

"Drop the pistol on the floor," Markus commands coldly.

The man quickly unholsters his pistol and drops it on the floor, "Please, I've got a family downstairs."

"Shut up, if you do as I say, we can all walk out of this alive," Markus asserts as he points his gun at the doctor, "You. Explain what's going on."

The doctor, an unkempt man with bags under his eyes and a growing beard, frowns slightly as he looks at the hostage with dissatisfaction. Underneath the man's messy beard are conspicuous scars and a deformed mouth as if a large hook had latched onto his lip and dragged half his face an inch down. Although still human, he taunted a parasite's look quite considerably.

"You can kill him," the doctor decides apathetically, "He's only getting in the way."

Markus narrows his eyes at the cold remark before flipping his knife around and slamming back of it into the hostage's temple. The man's head jerks to the side and he collapses like a ragdoll.

To be frank, he hadn't done that in years so he was a bit nervous that the effort would fail and he'd have to kill the man. Its relieving to see the man fall because it means he'll be able to see his family again. 

The cruel doctor watches his 'companion' fall to the floor with annoyance as he folds his hands behind his back. Then, with a sigh he turns to the other Eagle member in the room.

"Don't look at me," the unarmed man defends, "I don't know how to fight. What do you want me to do?"

The doctor glances back to Markus with a clinical gaze, as if studying a sample keenly. He then turns back to his 'companion', pulls a syringe out of a secret compartment in his coat, and offers it over to the Eagle member.

"What?" the man asks.

Markus debates intervening, but honestly, seeing this scene through may be more insightful than cutting it short.

"Take it," the doctor says, this time voiced more as a request than a command.

Hesitantly the man outstretches his arm to receive the suspicious gift and, upon doing so, is immediately injected with the serum before he can even react. The doctor performs the consensual injection with a quick, skillful jab before tossing the empty syringe on the floor. The entire event takes less than a second which surprises everyone in the room, but none more than the poor soul receiving the mystery serum.

"What the hell was that!?" the man exclaims as he stumbles away from the doctor with a hand over the injection site.

The doctor watches him coldly as if anticipating something to happen.

The man regains his footing and inches towards the door, "You know what, you're on your own."

He tries to get to the door but Markus intercepts him. Realizing that he'll likely have to knock another person out, Markus prepares the back of his knife for another strike. This effort is never seen through, however, as the man stops moving and simply stands in place with his gaze pointed towards the floor.

Very slowly, the man looks up to the doctor, "What did you put in me?"

The doctor smiles slyly, "The same truth serum you told me to put into her. I told you that it was only a prototype, so I thought it best to a quick dry run—to test the efficacy."

The man swallows hard as if holding back the urge to vomit, "This isn't..."

He never finishes his sentence. 

Suddenly the man opens his mouth to scream but no sound comes out. Instead his face begins to grow as red as a cherry. Some unspeakable pain covers his demeanor as he tries to keel over but his muscles resist and he ends up looking up instead of down. His body tenses up as he releases the dreadful silent scream imaginable. Then his head explodes.

Markus covers his eyes instinctively as human projectiles fly towards him. When he withdraws his hand from his eyes, he finds his all black attire covered in bits of blood. The doctor and Jaylin are in similar situations visually with the rest of the room now dotted red. A pronounced red ring has formed around the man's body as his headless self collapses onto the floor. 

Jaylin tries in vain to scream but her gag prevents any real noise from escaping.

"Aren't you glad I used it on him," the doctor comments to Jaylin as he wipes some blood off his sleeves.

Markus points his pistol at the doctor's head. He can feel a nauseousness rising inside of him, but he suppresses it as he recognizes the real threat in the room. Putting a bullet through this psychopath's head is likely the thing to do here, but there's one problem: this man is without a doubt a cultist. There's no drug in existence that has side effect as extreme as that. Only the Cult can procure such a diabolical substance. That being said, if he kills the cultist now, he'll never learn anything.

To kill or to question, the option has never been so hard before.

The doctor turns back to Markus and notices the gun, completely unconcerned, "Shall we get down to business?"

"Who the hell are you?" Markus says slowly as he tightens his finger around the trigger.

"Me?" the doctor says a little surprised by the question, "Ah, I guess you've forgotten. I'm Doctor Crane."

Something about that name makes the hair on the back of Markus' neck stand up. He swears he's heard that name somewhere, or maybe it's Deja vu?

Suddenly Markus hears a disturbance outside and decides this meeting is going to be cut short. In light of this he approaches the doctor, expecting him to try and keep distance, but instead he simply stays put. So, with nothing stopping him, Markus rears his pistol back and slams it into the side of the doctor's head. Once again, he plans on the sadistic doctor to try and dodge, but he does not such thing. The doctor simply takes the blow with a creepy smile on his face as he falls unconscious.

To follow, Markus cuts the gag off Jaylin's face, frees her arms and legs, and pulls down his mask to reveal his own identity.

Hyperventaling, Jaylin studies his face, "Markus?"

Markus covers her mouth and shakes his head indicating to her to be quiet. Then in a hushed whisper he says, "Stay quiet and follow me. I'm getting you out of here."

Looking around the room at the copious amounts of gore, Jaylin hesitates to respond. It requires Markus to repeat himself before she passively responds with a nod.

Once she's taken care off, Markus pulls out a rope from his bag, cuts off a two foot cord, and proceeds to bind the doctor's arms behind his back. He follows suit by binding the legs as well before picking the surprisingly light man up and plopping him on his shoulder. He takes a step towards the door but then a thought crosses his mind. 

Just to be safe, Markus drops the doctor back on the floor and checks his coat for any remaining unknown substances. He finds two flasks and quickly chucks them across the room, ensuring that no one is afflicted by them. After combing the rest of the doctor's hidden compartments, he decides enough is enough and plops the cultist back on his shoulder.

Markus gestures towards the door with his head as he makes eye contract with the frightened Jaylin.

The two slowly creep out of the storage room and onto the catwalk. He knows he can't go out the way he came in so he'll have to improvise. 

As they arrive at the catwalk stairs, Markus waves to Jaylin and points downward. She gets the signal and they both quietly descend the stairwell. Markus is only a few feet from the stairs when someone below on the ground floor yells out, "Hey, why is that guy being carried?!"

A commotion immediately erupts below. Markus mentally scans his current materials as he thinks of a plan. Right now he has a rope, one more climbing gadget, his pistol, a knife, some miscellaneous items of little use in the current situation, and lastly his cellphone. He planned on this being a stealth operation, but it seems that every operation has its kinks.

Instead of continuing downward, Markus grabs Jaylin's wrist and reverses their direction.

"Why are we going up, we need to get out of here?!" she argues.

"They have too many people on the ground, we'll have to take a different route."

Once they reach the highest floor, they find themselves in a control room. The room isn't used for controls anymore; instead it is another storage room containing various personal belongings. A window overlooking the roof sits against a nearby wall which Markus promptly busts open with his boot. He tosses the unconscious doctor out before jumping out himself and looks to the nearby guard tower, thankfully finding it empty.

"Listen to me," he says back to Jaylin while trying the rope to the frail cultists torso, "I going to lower this guy down to the ground and I want you to drag him over to the wall right there," he points to the exact spot on the brick wall that he came in from, "Someone is over there that will help you get out, just wait until he gets there."

Markus ties the other end of the rope to a pipe on the building and begins lowering the cultist to the ground.

"Wait, what are you going to do?" Jaylin prompts.

"I'll cover your escape," Markus answers with a slight smile as he finishes lowering the cultist, "Now head down."

Immediately afterward, Markus dashes back into the Eagle headquarters.

His job now is to cut the power in the compound otherwise spotting their escape would be too easy. He doesn't know the layout of the compound by heart, but he did a bit of reconnaissance so he should have a decent idea of where their generator will be hiding.

Footsteps murmur from below as he beats them to the fourth floor. He enters into the only room available on that floor and finds himself in a small training room. Then, he waits in the doorway until the first Eagle member spots him as they ascend the stairs. Before they yell, fire a round, or anything else, he then slams the door shut and runs hoping that his efforts will redirect their attention away from the open window a floor below.

By the time the Eagle guards reach the training room, Markus has already found a new window to bust open and climb out. Hanging off the side of a building is normally reserved for the really courageous or really stupid, but Markus feels like neither as he jumps onto a lower level roof. Given that he's now maybe two floors up, he feels confident that he can survive a jump which is only made easier as he finds a white van to drop on. From there, he manages to get around the factory and listen for the soft hum of a generator. 

The distant shouts warning of an intruder makes it a bit harder to listen carefully, but he disregards all that as he uses every bone in his ear to focus. Then, like a light at the end of the tunnel, he hears the low rumbling behind a propped open garage door. He slides underneath to find himself next to a large generator. He almost smiles at his luck, that is, until he spots three men standing next to it with guns in hand. 

He almost pities the guys who, just ten minutes ago, were discussing their poker hands or whatever else. Now, before any of them can understand what's going on, their poker filled minds have been replaced with a much more tangible chunk of lead. The rest of the faction quickly experiences a similarly profound darkness as Markus cuts the power.

It's a real shame. He intended to keep this operation as bloodless as possible, but the situation has unfortunately devolved into something much worse. Besides, he can't trust any of these people if they're dealing with a cultist.

With no moon to speak of on this particular night, Markus finds it child's play to walk right out the front door like a phantom. Then, with a quickly glance at the wall he told Jaylin to rendezvous at, he finds nothing. The subsequent exit leaves the Eagle complex behind well before the lights can be turned back on.

When Markus arrives to the external rendezvous point, he finds Jenks carefully inspecting the cultist and Jaylin simultaneously.

"I promise I'm fine," Jaylin reemphasis as she takes a deep breath, "Just a little overwhelmed."

Jenks glosses over the blood on both her and the cultist, "Some bloody situation you got into."

"That hostage is a cultist," Markus asserts, drawing the attention of the entire room, "Be careful with him."

Jenks' eyes widen a bit, "Serious?"

Markus nods.

Jaylin glances between each of them very carefully, "Um... what is a cultist? Does it have something to do with why I was taken prisoner."

"Maybe," Jenks responds with quickly, "There are no guarantees though."

Markus walks over to the window of the room and inspects the street outside. They're sufficiently far away that he doesn't anticipate any Eagle members following, but that doesn't stop him from check, just to be sure. Thankfully, the road remains dormant both before and after he checks.

"So what are we going to do with him?" Jenks wonders as he continues to inspect the unconscious cultist.

Markus pinches his chin, "We'll need to question him... but where."

He's come up with hundreds of contingency plans for cases when the Cult may try and attack him, but none of those pertain to a prisoner situation. His home is ill equip to hold a highly dangerous captive and he doesn't suspect Jenks has set anything up either, given how recent his arrival was. They could try and perform the interrogation here, but Markus would prefer to do it in a safer location. After all, this rendezvous point was thrown together at last minute, it's by no means a structured base of operations. To be frank, it's literally a random building with roof access and known hive inside—that's it.

"Uh..." Jaylin hesitates as the two men look over to her, "The HoV has infrastructure for prisoners. For saving me, I'm sure I can convince Cleo to help you."

Jenks looks over, "Is that a valid idea?"

Markus mulls over the proposition. The House of Vegas does have a formidable security detail and they most definitely have the infrastructure for an interrogation—he's heard stories. As much as he hates to admit it, that's probably as safer alternative than dragging the cultist all the way to Markus' house on the other side of the city.

"That's probably our best bet," Markus decides begrudgingly.

Jenks stands up, "Okay, you do that."

Giving him a confused look, Markus steps towards his brother in arms, "What do you mean? This is a cultist. You should want to interrogate him more than anyone else."

Jenks shakes his head, "You know how we operate. I trust that you'll get all the information for the both of us. In the meantime, I've got other pressing matters to attend to."

"Like what?" Markus persists.

"We have a cultist in our custody," Jenks explains, "If there is any additional Cult presence—which there likely is—I'll need to be on the lookout for it. While you handle the interrogation, I'll make sure nothing is headed your way nor organizing behind our backs. We have to strike while the irons hot and doubling up on baby sitting is inefficient."

Markus takes a step back and crosses his arms, "Yeah, you make a good point."

Jenks offers his signature smirk, "You always do jump the gun. That's what I'm here for.... now... why don't we secure this cultist a bit better before heading out."
