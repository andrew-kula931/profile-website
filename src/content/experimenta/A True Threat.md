Markus dives to the side and immediately pulls out a pistol from his jacket ready to fight back against whoever broke into this secure facility. He flips over a table and braces himself. If he recalls correctly, he noticed eleven unique guards stationed around the casino, all of which are completely competent with a gun. To top it off, he never heard a single gunshot or shout in the last few minutes. 

Across the room, Cleo jumps over the bar counter and grabbed an Uzi from behind it. She nods at Markus as they wait for the intruders to show themselves.

Suddenly the door bursts open causing Cleo and Markus to pop up from their cover. 

There standing in the doorway is a hooded figure with only a worn hoodie and dark sweatpants on. In his hand is a shiny, inverted karambit-like blade and a M1911 in the other. It's the same man who's crossed his path on multiple occasions already.

Between Markus and Cleo, about ten bullets are sprayed at the attacker with every single one missing as the intruder casually sidesteps the first volley and jumps onto a table to escape the rest. There's tactical bullet dodging that Markus uses quite often and then there's this—they are not the same, not even close.

Markus aims his gun carefully to release two rounds right to where the attacker is landing, but even then the man seemingly predicts the bullet velocities and promptly leans out of the way. It's a level of skill that he can only associate with the most skilled soldiers in Allegiance One. This guy might be as skilled as Grant himself.

The bullets come to an end as Markus and Cleo are forced to reload. The intruder stands menacingly across the room with his hood concealing his face and his body not giving any indication of potential movement. It's like staring at a tangible ghost.

"Who are you?!" Markus shouts from behind cover.

The intruder doesn't respond.

"What do you want?" Markus attempts again only to be returned with that familiar silence.

A bead of sweat slowly drips down Markus' head as he realizes that this is a threat he has not faced in a long time. Whether it be the tense silence or the intruder's intimidating presence, Markus can feel that this is not a battle he is equipped to win. He made that same assessment earlier and it holds true just as strongly now. If he can send a Revenant packing with a mere glance, how will Markus be able to take him down without proper preparation.

Suddenly Darek jumps into the room firing a few bullets at the intruder and quickly diving over the bar counter. By the time his focus returns to the real threat, he finds that they have disappeared leaving only a swinging back door indicating their escape.

"We have to go after him!" Markus says vaulting over the table.

"Wait!" Darek shouts, "Don't chase him!"

Markus slows his pursuit as he hears the roof door slam shut from a few floors above. Instead of following, he turns to Darek hoping for some answers to what happened.

"We can't let him escape," Markus commands about to break conversation in favor of pursuing the threat.

"Don't try it!" the aged man commands as he leans over the counter in a hand on his chest, "I'm sorry but you're not winning that fight alone."

"How'd he break in?" Markus says quickly as he steps over to a nearby window and looks up towards the roof.

Darek huffs as his old bone struggle with the excitement he just partook in. His black jacket damp around his collar as sweat runs down his temple.

"I was passing by and..." Darek looks towards the door, "You need to take a look outside."

Markus looks towards out the nearest door but says nothing.

Darek continues to catch his breath, "I hunt parasites for a living so naturally I've seen impossible things... but this... I've never seen something like this before."

Cleo glances at the door as well, "What's out there."

"Just go look," Darek reiterates, "And I'm sorry."

Upon rushing outside, the three of them are met with a horrific scene. All eleven guards lay on the ground around the perimeter of the House with a pool of blood beneath them. Some are draped over the edge of the walls while others are placed randomly across the street. Each guard wears the mark of a thorough slash through the neck or head—exactly like the Slender from the apartment building. 

From the placement of the bodies, Markus can't reverse engineer any consistent pathway between them. This could only mean that they were picked off without being alert of an attack at all. This monster can do the impossible.

Cleo covers her mouth as she witnesses all of her comrades and friends lie dead on the grounds they once protected. She finds her way to one particular guard and kneels down next to them, closing their cold eyes. Her shoulders shake as she presses her lips together with hurt boiling up behind her eyes. She tries to reach out to the person's hand but she can't quite make it before she recoils and clenches her fist.

"How did this happen?" she asks quietly over her fallen friend. 

"I'm not exactly sure," Darek begins, "As I said before, I was only passing by when I noticed there were no guards stationed on the wall. The front entrance was also cracked open. When I actually did get inside, I saw that man walking towards the building you were in," Darek hesitates for a moment as if contemplating the next part of the story, "That bullet that went off, I fired it. The man dodged it without even looking back. Well, he did look back, but he didn't give me the time of day before continuing on."

Markus crouches down next to a guard with a slashed throat. The cut is so clean that if it wasn't for the blood seeping out, he probably would have mistaken it for a papercut. It must be from that knife that the intruder used. After only seeing it briefly, he couldn't make out much from it, but it's evident that it's no ordinary knife. Either that, or this guy's skills are even more impressive than they already seem.

"What have you learned about him?" Markus asks as he stands up and approaches Darek. Cleo remains next to a dead body whispering something inaudible. "Surely you picked up something from what you saw."

"Unfortunately no," Darek responds sadly, "I don't know anything. You're guess is as good as mine."

Markus nods and places a hand on his chin in thought, "At this point, he's become a force of nature." Markus looks around at all the bodies one last time, "I've met him before but wasn't sure if he was a threat or not. This experience has cleared my mind on that."

Darek nods as he glances around, "I don't typically stray far from parasites, but this threat supersedes anything I've ever seen. If you'd like, I'd be happy to help you hunt him down."

The silent echoes of the dead seem to conflict his opinion, "I don't like the idea of anyone risking it against this guy."

"Well someone has to deal with it," Darek points out, "If one guy can threaten the entire security force of a major faction, we can't overlook this threat. Really, we take care of it now, or someone else will pay the price later."

Markus sighs. He'd prefer to handle the entire conflict alone, but that would be selfish. He has no idea how to track this guy down and if he can't produce results quickly, who knows how many people will die. Working together isn't just the best bet, it's the only logical choice—regardless of how cooperation makes him feel.

"Fine," Markus eventually says, "We'll have to think of something together."

"Very well. I'll get a group together. How about meeting at the Arena tomorrow morning at eight. If you sleep, do it with one eye open," Darek asserts as he takes his exit through the open, unguarded door.

Markus returns to Cleo's side who is kneeling on the ground, silently staring at her fallen friend. He attempts to place a hand on her shoulder in consideration but receives a comfort hug instead. He hesitates to hug back but decides that it's important to be comforting at a time like this. 

"Jen and I were tennis partners back before the Fall. We still had hopes of playing again…" Cleo sniffles, "We were laughing together just the other… day…" she begins to cry. Her sobs go straight into Markus' shoulder as she stands there… mourning people who didn't deserve to die.

"It's okay," Markus says slowly, "I'm not going to overlook this threat any longer."

_Carter rubbed his recently dislocated shoulder as he trudged over to his similarly roughed up comrades. Nox and Jenks laughed as Carter cursed under his breath accepting a cup of pity coffee from Wilbur. Each soldier was equally hurting in various parts of the body. None of them were complaining about their condition because they all deserved it. They all shared the struggle and shared the same source of defeat—Captain Grant._

_A very common thing for Allegiance One to do during downtime was spar. They all accepted that it was part of the process to avoid getting rusty, but none of them appreciated having to go head to head with an inhumanly skilled opponent. Despite sparring the man for months, none of them had ever lasted more than thirty seconds unless Grant was trying to teach them something. It was a futile process but having enough audacity to point that out would be tantamount to asking for death. They simply accepted their beatings and moved on. After all, none of them could disagree with the personal growth they've noticed in their abilities due to their training. Heck, Markus felt that hand-to-hand combat was quickly becoming one of his top strengths._

_Markus now stood before the colossal threat of a squad leader._

_"Are you ready?" Grant asked with his arms crossed in a relaxed position._

_Markus nodded bracing himself for what was to come._

_Grant dashed forward with an impossibly fast burst of speed. His fist chambered to his ribs for an initial powerful swing. Markus prepared to parry it only to forget that Grant never telegraphed his moves. The true strike was actually a leg sweep that knocked Markus off his feet. Luckily, he was able to recover by using his hands to flip himself back to his feet but Grant was already on top of him. Three consecutive punches all hammer into Markus arm as he narrowly absorbs the blow with something other than his ribs._

_Then, in a moment of counter strike, Markus stepped forward and threw his knee right into Grant's side. To his amazement, his blow actually lands causing Grant to exhale swiftly and intentionally. That was his undoing, however, as Grant locks down on the poorly placed knee before he could pull away. The captain then swung Markus around using his locked knee and threw him a few yards into the sandy ground. Markus pushed himself up as quickly as he could, bracing for another strike which turned out to be a mule of a kick right to his face. He leaned backwards with the kicks momentum to lessen the blow but it still put him solidly in the dirt. A small trickle of blood soon leaked out of his nose._

_"That's enough for now," Grant asserted as he brushed a bead of sweat from his brow. Markus wished he could take credit for that visible sign of effort, but the four guys who spared before him and the hot sun told him that he wasn't the sole cause._

_Markus stood up slowly and trudged over to the other squad members mimicking Carter exactly._

_"You did it man!" Carter practically jumped on Markus shoving a cup of coffee into his chest, "You landed a blow."_

_"Only for me to lose right after," Markus commented self-deprecatingly._

_Jenks rolled his eyes in the background, "You need to give yourself some more credit. I would have gone down after the first three punches."_

_Grant was quick to join the conversation, grabbing a victory coffee on his own accord, "You all are getting better."_

_"But we're still not even close," Markus grumbled, still a bit annoyed by his utter defeat. He was just a fifteen year old teenager after all, mood swings were common for him._

_Grant took a good swig of his drink and let out a deep breath following, "You don't have to be the best to win. If all of you teamed up on me right now, I think you'd have a decently high chance of winning."_

_Markus clenched his fist. He's not strong enough yet, he knows that, but something about teaming up to win felt shallow._

_"I may be more skilled than the rest of you," Grant continued, "But we are all better together. There are enemies out there that I can't take on alone, but with all of us together, we can win. Even if the odds are stacked against us."_

_"So the way I hear it," Nox began with an overconfident undertone, "If we beat you right now, together, you'll stop making us spare."_

_Grant shook his head not humoring the man with a response._

_"Sounds good to me," Jenks laughed as he rolled up his sleeve._

_Without further prompting, the entire Allegiance One started to converge on Grant with a newly formed fire in their eyes. If only they knew that Grant was being generous with them when he said that they could win._

"Have you found anything about this guy?" Markus inquires as he and Jenks walk up the stairs to the stadium.

Jenks glances down to a screen on his watch before looking back up and opening the door for Markus, "Not a thing. I saw him on a few security cameras but nothing out of the ordinary happened. He seems to just be walking around doing surveillance. That's it."

Inside the arena the squad has already assembled. Sitting down in the first row of stands are three people. Darek, Brian, and a woman Markus has never seen before. Darek notices them immediately as they descend the stairs to meet the group.

"I'm glad you brought Braedon with you," Darek calls out.

"You already know each other?" Markus whispers to Jenks.

"He had quite a presence during some of my surveillance," Jenks explains, "So naturally I got curious and reached out to him."

They arrive at the arena floor and Brian offers them a nod. Darek then takes the lead as he makes his quick, but effective introductions.

"You all know Markus—or at least know of him. Similarly, Brian is a pretty popular guy too, but this man," he waves to Jenks, "Is Braedon Jenkenson, an expert special ops operative and former cybersecurity analyst." Jenks waves his hands humbly as he shrugs nonchalantly. "Anyway, the woman sitting over here is Amber Sloan. She's my apprentice. I can vouch for her and say that she'll be a strong asset to our mission." 

Amber waves without saying anything or even looking happy to any extent. 

Darek looks around the room stroking his gray beard like it were a status symbol, "You all know me, so let's not waste any more time."

Markus recalls that Darek was the head of recruitment and influential member of the the Death Order. Although he and Amber had never met, Markus realizes that he's seen her before but can't remember if it was at some event or just in passing in the Market. 

Amber is a composed woman in her mid-thirties with a mean look in her eyes. Down her chest is a bandolier with a modified sheath to hold a knife in addition to the shells. She rests her elbows on her knees as she gives Markus a careful look. 

Darek nods to Jenks. The meaning of said motion is immediately understood.

Jenks pulls out a tablet turning it on to a map of the city. On the map is a red line running about the city without ever intersecting itself. Almost every point of interest was covered by the line except for a very select few. The line ends just past the House of Vegas.

"When Darek reached out to me, I went ahead and compiled all the security footage I could acquire. This is a subsequent trail the target. He seems to be doing surveillance on the whole city which means that his next point of attack will most likely be here," he points to a region in the downtown area - not so far from the Radio Tower. "From what I could gather, and from what Darek told me, this guy treats combat like an afterthought. I believe our best bet is not actually to fight him, but draw a bigger threat to him." Jenks zooms in on a singular block of the city, "There's a large supermarket right here chocked full of parasites of all horrid varieties. If we can get a horde to run right into the enemy, at the very least we'll tire him out and give us a chance to strike him down. I shouldn't have to say this, but this is a shoot to kill operation. We can't risk nonfatal shoots for the sake of interrogation. It's kill or be killed. Any questions?"

Markus glances over at Darek who has a hand on his chin analyzing the map. His face doesn't give any sign of confusion so he takes a deep breath and decides that this plan will suffice.

"I have a suggestion," Amber says out of the blue. "We should use explosives as a failsafe."

"Go on," Darek prompts.

"In the case that this guy escapes the horde, we should use weapons that can't be dodged. Darek informed me that this guy can dodge bullets like he knows they're always coming, but no one can dodge a frag. Maybe something bigger if push comes to shove."

Darek nods, "I like that, did you have something in mind."

"Let me handle that if the need arises," she says swiftly, "We just need to determine where".

"Alright then," Darek comments before addressing Jenks, "Where do you think we can set up the trap?"

Jenks places his hands on his hips and looks up at the stadium ceiling in thought, "It's hard to say. I suspected he'll be headed to the center of town—the Retainers faction I think it's called. That being said, we still need to pick a spot that is both close to the supermarket and in the path of the faction," his eyes widen as he swipes over on his tablet to find a destination, "This intersection seems pretty centralized, I think there's a good chance he'll cross through here."

Brian glances at the map with a keen eye, "That's 12th and L. Most people cross through there."

"Right," Jenks comments.

"Wouldn't that put bystanders in the line of fire?" Amber wonders.

Darek steps forward with his arms crossed, "I can ensure it's clear. The Death Order has procedures for these sorts of things. We can't keep it closed forever, but I can reroute traffic for a few hours or so."

"Alright then," Markus concludes looking around the room, "Darek reroutes civilian traffic. Amber handles planting bombs in the kill zone. Jenks, you get eyes on the target. Brian will lead the crowd of parasites towards the kill zone. Lastly, if any of those plans fail, I'll take the guy out myself."

He looks around for any rebuttals, but everyone seems quite on board with the plan. Whether it be necessity of the task or the soundness of the plan, this random bunch seems to be far more cohesive than Markus originally predicted. So people may have called that meeting too brief, but he knows better than most that highly skilled individuals don't need hand holding. They have their roles, any variables are up to them to be handled. This is one of many reasons Allegiance One worked so well—and they just so happen to have to Allegiance One members in the group.

"Just like old times," Jenks says, nudging Markus as they sit on the curb of a convenience store. Amber waits inside the store glaring at their casual demeanors before battle. Jenks eventually gets tired of the glares and waves his watch at her, "I'm watching surveillance as we speak. I'll know when the target makes his approach."

"No kidding," Markus agrees.

"You think?!" Jenks expression brightens as he smiles widely to his brother in arms.

Markus shakes his head, "Wilbur used to give you the same look when you went along with Carter's shenanigans."

Jenks whacks the back of Markus' head which sends a shock of pain through his skull but not enough for him to fight back. In all honesty, he kind of deserved it.

"I for one was always on good terms with Wilbur. Carter was the problem."

"I never said you were," Markus says semi-jokingly.

"You have a funny way of showing it," Jenks playfully pouts.

"For being battle hardened soldiers, you two seem really childish," Amber says as she leans against the doorway of the convenience store acting subtly superior.

"Thanks mom," Jenks says sarcastically, "I'll take this more seriously now."

Amber rolls her eyes, "To each their own."

"Yup, that's Wilbur all right," Markus says, causing both men on the curb to break out laughing. It's a good laugh, a delightful laugh, a necessary laugh as Jenks' watch suddenly beeps.

All three of them snap to attention as Jenks clicks the beeping away and stands up. After that, they all make their way up the ladder in the back of the store to a roof next door and await the action to go down.

One minute passes, then two, and then three. Only after the third does a mysterious hooded figure make their way down the empty street and into the intersection. All three of them make final preparations on their weapons and watch intently on Jenks' screen as the hostile foe walks right in front of the convenience store and stops. 

The hooded man looks up towards the roof their currently located, but does not speak. With the help of Jenks' camera, Markus is certain none of them are peaking over the edge, but it still leaves him unsettled. The enemy's face remains concealed as he promptly turns and walks into the store directly below them. Not a single one of them breaths as they can hear the audible footsteps below browsing through empty shelves and old trinkets.

Suddenly a dull roar approaches from down the street. Jenks changes the channel to see a flood of parasites mutated in a variety of ways from minor facial changes to entirely crude entities that look nothing like the former human. Said horde numbers around fifty with every creature charging down the street. Perhaps they were chasing Brian at one point, but now they seem to be stampeding towards nothing as they approach the position of the convenience store. The stampede passes by for the most part, which is more or less expected, but still annoying. If only the man hadn't arrived so soon, maybe the horde would have interacted with him. Thankfully, however, the camera picks up a few parasites that make their way into the store just to search around. Unfortunately there are no cameras in the building so they all wait patiently for something to happen. A gunshot, a scream, anything, but nothing happens. Soon the parasites slowly file out of the store and rejoin the stampede down the road. 

Jenks and Markus make eye contact before nodding to Amber. The devious woman smiles as she crouches down next to a board of almost a dozen switches. She looks to the convenient store and then to the board. Finally, with one last look she flicks one of them.

Immediately following the flick of the switch, an explosion completely engulfing the convenience store in flames. Markus watches in awe as the bright white and yellow flames covered in dark smoke erupt from the simple building and turn it into a nightmare from the war. Jenks beside him tells the same story with the dumbfounded expression on his face. 

Clearly Amber was the right person to call when it comes to assassinating people. To think she strapped half the buildings on the block with these explosives. And here Markus thought he was the only person with any C4 left. Even if that is still true, Amber proves that concept fundamentally flawed.

"We have to make sure he didn't survive," Amber says as she rushes down the nearby ladder and towards the inferno.

Markus and Jenks follow behind with them each searching the surroundings for any sign that this guy survived. He may be incredibly skilled but none of them saw him leave the building which can only imply that he was still in there. 

For a moment, Markus gets his hopes up. Maybe this ultra-skilled warrior will no longer be a threat to anyone. All those people he killed in the HoV will finally be laid to rest. 

It's a shame they were all naive.

In the blink of an eye, Amber receives a precisely placed bullet through the side of her head. 

By the time any of them could draw their weapons, the culprit walks out of the building adjacent to the convenience store pointing his gun at them all. Without further hesitation, Markus lifts his AR but a bullet pierces through his barrel causing the front of his gun to burst open shooting debris over his arm. Luckily his armor protects him from most of the debris, but a few cuts suddenly form on his face as he draws his pistol. 

That's when a sniper round from over a block away passes right behind the attacker's head with him only needing to lean a slight bit forward to dodge it. Instead of hitting its target, the sniper round slams into the concrete below throwing small little rocks everywhere. 

Jenks draws his own gun and fires multiple bullets with each one somehow missing the body of the man who simply shifts to and fro to avoid the volley of projectiles. The final round in Jenks' clip is deflected by the man lifting his knife and essentially ricocheting it past his face. The man then raises his gun and fires off into the distance—seemingly at random. 

"Wait!" Jenks yells at Markus, "Stop firing."

Markus glances over at his friend with confusion but quickly understands what he's intending, so he maintains his sights on the man but holds off on the trigger for now. 

"You're not here to kill us, are you?" Jenks asks as he stares intently at the hostile foe before him. Behind his back he messes with a small gadget in his back pocket, "Amber maybe, but you could have killed us both by now. Yet you haven't."

The kind of silence that ensues is one so nerve racking it would cause even the most Stoic to tremble. The kind of silence that exists between two nations on the verge of nuclear war—the kind of silence that lasts in nightmares. No one moves, no one thinks, no one breathes as the hooded figure simply keeps his head down concealing most of his face. Only the short whiskers on the man's chin can be seen beyond his long hood.

Then as if time had stood still for long enough, the man reaches into his worn hoodie pocket and pulls out a piece of paper. He drops it on the ground as he spins on his heels and casually walks off towards the alleyway.

Jenks promptly pulls the gadget out of his pocket and chucks it at the man. At the same time, Markus fires off a volley or rounds at him, fully intending for each to connect. In response, the man shoots the gadget midair causing a small explosion as he dives into the alleyway. 

Markus immediately presses forward into a pursuit, disregarding the small bits of shrapnel that hit him from the explosion. He rounds the corner and spots the man parkouring over a burning dumpster. 

Chasing after him, he finds it very obvious that the man is exceptionally fast and agile. He manages to tic-tac-toe between walls, speed vault any obstacle, and land every precision without even looking down. Markus soon finds it quite difficult to keep pace with the man having no intention of slowing down.

After a short, fast paced pursuit, Markus slows to catch his breath as the man jumps down a flight of stairs and disappears around a corner. Markus is left standing there truly contemplating how he's going to bring that guy down.

"Damn," Markus says under his breath as he glances back at his squad slowly catching up.

"That guy's no joke," Jenks comments, slowing down next to him, "He makes all of us look like rookies."

Markus nods as he stands up straight.

"I don't understand though," Jenks says, "The probability that he moves out of the way perfectly is nearly zero. I can't fathom the likelihood that he lucked out that way. Even if we were rookies who can't aim it still is near impossible to dodge all the bullets."

"What about that paper," Markus wonders, "The one he dropped before running off."

"Oh, that one," Jenks thinks as he pulls that small slip of paper out of his pocket and hands it to Markus.

Markus inspects the inconspicuous paper, but glances back at his friend first, "Do you know who that sniper was? Was it Brian?"

"I'll check," Jenks says as he pulls out a walkie-talkie and turns it on, "Brian come in. Are you alright."

Only silence is returned.

"Agh, sorry. The fucker shot me from all the way down there. I'll live, but that's one hell of a shot," Brian's voice sends out a wave of relief both of them.

Not wasting any more time, Markus begins unfolding the paper in hand. Jenks leans over as the words upon the page come into view. Written in rough handwriting is a single line.

"Roof of the tallest building at dusk. Markus - come alone."

Markus looks up from the page at his friend. Neither of them even have to say a word to come up with the same conclusion: He doesn't have any choice. He's going to meet this man and with any luck, this problem will be resolved one away or another.
