_"On your six!" Carter yells as Markus flips around and takes a cultist down with a clean shot through the head._

_"Some quick and stealthy mission we've got ourselves in now, right?" Wilbur calls out sarcastically as he wall-banged a cultist a few meters away._

_The squad had failed their stealth attempt and were now surrounded on all sides. The team's execution was flawless as always, the abnormality that gave rise to their current predicament was that their information was off to begin with. Whoever manned the UAV that scouted this base should be fired as there were not a "manageable" amount of cultists here. There were three times as many enemy combatants than the reports initially described._

_"What's the call Grant?!" Jenks yells over gunfire, his back to the bullet filled body of a jeep._

_Grant, who was cool the entire mission so far, looks around the battlefield, a single bead of sweat running down his face. On his face isn't a hint of doubt, yet his response is slower than usual, "We need to make a push to the aircraft hangar 300 meters to the We—"._

_"Agh!" Nox groans cutting Grant off and falling to one knee and pressing his hand against his shoulder, "The fucker hit me. Captain, are you sure I can't use my RPG?"_

_"No! We got new intel that they have a bomber here that we need to take down at all costs. The scientist comes second, otherwise that bomber is gonna kill thousands including our own forces."_

_Wilbur snickered, "And here I thought the Cult came before civilians. Good to know we still have a heart."_

_The squad was on edge even without the fact that fifty cultists were actively shooting at them with who knows how many on their way. Markus could tell that Grant was at a loss for words, the situation was dire and he couldn't show weakness even if they were truly and absolutely screwed. There wasn't a good option here and everyone knew something had to happen or this would be their last mission._

_"Damn it! We can't make it across with all this fire, what are we supposed to do, they're getting closer with every second." Jenks yells._

_"Give me a second, I'm coming up with a new plan…" Grant scanned the battlefield taking out three cultists with a single peak before being forced back behind cover by a barrage of aggressive fire; the cultists were not very frugal with their bullet usage._

_Markus had never been in this particular situation, but with every bullet shot from the cultists his hatred and anger grew; the memories of what they did to him now boiling to the surface. His knuckles grew white by his grip on his gun as he called out one simple command to his comrades, "I'll give you an opening, make it count."_

_Grant and Carter tried to protest and stop him but it was too late, Markus had already vaulted over the box giving them cover and let his bullets fly._

_While his squad was making the tactical decision to regroup, Markus had already analyzed the scene. He was actually surprised that Grant hadn't seen it first, or maybe he did spot it but willingly disregarded it due to the immense danger._

_Regardless, Markus shot the head of the closest target immediately after vaulting; there wasn't any time to wait for them to fall, they had to die without a chance to react. A few bullets scraped by him as he strafed as fast as he could muster. He shifted over and shot a barrel of propane right next to a group of cultists causing them all to dive out of the way. Gas barrels don't explode when shot so it just made them look silly on the ground and not able to fire back. Markus took another target down and ducked behind another cultist jeep just in time to dodge incoming fire of the now aware cultists._

_He glanced down and noticed blood dripping out of various spots on his body as he coughed up blood. The pain rose like an overdue geyser, but Markus used a pain suppression technique to prevent his thoughts from losing focus. As much as he detested the Cult, the combat techniques they taught him were extremely high quality which only gave him more satisfaction to use it against them. As the pain slowly subsided from his mind, he took note of the location and was thankful to know that none of the damaged regions were vital, or at least won't make him pass out within the next two minutes._

_He waited a moment for a barrage of bullets to pass and then he peaked out the other side of the jeep to take down another cultist._

 _By this point Markus could hear commotion behind him, clearly a sign that the rest of the squad was moving out. He kept moving forward shooting cultists down left and right. Every now and then he would get a new bullet skimming him here or there but never landing anywhere that would stop his barbaric charge. His skills at dodging bullets were second to none, but that didn't change the fact that he had dozens of guns all firing at him. After a few minutes of shooting, repositioning and then shooting again, he realized that the cultist forces were thinned. His squad had moved out and the others went after them. It was now his job to get the squad out of there. _

_He fought the remaining cultists until their numbers dropped low enough that they pulled back. They ran or drove away but left behind a vehicle or two, just enough for Markus to use. He hopped into a jeep and checked for the key, there was no keyhole to be found. The good news was that the cultists were in such a rush to get out of the jeep that they hadn't turned it off. He rode that bad boy all the way to the hanger where he heard even more gunshots._

_In the hangar the cultists had made another push against the squad and now had them held down in an office of the hangar. Markus used his flank advantage to catch the aggressing cultists off guard. Driving the jeep right into the cultists forces, he jumped out at the last minute. Upon landing and getting to his feet he opened fire at the 30 some cultists now. The rest of Allegiance One joined in. The fight was over in in a flash. After the last shot was fired, his adrenaline ended and his pain suppression technique wasn't enough to stop the flood of excruciating pain. In his fury he didn't feel any of the five new bullet wounds in his body. He was lucky none had hit a vital spot killing him instantly._

_Carter was the first to reach Markus as he lay on the ground, "You suicidal maniac."

_The rest of the squad caught up and Nox was the first to call out, "The plane's taking off! We have to shoot it down now!"_

_Grant made a signal and everyone ran off with Carter left to help Markus._

_Carter glanced over the extent of Markus' injuries; his face didn't look hopeful. "You're gonna be alright kid, just hang in there," he looked around nervously._

_"Quit yappin and help me up, I need to see the plane go down," Markus choked out, his injuries were the last thing on his mind._

_Carter started to protest but then shook his head while assisting Markus up and holding his arm around his neck. The two soldiers made their way to the entrance of the hangar and watched the runway carefully. The bomber plane lifted off but neither of them were worried, they had Nox shooting with Wilber spotting him. The next thing they saw was the massive bomber getting hit by a tiny little projectile._

_The heavily plated plane quickly erupted into a blinding mix of orange, yellow, and white. Markus and Carter used their free hands to shield their vision as the firework lit up the sky with a fiery victory cry._

_It was the second largest explosion that Allegiance One ever produced. It was also the last thing Markus saw in that mission before passing out._

Part of himself misses that deranged, suicidal version of himself—a time when taking down the Cult meant more than living another day. In a way, he's felt like life has drifted a bit since then.

_He awoke again in a dead field, dried shrubs scarred the cracking ground sparsely. A Cult facility stood maybe a kilometer or two in front of him. He realized that his body was severely wounded, and he immediately collapsed to the ground. The facility laid quiet and dark until suddenly, the night became day._

Markus comes to staring up at the ceiling of an unfamiliar building, a light hangs from it with a stationary fan. 

His heads spins with the mother of all migraines which only partially subsides as he takes a deep breath. The muscles in his arms and back feels so tight he doesn't dare move them at first. This hesitation leads him to clench his fists, finding the simple action evokes a peculiar sensation throughout his body. He wouldn't describe it as something moving inside of him, but rather his own internal systems adjusting slightly without his permission. To his surprise, it doesn't hurt, but it doesn't feel right. The sensation disappears quickly leaving his soreness as the solitary stimulus.

He surveys his surroundings, landing his eyes onto a girl laying next to him in bed. Who else could it be other than Cleo. Her head rests on the other end of his pillow with her eyes tranquilly closed. An IV stand positions itself next to the bed, but oddly enough it isn't stuck into his arm. The window across from the bed shines a warm, soothing light through it. If this were a movie it would be that cliche perfect scene. Bright, comfortable lighting, a soft bed, a beautiful girl sleeping next to him. Now, if only he didn't feel so disgusting and aweful.

He closes his eyes to rest a bit more, but is reawakened by the opening of the door. The new face is none other than Misty.

"Oh, god you're still alive," she exclaims with a sigh of relief, only then realizing that Cleo is here too, "Am I interrupting something?"

Markus rolls his eyes as Cleo finally breaks her own sleeping spell and sits up.

"Misty, what day is it?" Markus rubs a hand through his hair thankful that it isn't crusty with dried blood.

"The seventh, from what I know, you've only been here about twelve hours."

"So it seems that fight is famous enough for you to have heard about it already," Markus chuckles, trying his best not to groan and reveal his pain.

"Oh please," Misty scoffs, "That's really not saying much for me."

"So what exactly happened after I passed out?" Markus wonders.

Cleo, now aware of what is going on, joins in, "I can answer that. After you drew the Colossal away, David and his crew chased after you with Reign's artillery. By the time they reached that little settlement they saw you lying on the ground with piles of corpses all around," Cleo's tone falters, "Everything was dead. When they went to see if you were okay, you had deep wounds all over your body… and…" she struggles to get the last part out, "you weren't breathing."

"Well clearly that's not the case anymore," Misty butts in somehow immune to the heavy subject. Markus honestly appreciates it as he's not one to get all emotional, especially since he's still here.

Cleo glares at Misty and continues, "We managed to resuscitate you and bring your pulse back. After that, we brought you here for treatment. They said your odds of survival weren't good, but by the time we arrived, some of the lacerations had closed on their… You've been sleeping here ever since then."

He can still feel the scraps, cuts, and former lacerations that litter his body, but for some reason, none of them feel excessively abrasive. Unfortunately, this isn't new to him. Brushing up with death... well... he's walked out of it more times than he probably should have. It's a trait he can only tie to the Cult itself.

"I see," Markus finally lets out.

Cleo gives him a sympathetic look as Misty crosses her arms with a slight smirk.

Snapping him back to reality, Misty opens her know-it-all mouth and says, "I'm sure you two must be trying to catch up, but I was just stopping by to tell you that I found a heck of a lot of treasure in the museum. It's all thanks to you so I want to… give you a small share of the profit." She hands him an envelope and turns to leave, "Also, there's a note in there from somebody—not sure who. Caught a message on the new Beacon and they were adamant that you needed to see it. Not sure what it means, but you know where to find me if you have any questions."

The door closes leaving Markus and Cleo alone together. He shifts around the bed so his feet hang off the side. Cleo quickly follows to meet him there.

"Are you sure you're alright?" she insists.

Markus reaches out his, rather sore, arms for a quick stretch before standing up. His body screams at him for moving, but he brushes it off as a trifling concern. This is far from the first time he's woke up completely covered in injures—it's honestly just another day for him.

A smile flashes across Cleo's face briefly, "So the price for a night with you was just near death I guess."

Markus glances back at her with a careful look, "You didn't."

Cleo laughs, "I'm only kidding, I just got here a few hours ago and you were asleep that whole time. I hope you realize that the whole lustful thing is just a joke."

Markus sighs and brushes her comments off, "Where's my stuff?"

"It should be in the equipment room and your bike is out front," Cleo explains.

Glancing at the window, Markus takes a deep breath, "Thanks... for not being overly dramatic. I'm not sure I would have the patience for something like that right now."

Cleo smiles, "Oh course. I'm sure even you have limits to my damsel routine."

Markus turns and walks out of the room noticing that he's in a robe instead of his usual attire. Cleo follows him out.

After gathering his belongings again he makes his way out of the building which just so happened to be an outpost of the Nurse faction stationed nearby the battle scene. Outside Elizabeth talks to a group of people, most of which were recognizable from the meeting the day before. 

The crowd goes silent when he walks out of the building. He couldn't quite tell whether they were in awe of a hero or angry that he single handedly destroyed a whole settlement. To be honest, he wouldn't fault them for it. He wishes he felt more guilty for what he did, but simply put, grieving is not a privilege one gets during wartime. He learned that lesson good and well in the Middle East, he doesn't have to repeat that process now.

"What's going on here?" Markus leads with.

Elizabeth holds up a hand to prevent any of the other people from talking, "Most people don't know what to think. The meeting the other day was to discuss two things, the beacon set up, and the food crisis. We were about to lose the beacon and you saved it. And don't worry about the settlement, because of our newly found communication methods with the beacon, we got enough nearby support to help that group get back on their feet, I'll bet in a different location." She pauses for a moment looking around at all the intrigued faces searching for an answer she hasn't prompted for yet, "The other matter is how you are still alive. That was a Colossal class parasite that you took down single handedly AND we all thought you died fighting the hoard of Infecteds."

A hush falls over the crowd, all waiting eagerly to hear about what happened. Markus has to make a choice, either lie about what happened or tell the truth and make things worse. Volker had it out for him and that was about the worst death sentence you could have. Though most people don't even know who Volker is. The government never told anyone that the parasite was all one single organism; the populous really wasn't told much about the parasites at all.

This is not to forget about that strange hooded figure. Whoever he is, he could end up being a fantastic ally or a very dangerous adversary. Though, dealing with him will be a completely separate matter.

"I killed the Collosal with a bit of a fire trick and some explosives I had on hand, but then when the dust cleared it became evident that there was a nearby hive. All those Infecteds came to the surface. I tried to run, but they came from multiple directions and cut me off from getting to my bike."

All of this was true, of course, but it left Volker out completely. As it stands, he hopes to keep it that way.

Elizabeth follows up, "That doesn't explain how you survived after we found you practically dead."

To be rightly honest, he didn't quite know himself. Then again, the things the Cult did to him when he was young isn't something anyone probably knows. Regardless, he just has a knack for healing quickly, it's as simple as that. He has no plan on ever testing a lethal blow.

"I don't know… After I passed out I couldn't tell you what happened."

"Could it be that he's been infected?!" Brian steps up and claims. Markus hadn't realized it before, but the leader of the Raven Faction just so happened to be part of the group discussing things with Elizabeth. He can't honestly say he's surprised given that Brian and his followers are capable of securing areas in a flash.

"If he were infected he would have turned by now," Cleo argues.

Markus rolls one his shoulders, feeling his body arguing about whether they agreed or disagreed with the sentiment at hand. His injures in that fight were directly inflicted by parasites, so an infection is completely possible... that being said, parasitic infections have been on the decline ever since the Fall. If he really did turn, he'd be the first one in the last three months to turn. 

The cause for the decline is mostly due to parasites being largely territorial, that is, they tend not to wander into new areas. Furthermore, if someone is unfortunate enough to get injured by a parasite, they typically don't make it out alive anyway.

"I've seen people regrow themselves when they get infected and don't fully turn for 24 hours," Brian continues getting increasingly suspicious.

"That's a cold lie and you know it!" Cleo defends.

Brian walks up right into Markus' face glaring at him as if to try and intimidate. "So tell me, why don't ya, are you infected?"

His truthful answer: he's not sure. The logical answer in this situation... well, that one is a bit harder to decide on. Either way, he's not one to sit around and hope people take pity on him.

Markus pulls out a knife from his belt and holds the handle out to Brian. "If you think I'm gonna turn, then kill me."

Everyone around remains motionless, not a single murmur leaks from any onlookers. Brian, completely perplexed by the offer, stares with confusion for a solid ten seconds before answering, "Go ahead and die on your own time. I may have come off as confrontational, but I'm just trying to look out for those less fortunate than you," he marches off with vigor but Markus doesn't quite think he's storming off. Brian strikes him as someone more level-headed than that.

"Are we good now?" Markus asks motioning the knife to anyone else who would want to challenge him.

"You're not hiding something, are you?" Elizabeth asks, stepping right in front of Markus, "Look me in the eyes and tell me you're not hiding something important from us." She keeps her gaze firmly on Markus' own eyes. He'll be the first one to admit that she has a powerful presence, but his secrets are not going to come out so easily.

"We both know I have secrets I'll never tell, but I give you my word that I don't know what happened after I passed out."

"Anyone object to his statement?!" Elizabeth looks around the small crowd of people who at this point had grown to almost twice the initial size. 

The crowd remains silent. Eyes narrow in displeasure with his response but no one dares speak up, mostly because a much larger percentage of the crowd simply had no idea what to think.

After a minute with no one answering up, Elizabeth changes the subject. "Now, with the beacon up we need to rally people together to gain as much information about the new fungus as possible, we don't have much time before panic breaks out."

"What exactly is this beacon?" Markus questions.

Elizabeth gives him a confused sideways glance, "You haven't been told?"

Markus shakes his head.

"It's essentially a network tower. Like the ones phone companies used before the Fall. With it we can make calls and contacts to everyone in the city without the need for special equipment."

Markus nods, "I see now why it's so important, but didn't Reign already have one. You know, with the Radio Tower."

"That's true," Elizabeth confirms, "But the one we had before was limited in the sense that it could only send messages and not receive them. The beacon basically gives you full fledged cell service, you can contact anyone you want without the intervention of others."

Markus mentally kicks himself for being so skeptical of Reign all these years. Here he was thinking that he had to have good cybersecurity practices when Reign couldn't even receive a message from him in the first place.

"I've got work to do so I'll talk to you later," Elizabeth nods to him and Cleo before joining a group of people patiently waiting for her.

"What are you gonna do now?" Cleo wonders as she steps uncomfortably close to him.

"I'm getting out of here," Markus replies boldly and assertively.

Markus makes his way around the outpost until he finds the garage and is relieved to find his motorcycle waiting for him there. Cleo, who had followed him the whole way, stands watching Markus prepare to leave.

"Don't you have a faction to lead?" Markus asks, taking a seat on his bike.

"I have a hierarchy in place for when I'm absent, I don't live and breathe leadership," Cleo rolls her eyes as she places her hands against her hips pushing her hips to one side with a bit of attitude. "A girl can do other things once in a while."

"Guess that makes sense," Markus says dryly, "I'll see you around."

Markus starts the ignition.

"I just want you to know that the warehouse you opened had some military supplies that will come in handy in the future," Cleo shouts over the starting engine.

"Glad I could help."

"You're welcome to stop by the House of Vegas anytime, first round's on me," she says almost desperately.

Markus takes a deep breath and nods; the aching in his body reminding him that he needs more rest. He then begins his journey back home. 

It's blatantly clear that Cleo is obsessed with him for some reason but there's two factors to why a relationship will never work between them. One of those problems is the implications of his goal and the threats that revolve around it. The second is something a little harder to explain... it's better not to dwell on it.

_"That 40-37 finish was insane!" a random kid with long hair and a bit too much energy cheered as Caden and Markus enter into the house party._

_The party that Caden somehow convinced him to attend is nothing short of… lively. The football game that concluded just two hours prior was narrowly won by the home team. Their team had been down by three touchdowns for the entirety of the game, but then they made a fourth quarter comeback for the win. Every high schooler at this party had come directly here from the game and their energy hadn't lowered since the game winning touchdown was scored. The location of said party was none other than a rich kid's house with ample yard space and a pool in the back._

_Caden led Markus through the initial crowd of chatty teens by the front door and into the backyard with the pool. A few teens were already having the time of their lives in the chlorinated waters while others were content with conversation around the edge of the pool._

_"So anyway," Caden continued as they settled into a position next to a window overlooking the living room, "He told me not to worry about it and then, Bang! Our entire lab was kaputt."_

_Markus haphazardly chuckled as he glanced about the party, not quite comfortable with such a large crowd. He's never been one to socialize but parties with teens were the worst; everyone was loosey-goosey and impossible to predict._

_After a few moments a group of three guys meanders over to Caden and Markus. All three of them presented themselves as fellow athletes. Markus even recognized one of them as the star quarterback of the team._

_"Yo Caden!" one of them exclaims patting the fullback on his shoulder, "You gotta come check it out, Suzan and Doug are totally having a dramatic break up right now in the kitchen."_

_"Why would I want to watch that?" Caden asserts rather level-headedly._

_One of the other guys pipped in with, "No seriously man, Suzan is definitely trying to make it public for popularity. It's so funny you gotta check it out."_

_Caden shrugged his shoulders in futility, "Alright, let's go see," he turned back to Markus, "You coming too?"_

_Markus shook his head, "Nah, I could care less about all that drama, I'll just stick around here until you get done."_

_Caden nodded to confirm before lightly punching him on the shoulder, "Just make sure to give that wall some breathing room, you don't own it."_

_"Alright," Markus said as Caden took his leave with his other friends._

_From there, Markus is left alone with his hands in his pockets and a house overflowing with teenagers. He fits into the fold most certainly, but he just doesn't feel like he belongs. He's not so adolescent to believe that he doesn't belong anywhere, but this location is far from his comfort zone and one girl seemed to notice fairly quickly._

_After about two minutes of Markus standing alone watching through the window at two guys seeing who could chug soda faster, a curious girl approached him. Her disposition reminded him of his sister, though the complete opposite. His sister was loud, obnoxious, and attention seeking. This girl, however, had a quiet personality without even speaking as she walked with her arms crossed and her hair pulled pack into an inconspicuous hairstyle; he wouldn't know what it's called even if he tried. She glanced around looking for anyone that Markus might be waiting for before speaking._

_"Are your friends also watching the break up in the kitchen?"_

_Markus nodded slightly as she shrugged his shoulders, "I can't blame them, it must be quite the dramatic event. I'm not into that stuff though."_

_"Same," the girl said quickly as neither of them really had much fuel to continue the conversation._

_Markus glanced inside the window once more to find the same two boys now laughing up a storm as one of them had already thrown up all the soda. The entire room rattles with the obnoxious laughter of a dozen teens._

_"Your name is…" the girl started hesitating on the answer._

_"Markus," he answered quickly to avoid embarrassing her._

_"Right," she admitted, "I'm Monica by the way."_

_Markus smiled to save face. He wasn't disliking the conversation per say but it felt really awkward and he had now idea where to go from here._

_"Wait a minute," Monica said thoughtfully as a spontaneous energy illuminates her speech, "Aren't you in my history class?"_

_"With Mr. Ruthenthal?" Markus wondered, quickly recalling the notorious class to be an insomniac's worst nightmare._

_"Yeah," Monica exclaimed quietly, "That class is such a drag. Am I crazy or did half the class fall asleep today during the lecture? Heck, I think I might have been the only one still taking notes near the end."_

_Markus laughed as a memory of a dozen sleeping students flashes through his mind, "You're right, something about that guy just pisses me off."_

_"Is it his monotone voice?" Monica wondered playfully._

_"That or the fact that he treats military history like it's his pride and joy?" Markus pointed out._

_Monica raised an eyebrow, allowing for a slight smirk to cross her face, "Oh, are you a historian yourself."_

_Markus promptly crossed his arms into an X. "Heck no, I'll learn from history so I don't repeat it, but you won't catch me dead trying to teach it."_

_"I don't know…" Monica teased, "You sound like a philosopher now."_

_"Even harder pass," Markus asserted sternly, "But what about you, how can you stand his lessons?"_

_Monica hesitated for a moment as she pursed her lips and looked away. She scanned the area one more time before looking back at him meekly, "Promise you won't tell anyone. I feel like people would judge me for it."_

_Markus rolled his eyes attempting to make it as obvious as possible for her, "I have one friend here and he doesn't give too craps about how someone is able to pass a class he sleeps through too. There's no one I'll tell and frankly, I won't judge you even if you do say something absurd."_

_Monica shook her head slowly feeling only a bit reassured, "It's because I really want to impress my parents."_

_Markus's eyes widened a bit at the answer._

_"I know it's kind of weird for a teenager," Monica tried to defend._

_Markus couldn't help but let out a genuine laugh, "That's why?!"_

_"You said you won't laugh."_

_Markus continued to laugh for a solid three more seconds before wiping a metaphorical tear out of his eye, "That's not weird at all. Yeah other people might fault you for it but that just shows how seriously they take school," Markus smiled at Monica with as warm of an expression as he could muster, "I respect it. I think that family is really important."_

_He grew up alone, with no image of a family to speak of, so seeing the peaceful lives around him made him envious in a way. Wanting to make people who cared about you proud was an honorable pursuit in his mind, after all, he worked hard to earn Grant's recognition. He was actually surprised that Monica was insecure about that. It must mean that her friend group was not the right crowd for her._

_When Markus looked back at Monica her face could be easily read as sincerely touched. Her eyebrows were high, her eyes innocent. She looked like a child clinging on to such a simple gesture of affirmation. She didn't even have to say anything for Markus to know where this conversation needed to go._

_"Can I ask you something," Markus asked._

_Monica, in a sort of trance, shook her head to snap out of it, "Yeah, of course."_

_"Who do you hang out with?"_

_A thought visibly popped into her head which made the proceeding hesitation unnecessary. "Kennedy Johnson and Lauren Kronberg mostly."_

_Markus sighed. He, of course, didn't know either of these girls personally but he had heard plenty of things about them. They were textbook trouble. Drama, gossip, skipping class, you name it. These girls were the type of people that everyone feared, not because they were inherently scary, but because they were masters at poking their heads into situations they didn't belong. The only respectable quality they had was that they hadn't dipped their toes into drugs. Honestly hearing their names made him wonder if the whole break up scene was partially caused by them. Who's really to say at this point._

_"That's your first problem," Markus admitted, shaking his head._

_"What?" Monica suddenly argued all defensive, "They're my friends."_

_Markus bit his bottom lip debating whether to pursue this issue, but ultimately decided that he had plenty of time to help. It was not like he had ample experience in teenage friendships, but this situation was pretty universal. Someone had to break the news to her at some point._

_"Say Monica," Markus started, "What is a friend to you?"_

_Monica contemplated the idea only briefly, "Someone that I like and hang out with I guess. It's not an exact science."_

_Markus shook his head, "A friend is someone that cares about and supports you. When things get rough they've got your back… Do you know Caden Self, you know, the football team's halfback."_

_Monica confirmed with a nod._

_"He's my best friend. Do you know why we're best friends?" Markus allowed for a small moment for thought but not enough for her to reply, "It's because we look out for one another. I make sure he doesn't get flat out drunk with the team and he makes sure I don't sit at home all day. We want the best for each other. Now that I say it, I've actually had a lot of close friendships like that."_

_Monica pushed a stray strand of hair out of her face, "I guess that makes sense… it's actually oddly mature… are you trying to tell me that my friends aren't good for me? They're always helping me with tests and stuff. I don't think they are necessarily a bad influence."_

_Markus shrugged his shoulders, "I couldn't tell you one way or the other. All I'm saying is that I respect your mentality in school and wouldn't want you to give it up."_

_Suddenly, a random underclassman made the bold move to cannonball right into the pool. More than a few bystanders got mercilessly bombarded with water. This action was quickly followed up with a barrage of teens stripping halfway naked and following the first into the pool. A few girls covered their eyes at the sight of a bunch of boys in their boxers, but others simply stared, indulging their guilty pleasure. Another boy came running out of the door from the living room with a pungent odor leaking from his clothes. It quickly became evident to Markus why the sudden outburst of activity occurred._

_A moment later, Kennedy Johnson came walking out of the living room spotting Monica instantly._

_"Monica!" she exclaimed, "You'll never guess what just happened."_

_Monica smiled through her teeth, though only Markus knew that to be true._

_"I'll see you around," Markus told Monica before heading back into the house praying that Caden didn't get peer pressured into chugging the 'soda'._

Markus awakens suddenly only to vomit on the floor. His eyes tear up while he quickly repositions to a trash can in the corner of the room. A few more bouts later and he's able to wipe some spit off his lip and trudge into the kitchen to grab some cleaning agents. 

By the time he finishes cleaning the mess he made, he nearly collapses due to exhaustion. Shortly after that, his attention is captured by the sound of laughter: laughter from another episode of The Fredricks on his TV. It's a lackluster reality show about a family in middle class America, but it's about the only show he managed to record before all television ended for good. 

He takes a deep breath and lies back down on the lazy boy. The recollection of his dream, or rather memory, floods into his mind causing his heart to ache. He hates when he has those kinds of dreams; they always make him wish high school hadn't ended.

After a long pause on, he decides it's time to make himself breakfast—even if he'd rather sleep. He does a few other miscellaneous morning duties before finding himself once again in his office. 

He hasn't thrown up in years. This is genuine a shock and a concern. He's perished the thought for a while now, but could he actually be... infected? That can't be the case, it can't. He still needs to track down the Cult. There's no time to succumb to this parasitic illness. That's simply not a possibility he can afford.

Despite that scary thought, he still has so much to process at the moment. With Volker showing up and this whole fiasco with Harvey, he really can't spare much time to idle around and worry about something that might be inevitable. Either he turns or he doesn't. Given that there's no known cure, he might as well plan for the latter.

He sits in thought for a few minutes occasionally cross-referencing ideas with the map before a deciding that he needs something to eat. With a short exhale, he lifts himself out of his chair and heads for the kitchen.

As he arrives, he notices a small white piece of paper on the dining room table. It's the envelope Misty gave him. 

Out of sheer curiosity, he tears open the seal and pulls the contents out. Inside are three vouchers to Misty's mansion and card with one line on it "Day 403".

Day 403, what could that mean. If it was for Markus then it either was referring to his time being captured by the cult, his time with Allegiance One, or 403 days after the Fall. He couldn't remember any of them vividly so then he would have to put more thought into it. His 403rd day inside the cult's facilities was not something he wants to recall but he knows it wasn't out of the ordinary for that time in his life. He could easily brush past the third possibility because nothing special happened that day and he had a "dairy" to prove it. That means that it was a message from somebody about the 403rd day while fighting with Allegiance One. 

What happened then? A year after joining they were running small operations in a major city so a month after that they were stationed in a suburb outside the city. Could it be that it has something to do with…
