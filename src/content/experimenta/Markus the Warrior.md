The harbor of Pearson used to be the second largest in the country and because of that it also happened to be the second-largest pool of crashed ships. During the Fall, people made a last-ditch effort to evacuate via sea as the government directed. Sure, that may have been a good idea originally, but hundreds of thousands of scared, anxious civilians rushing to the same isolated location simultaneously did not have the intended effect. When everything fell apart and the port was overrun with parasites, all the escaping ships discovered their stowaways and sank or drifted out to sea with no one on board. Smaller vessels found their permanent graves on the bottom of the bay, while larger ships, instead crashed into each other or simply landed on a shallow section of the bay to become man-made islands. Now, every section of the port contains debris of broken-down ships with only a few larger ones still remaining mostly intact, but not serviceable. 

Section 6-A is blocked off by a barbed wire fence and remnants of military defensive barriers. This was the final standoff between the military and the overrunning monsters. The destroyed tanks, blast holes, and skeletal remains make it evident who won.

Markus vaults over a sandbag wall and crouches down next to the empty, burned out body of a jeep. Two Slender class parasites stand not 40 yards ahead overtop a fresh body—likely one of Cleo's men. 

The Slenders both have a humanoid body with long arms, bony hands harboring razor-like fingernails, a spiked tail, and an elongated neck. Their heads hang down to their chest and their vertebrae stick out their back like the thagomizer of a dinosaur. 

The class system is nothing official but a convenient way to categorize the monsters that infected the city. With each class, it becomes simpler to know what the monster's strengths are and how they can be beaten.

Markus mounts his AR to the side of the jeep; he has to make the first shot count if he wants to kill both of them. They are fast and agile so fighting two at once is a dead man's game. Though this couldn't have been what killed Cleo's squad.

He verifies that his silencer is attached and scopes in to the forehead of one of the Slenders. 

Each parasite can be killed in a unique way, but the most surefire method to bring humanoid ones down is to destroy the head or sever the limbs. Bleeding out occasionally works on parasites, but it's never a guarantee. The central processing unit, or brain by convention, is usually the safest bet.

He fires one round dropping the first stationary target, then quickly flicks the gun over to the other Slender but it is too late. The Slender notices the flash of the bullet immediately and bolts diagonally towards him. 

These damn beasts are smart enough to avoid an easy shot. 

Markus drops his AR and draws his sword. As much as he wants to waste another round on a moving object, he's been slacking in the melee combat training recently. This might provide a good exercise in his dodge and slash tactics.

The Slender closes the 40 yard distance in less than three seconds. Markus barely has time to draw his sword completely and dodge before the first swing of the Slender's blood stained claws. He luckily manages to evade the first but doesn't account for the tail. The monster's barbed tail hits him right in the shoulder sending him flying back a couple feet. Without hesitation he regains his footing and focuses back on the advancing Slender. In his momentary loss of focus, it had already closed the distance and reared its arm back to swing again. Markus reacted instinctively in his own counter strike; he steps forward right into the Slender's reach and then shoves his shoulder into the core of the 7 foot tall monster. 

The Slender loses its momentum with its swing and has to take a step back to compensate. This is its dying move as Markus slashes his sword across the neck of the Slender. He kicks the Slender back and quickly jumps back as the headless Slender begins to swing its arms wildly for a few moments before it's instinctually, violent twitching stops. The disfigured, former human finally reaches its final destination as it ragdolls onto the concrete below. 

Markus assesses his shoulder, there is no damage to him but the metal shoulder pad had deep dents in it—nothing he couldn't buffer out later. He then goes over and removes his sword from the corpse. A small trickle of green ooze leaks out of the creature's mouth along with an agonizing expression edged into its visage. 

He kicks the head away so it stops staring at him.

Markus continues on past a series of military tents. Some of the tents have large holes in them from where parasites would likely have broken through. Skeletons litter the ground both in and around such tents. This cemetery is eerily familiar to him, so he'd prefer to get through it as quickly as possible.

He continues to walk through the grid of mangled tents until his journey grinds to a screeching halt in front of a single, bland tent. The tattered tarp that once guarded against rain and wind is now but a shredded pile of useless fabric. Markus turns and lets his gaze wonder as he recalls the vivid memories of three years ago. Of course this has to be _that_ port? His heart burns with memories that he wishes he could forget. He'd gone almost a month now without dwelling on this forlorn memory, but now it seems it was all for naught. Sometimes he wishes his tear glands would work like they're supposed to so he could relieve himself, even momentarily, of this anguish.

His saving grace is no hero or friend with a passionate monologue. No, what rescues him from that pitiful state is nothing more than the current state of his life calling. A dull rumbling arises from further down the port.

He swiftly crouches down inside a nearby intact tent and waits. The rumbling gets louder and louder until a pair of old, ragged shoes suddenly tramples by. Then another pair passes by and another and another until an army of footsteps come running by the tent. Markus waits praying none of them would pass through that loose flap of the tent. An hour passes… or at least that's how it feels. Really only a minute or two goes by until the last pair of footsteps crosses his field of view. It must have been a horde of Infecteds—the parasite he killed earlier today was one such example. 

An Infected is a lightly mutated human. They're essentially the lowest class of parasites as they're threat level is no different from a human with an unpredictable set of tools. Their lack of massive body modifications separates them from more distinctive parasites like Slenders and Demos.

It's not uncommon for Infecteds to group together, but they typically take shelter in interior spaces for feeding. These "hives", as many call them, provide shelter and sustenance. Why a whole horde of Infecteds are wandering outside, it can only mean something died recently.

Markus waits for two more minutes before opening the flap with his gun drawn prepared for a potential confrontation. Thankfully, nothing awaits him outside except the same sorry sight of a forgotten camp. 

What lies beyond the military camp is the mass grave site of shipping containers. It's one of the only outdoor feeding grounds which is why there is just a densely populated group. From what he remembers, the parasites will often drag victims—humans and animals alike—to this port to feast. It's also the primary reason for why this port has never been completely looted or explored since the Fall. The abnormal part is that parasites typically take shelter indoors, so an outdoor feeding ground is quite peculiar.

The real question, however, is what could have caused them to all run away?

The shipping yard consists of half a mile of large single colored metal boxes that were once carried on cargo ships. Now they provide a natural barrier between the secluded feeding zone and the rest of the port. 

Markus holds his gun at the ready and advances into the bloody, metal labyrinth. 

Silence is a very common occurrence in the world these days, but in situations like these it is a very dangerous thing. He scans each and every opening for enemies. 

Left, right, a few steps forward, over a fallen forklift. Markus finally arrives at the warehouse unimpeded which only adds to the tension. 

The warehouse is small, probably the size of four shipping containers together. Blood stains of whatever sorry creature got caught lay around the walls of the warehouse. The pile of decomposing flesh is taller than Markus himself which reeks a foul odor so strong that it makes his eyes water. 

The already disturbing sight only induces more fear as it jerks to the side, First an arm comes out, then the other, finally the torso and head lifts out from the blood and guts. Two bright red eyes beam down on Markus. The mighty Demo is at least 20 feet tall with a body bulky enough to lift a tank and throw it. 

Demos are some of the most dangerous parasites because their skin is always plated with boney armor that can barely be pierced by a tank shell. This particular monstrosity wears that feature with pride all across its torso, arms, and legs. Even the joints are adequately protected with a sort of chainmail-like layer of dense organic matter. Of all the different parasite classes, Demos are easily in the top five most dangerous.

Slow to start, the Demo reaches for and grabs a chunk of wet, crimson flesh. Markus, however, takes this time to run and take cover. He ducks into an open shipping container just in time to dodge the barrage of guts that get catapulted by. The Demo grunts and then massive stomping begins to get closer and closer.

Markus dives out of the container evading the Demo crashing into the wall and knocking over the pile of metal storage units. It then takes a step back and refocuses on Markus.

Of all the positions he could be in, this is obviously not ideal, but there's no turning back now. Markus scans the surroundings for any promising opportunity to no avail so instead he raises his gun and takes two shots at the eyes of the Demo. Both are futile as the Demo raises its bulky arm to shield its eyes and charges forward still protecting its face. The parasite's hide is too thick to cut through so the only option is drive out of the way at the last moment. The Demo intelligently buries one foot in the ground to stop its momentum in front of him. It then turns to the, now prone, Markus and lifts its foot up in the air.

He can't roll out of the way, so in a sudden moment of reaction he downs four more bullets into the face of the Demo. As luck would have it one lands and takes out an eye. This causes the Demo to roar in pain and smash his raised foot down not four inches from Markus' side. He takes the opportunity to backwards roll to his feet and sprint over to the container behind the Demo. He then wall runs up the side of the container, grabs on to the top, and pulls himself up.

The disoriented Demo quickly shakes off the shock of losing half its vision and searches around for the little worm. Markus thinks about jumping over and manually dropping the grenade into its mouth, but getting within grasping range of the Demo is a one way street, the only safe alternative is something much more risky. He looks back to see a last line of containers sitting right in front of the calm waves of the bay.

After a 180 spin the Demo finally locks onto its target. 

Markus gets a two step running start before he jumps across the gap of containers and barely makes it to the other side. The Demo wastes no time going around and instead pushes the containers out of the way making space for it to advance towards him once more. 

Markus intentionally keeps his AR down and once the Demo gets closer he decides to act. He quickly draws his pistol and takes a shot at the Demo's face. The Demo predictably lifts its arm up just in time to take the blunt of the round. Firing another round, he rips the grenade off his belt and pops the pin. 

One. 

The Demo rushes forward with its arm still shielding its face and vision as it hits the container Markus is standing on. 

Two. 

He jumps off as the container goes crashing into the waves. The Demo stops just shy of falling into the water. 

Three. 

Markus lands with a roll and begins sprinting away. He only gets a few yards when he turns and chucks the grenade right at the turning Demo. 

Four. 

Markus continues his mad dash away as the Demo finishes turning around inches from the edge of the port. 

Five. 

The grenade explodes.

The blast forces the Demo a step back, only there's no ground behind it to land. It's leg slips off the edge which leads to its massive body plummeting into the waves. Given the inherent nature of having such bulky armor, the Demo sinks to the rocky bay floor like a literal rock. 

Markus' adrenaline rush ceases and he collapses to the ground panting. It's been a minute since he had a fight like that. At some point he even finds himself laughing… that is, until he feels the port itself begin to shake. 

Without thinking, he's already on his feet rushing over to where the Demo disappeared. There, right where the waves splash against the port is the Demo clinging to the concrete wall like it's a jungle gym; its powerful fingers creating their own holds in the port foundation. The Demo pulls itself a bit higher and slams its hand into the concrete creating another hold. With its one good eye, it spots Markus and lets out a deafening roar.

Markus sighs, "You bastards never learn."

He promptly lifts his gun and fires a round into the Demo's other eye causing it to try in vain to protect its already blind face. Then, whether it be the poorly formed hold or the cracking of the port itself, the Demo loses grip with its other arm and collapses into the water, this time for good.

Markus runs his forearm across his brow to remove some sweat as a subtle clap arises in the distance.

"Impressive Markus… as per usual." 

Markus flips around to see the new variable in the situation. It is a man dressed in all black with a red Japanese Oni mask and a crimson crest on his chest: the Death Order. It's a group of the highly specialized parasite slayers. They rarely engage in the quarrels of the other factions, but if they did, he'd put their fighting force as third in the city. 

"You managed to take down a whole Demo single-handedly, that one was giving my apprentice trouble for quite some time, and you just killed it like that."

"Who are you?" Markus asks, though by the sound of his voice, he already has a suspect in mind.

"My apologies, you probably don't recognize me through the mask," the man takes off his intimidating mask revealing a slightly aged face. "It hasn't been that long since we last spoke."

"Ah, Darek," Markus knows him well, he is the Death Order's primary recruitment agent, he has been recruiting promising parasite slayers since the Fall. Over the years, he tried on multiple occasions to recruit Markus to their ranks only for it to result in little success. Markus can't say that he particularly cares for any of these parasite obsessed individuals, but Darek is definitely the outlier as a man seems the most down-to-earth and least idealistic. The entire faction is hell-bent on freeing the city of these parasites, but Darek does a good job of preventing it from becoming his personality.

"I fully understand your position on the Death Order but you are always welcome to join our ranks at any time. To most of us, you are a legend, more revered then even out leader," Darek gives a slight smile which brings out the age and wisdom in his sixty year old face.

"Is that all you came here for?" Markus replies as he pops out his pistol magazine and begins refilling it with some loose rounds in his backpack pocket.

"You got me there, if it wasn't already obvious your services have always been in high demand. The Death Order has been getting more business in the past month. That's all due to the fact that the parasites have been getting bigger, stronger, faster, and, more importantly, _smarter_."

Markus looks up but promptly rolls his eyes dismissively, "Tell me about it, this one blocked its eyes."

Darek nods, "True, thought there's also the issue with the Colossal class."

Markus pushes the filled magazine back into his gun and holsters it at his side.

Darek continues, "There have been a few sightings recently, there are very few people I trust can handle such a threat, but this one is even bigger than anything we've seen before. I even hesitate to offer the bounty to you."

"Get to the point."

With a small sigh, Darek responds, "Alright… I won't ask you to handle it yourself, but be aware that your services may be required if it strays from its docile nature." 

Markus crosses his arms, "It's unlike you to doubt the skill of the Death Order."

Darek closes his eyes and exhales as he puts his mask back on, "Six of ours died in an operation the day before last. It was the worst incident of the last year. Those deaths were at the hands of this Colossal."

"I see," Markus quietly responds.

"Just take it as a warning if nothing else. With that... Farewell and good luck," Darek takes his leave and disappears with a light tapping of footsteps left behind.

Colossals. The word itself is a bit of an under-the-table dirty word in Pearson. Colossals are extremely rare but they're also one of the most dangerous there is. If the class name itself wasn't enough to explain their danger, then it should be no surprise that they're massive and incredibly hard to bring down. The last time a Colossal appeared, nearly a hundred people lost their lives. 

Markus refocuses on the pressing matter at hand, namely the warehouse. He makes his way to the front side of the building and finds the door. There is a pile of guts right next to the entrance, but just enough room left to get through. Next to the door a keypad lies on the wall. Markus types in the memorized passcode 3-0-5-1-3-2. A click signified the unlocking of the door and he wastes no more time anticipating what will be on the other side.

Metal shelves holding small, wooden cargo crates line the interior of the warehouse. These crates could easily hold valuable military supplies and other materials, but none of those are what interested Markus. Judging by the size and identification numbers tattooed on the shells of the crates, he can already tell it is all non-combat equipment. Things like tents, cookware, and miscellaneous other things; all things he had an ample amount of. He will just report all of it back to Cleo. 

Four rows of shelving lined the room concealing a desk in the back corner. On the desk sits a single item, a laptop. Laptops are a rare find, and even rarer if they still have power. Markus carefully makes his way to it, checking for any traps that might have been set up in such a conspicuous place. 

Once he reaches the desk he opens the laptop fully expecting there to be no power and, to his surprise, it turns on. A dim login screen flashes to life in which Markus types in a specific key. An old friend of his used to hack religiously and told him about a master key that could breach any military computer. His friend explained it to him—something about a supply chain vulnerability—but honestly he couldn't follow all of it. Sure it was highly illegal for him to know something like that, but with the nature of their work, desperate times called for desperate measures. One military didn't trump the interest of the entire world. At least, that's what his friend used to say. 

Thankfully the master key still works and the screen switches to a video recording, probably the same one that was being watched right before this place got abandoned. Markus takes one last glance at his surroundings before pressing play.

The screen flashes to a man, about fifty, wearing a naval captain uniform, "This is a message for Colonel Prown. Things are getting worse by the day. The parasite is relentless and many of our strongholds have already fallen. As a last resort we are sending VIPs to Operation Preservation. _You_ are lucky enough to be one of those VIPs. A chopper will pick you up in the morning. I understand that you have to leave your unit to die, but we need you and your research team with us immediately... It's for the greater good." 

The man pauses for a moment looking off screen to something behind the recording device, "If it helps you sleep at night, you can tell your unit to move to these coordinates," coordinates appear on screen but he isn't able to decipher them as the begins to glitch out and tear any character continuity. Only when the coordinates disappear does the video return to a normal resolution and consistency, "That's the location of the mayor's bunker, they may have a fighting chance if they find it in time… Also, one last thing, this is classified information so make sure you are the only ears that hear this—" in the middle of his sentence the laptop shuts off. 

Markus presses the spacebar to make sure it didn't go to sleep or something only for his hand to get minorly burned by blazing hot keys. He glances down to find the small keys of the laptop slowly becoming a gelatin-like goop. It's an indicator that this particular laptop is a special model that literally melts down if it's used beyond a certain predefined scope of dates. It's a very uncommon model—only cultist insiders use it.

"Damn!" Markus grunts and slams his fist down on the table. He was so close to getting more information. Information on a lead—a lead to the Mayor's bunker—a lead to the Cult. The Mayor was a very rich man who, when the outbreak was on the rise, built a massive bunker somewhere and stored enough supplies to last him and a hundred other people for thirty years. Markus didn't need the supplies, per say, but he has reason to believe a cultist is down there. That's his real target. 

In his mental rant, he notices a piece of paper on the floor right next to the desk. Lifting it above the table, he inspects its contents thoroughly. A single handwritten sentences is written "Bunker - Harvey's House". Markus stares at it blankly for a few moments trying to find something he's missing. Nothing. But Harvey's House is still a clue, something that could lead him in the right direction, even if only a minute amount. His thoughts are once again interrupted by the buzzing of his phone in his pocket.

It is a message from "No Caller ID". A clear indicator that General Reign wants to speak with him.
